## 2. Complement discriminants

Complete transverse saturation is equivalent to the Joukowski image being contained in `J_s`.

Equivalently, every lane label outside `J_s` must have zero Joukowski preimages.

A lane label `j` has preimage count

`1+Legendre(j^2-2s,q)`.

Thus every outside label must satisfy

`Legendre(j^2-2s,q)=-1`.

The outside labels occur in opposite pairs, so only `r` conditions are distinct.

Write the positive outside labels as

`j=(s-1)/2+t`, `t=1,...,r`.

Modulo q, with `s=q-2r`, put `u=r-t`. Then

`4*(j^2-2s)`

`=(2u+1)^2+16r mod q`.

Since4 is a square, define the fixed integers

`A_(r,u)=(2u+1)^2+16r`, `u=0,...,r-1`.

Freeze:

`q=s+2r IS AN EXTRA SATURATION GATE`

iff

`Legendre(A_(r,u),q)=-1 FOR ALL u=0,...,r-1`.

The sector count disappears from the list of test integers; it survives only through the prime `q=s+2r` at which the symbols are evaluated.

## 5. Multiquadratic compatibility criterion

`alpha_u=[A_(r,u)] in Q^*/Q^(*)^2`.

Let

`V_r=span_F2{alpha_0,...,alpha_(r-1)}`

We seek primes q for which every

`Legendre(A_(r,u),q)=-1`.

A prescribed all-minus sign pattern defines a homomorphism

`ell:V_r->F_2`,

`ell(alpha_u)=1`,

iff it respects every square-class relation.

Therefore the sign pattern is compatible iff:

`EVERY SUBSET OF THE alpha_u WHOSE PRODUCT IS A SQUARE HAS EVEN CARDINALITY`.

Equivalently:

`NO ODD-CARDINALITY PRODUCT OF A_(r,u) IS A RATIONAL SQUARE`.

When compatible, let

`K_r=Q(sqrt(alpha): alpha in V_r)`.

`COMPATIBLE OVERHANG r`
