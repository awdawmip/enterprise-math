### Why eight steps always suffice (hand proof)

Fix `π,w`. Work in `V=R^{10}` and let `K_h` be the span of the two observer columns `F,P` and their `T`-iterates through degree `h`. `F,P` are linearly independent, so `dim K_0=2`. If `K_{h+1}=K_h`, then `TK_h⊂K_h`, and all later vectors lie in `K_h`. Otherwise dimension increases by at least one. Since `dim V=10`, `K_8` is invariant under `T`. Therefore agreement on steps `0,...,8` implies agreement on all future steps. This uses no assumption about the weights beyond the finite matrix definition; the uniform eight-step bound is an independent linear-algebra lemma reused from R762CR.
