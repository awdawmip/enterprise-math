## Model and precise result

An event of support `S` chooses one edge between two of the three Ports contained in `S`. A closed legal Port history is a connected even-degree multigraph on its positive-degree Ports `V`; it can begin at any prescribed `p∈V`. The support multiplicity vector is `n=(n_S)`.

## Why the finite domains are complete

An all-positive-support history cannot have two inactive Ports sharing an axis: those two are contained in the same support triangle and leave fewer than two Ports for a positive event. Thus inactive Ports constitute a matching in `K_6`. These matchings number `1+15+45+15=76`, grouped by sizes 0, 1, 2, 3.

Seven chosen triple supports yield `C(20,7)=77,520` support masks. Apply the `S_6` action generated by the five adjacent axis swaps to these masks. Its 161 orbits account for all 77,520 masks. An exact union-find test finds 23,700 all-essential masks forming 55 of these orbits. The action also permutes Ports, allowed edges and inactive matchings and preserves the target theorem.

For each representative family use the **smallest support index in the seven** as the triple support to reduce to one; all other six triple types remain three, and all thirteen double types remain two. The resulting vector has exactly `1+6×3+13×2=45` events.
