Define A ⪯ B when, for every remaining B action b, there is an A action a of price at most price(b) such that **each** maximal mask S of a is contained (under the fixed bijection) in at least one maximal mask T of b. The matching T may depend on S and the choice a may depend on b. Then for *every* integer f>=0,

    V_f(A) <= V_f(B).

**Proof.** Simulate an optimal B policy. Whenever it requests action b, execute a certified action a. Along the resulting transcript, each mismatch against the virtual B mask is also a mismatch against the actual A support. Hence any true hypothesis respecting at most f actual errors also respects at most f virtual errors. The B policy therefore identifies the same hypothesis and terminates; the simulated A procedure incurs no greater cost on any transcript, because price(a)<=price(b) at every step. The result is independent of f. The theorem requires *joint mask inclusion*, not pairwise separation or equality of marginal observations.
