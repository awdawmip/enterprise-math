## 1. General slack-k basis quotient

After maximal tight-cut contraction, all remaining exclusive targets U are matchable, with s(V)=c(V)-f_U(V), s(empty)=0 and s(V)>=1 for nonempty V. For additional labeled target subset Q, r(Q)=H(U union Q)-|U|=|Q|-D(Q), D(Q)=max_V[f_Q(V)-s(V)], where f_Q(V) counts targets wholly confined to V. s is submodular. Fix a resource set B with slack k and an independent set I of k extra targets confined to B (independent means r(I)=k). For any Q containing I, any V satisfies s(V intersect B)>=f_I(V intersect B)=f_I(V) by independence. Thus s(V union B)-s(V)<=k-f_I(V), while f_Q(V union B)-f_Q(V)>=k-f_I(V). Some maximizing cut for D(Q) therefore contains B. Adding any distinct extra target x confined to B raises D by exactly one (the maximizing cut increases by one, and no cut increases by more than one). Hence r(Q+x)=r(Q). Equivalently, keep I and delete any number of other confined targets without changing rank. The statement applies for arbitrary outside targets, for fixed U, capacities and adjacency; target labels and deletion multiplicities are retained. Rank is preserved, deficiency requires adding back the deleted cardinality. This generalizes GBE slack-one representative reduction.

## 2. Complete rank-two classification

Work on the fixed simplified population X after loop and parallel removal (GBE). Every singleton and pair is independent. A triple is dependent iff its three neighborhoods are contained in a slack-two resource cut: Hall deficiency forces a cut containing all three since all proper subsets are independent, and pair independence forces slack >=2. Conversely such a cut limits rank to two. For each slack-two cut B form group G(B) of simplified targets confined to B. Retain inclusion-maximal groups of size >=3. If G(B) and G(C) have two common targets, s(B intersect C)>=2 by independence; submodularity then gives s(B union C)<=2, with >=2 by the same pair. Thus their union is realized within another slack-two group. Distinct maximal groups intersect in at most one target. Every dependent triple is captured; maximal groups are rank-two flats of size >=3 on the simplified population (any extra element with rank still two gives a dependent triple with a fixed pair, and the two groups merge). For any Q subset X, visit groups in any order, retaining any two currently present if more than two remain. Each step is the slack-k basis quotient with k=2 and preserves the rank of the entire current set, even when groups intersect. This is a sequential reduction, not independent simultaneous deletion prescriptions. Full labeled membership and multiplicity allow exact deficit recovery. No claim for arbitrary future changes to U, capacities, adjacency or physical configurations.

In disjoint resource pools, s is additive. A slack-two cut is either a slack-two cut of one pool or union of two slack-one cuts from different pools, since every nonempty local cut has positive integer slack. The latter includes genuinely cross-bank lines; inspecting each bank separately would miss them. The present implementation scans local cuts and forms the required cross pairs; no measured speed improvement. A general obstruction to fixed rank cutoff: for k+1 unit resources and one exclusive target with all resources allowed, add k+1 labeled singleton-neighborhood targets. Every <=k subset extends the exclusive matching; all k+1 together have increment k. For k>=3 there is no dependent triple yet a nonzero deficiency, so rank-two completeness for frozen records is not a universal theorem.
