## 1. Bounded-slack completion theorem
After GBB contraction, let U be the fully matchable remaining exclusive targets, C positive-integer capacity resources, E(u) their nonempty adjacency, f_U(V) the number of targets wholly confined to V, and s(V)=c(V)-f_U(V). Every nonempty V has s(V)>=1. Fix integer k>=1 and any full matching of U, with owner sets M(j). Build a directed capacitated graph with resource vertices, target vertices and a sink. For each matched u at j put j->u of cost 1, u->a of cost k+1 for every a in E(u), and j->sink of cost c_j-|M(j)| if positive. Costs are exact integers. For every edge subset F with total cost <=k, let L(F) be the resources unable to reach sink after deletion of F. Then the inclusion-maximal nonempty L(F) are exactly the inclusion-maximal nonempty resource sets V with s(V)<=k.

Proof: the set of all unreachable vertices is closed under undeleted edges, and all outgoing cut edges belong to F. Since target->resource arcs cost k+1, every unreachable target has all neighbors in L(F). The outgoing cost is at least the sum of unused slots in L(F) plus the count of occupants of its resources not among the unreachable targets, hence >=c(L(F))-f_U(L(F))=s(L(F)). Thus each candidate is admissible. Conversely for any V with s(V)<=k, take the vertex set V plus exactly the targets confined to V. All such targets are assigned inside V, and this set has outgoing cost exactly s(V): free slots plus occupied slots whose occupant is not confined. Delete those outgoing edges. All vertices in the set are now unreachable, so V is contained in an admissible L(F). Both containments imply equality of maximal families. Matching choice cannot alter the resulting family, since the family depends only on s. Weighted edges must be charged their entire cost, not merely their number. No claim that maximal cuts recover all exact-slack resource sets themselves.

With P deletable edges, F_k=sum_{j=0}^k binom(P,j) is an upper bound on probes (integer costs >=1). For fixed k, this is polynomial in P. After matching, graph construction and each reachability probe are linear in explicit graph size, plus enumeration overhead and maximal-mask filtering (naive quadratic in number of distinct masks, times mask width). Do not claim end-to-end acceleration merely from probe counts; bankwise enumeration can be faster at small width. The procedure extends GBE's single-edge construction.

## 2. Exact bounded observer and seed closure
Let M_j be the maximal slack<=j family, adding the empty set at j=0. For every added labeled subset Q, put f_Q(V)=# targets wholly confined to V. Define D_K(Q)=max_{s(V)<=K}(f_Q(V)-s(V)). Then
D_K(Q)=max_{0<=j<=K, W in M_j}(f_Q(W)-j).
Proof: each right candidate is bounded by D_K since s(W)<=j. For each admissible V take j=s(V) and a maximal W containing V; f_Q(W)>=f_Q(V), so the reverse bound follows. Consequently r(Q)=|Q|-D_K(Q) whenever |Q|<=K+1: any strictly positive deficit requires slack <=|Q|-1; zero is supplied by the empty set for nonempty-neighborhood Q (or counts loops exactly). More generally the integer threshold r(Q)<=L, for 0<=L<|Q|, is equivalent to D_L(Q)>=|Q|-L: every witnessing V satisfies s(V)<=f_Q(V)-(|Q|-L)<=L. This preserves all such labeled queries at once under fixed U,c,E and keeps original memberships; it is not a quotient of physical configurations. These consequences are proved, not separately benchmarked.

For any fixed subset I of additional targets, construct maximal tight cut W_I of U union I with the GBC reverse reachability algorithm. For each x outside I, r(I+x)=r(I) iff E(x) is contained in W_I, by GBC insertion rule (or the maximum deficiency equality). Therefore the closure on the fixed labeled ground set X is I union {x outside I:E(x) subset W_I}. The explicit inclusion of I is necessary; a seed can have a neighbor outside W_I, e.g. two unit resources and one seed with both neighbors. For independent seed I of size k, this gives its exact rank-k closure; every rank-k flat is the closure of any independent basis, by the matching matroid rank law (equivalently repeated monotone tight-cut insertion). This is a target-driven alternative to scanning cuts. For simplified X after loop and parallel removal, every pair is independent. Taking closure of each pair and keeping unique groups of size>=3 reconstructs precisely GBF's maximal rank-two groups. If the entire simplified X is independent, no group of size>=3 exists and all seed queries may be skipped. For deficient X that skip is not permitted; absence of rank-two groups need not imply higher-rank independence. Full masks and labels remain available for subsequent operations.
