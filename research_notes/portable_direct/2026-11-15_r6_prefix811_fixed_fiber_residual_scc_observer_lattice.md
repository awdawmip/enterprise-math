## 1. Domain and question

Let `G=(U,V,E)` be a finite **named bipartite multigraph**. Each distinct named edge `e=(u,v)` has an integer capacity `K_e>=0`, including separate capacities for parallel edges. Write `B` for the unsigned vertex–edge incidence matrix and fix integral margins `b`. Assume the bounded integer fiber

`F(b,K)={x in Z^E : Bx=b and 0<=x_e<=K_e}`

is nonempty and choose **any one feasible** `x` in it. Externally fixed named edges may first be subtracted from capacities, margins and observation offsets; the residual graph then has this same form.

For a declared family of `k` integer linear observers, let `A in Z^{k x E}` and `W_A(z)=Az`. We ask for the exact additive subgroup generated by actual differences `A(y-x)` with `y in F(b,K)`, **for these fixed capacities and margins**. PREFIX-810 characterized the universal group as capacities and margins vary; PREFIX-810A showed that a fixed fundamental-cycle basis may fail as a set of *feasible* moves in one bounded fiber. The present theorem replaces universal undirected cycle availability by a one-state, capacity-sensitive directed residual graph.

## 2. Construct the residual directed multigraph

At the chosen feasible `x`, form a directed graph `D_x` on `U disjoint_union V`. For each **named edge** `e=(u,v)`:

- if `x_e<K_e`, create an arc `a_e^+: u -> v` with observer weight vector `c(a_e^+)=A_e` (column `e` of `A`);
- if `x_e>0`, create an arc `a_e^-: v -> u` with weight `c(a_e^-)=-A_e`.

Keep both arcs when both operations are allowed. A directed cycle may be of length two for parallel named edges or for opposite arcs belonging to one named edge; a latter two-arc cycle has zero net change and zero observer signature.

Take the strongly connected components (SCCs) of `D_x` and retain **only arcs whose endpoints lie in the same SCC**, calling this subgraph `H_x`. Every directed cycle lies in `H_x`, and each arc in `H_x` belongs to a directed cycle.

Choose an arbitrary spanning forest `T` of the **underlying undirected arc multigraph** of `H_x`. For each observer coordinate, assign integer vertex potentials `p` recursively along tree arcs so that `c(a)=p(head(a))-p(tail(a))` on every tree arc. For each non-tree arc set

`gamma_a=c(a)-[p(head(a))-p(tail(a))] in Z^k`.

These are the **residual-SCC fundamental signatures**. No explicit enumeration of feasible full tables or simple directed cycles is needed.

## 3. Main theorem — exact fixed-fiber observer-difference group

**Theorem 811.1 (residual-SCC lattice formula).** Under the assumptions above, for every chosen feasible table `x` and every integer observer matrix `A`,

`D_F(A) := < A(y)-A(x) : y in F(b,K) >_Z = sum_{a in E(H_x)\T} Z gamma_a  subseteq Z^k.`

In particular the right side is **independent of which feasible starting table** `x` is chosen. It gives the exact integral congruence lattice of all observable differences in this *specific bounded fiber*.

**Proof, step 1 (differences are residual circulations).** Let `y in F` and `d=y-x`. On each named edge with `d_e>0`, use `d_e` copies of its forward residual arc; for `d_e<0`, use `-d_e` copies of its backward residual arc. Their availability follows from `0<=y_e<=K_e`. Since `Bx=By`, each vertex has equal directed indegree and outdegree, with multiplicity. This is a nonnegative integral circulation on `D_x`. It decomposes into directed simple cycles. Its observer arc-weight sum is exactly `Ad=A(y)-A(x)`, so every actual difference is an integral sum of signatures of directed cycles of `D_x`.

**Step 2 (directed cycles are actual one-step differences).** Let `C` be any directed simple cycle in `D_x`. Increase each named edge corresponding to its forward arc by one, decrease each edge corresponding to its backward arc by one (opposite uses of the same edge cancel if present). By the residual arc conditions, the resulting table `x^C` satisfies every capacity bound; directed flow balance preserves all margins. Hence `x^C in F` and `A(x^C-x)=sum_{a in C}c(a)`. Consequently, the group generated by actual differences equals the group generated by all directed-cycle signatures.

**Step 3 (integral circulation lattice inside SCCs).** Regard arcs of `H_x` as separate integer coordinates, with the usual **directed** vertex–arc incidence matrix `N`. Every nonnegative integral circulation decomposes into directed cycles. Conversely, every signed integral circulation `z in ker_Z N` is an **integer** combination of directed cycles: because each arc of `H_x` lies on a directed cycle, summing one containing cycle for each arc produces a strictly positive integral circulation `h` on all arcs; take a sufficiently large integer `M` so that both `z+Mh` and `Mh` are nonnegative integral circulations. Their directed-cycle decompositions, subtracted, give `z`. Arcs between different SCCs are on no directed cycle, and a circulation on the full directed graph assigns zero total flow to them (the SCC condensation is a DAG). Thus the integer group of all directed cycles is `ker_Z N` on `H_x`.

**Step 4 (integral fundamental cycles).** On any finite undirected multigraph, the signed fundamental-cycle vectors of a spanning forest constitute a `Z`-basis of the directed-incidence integer kernel: eliminate each non-tree arc coefficient from a circulation by subtracting its fundamental vector; the remaining circulation on a forest is zero by leaf elimination. The observer image of the fundamental vector for chord `a` is exactly `gamma_a` (up to the convention of global sign), because tree-edge costs are potential differences. Combining steps 1–4 proves the formula and independence of `x`: both sides equal the intrinsically defined group of all within-fiber differences. QED.

**Corollary 811.2 (fixed-fiber constancy in linear time).** A scalar integer observer `w` is constant over `F(b,K)` if and only if **every** residual-SCC fundamental signature vanishes; equivalently, its residual arc weights admit vertex potentials inside each SCC. After a feasible table is found, SCC decomposition, a spanning forest and potential checks take `O(|U|+|V|+|E|)` integer-arithmetic operations. With `k` observers, forming the full signature matrix takes `O(k(|U|+|V|+|E|))`; Smith normal form can subsequently characterize its subgroup and congruences. These operation bounds exclude the cost of finding an initial feasible table and the bit complexity of optional Smith reduction.

**Corollary 811.3 (scalar exact congruence modulus).** Set `g=gcd({|gamma_a|})` (with `g=0` when there are no nonzero generators). Then

`< W_w(y)-W_w(x): y in F >_Z = g Z`.

All attainable values lie in the coset `W_w(x)+g Z`. For `g=0`, there is precisely one attainable value. For `g>0`, this is a congruence certificate, **not** an assertion that every integer in the coset is attained or that every value between the minimum and maximum is attained.

## 4. Discriminating exact witnesses

**Bounded fiber constant, universally nonconstant.** On the complete named 2x3 graph with all six edge capacities one, choose row margins `(1,1)` and column margins `(0,1,1)`. The two tables, in row-major edge order, are
`x=(0,0,1,0,1,0)` and `y=(0,1,0,0,0,1)`.
The observer `W_0(z)=z_(0,0)` is identically zero on the fiber, although its weight vector has nonzero alternating curl on an *unconstrained* full-graph 4-cycle involving column 0. In `D_x`, the column-0 forward arcs have no return arcs and are excluded from `H_x`. The residual-SCC signatures are all zero. By contrast `W_1(z)=z_(0,1)` takes values zero and one; its scalar residual gcd is one.

**Integral lattice versus holes.** In the 2x2 unit-capacity fiber with both row and column margins `(1,1)`, the only tables are `(1,0,0,1)` and `(0,1,1,0)`. For `D(z)=z_(0,0)+z_(1,1)`, the attainable set is `{0,2}` and `g=2`. The local congruence is exact; the intermediate value one is not attained.

## 5. Complete finite exact validation

The executable `PREFIX811_R6_FIXED_FIBER_SCC_LATTICE_CHECK.js` uses only integer arithmetic. For each declared capacity vector, it **enumerates all integer source tables** `0<=x_e<=K_e`, partitions them by every realized row/column margin vector, selects the first table of each nonempty fiber, computes `D_x` and its SCC/undirected forest, and compares the predicted fundamental signature gcd with the independent exhaustive gcd of all actual observer differences `W(y)-W(x)`. For every multi-table fiber it also reanchors at its last table and repeats a fixed set of observer checks. The program checks the two stated witnesses explicitly.
