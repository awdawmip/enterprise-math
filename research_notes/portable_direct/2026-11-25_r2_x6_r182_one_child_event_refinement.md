## 2. The one-child sufficiency theorem

For an original root sign class C, let S_+(r), S_-(r) be the actual first-clipped support-component closures compressed to their two extreme endpoints per retained support, as R179. Let F_sigma(r) be the boolean result of the R178 fixed-endpoint depth-five necessary capacity relaxation for that one child. The full relaxed root feasibility is F_+(r) AND F_-(r). Six-comparison correctness requires both true; a false result in either child excludes r.

For each sigma independently, let A_sigma(C) be the retained static endpoint rays in that child plus the three auxiliary axes, J_sigma(C) the retained dynamic support normals. These labeled sets and their orientations are constant throughout the relative interior C by the fixed original ternary signs and exact component clipping. Define E_sigma(C) as the nonzero varying primitive event rays up to sign
   l cross (a cross b), l in J_sigma(C), a,b in A_sigma(C).
Varying is tested by opposite strict signs on the extreme rays of closure(C). The R181 event list is the union E_+ union E_-.

Claim: If r,r' in C have equal ternary signs on E_sigma(C), then F_sigma(r)=F_sigma(r') for every fixed remaining depth.

Proof: With one dynamic ray p=orientation*(r cross l), its determinant with two static rays is the oriented linear event r dot [l cross (a cross b)]. Nonvarying events have a fixed sign throughout the relative interior; exact zeros are retained. A triple with two dynamic rays and one static ray factors, up to fixed orientations, as det(r,l_i,l_j)*(r dot a). In this frozen bank there are no common strict interior crossings of two active components (R180/R181). Thus two distinct retained strict dynamic rays cannot coincide while both remain in their actual components; the first determinant never changes sign in C, and the second factor has its fixed original sign. The case of the same support is identically dependent. Three dynamic rays lie on the root equality line and are dependent; three static rays are fixed. Axes span the auxiliary coefficient space, so rank-three oriented determinant signs preserve all realizable ternary observation patterns by the R180 sign-realizability argument. Apply this independently to the chosen child's endpoint labels. The later fixed-endpoint recurrence depends only on these patterns, the endpoint-to-support incidence and remaining depth, all fixed. This proves the claim without imposing a common event refinement on the opposite child.

Corollary (separate feasible carriers): let T_sigma={r in C:F_sigma(r)=true}. A whole root class is excluded if either T_sigma is empty, or more generally T_+ intersection T_- is empty.
