# EC49FWN — Three-hole blind CRT inverse and parity decoder

Date: 2026-11-22 (Asia/Shanghai). Frozen lineage: BRC16943, previous EC49FWM at 1b8aadb66a9db1b24e99b8bd45e86dbd1861c573. Independent portable auxiliary mathematics. Status: AUTHOR_PROVED / EXACT_FINITE_CROSSCHECK / PARENT_DEPENDENT / NOT_REVIEWED.

## Exact theorem

Let n>=1 and odd q>=5 satisfy gcd(n,q)=1. Take a nonempty U subset of Z/n, w=|U|, and an unknown three-element hole set Z={z1,z2,z3} subset of Z/q. Put V=(Z/q)\Z and W=U x V under CRT. The model (exactly three holes), n,q, and all *indexed* defects Delta_W(a,b)=|W triangle ((a,b)-W)| are given. Neither w nor Z is known.

Write Delta_U(a)=|U triangle(a-U)|, kappa(b)=#{(z,z') in Z x Z:z+z'=b mod q}, and R(b)=sum_a Delta_W(a,b).

The following identities hold, exactly:

(1) Delta_W(a,b)=(q-6+kappa(b))*Delta_U(a)+2*w*(3-kappa(b)).
(2) R(b)=2*n*(q-3)*w-2*(q-6+kappa(b))*w^2.

The possible multiplicity histograms of kappa are:
- GENERIC: values 0,1,2 occur q-6,3,3 times; 3 does not occur.
- ONE_AP: values 0,1,2,3 occur q-5,2,2,1 times. For q=5 the zero-level is absent.
- EQUILATERAL: values 0,3 occur q-3,3 times, only if 3 divides q.

For q=5 all triples are ONE_AP. For q>=7 the minimum of kappa is zero. These cases exhaust all three-hole subsets.

The complete indexed spectrum of W **uniquely recovers** (Z,w,Delta_U) by:

(A) If R has exactly two distinct levels, w^2=(Rmax-Rmin)/6. Otherwise w^2 equals half the smallest positive gap between consecutive distinct levels of R. Its positive integer square root is w.

(B) Set m=1 if q=5, otherwise m=0. Then kappa(b)=m+(Rmax-R(b))/(2*w^2).

(C) Z={2^(-1)*b modulo q : kappa(b) is odd}.

(D) Pick b of maximal kappa; then q-6+kappa(b)>0, and
 Delta_U(a)=[Delta_W(a,b)-2*w*(3-kappa(b))]/[q-6+kappa(b)].

All steps require O(nq) arithmetic operations, after the full indexed spectrum is supplied. The unsigned defect observer still generally loses original U membership: EC49FWM gives two different ten-cycle supports of equal size with identical indexed Delta_U.

## Proof

Let K_U(a)=|U intersect(a-U)|. Inclusion-exclusion in Z/q gives K_V(b)=q-6+kappa(b). CRT makes K_W(a,b)=K_U(a)K_V(b), and |W|=(q-3)w. Using Delta_U(a)=2(w-K_U(a)) gives (1). Every ordered pair of U has one sum a, so sum_a K_U(a)=w^2. Summing (1) establishes (2).

Among three distinct holes, the three diagonal pair sums 2z_i are distinct because q is odd, and the three unordered off-diagonal sums z_i+z_j are distinct by cancellation. Each diagonal contributes 1 to kappa; each unordered pair contributes 2. Collisions can only be 2z_i=z_j+z_k, i.e. one hole is the midpoint of the other two. If any two different midpoint relations hold, 3(z_i-z_j)=0, forcing all three relations and a translate of the unique order-3 subgroup. Thus either no collisions, exactly one, or all three; this yields the listed histograms. At q=5 six formal sums must collide and so exactly one midpoint relation holds. For q>=7 at most six centers carry kappa, giving a zero center.

By (2), row sums strictly decrease as kappa increases because w>0. In GENERIC and ONE_AP the present kappa values are consecutive, giving adjacent R gaps 2w^2. In EQUILATERAL the only gap is 3 and the R gap is 6w^2. Hence (A)-(B). Off-diagonal contributions occur in order-swapped pairs and are even. Exactly three distinct diagonal centers contribute an odd 1, so odd kappa(b) iff b=2z_i. Invertibility of 2 modulo odd q gives (C). Finally kappa_max>=2, and q-6+kappa_max is positive at q=5 (where kappa_max=3) and q>=7 (at least q-4). Equation (1) yields (D). QED.

## Family census

Let T=C(q,3). If 3 does not divide q, exactly q(q-1)/2 triples are ONE_AP, none EQUILATERAL, and the remainder GENERIC. If 3 divides q, q/3 triples are EQUILATERAL, q(q-3)/2 are non-equilateral ONE_AP, and the remainder GENERIC. Reason: count midpoint and unoriented nonzero step, q(q-1)/2 representations; EQUILATERAL triples each have three midpoint choices, all other AP triples one.

## Worked exact rows

- n=4, q=5, U={0,1}, Z={0,1,2}: kappa=(1,2,3,2,1), distinct R values 16,24,32, and w=2.
- n=10, q=9, U={0,1,2,3,4}, Z={0,3,6}: kappa=3 on b=0,3,6; distinct R values 300,450, and w^2=(450-300)/6=25.
- n=10, q=11, same U: generic Z={1,2,4} gives R levels 450,500,550; ONE_AP Z={1,2,3} gives 400,450,500,550.

When U is empty, every defect is zero and Z is unidentifiable. The theorem assumes the number of holes and center indices are known. It does not recover original signed membership from the unsigned spectrum, prove native dynamics, or change P000.
