## 1. Explicit unbounded family

For any integers n≥1 and q≥0, define a named bipartite graph with rows r₀,r₁,…,rₙ and columns c₀,c₁,…,cₙ. It has exactly 3n+1 named edges:

- Hub h=(r₀,c₀), integer capacity q;
- for each i=1,…,n, aᵢ=(rᵢ,c₀), bᵢ=(rᵢ,cᵢ), dᵢ=(r₀,cᵢ), each capacity 1.

All rᵢ (i≥1) and cᵢ (i≥1) have margin 1; r₀ and c₀ have margin n. Fix a feasible seed x⁰ with x⁰_h=0, x⁰_{aᵢ}=1, x⁰_{bᵢ}=0 and x⁰_{dᵢ}=1. For an optional set J⊆{1,…,n}, additionally fix bⱼ=0 for j∈J. Initially h=0 is also pinned. Releasing only h means dropping its pin while retaining its actual capacity q.

Define the **joint named-source observer** T(y)=(y_{b₁},…,y_{bₙ})∈ℤⁿ and the hub observer U(y)=y_h. Do not collapse named branches.

**Theorem 815.1 (exact fiber, all n and q).** The released feasible fiber is in bijection with subsets S⊆{1,…,n}\J of cardinality at most q. The unique table corresponding to S has

\[
y_h=|S|,\quad
y_{b_i}=\mathbf1_{i\in S},\quad
y_{a_i}=1-\mathbf1_{i\in S},\quad
y_{d_i}=1-\mathbf1_{i\in S}.
\]

**Proof.** Row rᵢ and column cᵢ each have margin 1, implying y_{aᵢ}+y_{bᵢ}=1 and y_{dᵢ}+y_{bᵢ}=1. Thus the integral value tᵢ=y_{bᵢ} is 0 or 1 and determines both other spoke edges. Row r₀ gives y_h+Σ_i y_{dᵢ}=n, so y_h=Σ_i tᵢ. Column c₀ then holds identically. The hub bound is exactly Σ_i tᵢ≤q and the optional pins require tⱼ=0. Every such choice satisfies all margins and capacities, proving both directions and uniqueness. □

Hence the full joint Laurent/polynomial histogram, including the exact hub readout, is

\[
H_{n,q,J}(u,z_1,\ldots,z_n)
 = \sum_{\substack{S\subseteq[n]\setminus J\\|S|\le q}}
     u^{|S|}\prod_{i\in S} z_i
 = \sum_{k=0}^{\min(q,n-|J|)}
     u^k e_k(z_i:i\notin J).
\]

This counts every **named** feasible source table once, rather than just recording its scalar total. The number of states is Σ_{k=0}^{min(q,n-|J|)} \binom{n-|J|}{k}. With h still pinned at 0 there is precisely one feasible table.

**Theorem 815.2 (unbounded rank jump from releasing one edge).** If q≥1, the integer group of joint-observer differences after releasing h is the coordinate sublattice

\[
L_T(F_{J})=\bigoplus_{i\notin J}\mathbb Z e_i
\subseteq\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad \operatorname{rank}L_T=n-|J|.
\]

With h pinned it is zero. Thus one **named edge** release (already with q=1 and J=∅) can raise the observer-difference lattice rank by an arbitrarily large n, even though the enlarged fiber contains only n+1 states.

**Proof.** All differences are supported on the unpinned coordinate indices, giving the upper bound. For every i∉J, the empty subset and singleton {i} are feasible when q≥1. Their T difference is e_i. Hence the full free coordinate lattice is generated. The pinned fiber is a singleton. □

**Theorem 815.3 (sharp SCC coalescence for q≥1, J=∅).** At x⁰ and with h pinned, the directed movable residual graph consists of n directed paths c₀→rᵢ→cᵢ→r₀ and no directed cycles: its 2n+2 vertices are singleton SCCs. Releasing h with q≥1 adds r₀→c₀; every vertex can now reach every other, yielding one SCC. Consequently a local unpin may merge arbitrarily many components and activate arbitrarily many joint-observer generators.

**Proof.** At unit capacities, x⁰_{aᵢ}=x⁰_{dᵢ}=1 leaves their backward residual arcs, while x⁰_{bᵢ}=0 leaves its forward arc. There is no arc out of r₀ while h is pinned. When h is released with q≥1, its forward residual arc closes each of the listed paths into a directed cycle, and any vertex reaches any other through r₀ and c₀. □

## 2. Exact distinction between marginal gcds and a joint lattice

Take n=2, q=1, J=∅ and instead observe W₁=T₁+T₂, W₂=T₁−T₂. The joint observed support is exactly {(0,0),(1,1),(1,−1)}. Both scalar observer-difference gcds equal 1, but the two-dimensional difference lattice is generated by (1,1) and (1,−1), of index 2 in ℤ². In particular W₁≡W₂ (mod 2) for every legal difference; the scalar gcd pair (1,1) loses that parity coupling. Its integer Smith invariants are (1,2), up to sign and basis conventions. This example requires retaining the **joint** integer lattice, not only one gcd per output coordinate.
