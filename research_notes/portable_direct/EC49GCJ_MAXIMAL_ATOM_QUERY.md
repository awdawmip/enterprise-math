## 1. Supermodular deficit on arbitrary port masks

Keep finite nonempty supports E_x, positive integer port capacities, independent prefix S and remaining labeled suffix F. N=(M/S)|F is the contracted strict-Hall matroid. For *any* port subset U (not necessarily a support-union closure coordinate), write F_U={x in F:E_x subset U}, D(U)=|F_U|-r_N(F_U). The closure interior U*=union_{x in F_U} E_x has the same F_U and D(U). D(empty)=0.

Lemma: D is monotone and supermodular on the full port Boolean lattice. Proof: matroid nullity nu(X)=|X|-r_N(X) is monotone and supermodular by the matroid rank axioms. F_U intersection F_V=F_(U intersection V) and F_U union F_V subset F_(U union V). Thus D(U)+D(V)<=D(U intersection V)+D(U union V). This needs no closure under intersection of the support-union coordinates.

## 2. Exact additivity descends to all restricted queries

Lemma: if W is partitioned into pairwise disjoint port blocks P_i with D(W)=sum_i D(P_i), then for every U subset W, D(U)=sum_i D(U intersection P_i).

Proof: for disjoint X,Y the supermodular synergy Delta(X,Y)=D(X union Y)-D(X)-D(Y) is nonnegative and monotone in each argument while disjointness is preserved. For X subset X', disjoint from Y, apply supermodularity to X union Y and X' (intersection X); the inequality is exactly Delta(X,Y)<=Delta(X',Y). Symmetry handles Y. For two blocks with zero synergy, every restricted pair has zero synergy. For multiple blocks, write W=P_1 disjoint union R. Superadditivity gives D(W)>=D(P_1)+D(R)>=sum_i D(P_i). Equality forces equality at both steps, so apply the two-block result and induction on R. Empty blocks and zero-deficit blocks are allowed.

## 3. Maximal-atom theorem

Let A(D) be GCI's unique minimal list of nonempty support-union masks with positive weights D(A), using the disjoint-packing generator language. For any port query U, let Max(U) be the inclusion-maximal members A of this list satisfying A subset U.

Theorem:
(a) Max(U) is pairwise disjoint.
(b) D(U)=sum_{A in Max(U)} D(A).
(c) If two atoms intersect, their union is an atom.
(d) Sort the atom list by decreasing cardinality. For one query U, scan once; select an atom A iff A subset U and A is disjoint from the accumulated selected union. Sum selected weights. This gives D(U) exactly with O(a) mask operations for a atoms, plus sorting once (O(a log a) comparisons if cardinalities precomputed), O(a) stored masks/weights, O(1) additional masks per query. Multiword bitset and integer costs are additional. Empty atom list returns zero.

Proof: GCI reconstruction at U*=closure interior gives a disjoint optimal packing of atoms P_1,...,P_m inside U, weights summing to D(U). Let P_0 be the leftover ports in U. Superadditivity forces D(P_0)=0 and exact additivity on this partition. By section 2, every atom A subset U has D(A)=sum_i D(A intersection P_i). If A is not contained in a single block, all intersections are proper subsets of A. Replace each by its closure interior; these are disjoint proper support-union masks, with unchanged deficits. GCI reconstructs their deficits by atoms wholly inside these proper masks. Their union packing would attain D(A) without using A itself, contradicting GCI essentiality. Hence A is contained in a single P_i, never in P_0 since D(A)>0. It follows that the optimal packing consists of exactly the inclusion-maximal atoms: every atom lies in one packed atom, and no packed atom can be strictly contained in another atom (the latter would have to lie in a packed atom disjoint from the first, or in the first itself). This proves (a,b). Apply (a) to U=A union B for intersecting atoms: each is contained in a maximal atom, and the maximal atoms cannot be different; that maximal atom equals U. This proves (c). In the sorted scan, all eligible maximal atoms precede their proper subsets; different maximal atoms are disjoint. Every eligible nonmaximal atom intersects an already selected maximal atom containing it, so is skipped. This proves (d).

Consequence for GCI dominance: to test g_S>=g_T at the fixed suffix, query D_T(A)>=D_S(A) only on S's atoms; no dense target cache or general weighted set-packing solver is required. Worst-case O(a_S a_T) mask operations using a direct scan, excluding earlier objective filters, sorting and mask width. This is a query algorithm, not an end-to-end runtime or physical memory bound.

## 5. Exponential growth even for unit-capacity ordinary graphs

For any k>=1, take a friendship graph: hub h and k disjoint pairs (a_i,b_i), with labels the three edges (h,a_i),(h,b_i),(a_i,b_i) of each petal triangle, all ports capacity 1, S empty. There are 2k+1 ports and 3k labels. Strict Hall independence is exactly graph forest independence: every nonempty chosen edge subset must have strictly fewer edges than incident vertices, which holds for forests and fails for a cycle. Its nullity on any induced port query W is exactly the number of complete petal triangles in W: each complete triangle contributes one independent cycle, incomplete petals contribute none, and different petals meet only at h. Equivalently each petal contributes one cycle iff all three of its vertices are present, by adding its edges to a spanning forest.

For each nonempty J subset [k], let W_J={h} union the pairs of petals in J. D(W_J)=|J|. Every proper subset loses at least one complete triangle. Any disjoint proper-mask packing inside W_J can have at most one member containing h, and all its other members have zero deficit. Thus its weight is strictly less than |J|; W_J is an atom. Conversely, every positive-deficit W not of this form has a proper closure subset W_J consisting of its complete petals and the same deficit, so is not an atom. Exactly 2^k-1 atoms, with total suffix nullity k and only 3k labels. This is exponential in port count, label count and total nullity. It refutes a uniform polynomial bound on the size of GCI's canonical atom lists in these parameters. It does not refute compact alternative representations: the very same family has an O(k) additive overlapping-petal formula, and the original graph is O(k). That alternate formula is not a GCI disjoint-packing generator.
