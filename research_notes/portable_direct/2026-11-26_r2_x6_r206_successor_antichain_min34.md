## Lemma: statewise successor antichain

Write S=(u,v), with componentwise inclusion order S<=S' iff u⊆u', v⊆v'. Fix a bank Q of endpoint observation quadruples and the R205 necessary-relaxation recurrence F_Q(S,d). For each q define its positive and negative successors by the R205 masks pa=(u&au)|(v&av), pb=(u&bu)|(v&bv), S+=(u&~bu&pa,v&~bv&pa), S-=(u&~au&pb,v&~av&pb). Each successor is monotone in S because intersections with constant masks, intersections of increasing masks and unions are monotone. The capacity count |u∪v| is monotone. By induction on d, if S<=S' and F_Q(S',d)=true then F_Q(S,d)=true: a true base |u'∪v'|<=d implies the smaller base; otherwise take the successful q at S', whose smaller child states meet capacity and succeed by induction. The capacity rejection base cannot trigger at S if S' succeeds. At d=0 the only successful state is empty. This proves downward closure of feasibility for this relaxation, with Q fixed.

At any state S, collect unordered pairs (S+,S-) from capacity-eligible q. A pair A dominates B if, after a possible swap of its two children, both children of A are componentwise subsets of the matched children of B. If B can succeed, A can succeed by downward closure; A is itself realized by an available q. Therefore keep only minimal pairs under this preorder (one representative per equivalence); the OR of the two-child feasibility conjunctions is unchanged. The proof is by finite descent in the pair preorder and induction on remaining depth. This is a state-local solver optimization, not permission to discard observation masks from Q or merge support labels. It applies to any rank once a valid bank on common ordered labels is available, including the R204 enlarged union bank. Distinct pairs need not be comparable; comparison cost is quadratic in eligible pairs in the simple implementation, so reductions in recursive states do not automatically establish a wall-clock speedup.
