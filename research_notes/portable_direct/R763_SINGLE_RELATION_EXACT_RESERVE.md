## Model and statement

Generalize the unsigned terminal-boundary observer of KZ. Take a finite family of binary rank-two local vector spaces H_i, injected into an ambient binary vector space V. Put M_i=H_i minus {0}. Let A: direct sum H_i -> V be the addition of the inclusions; replace V by its image. Define the stock reachability P(p)=A(product_i S(p_i)), where S(0)={0}, S(1)=M_i, S(2)=H_i. All nonnegative stocks may be clipped at two because M_i+M_i=H_i and H_i+M_i=H_i. k_p(b) is the least suffix cost sum t_i for which b+P(p) is contained in P(t); b in im A, suffix stock independent of the prefix choice. No claim about signs, mixedness, counts, ordering, or native BRC.

Assume ker A is one-dimensional, generated by z=(z_i). Put C={i:z_i!=0}, with |C|>=2 by local injectivity. For arbitrary p use clipped p throughout. For any lift w of b, define local costs (0,1,2):
f_i(p,w)=min t such that S(p)+w subset S(t).
Explicitly f(0,w)=0 if w=0 else 1; f(1,w)=1 if w=0 else 2; f(2,w)=2.
For i in C define g_i(p,w)=min t such that S(p)+w and S(p)+w+z_i are BOTH contained in S(t).
Explicitly g(0,w)=1 if w not in {0,z_i} else 2; g(1,w)=g(2,w)=2.
Define h_i(p,w)=min t such that S(p)+w subset S(t) union (S(t)+z_i).
Explicitly h=0 iff p=0 and w in {0,z_i}; otherwise h=1.

Theorem: with F_out=sum_{i outside C} f_i(p_i,w_i),

k_p(b)=F_out+min(
 min_{e in {0,1}} sum_{i in C} f_i(p_i,w_i+e z_i),
 min_{j in C} [h_j(p_j,w_j)+sum_{i in C, i!=j} g_i(p_i,w_i)]
).

In particular k_p(0)=sum_i p_i - 1_{all i in C have p_i=2}, and k_p(b)>=k_p(0) for all b. A single joint relation can save at most one event, and saves one exactly when its entire support is doubled. Zero optimality is not peculiar to the cube for this class. For a terminal lift supplied, evaluation uses O(n) small integer operations (two sums and one minimum of h_j-g_j), excluding construction of the linear map/lift.

## Proof

For every local prefix tuple x, the only possible lifts of the target A(x)+b are x+w and x+w+z. At a fixed suffix stock t, define bad sets A_i={x_i in S(p_i):x_i+w_i not in S(t_i)} and B_i={x_i in S(p_i):x_i+w_i+z_i not in S(t_i)}. A counterexample tuple exists iff both failures can occur at some tuple. This holds if A_i and B_j are both nonempty for distinct i,j (independent choices), or A_i intersects B_i at some i. If neither holds then either all A_i are empty, all B_i are empty, or both families of nonempty sets are supported on the same unique j with A_j intersect B_j empty. In the last case j must lie in C because otherwise A_j=B_j. Outside C all coordinates must satisfy ordinary local containment. The first two cases give the first minimum (one common lift suffices for every x). The third case gives the second (both lifts work in all other coordinates, and their union covers j). Conversely any stock satisfying either mode covers every tuple, so the local minima are achievable independently and exact. Swapping w by w+z only exchanges the common-lift modes and leaves the other conditions invariant.

Explicit local costs follow from the nested cardinalities 1,3,4; two distinct translates of a three-element local set cover H_i; and M_i union (M_i+z_i)=H_i for z_i nonzero. For p=0, two points w,w+z_i are both in M_i exactly when neither is zero.

For the zero-optimality inequality the common-lift mode costs at least sum p. In the exceptional-coordinate mode, g_i>=p_i with an increase of at least 1 for each p_i<2, while h_j>=p_j-1, with a saving possible only if p_j=2. Thus it can cost less than sum p only when every coordinate in C is doubled. In that case a singleton at any one coordinate j, and doubled stock at the other cycle coordinates, achieves sum p-1 at b=0 (outside C use stock p). Otherwise the original clipped stock works at zero with cost sum p. This proves the formula and the universal inequality. It includes the direct-sum case with ker A=0 by omitting the second mode and using f everywhere.

Corollary: if ker A has a basis z^(1),...,z^(r) with pairwise disjoint coordinate supports C_l, the quotient decomposes as the direct sum of the individual one-relation blocks and free remaining coordinates. Every prefix and suffix reachable set is a product in that decomposition, and translated containment is equivalent to componentwise containment, so costs add. Therefore k_p(0)=sum p_i - number of C_l entirely doubled, and k_p(b)>=k_p(0) for all b; the full spectrum is the sum of the block formulas. This includes vertex-disjoint cycle relations in incidence support graphs when the only relations are these cycles. It does not require finite enumeration or an assumption about event order. A fixed cube subfamily with one cycle and no other allowed suffix supports is a special case; unrestricted eight-support cube remains the KZ finite result, not covered by this analytic theorem.
