## Theorem and proof
Let C_s,C_t denote the nonzero exact sign cones on a spanning list P in auxiliary real three-space. Their closures are pointed and generated by finite nonzero rays R_i,Q_j, as established in R209. Every exact sign-cone vector is a strictly positive combination of all generators (the relative-interior argument in R209). For nonzero w, the strict section C_s intersect w-perp is nonempty iff the numbers w dot R_i are all zero or include both strict signs. Indeed strictly positive coefficients cannot sum to zero otherwise. For the mixed case write P_R,M_R,Z_R for the sums of generators having positive, negative and zero w dots. Then a strict section representative is
r(w)=-(w dot M_R) P_R+(w dot P_R) M_R+Z_R.
Its dot with w is zero, and its coefficients are strictly positive. For all zero use sum R_i. Identically construct q(w). The dimension of the section closure is rank(R) if all zero, otherwise rank(R)-1 in the mixed case; the intersection meets the relative interior, so no further dimension loss. The strict representative is continuous within each exact sign stratum of the generator walls.

Refine w-space only by the walls with normals R_i, Q_j, and the moving support normals n_k. For nonzero w the resulting oriented strata are relatively open convex cones minus zero. To ensure pointedness and connectedness in enumeration, append independent auxiliary coordinate normals if these normals do not span; this only refines the strata. Section feasibility and dynamic signs are constant on each stratum.

If t is neither s nor -s, r(w) and q(w) cannot be parallel since nonzero parallel vectors have equal or opposite signs on P. Both lie in w-perp, so w dot (r(w) cross q(w)) is nonzero and continuous, hence its sign is constant on each connected stratum. Furthermore for fixed w the strict sections are convex and connected, so the same nonzero sign is shared by every choice of r,q in those sections (the dot of w with their cross cannot vanish). Consequently a single representative decides existence of a positive cross multiple of w; rescale positively for equality. If t=s or -s, a nonzero oriented lift exists iff the common section has dimension at least two: take two independent strict vectors and swap them (with a sign reversal of one vector in the opposite-cone case). For dimension one every cross is zero. For w=0, precisely equal or opposite sign cones allow nonzero parallel vectors; this is handled separately.

This proves that local generator-wall plus moving-normal strata suffice for the full exact moving-sign lift for each cone pair, including lower-dimensional equality strata. Unlike R210, no global endpoint-pair determinant arrangement is needed. This is a smaller exact *local* arrangement, not a theorem of improved total runtime. Its root capacity use is still only a necessary condition for a decision tree. The old bilinear masks are retained as sound preliminary filters. The R209 support labeling and component conventions are unchanged.
