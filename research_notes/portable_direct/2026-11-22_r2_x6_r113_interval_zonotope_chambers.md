## 2. Theorem A — four-interval identity and the exact three-dimensional ball

```

In particular, the six joint pair inequalities are its six genuine *pairs of opposite facets*; this proof retains every observer.

**Proof.** The six original pair inequalities `|t_i-t_j|<=r(w_i+w_j)` say exactly that the four real intervals `I_i=[t_i-r w_i,t_i+r w_i]` intersect pairwise. This gives `|t_i-c|<=r w_i` simultaneously, and the reverse implication is the triangle inequality. Set `z_i=t_i-c`, so `(u,v,h)=(z_1-z_0,z_2-z_0,z_3-z_0)`, proving the zonotope identity.
