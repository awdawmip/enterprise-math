## 1. Cut carrier (reuse and implementation)
Let nonempty supports E_i represent classes H_i of remaining labels, m_i their multiplicities, x_i a choice count in [0,m_i]. For each nonempty original port cut U, retain the inequality x(I(U))<=c(U)-1-f_S(U), I(U)={i:E_i subset U}, where S is the independent selected prefix and c is the sum of positive integral port capacities. Rows are (J,b), J a class mask, b an integer credit. This is the original strict-Hall observer, not a new independence criterion. All statements are on the declared finite labeled population and its selection/deletion minors.
Choose legal r and skip q disjoint, r+q<=m. Substitute x+r, new box m'=m-r-q; each row becomes (J,b-r(J)), with zero-count classes removed from masks. This is exact for arbitrary subsequent selections, not merely the current value. Deduplicate equal masks keeping the least credit. Remove rows b>=m(J); also a row (J,b) is redundant whenever another (K,h) satisfies h+m(J minus K)<=b, since x(J)<=x(K)+m(J minus K). Remove one at a time to avoid simultaneous equivalent-row deletion. These reductions preserve the entire count independence language under the current box and hence every legal future minor; they need not produce a canonical minimum row set. Store original label IDs, objective values and selected witness separately. No assertion about preservation of native phase/path data outside this observer.
Raw cut enumeration is O(2^P(P+k)) with P ports and k classes; pairwise row cleanup at most O(k u^2) for u rows. Union closure or a separate proven separation oracle may improve construction; not implemented here.

## 2. Count greedy and exact dominance
The strict-Hall system is a matroid on labeled targets (as in GCD–GCM); equivalently its positive integral capacities can be represented by replacing each port by its c_p private copies and each label support by the union of the corresponding copies, with independence defined by every nonempty label subfamily having at least one more incident vertex than labels. For completeness, maximal tight nonempty port cuts for an independent set I are disjoint: if two tight cuts intersect, the contained-label count is supermodular, and c(U)-1 is modular on such pairs, so both union and intersection are tight. Every blocked augmentation is contained in one of the maximal tight cuts. If a larger independent J could not augment I, its labels outside those tight cuts are a subset of I there; its count inside each cut cannot exceed I's tight count, a contradiction. Minors remain matroids.
To obtain rank on the union of classes indexed by J, scan those classes in any order, increasing the count by the minimum of its remaining multiplicity and all credits of rows containing that class, debiting those rows. At termination no single label can be appended, hence by matroid augmentation the result is a basis on J. Costs O(k u) row/credit checks once the row incidences are available. For two states S,T with the same suffix, I(T) subset I(S) iff for every surviving row (J,b) of S, r_T(H_J)<=b. Necessity follows by a basis witness; sufficiency follows by applying all S rows to any T-independent set. The greedy count vector that violates b lifts to an explicit labeled witness. This reuses the rank-domination idea; the new executable route requires no count-antichain expansion.

## 3. Direct class-component theorem
Let B be any basis represented by counts beta_i. For each i with beta_i<m_i, take a nonbasis label x in H_i. Let T_i be the family of row masks J containing i which are tight on beta. T_i is nonempty by maximality of B. Define
  C_i={j: beta_j>0 and j belongs to every J in T_i}.
For every basis label y in H_j, B-y+x is independent iff j belongs to C_i: rows not containing i cannot worsen, nontight rows containing i have at least one integer unit of credit, and each tight row containing i must lose one via y. By the basic-circuit exchange property, the fundamental circuit of x is x together with exactly those basis labels. This includes loops (empty exchange list). Within-class permutations fixing B act transitively on the nonbasis labels of that class, so no per-label expansion is needed.
Build a graph on active classes i, edges i--j for j in C_i (self edges harmless), and take connected components G. Every fundamental circuit is contained in a single component's label union F_G. Each B intersect F_G spans F_G by the fundamental circuits, hence rank(F_G)=|B intersect F_G|. The sum of these ranks equals rank(F). For completeness, take any basis of each F_G: their union spans F and has cardinality rank(F), hence is independent. Extend any local independent choices to such bases, proving that the whole independence language factors as a direct product over G. Conversely every direct-product partition respecting classes must place each fundamental circuit in one part, hence must contain these graph edges. The graph partition is therefore the finest exact class-respecting product partition (not necessarily the finest labeled partition: a class may group separate coloops/loops).
Construction is one greedy basis plus tight-row intersections and graph components, polynomial in k,u after the row carrier exists (naive O(k^2 u) scalar incidence tests, O(k u) mask operations if row tightness precomputed and mask word costs charged separately). No circuit enumeration. Components can be recomputed after arbitrary legal minors, and old components cannot merge under those operations. To query local choices, keep original rows with all other coordinates zero; product theorem justifies discarding cross-component restrictions for simultaneous feasible local choices.

## 4. Exact all-budget convolution
Reuse GCM/GCE real and auxiliary label types: additive p>=0 for real labels with zero s, additive s>=0 for aux labels with zero p. At fixed counts of the two types within a class, sorted largest-value/largest-saving prefixes are optimal; preserve actual original mask witness. For each component enumerate or solve its own local Pareto front P_G of (p,s), removing a pair only if another has both coordinates at least as high. The total front is Pareto{sum_G v_G:v_G in P_G}; compute by successive pairwise convolution and prune after each combination. Direct-product feasibility proves arbitrary local witnesses combine legally; coordinatewise dominance is preserved by addition, proving exactness. The budget objective p maximized subject to D-s<=budget, D=sum all auxiliary savings, is preserved for every budget with at least one actual labeled optimal witness. No claim to retain all tied witnesses or to give polynomial global solving complexity. Local candidate counts for enumeration sum across components instead of multiply; convolution still has output/intermediate cost and can dominate on other inputs. This replay uses enumeration within each component, not the previous full DP, and no timing or physical-memory claim.

## 5. Exponential separation even with all distinct supports
For d>=5 unit-capacity ports on a cycle, take 2d distinct nonempty supports: all ports except i (d of these) and all ports except consecutive i,i+1 (d of these), one label per support. In a proper cut of size d-1 at most three labels are contained (one singleton omission and two adjacent-pair omissions), so 3<=d-2. At size d-2 at most one label is contained and 1<=d-3. Smaller nonempty cuts contain none. Every proper cut is automatically satisfied. Only full cut imposes sum x_i<=d-1. This is U_{d-1,2d}, and GCM's count antichain equals the labeled circuits, of size binom(2d,d), despite distinct supports. It grows at least 4^d/(2d+1) by the binomial sum and maximal central term. Compact carrier has one row. At d=30 this is 118264581564861424 count vectors vs one inequality (theoretical; no 2^30 cut enumeration). d=5..10 actually enumerated all port cuts and verified one-row reduction. Thus the prior high-multiplicity example is not required for an exponential separation. This connected uniform matroid also shows component factorization need not split every large antichain.
