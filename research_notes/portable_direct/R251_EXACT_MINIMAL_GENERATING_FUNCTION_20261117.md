## Objective and exact construction

Let the 63 proper-source masks, positive weights \(W_{|D|-1}\), and matrices \(P_D\), \(\rho_D\) be defined exactly as in R250. For the whole real interval \(39/100<s<41/100\), including the endpoint analytic extensions, write

\[
\Phi_n(s)=\sum_{D}W_{|D|-1}\rho_D^\top P_D^{n-1}\mathbf1,
\qquad
F_s(z)=\sum_{k\ge0}\Phi_{k+1}(s)z^k.
\]

For each proper mask \(D\) of cardinality \(d\), define the reverse characteristic polynomial, normalized by its natural constant term,

\[
q_D(z)=\det(I-zP_D)=\sum_{j=0}^{d}q_{D,j}z^j,\qquad q_{D,0}=1.
\]

Obtain only the first \(d\) exact weighted scalar moments using the rank-one linear update:

\[
h_{D,k}=W_{|D|-1}\rho_D^\top P_D^k\mathbf1,
\quad 0\le k<d,
\quad
(v^{k+1})_i=a_i v_i^k+(1-a_i)\sum_{j\in D}\pi_j v_j^k,
\quad v^0_i=1.
\]

Construct the block numerator explicitly:

\[
N_D(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{d-1}\left(\sum_{j=0}^{k}q_{D,j}h_{D,k-j}\right)z^k.
\]

Now take the **monic LCM** of the 63 \(q_D\) over \(\mathbb Q[z]\), then normalize its constant coefficient to one:

\[
Q_s(z)=\frac{\operatorname{lcm}_{D}^{\mathrm{monic}}q_D(z)}{
\operatorname{lcm}_{D}^{\mathrm{monic}}q_D(0)},
\qquad
P_s(z)=\sum_{D}N_D(z)\frac{Q_s(z)}{q_D(z)}.
\]

All arithmetic is exact over \(\mathbb Q\) when \(s\) is rational and over \(\mathbb Q(s)\) formally away from its exceptional rational-function poles.

## Theorem R251 — exact rational generating function and minimal recurrence

For each fixed real \(s\in(0.39,0.41)\),

\[
\boxed{F_s(z)=\frac{P_s(z)}{Q_s(z)},\qquad Q_s(0)=1,\qquad \gcd(P_s,Q_s)=1.}
\]

The polynomial \(Q_s\) has degree at most 229. It has degree exactly 229 at \(s=2/5\) and for all but finitely many \(s\) in the interval. If \(d_s=\deg Q_s\) and \(Q_s(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{d_s}q_j(s)z^j\), then

\[
\boxed{\sum_{j=0}^{d_s}q_j(s)\Phi_{k-j+1}(s)=0\qquad(k\ge d_s).}
\]

Moreover, this is the **minimal constant-coefficient scalar recurrence**. Its initial \(d_s\) scalar coefficients follow directly from the rank-one mask propagation.

**Proof.** Cayley–Hamilton applied to each \(d\)-dimensional \(P_D\) makes \(q_D(z)\sum_{k\ge0}h_{D,k}z^k\) a polynomial of degree less than \(d\). Expanding its first \(d\) coefficients yields precisely \(N_D(z)\). Thus each block is \(N_D/q_D\), and putting them over the common polynomial LCM proves the displayed formula with \(\deg P_s<\deg Q_s\). By R250, the symmetric similar form of every \(P_D\) has a simple spectrum in \((0,1)\), and every scalar eigenmode has a *strictly positive* coefficient. After identifying coincident eigenvalues across masks, the partial-fraction expansion of \(F_s\) is a sum of \(c_\lambda/(1-\lambda z)\) with \(c_\lambda>0\). Therefore no pole of the LCM can cancel and \(\gcd(P_s,Q_s)=1\). As the denominators are products of distinct linear poles, \(\deg Q_s\) equals their number. R250's fixed collision enumeration bounds that count by 229, gives exact equality at \(s=2/5\), and proves generic equality outside a finite exceptional set. Coefficient comparison against \(Q_sF_s=P_s\) gives the recurrence for every \(k\ge d_s\). Finally, a lower-order recurrence would yield a lower-degree rational denominator, contradicting coprimality. \(\square\)
