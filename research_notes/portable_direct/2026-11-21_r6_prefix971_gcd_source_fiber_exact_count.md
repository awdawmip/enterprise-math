## Object, signatures and exact scope

Let p>=4 and D_p={2^i-s*2^j: 0<=i,j<p, s in {-1,+1}}. Every integer c retains its complete fiber of ordered signed source triples (i,j,s). D_p here denotes only the distinct-numeric projection, not a complete source observer. For odd g>=1, D_p(g)={c in D_p: g|c}.

## Theorem 971.1 (all odd moduli)

For any odd g>1, set h=ord_g(2). Let eta=1 if and only if h is even AND 2^(h/2)≡-1 (mod g); otherwise eta=0. Define

A_p(h) = SUM_{r>=1, rh<p} (p-rh),
B_p(h) = SUM_{r>=0, h/2+rh<p} (p-h/2-rh) when eta=1, and zero otherwise.

Then the exact number of DISTINCT NUMERIC residuals divisible by g is

    |D_p(g)| = 1 + 2 A_p(h) + eta B_p(h) - [g=3](p-2).

The exact number of ORDERED SIGNED SOURCE TRIPLES (i,j,s) with g | (2^i-s*2^j) is

    T_p(g) = p + 2 A_p(h) + 2 eta B_p(h).

For g=1, |D_p(1)|=(3p^2-5p+10)/2 and T_p(1)=2p^2. Empty sums are zero.

Proof. For distinct indices write e=|i-j|>0 and r=min(i,j). Since g is odd, 2^r is invertible mod g. Thus divisibility is equivalent to 2^e≡s mod g. If s=+1, e is a positive multiple of h. If s=-1, -1 must belong to the cyclic subgroup generated by 2 modulo g: that occurs precisely for eta=1, and then e≡h/2 mod h. For each e, the p-e choices of r give (p-e) positive and (p-e) negative differences, and 2(p-e) ordered positive-sum source triples representing (p-e) numeric sums. Within each family, the 2-adic valuation r and strictly increasing odd factor (2^e±1) make values distinct. A positive sum can equal a positive difference only if 2^e-1=2^f+1; writing 2^f(2^(e-f)-1)=2 gives the unique positive solution (e,f)=(2,1). Their shared value 3*2^r is g-divisible for odd g>1 only at g=3; the overlap has p-2 choices of r. Negative differences cannot equal positive sums. Also c=0 has p ordered triples i=j,s=+1; i=j,s=-1 gives a pure power, indivisible by odd g>1. Subtracting the overlaps proves both formulas. For g=1, count the difference family 1+p(p-1), unequal-index sums p(p-1)/2 and equal-index sums p; their two overlap families each contain p-2 values, giving (3p^2-5p+10)/2. QED.

Important composite-modulus subtlety: even order alone is insufficient. For g=15, h=4 but 2^2=4, not -1 modulo 15. The eta test is essential.

## Theorem 971.2 (parity-balanced exact k-average)

Let k be any positive odd integer. For any odd quotient gap m for which the preceding parity rule applies, define

F_p(k)=SUM_{t=1}^{2k} #{c in D_p: gcd(k,t)|c, c≡1-t (mod 2)}.

Then

    F_p(k) = SUM_{g|k} phi(k/g) * |D_p(g)|.

Before imposing gcd divisibility, exactly k*|D_p| parity-compatible numerical (t,c) pairs are examined. Thus exactly k*|D_p|-F_p(k) are excluded by gcd filtering. All three counts are independent of the selected odd gap m.

Proof. Pair t=r and t=r+k for 1<=r<=k. Since k is odd, these two t have opposite parity and the same gcd g with k; each c in D_p(g) appears exactly once over the pair. There are exactly phi(k/g) choices of r with gcd(k,r)=g, including r=k for g=k. Sum over g. QED.
