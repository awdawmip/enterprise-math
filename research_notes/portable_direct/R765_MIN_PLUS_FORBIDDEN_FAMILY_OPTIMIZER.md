## Theorem: optimize the reserve, not just test a fixed reserve

Keep R764's Boolean auxiliary observer A: direct sum of n local binary rank-two spaces -> quotient by a fixed relation kernel K of dimension r. For arbitrary nonnegative prefix inventory p, clip each coordinate at two. Local attainable sets S0={0}, S1={1,2,3}, S2={0,1,2,3}, binary XOR coordinates. For terminal lift w and suffix t in {0,1,2}^n, the objective is min sum(t_i) subject to A(w)+A(product S(p_i)) subset A(product S(t_i)). All terminal lifts are equivalent modulo K. This statement preserves the exact universal-prefix compensation predicate only.

For a local suffix choice t define F_i(x;t)={z in K: x+w_i+z_i not in S(t)}. Write B_i(t)=downward closure of {F_i(x;t): x in S(p_i)}, a family of subsets of K, including the empty subset. For families U,V define U star V={a union b: a in U,b in V}. Downsets remain downsets: any subset of a union splits into a subset of a and a subset of b. The complete family for a fixed suffix is B_1(t_1) star ... star B_n(t_n). It contains K iff an actual prefix tuple rejects every lift. Indeed each local mask in its downclosure is contained in a realizable local mask, and choices of prefix coordinates are independent. This is precisely R764's forbidden-mask condition with a proved sufficient state reduction.

Maintain D_i(U), minimum inventory cost among length-i suffix choices with family exactly U. Initialize D_0({empty})=0, others infinity. For t=0,1,2 compute V=U star B_{i+1}(t). If K belongs to V discard the transition; otherwise update D_{i+1}(V)=min(D_{i+1}(V), D_i(U)+t). Final answer min_U D_n(U). Proof: induction on i gives exact least cost for each retained family. Future unions depend only on U, not its generating suffix. Unsafe prefixes remain unsafe under any subsequent transition, since every local family is nonempty. Thus neither discarding unsafe states nor merging equal families can remove a better feasible solution. S2 at all coordinates is feasible, so the final minimum is finite. Backpointers recover an attaining suffix in O(n) steps; actual prefix count, order, signed data, weights and common slice depth are not compressed by this theorem.

The number of safe states for fixed r is the Dedekind number M(2^r) minus two (empty family and the downset containing K). For r=2 the exact state bound is 166, verified by checking all downsets on a four-element set; a state fits in 16 bits. Each step has at most 498 transitions after precomputation. With fixed r and given local projections and terminal lift, exact optimal cost takes O(n) word operations and O(1) state memory (cost words have O(log n) bits); reconstructing the witness uses O(n) backpointer storage. Preprocessing is finite and independent of n for fixed r. One can precompute local families and joins or compute joins by at most 16*16 unions for r=2. This is not linear uniformly in r; explicit state bounds grow doubly exponentially in 2^r, and the cost of constructing K and w from an original input is additional. Compared to 3^n suffix search this is an exact fixed-parameter optimization, not a measured timing claim. For all terminal lifts separately repeat the algorithm; no claim that the full terminal spectrum costs O(n).

## Why R764 is not itself an incidence graph counterexample

Let a loop-free graph of maximum degree three define one local even triple per vertex, with unused ports private, and shared ports added modulo two along edges. The kernel consists exactly of Eulerian edge subsets: a shared port must agree at its two endpoints and a private port is zero. Remove bridges, which occur in no cycle. In each nontrivial connected component of the remaining graph every vertex has degree 2 or 3. At a degree-two vertex the local cycle projection has rank one. At a degree-three vertex it has rank two: each incident edge is nonbridge so no incident coordinate is identically zero; a one-dimensional even triple image would have one zero coordinate, contradiction. If a component has cycle rank r_j, degree sum yields number of degree-three vertices = 2(r_j-1). Thus for total rank r with c nontrivial cycle components, the number of rank-two projections is exactly 2(r-c). Rank two kernels arising from these graph incidences have either zero or two rank-two projections, never three. R764's three rank-two projections and one rank-one projection therefore cannot be a rank-two graph incidence kernel under any local basis changes or subdivisions that preserve that kernel. A theta graph has two rank-two endpoints; its internal degree-two vertices carry the three possible rank-one functionals. This explains the structural distinction, but does not settle zero-terminal optimality for arbitrary theta path lengths.
