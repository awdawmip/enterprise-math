## AR1. A universal transpose ambiguity

For every square matrix `C` over a commutative field and independently named diagonal entries `D=diag(d_i)`,

`F_C(d)=det(D-C)=det((D-C)^T)=det(D-C^T)=F_{C^T}(d).`  (AR1)

Hence all complementary principal-minor coefficients, every simultaneous diagonal edit, and every rank/activation-order inference drawn solely from `F_C` are identical for `C` and its transpose. This is exact, not a numerical approximation.

Diagonal similarity is another sufficient invariance: `F_{SCS^{-1}}=F_C` for invertible diagonal `S`. The two invariances are genuinely distinct already among strictly positive stable rational matrices of size three.

## AR2. Dimension three is the smallest strictly positive nongauge case

**Theorem AR2.** Suppose every entry of both `C,C'∈Q^{m×m}` is strictly positive. For `m≤2`, equality `F_C=F_{C'}` implies `C'=SCS^{-1}` for some positive invertible diagonal `S`. For `m=3`, this implication fails even when both matrices are strictly row-substochastic and invertible.

*Proof at m=2.* Write `C=[[a,b],[c,d]]`. The diagonal polynomial
`F_C(x,y)=(x-a)(y-d)-bc` identifies `a,d` and the product `bc`. For another positive matrix with the same data, choose `λ=b'/b>0`, `S=diag(λ,1)`. Then `SCS^{-1}` has upper off-diagonal `λb=b'` and lower off-diagonal `c/λ=c'`, since `b'c'=bc`. The diagonal is unchanged. For `m=1` the assertion is immediate.

*Exact m=3 witness.* Let `ε=1/100` and

`A=[[1,2,1],[1,1,2],[2,1,1]]`, `C=εA`, `C'=εA^T`.

Both are strictly positive, with every row sum equal to `4/100`; `det C=4ε^3=1/250000≠0`. AR1 gives the identical full diagonal polynomial

`F_C(d)=F_{C'}(d)=d_1d_2d_3−ε(d_1d_2+d_1d_3+d_2d_3)−ε²(d_1+d_2+d_3)−4ε³.`  (AR2)

They are **not** diagonally similar. If `C'=SCS^{-1}` then the product of entries around any directed cycle is invariant. But the products around `1→2→3→1` are `8ε³` for `C` and `ε³` for `C'`. This contradiction proves the claim and minimality.

## AR3. One off-diagonal named Port edit distinguishes the two stable matrices

Let `E_{21}` have its single nonzero entry in row two, column one and define
`G_C(d,s)=det(diag(d)-C-sE_{21})`. Direct exact Leibniz expansion gives

`G_C(d,s)-G_{C'}(d,s)=εs(4ε-d_3).`                        (AR3)

At `d_1=d_2=d_3=1`, `s=ε`, both physical auxiliary cores remain nonnegative and strictly row-substochastic, while

`G_C(1,ε)=969497/1000000`,
`G_{C'}(1,ε)=969593/1000000`,
`G_C-G_{C'}=-3/31250.`

The case shows that a principal-minor-complete diagonal observer can still identify two *distinct*, strictly positive, rank-three rational Port systems. A single declared directed intervention exposes the distinction. The R284AP closure algorithm remains fully correct for its named **diagonal** observation domain; AR2 gives an exact boundary against silently extending that domain to arbitrary off-diagonal edits.
