## Theorem (reachability, convergence, positive Fourier spectrum and a uniform bound)

    H_q=(2Z/qZ)^3 <= (Z/qZ)^3,   |H_q|=Q^3.

Then:

1. The subgroup generated by the full modular support of μ is exactly H_q. For q odd this is the entire area group; for q even it is precisely the three-coordinate even-residue subgroup. For q=2 it is the trivial group.
2. The random walk from zero driven by independent copies of μ is irreducible and aperiodic on H_q, with uniform stationary measure U_{H_q}. Its endpoint law p_k converges to U_{H_q} as k increases, for every fixed q.
3. Every eigenvalue of convolution by μ on H_q is real and nonnegative, lying in [0,1]. Choose ANY extension of a character χ of H_q to the ambient group (Z/qZ)^3. The eigenvalue is

       λ_χ = |(1/6) sum_{s in S} χ(s)|^2.

   The expression is independent of the chosen extension, although the unsquared sum need not be. This is an exact finite-group identity, not an analytic approximation.
4. For Q>=2 define

   Each nontrivial eigenvalue satisfies λ_χ <= ρ_Q, and for every k>=1,

The measure ν is symmetric under negation because S=-S. Let G=(Z/qZ)^3. Then μ=ν*ν*, where ν*(x)=ν(-x). Thus for every character χ of G,
