## 1. Symmetric count quotient
Partition the remaining labeled set F into support classes H_i with identical nonempty port supports E_i and multiplicities m_i. Assume the independence language is invariant under all within-class permutations, as follows directly from strict Hall counts and persists after fixing a selected prefix and deleting labels. Put v(X)_i=|X intersect H_i|, box B(m)=product of integer intervals [0,m_i]. Define the canonical count antichain K as the componentwise minimal infeasible vectors in B(m). Then X is independent iff there is no a in K with a<=v(X) componentwise. Downward closure gives necessity and sufficiency; symmetry makes the choice of representative immaterial. Every circuit has a vector in K (if a smaller infeasible vector existed, it could be lifted to a subset of that circuit); conversely every label realization of a in K is a circuit. Therefore K is the orbit quotient of GCL's circuit antichain, and its exact labeled expansion count is sum_{a in K} product_i binom(m_i,a_i). K is a unique equality key for the independence language on a fixed suffix and partition. Label IDs, objective values and actual selected-prefix witness must remain separate. Ranks can be recovered by maximizing sum y_i over feasible y<=v(X), not claimed a linear readout.

## 2. Direct construction without labeled circuit or signed expansion
For the original strict Hall model with positive integral port capacities c_p, let c(U)=sum_{p in U} c_p, I(U)={i:E_i subset U}. For each nonempty cut U define
 B_U={a in B(m): a_i=0 for i not in I(U), sum_{i in I(U)}a_i=c(U)}.
Then K=Min_componentwise union_U B_U.
Proof: every member of B_U violates U. Conversely if v violates U then sum_{i in I(U)}v_i>=c(U); reduce these coordinates to sum c(U) and set others zero. This gives a member of B_U below v. Minimality proves the formula. For a previously selected independent prefix S, replace c(U) by c(U)-f_S(U), which is positive and gives the same proof. It suffices to enumerate distinct I(U) with the smallest threshold among identical I(U); ignore cuts with sum_{i in I(U)}m_i less than threshold. Because supports are nonempty and c is nondecreasing, one can equivalently enumerate nonempty unions of supports: replacing U by the union of supports contained in U leaves I(U) unchanged and weakly reduces the threshold (for a fixed prefix, either keep full cuts or include selected supports before projecting; no claim that residual thresholds are monotone). The implemented constructor enumerates all nonempty port cuts and bounded compositions with suffix-sum bounds, then componentwise minimization. If P is port count, k class count, B candidate count, naive cost O(2^P(P+k)+composition traversal+k B^2) plus output/storage; candidate enumeration may itself be exponential in k. This is an exact constructor, not a general polynomial bound. No full label power set is used by the constructor.

## 3. Arbitrary partial batch transfer
Select an independent R, skip disjoint Q, with counts r=v(R), q=v(Q), r+q<=m. Set m'=m-r-q. Then
 K'=Min{(a-r)_+ : a in K, a<=m-q}.
Proof: for x<=m', x is forbidden in the minor N/R minus Q iff x+r contains an old forbidden vector. Such a can occur only if a<=m-q; for these candidates a<=x+r iff (a-r)_+<=x. Independent R excludes the zero residual. Minimalization gives the unique new antichain. No need to enumerate choices of which same-class labels have been used; all remaining permutations extend fixing R,Q. The number of vectors cannot increase along any legal path. In full-class processing q_i=m_i-r_i, keep only a_i<=r_i and drop that coordinate; partial batch formula covers arbitrary multiple classes as well. Repeated operations are exact by induction, provided individual label membership and objectives are maintained separately.

## 4. Exact dominance and witness
For identical suffix box and classes, I(T) subset I(S) iff every a in K_S dominates some b in K_T componentwise. Failing a gives a count vector feasible in T and infeasible in S, any label realization in the common suffix a witness. This is GCL's direction after orbit quotient; additive p,s dominance and sorted same-class real/aux prefix options reuse GCE/GCL. All budget values and at least one labeled witness preserved; no claim to preserve all distinct optimal prefixes. A direct comparison costs up to k |K_S||K_T| scalar comparisons, antichain minimalization costs up to O(k |K|^2). Thus fewer entries or tests does not automatically imply less memory or wall-clock time than a bitmask.

## 5. High multiplicity separation
One port of capacity h with m labels (1<=h<=m) has K={(h)} and exactly binom(m,h) labeled circuits. This generalizes GCL's rank-one example. For m=100,h=33 the actual direct constructor returns just [33] from one composition; select 20 and skip 30 leaves 50 labels and K'={[13]}. The labeled circuit family can be enormous while this count representation stays one integer coordinate. This is a representation separation, not a universal complexity claim or a physical example.
