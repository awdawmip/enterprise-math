## 1. Result and exact scope

R791 leaves open whether sharing a small source network makes worst-prefix rank evaluation easy. We prove a computational obstruction for the actual R779 local prefix stock model, at a declared fixed-cardinality observation level. For every finite simple graph G=(V,E), m=|E|>=1, there is ONE loop-free subcubic Port network of O(|V|+m) vertices and edges, b=|V|+m labeled boundary ports T=V disjoint union A with A={a_e:e in E}, fixed target and reserve, and 3^m actual prefixes, such that
    min_x max_{D in E_x, |A xor D|=m} |(A xor D) intersect V| = tau(G),
where tau(G) is minimum vertex cover. Every prefix has a nonempty level-m relation, indeed it is a transversal matroid basis family of rank m. The *whole* boundary relation E_x may have additional states at levels m+2j; we do not assert that the entire family satisfies R791's single-type hypothesis. A real fixed exterior of that type isolates the level and observes the formula without treating other levels as redundant for arbitrary future operations.

## 3. Rank envelope and vertex cover

Write W=V. For a fixed prefix, rank on W is the maximum matching size from the m slots to their allowed endpoint labels; unmatched slots extend using their distinct private labels. For every prefix choose one allowed endpoint of each edge. The distinct chosen endpoints form a vertex cover. Each distinct endpoint can be assigned its own choosing slot (each slot chose only one), so they form an independent subset of W. Consequently every prefix has rank at least tau(G).

Conversely choose any minimum vertex cover C, choose one endpoint in C for each edge, and select the prefix excluding the opposite singleton at that slot. Each slot now has only its chosen endpoint available at level m. The maximum matching to W equals the number of distinct chosen endpoints, at most |C|. This proves equality, and yields an explicit minimizing prefix and a rank certificate. Equivalently min over one-endpoint-per-edge choices of the number of distinct endpoints is exactly minimum vertex cover.

For arbitrary W subset V, the same proof shows rho(W)=tau(G[W]): edges entirely inside W must choose an endpoint in W; all other edges can choose outside W. This stronger formula gives the whole restricted envelope on V, including empty W, and immediately supplies non-submodular examples (a single edge). It does not claim a formula on mixed W containing private labels.
