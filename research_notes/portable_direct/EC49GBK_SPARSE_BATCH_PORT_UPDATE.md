## Exact single-insertion pruning
Let P={(W,v):v=g(W), g(T)>v for every strict superset T of W}, as GBJ. For mask E, split P into changed masks E subset W and unchanged masks. If min_{changed} v=0 then h=0 and P is unchanged by GBJ. Otherwise h=1, changed weights decrease by 1 and unchanged weights stay fixed.
For every two old support masks W proper subset T, v_T>v_W by definition; integrality gives v_T-v_W>=1. If W changed, T is also changed; their strict difference remains. If both unchanged, difference remains. The only possible new domination is unchanged W and changed T, with exactly v_T=v_W+1. Hence remove precisely those unchanged W for which such a T exists. All changed masks survive, so simultaneous deletion is safe, without a recursive dominance pass or dense expansion. The min over surviving supersets is the canonical new g by GBJ. This is exact for arbitrary finite integer capacity graphs and any number of fresh insertions, with the same scope as GBJ.
The implementation groups changed masks by old integer weight and tests only the group v+1 for each unchanged mask. If L=|P|, group counts a_j changed, b_j unchanged, at most sum_j b_j*a_{j+1} subset tests plus O(L) dictionary operations, each charged mask width. Worst case quadratic in L, L itself may be exponential in port count. Dense initialization remains O(m*2^m); no universal speedup or bit complexity improvement claimed.

## Exact batch update and domination gap
For a finite multiset Q of fresh labels, F(W)=#{x in Q:E(x) subset W}. D=max_{(W,v) in P}(F(W)-v), increment h=|Q|-D. This follows from GBJ since any S is contained in a support mask W with g(W)=g(S) and F(W)>=F(S). D>=0. Let s_A be GBJ normalized cut slack. Then s_{A+Q}(V)=s_A(V)+D-F(V). For any V choose T superset V with s_A(T)=g_A(V), then choose a support W superset T with g_A(W)=g_A(T)=g_A(V). Such W has a minimizing superset attaining s_A=g_A; maximal equal-level property forces that superset to be W itself. Monotonicity gives F(W)>=F(V) and s_A(W)<=s_A(V). Hence minimizing over supersets of S gives precisely
g_{A+Q}(S)=min_{(W,v) in P, W superset S}(v+D-F(W)).
This proves one-pass reweight and prune without rebuilding old graph, support only shrinks; W can be deleted exactly when some strict superset T in old P satisfies v_T-v_W<=F(T)-F(W). Dominated witnesses can be replaced by a maximal nondominated witness through finite inclusion; surviving pairs equal the canonical new support. Batch rank and envelope agree with any sequential insertion order. Duplicate masks carry multiplicity; identities preserved in external history. Cost of direct batch cover O(L*d) mask tests for d distinct masks and O(L^2) worst-case dominance after sorting. No speed claim for batch yet.
