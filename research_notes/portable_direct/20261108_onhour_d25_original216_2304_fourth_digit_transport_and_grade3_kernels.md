## Result B — PROVED all-prime source-preserving third-grade tail formulas

The *unweighted coefficient* grade is g_W(n)=floor(2n/p)+floor(3n/p) and g_V(n)=floor(4n/p). At fourth-digit precision, both models have a genuine grade-three tail. Define the original grade-three observers (by dividing the actual original sums by p^3 before reducing modulo p):

where w_n,v_n include the exact original linear weight. For **every prime p>3**, the following finite, p-unit-denominator identities hold:

    T_W3 = 2 sum_(r=0)^floor((p-3)/3)
               (6r+5) (r!)^5 216^r / ((2r+1)!(3r+2)!)       (mod p),  (T-W)

    T_V3 = 6 sum_(r=0)^floor((p-4)/4)
               (8r+7) (r!)^4 2304^r / (4r+3)!                (mod p).  (T-V)

**Proof.** For 0<=r<p and q=0,1,2,3, with qp+r<p^2, remove every factor p from (qp+r)! to obtain
    (qp+r)! / p^q = (-1)^q q!r! (mod p).
This follows from Wilson's theorem applied to each complete nonzero residue block and the q factors p,2p,...,qp. In the W grade-three interval, 2n=p+a and 3n=2p+b, so the original unweighted numerator divided by p^3 reduces to -2 a!b!; in the V interval, 4n=3p+c, giving -6c!. The denominators are (n!)^5 216^n and (n!)^4 2304^n respectively. Substitute n=p-1-r, use (p-1-j)! = (-1)^(j+1)/j! (mod p), and Fermat 216^(-(p-1))=2304^(-(p-1))=1 (mod p). The linear weights become -(6r+5) and -(8r+7), canceling the negative signs. The interval endpoints become precisely r=0,...,floor((p-3)/3) and r=0,...,floor((p-4)/4). All factorials in the displayed denominators have argument <p. QED.
