## Theorem 1: exact nested collision criterion

1. S_(D_U)(lambda)=1;
2. lambda differs from alpha_i for all i in D_V;
3. sum_(i in A) (1-alpha_i)*pi_i/(lambda-alpha_i)=0.

Proof. A root of C_D avoids every holding pole in D. For distinct holdings this follows from C_D(alpha_i) != 0. For repeated holdings, R229's grouped holding polynomial has the same property.
