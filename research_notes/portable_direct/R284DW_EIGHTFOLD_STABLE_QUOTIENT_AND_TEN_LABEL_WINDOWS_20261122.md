## Theorem C — B-flip label preservation and an exact gap law

Let `B` be the least significant bit of the four upper source coordinates (in the R284DU encoding). Flipping `B` changes the unreduced determinant by the **exact** amount

\[
T(B=1)-T(B=0)=-q_3,\qquad
q_3=(44-xw)u_3+(11+x)g+(4+w)h.
\]

On this 4096-state family, `59≤q3≤120`. There are **2048** such B-partner pairs. All pairs have the same farthest-arc label at both ends and the same affine branch, so the signed numeric difference is

\[
D(B=1)-D(B=0)=-s_{L}q_3,\qquad |D(B=1)-D(B=0)|=q_3.
\]

The smallest winner-over-runner-up margin among all 2048 `B=0` endpoints is **37,469**. Since each circular arc-distance is 1-Lipschitz and `2q3≤240`, this also independently certifies that no B-flip can change the winning label. The 19 distinct gap values and their exact multiplicities are in the JSON certificate; in particular, the original 256 minimal-gap witness pairs remain (59:64 pairs; 60:192 pairs), while the largest new gap is 120 (192 pairs).
