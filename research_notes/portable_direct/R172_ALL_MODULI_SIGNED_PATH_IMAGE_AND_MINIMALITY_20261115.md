## 1. R172 main theorem: exact reachable image for all q

Work first with d>=1 signed coordinate directions ±e_i and q>=2. Define

    K_q(P)=(a,b,c)=(|P|,D(P),J(P)) mod q
    in H_q^d=(Z/qZ) x (Z/qZ)^d x (Z/qZ)^d,

with the exact R171 product

    (a,b,c)*(r,s,t)=(a+r,b+s,c+t+r*b) mod q.

Let M_{q,d} be the image of all *positive-length or empty signed-step words* under K_q. Then:

**Theorem R172A (complete membership certificate).** A triple (a,b,c) lies in M_{q,d} exactly under the following conditions:

1. q odd: every ambient triple is reachable.
2. q ≡ 2 (mod 4): a ≡ sum_i b_i (mod 2); no other restriction.
3. q ≡ 0 (mod 4): a ≡ sum_i b_i (mod 2), and
   sum_i c_i ≡ a(a+1)/2 (mod 2).

In condition 3, a(a+1)/2 (mod 2) is well-defined from the residue a modulo q because 4 divides q. For q ≡ 2 (mod 4), it cannot be inferred from a modulo q alone; the unrestricted c parity in case 2 is a genuine reachable freedom.

**Theorem R172B (exact context quotient cardinality).** For every q>=2 and d>=1,

    |M_{q,d}| = q^(2d+1) / gcd(q,4).                 (1)

By R171's context-equivalence theorem, this is also the **exact minimum number of deterministic states** needed to preserve the mod-q vector-valued J readout under arbitrary signed-path left and right concatenation contexts.

For the six native axes d=6, the sharp numbers include q=2: 4096; q=3: 1,594,323; q=4: 16,777,216. These are observer-relative finite quotients, not counts of native Cells or amplitudes.

## 2. Proof: projection onto length and displacement

Since H_q^d is a finite group under R171's law, the submonoid generated by the twelve (or 2d) primitive step images is a subgroup: each generator inverse equals a positive power of that generator. Thus ordinary group-generator calculations prove reachability by nonnegative path words.

Project (a,b,c) to (a,b). Every signed elementary step contributes (1, ±e_i). For even q, both signs have total coordinate sum ≡1 mod 2, hence every word satisfies

    a - sum_i b_i ≡0 mod 2.                          (2)

Conversely, any b residue is reached by a path using b_i representatives in {0,...,q-1} copies of +e_i. A zero-displacement loop (+e_1,-e_1) changes length by +2 without changing b. The attainable a for a fixed b therefore form the coset sum_i b_i +2(Z/qZ). For odd q, multiplication by 2 is surjective and every a occurs; for even q this is exactly (2). This completely describes the (a,b) projection.

## 3. Proof: center of the generated subgroup

Let C be the subgroup of all reachable (0,0,c). The products of signed generators and their reversals give central commutators:

    (+e_i,+e_j) versus (+e_j,+e_i):  (0,0,e_i-e_j),
    (+e_i,-e_i) versus (-e_i,+e_i):  (0,0,2e_i).

These elements generate all c with even total coordinate sum when q is even. Indeed,

    c = sum_(i<d) c_i(e_i-e_d) + (sum_i c_i)e_d,

and the final term is an integer multiple of 2e_d precisely when sum_i c_i is even. When q is odd, 2e_i generates every coordinate, so C is the full central group.

For q even, **necessity** of any further central restriction comes from the parity identity for every raw signed path of length m:

    sum_i J_i(P)
      ≡ sum_(r=1)^m (m-r+1)
      ≡ m(m+1)/2             (mod 2).               (3)

Here each signed unit step has coordinate sum ±1 ≡1 mod 2. If 4|q and a=m mod q, formula (3) depends only on a mod 4. A central element must have a=0 mod q, so its total c parity is even. The commutators above already generate *all* such c, hence C is exactly the index-two even-sum central subgroup.

If q≡2 (mod 4), take q/2 copies of the two-step zero-displacement loop (+e_1,-e_1). Its image is

    (q,0,(q/2)e_1)=(0,0,(q/2)e_1) mod q.

Because q/2 is odd, this supplies an odd-sum central vector. Together with the even-sum center, it generates the full central group. Consequently C is full for q≡2 mod4.

## 4. Complete membership and exact count

The generated subgroup's projection has q^(d+1) states for odd q, or q^(d+1)/2 for even q. Each nonempty projected fiber is a coset of the central subgroup C. Thus:

- q odd: projection full and C full; M=H, cardinality q^(2d+1).
- q≡2 mod4: parity (2) is necessary and sufficient; C full; cardinality q^(2d+1)/2.
- 4|q: (2) and (3) are necessary. The projection has q^(d+1)/2 states and C has q^d/2. Their product is q^(2d+1)/4, exactly the number of ambient triples satisfying both displayed congruences. Therefore the congruences are jointly sufficient as well.

This proves Theorems R172A and R172B. QED.
