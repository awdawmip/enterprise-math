## 1. Scope and disjoint-deficit theorem

Let the finite port set C have positive integer capacities c(v), each label x have a nonempty support E_x, and strict Hall independence mean f_I(V)=#{x in I:E_x subset V} <= c(V)-1 for every nonempty V subset C. Let S be an independent fixed prefix and F a disjoint finite suffix. For nonempty U define q_S(U)=min_{V superset U}(c(V)-1-f_S(V)), as in GBX; the suffix independent sets are those satisfying f_Q(U)<=q_S(U). Let W(F) consist of unions of suffix supports, including empty, let F_W be the labels whose support is contained in W, and n(W)=|F_W|. Set g(W)=r_(M/S)(F_W), g(empty)=0.

Claim: for ANY upper quota function a on nonempty support unions satisfying
    g(U) <= a(U) <= q_S(U),
the exact canonical rank is
    g(W) = n(W)-D(W),
    D(W) = max_{pairwise disjoint nonempty U_1,...,U_l in W(F), U_j subset W}
                 sum_j [n(U_j)-a(U_j)],                              (1)
where the empty family has weight zero. In particular one can normalize the true quotas q_S, or any valid intermediate upper quotas squeezed between g and q_S. No nonnegativity of individual packing weights is needed. Quotas used here are integers.

Proof: h_S(U)=q_S(U)+1 for U nonempty extends to h_S(empty)=0, the monotone submodular lower envelope of c(V)-f_S(V) over supersets (including empty). Indeed c-f_S is submodular by supermodularity of f_S, and minimizers for U,V give the submodular inequality by union/intersection; monotonicity is immediate. Thus q_S is intersecting submodular, meaning submodular on pairs with nonempty intersection. Take a maximum independent suffix subset I of F_W. Every excluded label x in F_W is blocked by some tight set U containing E_x; intersect U with W. Since every selected label is supported in W, f_I(U intersect W)=f_I(U), and monotonicity of q plus feasibility show U intersect W is also tight and nonempty. If two nonempty tight sets intersect, intersecting submodularity of q and supermodularity of f_I imply their union and intersection are tight. Hence inclusion-maximal tight sets inside W are disjoint and cover the supports of every excluded label. Shrink each such set U to the union of supports of F_W contained in U, discarding empty unions. This does not change the contained labels or f_I; monotonicity and feasibility force the shrunk set still to be tight. These shrunk sets are disjoint support unions and every excluded label is still contained in one. Their sum of n(U)-q_S(U) is exactly n(W)-|I|. Replacing q_S by a<=q_S cannot decrease this sum. Conversely for any disjoint family, each label with nonempty support is counted in at most one member, and any independent I has |I intersect F_U|<=g(U)<=a(U). Therefore sum(n(U)-a(U))<=n(W)-|I| for maximum I. Both bounds prove (1). Empty W is immediate.

The result is a support-level packing certificate, not a claim that the original native relation is decomposable into single supports.

## 2. Closed canonical transition without rank queries

Process a whole nonempty same-support class H of support E, choose R subset H of size t, and remove the rest from the suffix. Write F'=F minus H. Legal t are exactly 0<=t<=min(|H|,g(E)): any t-subset of H is symmetric, and t<=g(E) implies t<=q_S(E), which ensures feasibility since every set containing E has q_S at least q_S(E). Necessity is immediate. For each nonempty W in W(F'), set
    a_t(W)=min(g(W),g(W union E)-t).                                  (2)
Both old coordinates exist in W(F). Every legal future subset Q of F'_W has size <= g(W) and |Q|+t<=g(W union E), so its true child rank is <=a_t(W). On the other side, by the exact GBX quota update (or splitting the defining minimum by whether V contains E),
    q_(S union R)(W)=min(q_S(W),q_S(W union E)-t),
and a_t(W)<=q_(S union R)(W). Apply (1) in the child state with a_t. This produces the exact canonical GCC child profile, without a cached basis, greedy rank query, full class-union table or new Hall scan. Legal t also ensures nonnegative intermediate quotas. The zero coordinate is always set to zero.

A multi-class extension is proved by the same squeeze: for disjoint processed classes H_i with supports E_i and legal counts t_i, write E_J=union_{i in J}E_i, t(J)=sum_{i in J}t_i. Put
    a(W)=min_{J subset [k]} [g(W union E_J)-t(J)].
Every feasible child subset Q satisfies each term's bound using Q plus the selected labels in J. The analogous true q update follows by interchanging the minimum over V superset W with the minimum over J having E_J subset V (all t_i nonnegative). Thus (1) also normalizes the simultaneous update. When multiple J have the same E_J, retain only the largest t(J); this is an exact certificate for dropping duplicate corner terms. The multi-class extension is proved here; the executed DP below uses one processed support class per step.

## 3. Support-closure normalization recurrence and counterexample

Let d(W)=n(W)-a(W). In inclusion order over W(F'), compute D(empty)=0 and
    D(W)=max(0,d(W),
             max_{V proper subset W, V in W(F')} D(V),
             max_{V,Z nonempty disjoint, V union Z=W} [D(V)+D(Z)]).    (3)
All operands are strict predecessors. The recurrence is exact: any optimal packing either has union strictly smaller than W (its union is in the closure), consists of the single set W, or splits into two nonempty disjoint subpackings with unions V,Z and V union Z=W. Conversely each option yields a packing. No need to enumerate packings or labels. Precompute proper containments and disjoint pairs once per suffix. If u is the number of nonempty support-union masks, p the count of proper containment pairs and z the count of unordered disjoint nonempty pairs, each normalization costs O(u+p+z) scalar work; construction uses O(u^2) mask comparisons and O(u+p+z) pair storage per stage. Each t has u' scalar minima for (2); sharing same t for distinct objective options is exact. Include mask-width cost, objective-state/witness cost and dominance comparisons separately. u is potentially exponential in port count and class count. This bound is not a universal runtime improvement. One may replace all proper containments by cover edges of the inclusion poset without changing (3), by monotonicity; not implemented here.

Counterexample to skipping normalization: four unit-capacity ports a,b,c,d, two labels of support ab, two of support cd, and one label H of support abcd. At empty prefix g(ab)=g(cd)=1 and g(abcd)=3 (one ab, one cd, H feasible). Skip H (t=0). Raw (2) on abcd remains 3, but true child rank is 2; at most one ab and one cd. The disjoint pair in (3) gives D(abcd)=2, correcting raw rank 3. Thus the raw upper quotas can be exact as a feasibility description yet noncanonical as a rank signature.
