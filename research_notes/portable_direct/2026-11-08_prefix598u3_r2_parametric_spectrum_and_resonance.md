## Model and exact statement

- Q_ij=2 x_{a(i)} for i!=g, j in N(i), j!=g.
- Q_ig=(3+z)x_{a(i)} for i!=g and g in N(i).
- Other entries are zero.

For nonnegative w,z this is the previous positive native carrier. The following polynomial identity holds formally over Q[w,z,t], and hence for arbitrary real w,z. The variable t is spectral, not time.

## Exact correction of the preceding universal minimum-degree claim

For the stationary two-class family at **w=2**, the correct sharp minimal-polynomial degree is

| Gate z | Sharp degree |
|---|---:|
| z=1 | 22 |
| z=109/17 | 26 |
| z=153/13 | 26 |
| Every other real z | 28 |

In particular the previous statement in PREFIX-598U2 that the degree is 28 for every z!=1 is disproved by the two strictly positive rational gate weights above, under the same model and original hypotheses.

Let R_z(t)=P(2,t)-(z-1)B(2,t). At z=109/17, gcd(H(2,t),R_z)=t^2-8; at z=153/13, gcd(H(2,t),R_z)=t^2+24. Exact rational Horner evaluation of all 2304 matrix entries verifies

L_z(Q(2,z))=0, where L_z=H(2,t)R_z(t)/gcd(H(2,t),R_z)

in both cases. L_z has degree 26. Independently, the 48 by 26 Krylov matrix generated by the explicit integer seed v_i=(i+1)^3+5i+1 for i=0,...,47 has rank 26 modulo the prime 1000003 at each exceptional z. Since 13 and 17 are invertible modulo this prime, a nonzero rank minor modulo the prime is also nonzero over Q. Therefore the true minimal polynomial has degree at least 26, matching the degree-26 annihilator.

The w=2 base matrix A=Q(2,1) has exact degree-22 squarefree annihilator H*C1*C2*C3*C4 and an explicit degree-22 modular Krylov witness. The gate perturbation has the original rank-one form Q(2,z)=A+(z-1)u e_g^T. The A-Krylov space U generated by u has exact dimension 18, is invariant under Q(2,z), and has cyclic polynomial R_z; the quotient V/U is the unchanged diagonalizable base quotient with minimal polynomial H. It follows that H R_z annihilates Q(2,z) for all z and each minimum polynomial is divisible by lcm(H,R_z). For z!=1, exact factor/remainder calculations show the *only* shared-factor cases are 109/17 and 153/13; elsewhere gcd(H,R_z)=1, yielding sharp degree 28. The degree-26 cases are independently checked as above. At z=1, the base degree-22 result applies.

The error in the previous proof was treating gcd(H,B)=1 as if it implied gcd(H,P-(z-1)B)=1 for every z!=1. Even when the first gcd is 1, the two terms may cancel at a root of H for a special z. The corrected statement preserves the prior characteristic-polynomial identities and nonresonant results at their precise scope.
