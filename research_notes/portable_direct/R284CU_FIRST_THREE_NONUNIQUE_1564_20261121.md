## General finite-set truncation lemma and proof

Given finite X and real-valued functions f_k, let t_{k,R} be the R-th largest value with multiplicity, H_{k,R}={x:f_k(x)>t_{k,R}}, E_{k,R}={x:f_k(x)=t_{k,R}} and q_{k,R}=R-|H_{k,R}|. A subset C retains the first R values of f_k with multiplicity if and only if H_{k,R} is included in C and |C intersect E_{k,R}|>=q_{k,R}. Necessity follows because omitting any higher value or a required equal-cutoff copy destroys the top-R multiset; sufficiency follows directly by collecting the greater values and enough equal-cutoff copies. Intersect constraints across all k.

In the frozen population and all R<=1564, exhaustive integer sorting gives exactly 19 distinct tied rank boundaries, each at ONE level, each a double tie occupying precisely ranks R and R+1. At all other levels the rank-R cutoff is strict. Therefore those levels force their entire rank-R prefix into C, and the tied level forces its first R-1 points plus one member of the tied pair. Let M_R denote the union of such forced codewords.

If both or one of the two tied sites already belongs to M_R, the unique cardinality-minimum core is M_R. If neither does, the only minima are M_R union {a} and M_R union {b}. Where there is no tie, the unique minimum is the union of the strict top-R prefixes. The d_1(x)=0 level is automatically preserved because every such core has at least R sites.

The 19 tied boundary ranks in order are 155,599,779,905,957,1017,1101,1139,1177,1195,1263,1337,1377,1385,1395,1397,1443,1487,1563. All sixteen events other than 779,1263,1563 have at least one tied member already forced by other levels. The three displayed pairs have no member in M_R. This proves the theorem and minimum sizes for the exact declared finite model and rank range.
