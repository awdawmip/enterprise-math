## Theorem and reduction

Let U be a finite tagged directed-germ bank on nondegenerate open projective segments, with at least one germ per necessary support. A real nonzero linear factor M has no zero germ iff its normal is not proportional to any necessary support normal: a zero germ means identically zero on the segment by the R238 factor-recovery argument. Therefore, for a product Q=MN with neither factor proportional to a support, each factor has a full binary germ partition. Write A_M for one sign branch, U\\A_M for the other. The product's negative branch is A_M symmetric-difference A_N and positive branch its complement, up to global sign reversal. Reversing either factor just complements the result. Thus unordered full binary partitions of the linear bank give exhaustive unordered strict product partitions by XOR; repetitions and squares are covered. No zero-germ deletion occurs at the root. Deduplicating root masks is exact for all future finite-germ linear capacity tests, not for the full continuous output problem. Multiplicities are counts of unordered factor-partition pairs, not a measure on coefficients or counts of actual polynomials.

For degree two in three homogeneous variables, any singular indefinite real symmetric matrix has rank two and real factorization: by orthogonal diagonalization, Q=lambda u²-mu v²=(sqrt(lambda)u+sqrt(mu)v)(sqrt(lambda)u-sqrt(mu)v), lambda,mu>0. A rank-one or semidefinite nonzero Q with no support factor has constant eventual strict sign on the bank, so all 47 labels enter a single depth-five branch, exceeding 31. Consequently, once the reducible root family is settled the remaining possible root matrices must be nonsingular indefinite; the present computation is a partial screen of the factor-free reducible family only. The same semidefinite exclusion applies even with a support factor since at most two labels are annihilated and >31 remain. Zero polynomial likewise provides no split; correct tree would need all 47 labels resolved by one depth-five child, so it is excluded directly (not by the zero-germ relaxation).

The depth-five child capacity is 31 labels. For a child with 31 remaining labels, its next linear query must annihilate at least one whole label: otherwise the sum of child label counts is >=31 while the two depth-four children have total capacity <=30. An annihilated label implies the query is proportional to its support. Hence only the 47 support queries need be tested in that state; for all other states with c>15 enumerate the full R229/R233 rank-three bank. For c<=15, the one-step capacity test necessarily passes (all child subsets have <=15 labels). This proves correctness of the stated two-level necessary-condition screen, not feasibility of suffix trees. The finite germ lower-bound implication is inherited; no full direction scoring repeated.
