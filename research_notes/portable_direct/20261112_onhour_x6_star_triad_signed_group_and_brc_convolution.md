## Theorem 1: signed C6 generator and full group

```text

R(e_l)=e_l for l outside {i,j,k}.
```

On the six signed unit directions supported in this triple, its action is the six-cycle

`e_i → e_j → e_k → -e_i → -e_j → -e_k → e_i.`

It obeys `R_{ijk}^3=-I` **on the selected three coordinates** and the identity on the others, `R_{ijk}^6=I` globally. It preserves signed primitive adjacency and the established quadratic component readout. This is a fully specified integer map, not a claim of a primitive one-edge movement between `x` and `R(x)`.

