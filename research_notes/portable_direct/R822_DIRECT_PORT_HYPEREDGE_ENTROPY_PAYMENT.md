## 2. New connected hyperedge enumeration bound

For a group B with b_B>0 put ell_B=ceil(log2 b_B), and ell_B=0 if b_B=0. Define
 L_v=max_{B in F_v} ell_B,  c_v=s_v+L_v.
All costs are positive integers; compute ceil log by doubling. A selected hyperedge set C is connected when its incidence graph on the participating groups is connected. Then all nonempty connected C with c(C)=sum_{v in C}c_v<=k can be enumerated in O(g 2^{k+2} poly(N,k)) time, where g is the number of groups and N is explicit input size. No bound on b_B or s_v is needed.

Proof. Root a connected support at one incident group. Traverse discovered group nodes in FIFO order. At a group B list selected hyperedges not discovered earlier in increasing local incidence order. For each newly discovered v write a 1 followed by its local index in ell_B bits. Discover *all* its incident groups in fixed order (no choice or cost of ordering). Mark v seen. After the list write a 0. Each selected v is emitted once; every participating group is processed once because the support is connected. There are at most 1+sum_{v in C}(s_v-1) groups. The bit length is thus
 sum_{v in C}(1+ell_{parent(v)}) + number of groups <= 1+sum_{v in C}(s_v+L_v)=1+c(C).
The root and code uniquely reconstruct every emitted labeled hyperedge using the same queue and incidence lists. Enumerate binary words up to length k+1 for each root; reject malformed indices, repeated factor labels, incomplete/extra codes and sets exceeding budget; deduplicate outputs. All valid connected sets occur by their canonical encoding. Decoding uses polynomial time, including scanning the full listed incidence of a hyperedge; c_v>=s_v so the retained support has at most k total incidences. This improves on an incidence Euler walk paying every group-to-hyperedge choice: one group-index choice suffices per discovered hyperedge. The s_v cost pays queue delimiters and newly exposed groups. The result concerns labeled hypergraphs even with coincident footprints.

## 3. Uniform local-load zero-free theorem for the original soft factors

Let q_v=2^{c_v} delta_v (omit zero factors), and for each group B let beta_B=sum_{v:B in F_v}q_v. If
 beta_B<=1/8 for every B,

Proof. Use the R820 section 2 dependency-polymer lemma: moment factorization across disconnected supports follows from independent group laws; every joint moment of these indicators lies in [0,1]. On the factor dependency graph (intersecting footprints) the sufficient condition is

Multiply the per-group products over F_v, including repeated neighbors only to decrease the product, and excluding v. Since product(1-q)>=1-sum q, the right side is at least q_v (7/8)^{2s_v} >= q_v(7/8)^{2c_v}. At R=3/2 the inequality follows from 2(7/8)^2=49/32>3/2. R820 then bounds every deletion ratio by q_v from 1 in modulus. Telescoping its analytic logs yields |log P|<=sum q_v/(1-q_v)<= (8/7) sum_B beta_B<=g/7. Unlike R821's 1/16 footprint bound, this uses cost at least the number of incident groups, not merely edges plus an extra vertex, and allows load 1/8. ## 5. Direct degree certificate and explicit new network family

Since q_v<=2^{s_v+L_v-2d_v-1}, a sufficient vertexwise certificate is
 2d_v-s_v >= 2L_v+2.
Indeed q_v<=2^{-L_v-3}<=1/(8 b_B) for every incident B (b_B<=2^{ell_B}<=2^{L_v}); summing over its b_B shared vertices proves beta_B<=1/8. This is an exact integer certificate; it can be tested without forming all interface weights. It is only sufficient, not a hardness threshold.

Example: take disjoint retained biconnected ladders of arbitrary sizes, each with maximum b shared vertices, and add nonretained simple edges between their vertices. Assume every ladder vertex has p_v>=2 ceil(log2 b)-1 such nonretained incident edges, every added edge meets ladder vertices, and no other retained blocks touch there. These are ordinary explicit graphs (one can take two sufficiently large ladders with complete bipartite extra edges, or a sparse graph of appropriate degree connecting sufficiently many ladders). At each vertex retained degree k_v>=2, d_v=k_v+p_v and s_v=1+p_v. Hence 2d_v-s_v=2k_v+p_v-1>=2ceil(log2 b)+2>=2L_v+2 (b>=2; singleton groups have <=2 ports). Local ladder width is 2 and singleton widths trivial, so R813 supplies polynomial-time joint observers even for unbounded ladder cycle rank and b. Both the direct hypergraph arity and group degree can grow without bound, and the factor-incidence graph can have arbitrary cycles; complete bipartite added connections give unbounded graph treewidth. The extra degree is actual original degree (not a long weak separator inserted into an edge). All edge weights and all feasible charges in the specified R812 model are allowed. This supplies a concrete class beyond the explicit small-interface R812 and acyclic R813 conditions, without R821's long-road assumption.
