N_k = { (E15 minus D) union A : D subset E15, |D|=k, A subset (Omega minus E15), |A|=k-1 }.

Every E in N_1 union N_2 union N_3 has exactly 14 distinct arcs. **Theorem R722 (local edit barrier):** For every such E, at least one of the six V17 slot permutations is unattainable by a rooted CNB closed positive path of length m=4 or m=5. This is a complete theorem about the specified finite neighborhood (455,190 distinct 14-arc candidates). Existence of 14-or-fewer arc solutions elsewhere in the 60-arc universe is an open question.
