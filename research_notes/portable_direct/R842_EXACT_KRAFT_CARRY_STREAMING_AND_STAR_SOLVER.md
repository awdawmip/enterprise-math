## 1. Exact local carry coordinate

Let a Kraft scope have d labeled variables with lengths L_i in {0,...,M} (possibly smaller individual caps). Put c_j=#{i:L_i=j}. Define z_M=0 and, for j=M,...,1,
    z_(j-1)=ceil((c_j+z_j)/2).
Then
    sum_i 2^(-L_i)<=1  iff  c_0+z_0<=1.
For M=0 the recurrence is empty and the statement is immediate. Indeed z_j=ceil(sum_(l=j+1)^M c_l 2^(j-l)), by descending induction and the identity ceil((a+ceil(x))/2)=ceil((a+x)/2) for integer a and real x. The final integer is ceil of the Kraft sum. Each c_j and z_j is in [0,d] (ceil of a nonnegative sum of at most d terms each <=1). For each level j compute c_j by the exact count chain C_(j,0)=0, C_(j,i)=C_(j,i-1)+1[L_i=j]. Impose the descending carry transitions and terminal inequality. This is a constant-arity (at most three variables per non-unary transition) exact constraint expansion with O(d(M+1)) auxiliary variables/constraints and at most d+1 count/carry states per variable. It avoids the single domain 2^M+1 of a direct saturated weighted-sum accumulator. The factor count includes all equality/indicator transitions; no sorting or enumeration of 2^M sums is needed. The lengths are retained and can be recovered unchanged; the carry coordinate decides only the Kraft observer for that assignment. It introduces no radius or margin loss, no new rounding of payments, and can replace every Kraft constraint in R841 independently. R839 allows M<=max(0,Delta-1). This is a domain/arity reduction, not a claim that expanded treewidth stays bounded independently of M.

## 2. Exponential obstruction for a single arbitrary-order exact streaming state

For each m>=2 take M=m+2 and a scope of d=2m+2 labels. In the first m positions, at position i=1,...,m choose L_i=i+1 or i+2. Their 2^m sums P are distinct: changing the i-th choice changes P by 2^(-(i+2)); these are different binary weights. Every P is strictly between 0 and 1/2 and is a multiple of 2^(-M). For any smaller P among a pair P<P', the remainder 1-P has a binary dyadic decomposition with at most M=m+2 positive terms at lengths <=M. It contains >2^(M-1) units of 2^(-M). Split any term of length <M into two terms of the next length until there are exactly m+2 terms. Such a split remains possible until that count is reached: if all terms were already at M, the term count would exceed 2^(M-1)>=m+2. This yields a common suffix of exactly m+2 lengths, whose sum is 1-P. The prefix P followed by this suffix is legal; P' followed by the identical suffix is illegal. Consequently any deterministic one-pass exact Kraft decision automaton reading lengths in that fixed position order must distinguish all 2^m prefixes after position m. Its state domain there is at least 2^m, even with R839's cap (M<=d-1). This is a cardinality bound, not an exponential bit-memory or general algorithmic lower bound. It explains why the multi-coordinate carry construction is necessary for this style of small-domain compression. Approximate or restricted-order methods are outside this obstruction.

## 3. Mixed-incidence width theorem for the R841 gap solver

Use the R841 finite domains and local constraints for anchor residuals, bundle residuals, propagation, minima and objective (all scopes <=4, apart from Kraft). Let D be the maximum non-Kraft variable domain size, including original lengths/payments. Given a tree decomposition of B of width w, replace each non-Kraft constraint vertex in every bag containing it by its at most four variable neighbors (retain variable and Kraft vertices). Each modified bag contains at most 4(w+1) variable vertices and at most w+1 Kraft vertices. Running intersection is preserved for each variable: its old subtree intersects each added constraint subtree by incidence edge coverage. Every non-Kraft scope is now contained in a bag. Every Kraft incidence edge still has a bag containing both its endpoints. Root/binarize the decomposition by duplicate bags and assign each Kraft incidence edge and non-Kraft factor to one such bag, once.

A DP state stores the assignments of the variables in the bag and, for each Kraft vertex still in the bag, the vector (c_0,...,c_M) counting the assigned incidence edges in the subtree. Contributions on edges assigned to the current bag are obtained from the current variable assignments. Child states agree on shared variable assignments; their histogram vectors are added along with the current contributions for every Kraft vertex present at the parent. For a Kraft vertex absent from the parent, all its bags lie in the child subtree by connectedness; at that boundary enforce the carry test of section 1, including edges assigned to its top bag. At root enforce remaining Kraft vertices. A non-Kraft factor is checked only at its designated bag; add each objective summand exactly once. No variable outside a subtree affects its already closed factors except through a retained bag variable or a still-open Kraft histogram, by running intersection. An empty scope requires no histogram. ## 4. Exact high-degree star optimization without the general M exponent

Specialize to R839 one analytic anchor (any fixed bundle partition), R>1 rational, b_f>0 rational. For a star K_(1,k), k>=1, the center has optimal L=0 because its only own constraints are singleton appearances in leaf neighborhoods, and its activity increases with L. All leaf lengths are governed by the one Kraft constraint; R839 caps them by k-1. The exact objective is the center factor times prod_(i=1)^k(1+b_i R^(L_i+2)); any bundle partition at the single anchor yields that same product. Sort b_1>=...>=b_k. There exists an optimal code with L_1<=...<=L_k: for b>=b' and c<=c', (1+b c)(1+b' c') <= (1+b c')(1+b' c) because the difference right minus left is (b-b')(c'-c)>=0. Swapping lengths preserves the common cap and Kraft. With strictly positive b and R>1, every optimum has a full prefix trie (otherwise contracting a unary node strictly lowers the objective). The same argument includes k=1 with length 0.

Use depth-l DP state (i,s): i sorted leaves assigned before depth l, s available nodes at depth l, initially (0,1) at l=0. Choose x in [0,min(s,k-i)] to make the next x leaves at depth l. Multiply by prod_(j=i+1)^(i+x)(1+b_j R^(l+2)); next state is (i+x,2(s-x)). Require s'<=k-i' and s'=0 iff i'=k; discard paths unfinished after depth k-1. All full prefix trees are represented by their depth counts; conversely every accepted transition path makes a full prefix tree by assigning x current slots as leaves and splitting all other slots into two children. Thus exact minimum of products in this DAG is the true optimum; no real-payment grid or radius shrink is needed. The number of depth/state triples is O(k^3), O(k) choices each, hence O(k^4) exact rational operations (segment products can be accumulated/precomputed); rational powers and products have polynomial bit lengths, and backpointers yield the optimal grading. Compare objective with 2-eta and recover minimal d_H=w_H if feasible. This gives a high-degree tractable family even when the R839 Kraft primal clique has width k-1.
