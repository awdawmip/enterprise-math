## Theorem 1: universal observer constancy is exactly zero cycle curl

For any integer weights w on the named edges, the following conditions are equivalent:

1. For **all** nonnegative integer capacity vectors K and integral margin vectors b, W_w is constant on every nonempty fiber F(b,K).
2. Every named simple even cycle has zero alternating signed weight sum; parallel named edges yield allowable two-edge cycles.
3. There exist **integer** vertex potentials alpha_u and beta_v such that w_(u,v)=alpha_u+beta_v on every named edge.
4. For any chosen spanning forest of G, the signed weight sum vanishes on all fundamental cycles (one per non-tree edge).

When these conditions hold, the exact observer value on each fiber equals sum_u alpha_u b_u + sum_v beta_v b_v.

**Proof.** Orient every named edge from its row vertex to its column vertex for bookkeeping. For each non-tree edge, adjoining the unique tree path creates a signed fundamental cycle g with components 0,+1,-1 and Bg=0. Potentials imply w.g=0 by w=B-transpose p. To establish the reverse, root each tree component with vertex potential zero and recursively solve w_(u,v)=alpha_u+beta_v on its tree edges. All such values are integers. The discrepancy on a non-tree chord is its fundamental-cycle curl; if these are zero, the potential representation holds on all edges. Hence W_w(x)=p.(Bx)=p.b.

Conversely, fix any named simple even cycle C. Set capacities equal to 1 on its edges and 0 elsewhere, and take two complementary alternating 0/1 edge tables x,y along C. Each cycle vertex has margin 1 in both, all other margins zero. Universal constancy forces W_w(x)=W_w(y), precisely zero alternating curl. Thus (1) implies (2). Every simple cycle is an integral combination of fundamental cycles, because its non-tree coefficients can be eliminated one at a time and no nonzero circulation remains on a forest. Conditions (2) and (4) are equivalent. This completes the proof.
