## Abstract

For the nine-state AK4 conditional source/frame model, removing the initial free, reliable observer reading changes neither the exact minimum number nor the exact minimum total event-letter cost of independent-reset, fixed, fault-tolerant terminal diagnostics. With \(d=e+2t+1\), the simultaneous optima remain
\[
N_{\min}(d)=\lceil 7d/2\rceil,\qquad
L_{\min}(d)=\lceil 9d/2\rceil.
\]
In contrast, when independent reset is removed too, a single trajectory with no observation before the first action cannot identify the initial state, even with arbitrary adaptive future actions. This is an exact interface separation between reset-based provenance recovery and destructive trajectory observation.

## 1. State, actions and observer

Let \(E=012\), \(c=120\), \(t=102\) be permutations in image notation, with left multiplication. The live state set is
\[
X_{\rm live}=\{(0,E),(1,E),(2,E)\}\cup\{(0,h):h\in S_3\setminus\{E\}\}.
\]
Adjoin an absorbing undefined state \(U\), yielding nine possible initial states. Define the totalized actions:
\[
S(s,E)=((s+1)\bmod 3,E),\quad
C(0,h)=(0,ch),\quad
T(0,h)=(0,th),
\]
and send all other applications, including actions at \(U\), to \(U\).
The terminal observer \(O\) distinguishes \(U\) and otherwise gives the source-zero/nonzero flag together with the frame cycle class: \(EE,EC,ET,CE,U\). These two-letter labels are not the original source identity.

For **AP1**, the observer never supplies \(O(x_0)\). Instead, each fixed nonempty diagnostic word \(w\in\{S,C,T\}^+\) acts on a separately reset copy of the same unknown \(x_0\), and yields exactly one terminal symbol \(O(w x_0)\). The nonadaptive probe multiset is chosen in advance. At most \(e\) terminal symbols are erased at known positions and at most \(t\) are adversarially substituted at unknown positions. The objective is unique recovery of \(x_0\) for every possible initial state and admissible fault pattern. Probe count \(N\) and total primitive-letter count \(L=\sum |w|\) are minimized separately and simultaneously. All variables are exact integers.

For **AP2**, a single trajectory is followed with no resets, no initial observation, and outputs after each action. Later actions may adapt to observed outputs.

## 2. Theorem AP1: removing the free initial read has zero optimal cost

**Theorem.** For all integers \(e,t\ge0\), put \(d=e+2t+1\). On AP1's nine-state domain, both sharp independent-reset minima are
\[
N_{\min}=2d+\lceil3d/2\rceil=\lceil7d/2\rceil,\qquad
L_{\min}=\lceil9d/2\rceil.
\]
The same multiset realizes both minima.

**Lower bound.** Restrict the initial state to either of the two CE source states, either of the two EC frame cycles, or the three ET frame transpositions. There are five equal-baseline unordered pairs. The exhaustive arbitrary-word separation-mask classification of R762AO applies unchanged even though the baseline is not observed: every nonempty word has either zero mask or exactly one of the five masks, represented at shortest cost by \(S,C,T,CT,TC\), of lengths \(1,1,1,2,2\). The classification has a direct complete finite proof by closure of the 75 transformations generated by \(S,C,T\) on the nine-state set: every word induces a map in this closure; applying terminal \(O\) yields 43 observer vectors and exactly five nonzero masks on the five pairs. The verifier builds the closure to its fixed point and checks the shortest witnesses; the abstract classification in AO is also available.

Let \(a,b,x,y,z\) be the multiplicities of these five mask types. Since a code corrects \(e\) known erasures and \(t\) unknown substitutions exactly when every pairwise Hamming distance is at least \(d=e+2t+1\), all such diagnostic sets satisfy
\[
a\ge d,\quad b\ge d,\quad x+y\ge d,\quad x+z\ge d,\quad y+z\ge d.
\]
Consequently
\[
N\ge a+b+x+y+z\ge 2d+\lceil3d/2\rceil
\]
and
\[
L\ge a+b+x+2y+2z
\ge2d+\tfrac12(x+y)+\tfrac12(x+z)+\tfrac32(y+z)
\ge\lceil9d/2\rceil.
\]
Zero-mask words cannot improve either lower bound.

**Construction and all-state sufficiency.** Choose \(a=b=d\). For \(d=2k\), set \(x=y=z=k\); for \(d=2k+1\), set \(x=y=k+1,z=k\). Execute the corresponding repeats of \(S,C,T,CT,TC\).

For clarity, the complete output table for the two one-letter probes is:

| Initial state | \(O(Sx)\) | \(O(Cx)\) |
|---|---|---|
| \((0,E)\) | CE | EC |
| \((1,E)\) | CE | U |
| \((2,E)\) | EE | U |
| \((0,c)\) | U | EC |
| \((0,c^2)\) | U | EE |
| any of \((0,h)\), \(h\) a transposition | U | ET |
| \(U\) | U | U |

Thus two distinct initial states have identical \((O(Sx),O(Cx))\) **only if both are ET transpositions**. Every other state pair is distinguished by \(S\) or \(C\), each repeated \(d\) times, hence at distance at least \(d\). The three transposition pairs are distinguished by the \(T,CT,TC\) probes with respective pairwise distance \(x+y,x+z,y+z\), each at least \(d\). Therefore all \(\binom92=36\) state pairs are at distance at least \(d\); standard unique decoding under erasures/substitutions applies. The specified word multiset has exactly \(N=\lceil7d/2\rceil\) and \(L=\lceil9d/2\rceil\), meeting both lower bounds. QED.

An equivalent sufficient residual certificate consists of the five exact separation multiplicities with the reset boundary and terminal output alphabet preserved. It is not a scalar commutative BRC weight.

## 3. Theorem AP2: no-reset adaptive recovery without initial output is impossible

**Theorem.** Under AP2, no deterministic adaptive single-trajectory policy identifies every initial state with certainty after any finite number of actions. The same holds even with unlimited future readouts/actions once the first action has occurred.

**Proof.** Before the first action no observation is available, so the policy must choose one symbol \(a\in\{S,C,T\}\) independent of the initial state. If \(a=S\), the two distinct initial states \((0,c)\) and \((0,t)\) both map immediately to \(U\). If \(a=C\) or \(a=T\), the two distinct states \((1,E)\) and \((2,E)\) both map immediately to \(U\). Since \(U\) is absorbing, their entire future observation streams coincide under every possible subsequent adaptive action. Thus for each allowed first action there is an indistinguishable pair. QED.

The same argument applies to a protocol whose initial observation may be completely erased before choosing the first action: its worst-case erased branch has AP2's information deficiency. Independent reset in AP1 is therefore an operational resource, not merely a coding convenience.
