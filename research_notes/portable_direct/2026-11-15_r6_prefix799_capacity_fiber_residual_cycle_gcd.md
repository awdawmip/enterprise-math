## Mathematical question and model

PREFIX-798 proved a structural congruence from alternating cycle weights of a positive-capacity support graph. Margins and saturated capacities can prevent those structural cycles from being traversed. Determine the **largest universally valid congruence modulus for a particular nonempty capacity-bounded fiber** using only one of its feasible tables.

Fix b,c positive integers, capacities K_ij >= 0, row margins r_i >= 0 and column margins s_j >= 0 with equal sum. Let the nonempty finite integer transportation fiber be

\[
F(K,r,s)=\{x\in\mathbb Z^{b\times c}:0\le x_{ij}\le K_{ij},\ \sum_jx_{ij}=r_i,\ \sum_ix_{ij}=s_j\}.
\]

Let w_ij be any integer observation coefficients and Q_w(x)=sum_ij w_ij x_ij. Multiplicities x_ij remain nonnegative, even when observer coefficients are negative.

For a fixed x in F, form the directed residual multigraph D_x on disjoint row vertices R_i and column vertices C_j. Include R_i→C_j of arc weight +w_ij whenever x_ij<K_ij; include C_j→R_i of arc weight -w_ij whenever x_ij>0. A directed simple cycle C has integer gain γ_w(C) equal to its signed arc-weight sum. If both arc directions exist, the two-arc cycle has gain zero. Define g_res(x,w) as the nonnegative gcd of all simple directed-cycle gains, taking gcd of an empty or all-zero family as 0.

## Theorem 799.1 — Exact feasible-difference lattice

For every feasible base table x,

\[
\boxed{\left\langle Q_w(y)-Q_w(x):y\in F(K,r,s)\right\rangle_{\mathbb Z}=g_{\rm res}(x,w)\mathbb Z.}
\]

In particular g_res is independent of the choice of x, so write g_F(w). Every attainable Q_w-value belongs to one congruence class modulo g_F(w). This is the **sharpest common additive congruence** in the sense that the additive subgroup generated by all actual differences is exactly g_F(w) Z. The observer Q_w is constant on F if and only if g_F(w)=0.

Proof. For any y in F, let d=y-x. At cell ij orient |d_ij| integer flow units R_i→C_j if d_ij>0, or C_j→R_i if d_ij<0. Such arcs exist because x and y obey the same capacity bounds. The equality of row and column margins makes this a nonnegative integral directed circulation. Repeatedly removing a directed simple cycle with positive flow multiplicity decomposes the circulation into nonnegative integer multiples of directed simple cycles. Hence Q_w(y)-Q_w(x) is an integer combination of their gains.

Conversely, given any directed simple cycle C in D_x, add one to each cell traversed forward and subtract one from each cell traversed backward. The residual-arc conditions ensure each capacity bound and the directed-cycle incidence balances every row and column. The result x^C is feasible and Q_w(x^C)-Q_w(x)=γ_w(C). A two-arc cycle changes nothing and has zero gain. Therefore every directed-cycle gain occurs as an actual difference, and every actual difference belongs to their integer span. The subgroup equality follows. Its left side depends on F and Q_w, not the base point, proving the independence. Finally g=0 exactly when all differences vanish. QED.

**Important scope:** This identity characterizes the lattice generated by attained value differences, not the entire attainable set. Gaps within a congruence class can remain; PREFIX-798's exact histogram continues to describe those gaps.

Let g_struct(w) be the gcd of alternating cycle gains in the undirected graph of cells with K_ij>0. All feasible differences are integer cycles in that graph, so

\[
g_F(w)\mathbb Z\subseteq g_{\rm struct}(w)\mathbb Z.
\]

Thus actual saturation can strengthen the congruence, even making the observer constant when it is not of row-plus-column potential type on the full capacity graph.

## Theorem 799.2 — Linear-size SCC certificate

Find the strongly connected components (SCCs) of D_x. In each SCC S choose a root o, and for each v in S choose any directed path P_v from o to v. Let d(v) denote the total integer arc weight of P_v and d(o)=0. For every directed residual arc e:u→v of weight a_e whose endpoints lie in the same SCC, define

\[
\delta_e=d(u)+a_e-d(v).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{g_F(w)=\gcd\{|\delta_e|:e\text{ is internal to an SCC}\}.}
\]

Consequently one SCC decomposition, directed path labeling and integer gcd pass evaluates the exact modulus using O(b+c+bc) graph operations and gcd operations. It never enumerates F or its cycles; bit complexity additionally depends on input coefficient sizes.

Proof. Along any directed cycle, the d(u)-d(v) terms telescope, so its gain equals the sum of its δ_e. Thus gcd(δ_e) divides all cycle gains. Conversely, take any internal edge e:u→v. Strong connectivity gives a directed path T_v from v back to the SCC root. The closed directed walks P_u,e,T_v and P_v,T_v both decompose into directed simple cycles. The difference of their weights equals δ_e. Hence δ_e is in the integer span of directed-cycle gains. The two spans coincide. Apply Theorem 799.1. QED.

**Constancy corollary.** Q_w is constant precisely when every internal SCC edge satisfies a_e=d(v)-d(u). Edges between SCCs cannot lie on directed cycles and impose no additional constraint. The criterion may hold even for structurally non-potential w when the margins saturate the support.

## Theorem 799.3 — Target-monotone cycle connectivity

For every pair x,y in F, there is a finite sequence of feasible tables beginning at x and ending at y, each obtained by adding one unit along a directed simple residual cycle of its current table. Every adjusted cell moves toward its final value y_ij with no overshoot, and the number of steps is at most

\[
\frac14\sum_{ij}|y_{ij}-x_{ij}|.
\]

Proof. Orient the nonzero signed difference y-x as in Theorem 799.1. It is a balanced nonnegative integral directed circulation. Extract a simple directed cycle whose arcs still carry positive target demand. Applying its unit update preserves margins and capacities, moves every participating cell one step toward its final value and decreases the remaining circulation. Repeat until the demand vanishes. A nontrivial simple cycle in the row-column bipartite graph has at least four arcs, so the L1 distance decreases by at least four per step. QED.

## Exact saturation counterexamples

Take a 4×4 grid with K_ij=1 only within two disjoint diagonal 2×2 blocks (rows and columns 1–2, and rows and columns 3–4), and K=0 elsewhere. Set weights w_11=2, w_33=3 and all other weights zero. The capacity graph has two independent square cycles with alternating gains 2 and 3, hence g_struct=gcd(2,3)=1.

**Example A:** Give the rows and columns margins (1,1,0,0). The second capacity block is forced to zero, while the first block has precisely two permutation tables. Their observer values are 0 and 2, so g_F=2. The true capacity-sensitive congruence is strictly stronger than the support-graph congruence.

**Example B:** Set every margin to zero while keeping the same capacities and weights. Only the all-zero table is feasible. Thus g_F=0 while g_struct=1: a structurally non-potential observer can be invariant on the actual fiber.
