## KU.1 — Reversible suffix-count carrier

Use the KT unsigned auxiliary model, fixed support schedule s_0,...,s_{N-1}, each s a cube vertex (i,j,k), Port vertices A_ij,B_ik,C_jk, three edge masks e_s,c. XOR boundary group on twelve bits, and final b even in each four-Port component. Each color choice is a labeled unit positive branch, serial composition is concatenation, XOR is the declared boundary observer; quotient connectivity means at least two distinct colors in the complete word. No native signed amplitude, physical time law or BRC dynamical attribution is asserted.

Write J_s=sum_c X^{e_s,c}. Because the three nonzero masks form the nonzero elements of a two-dimensional binary subspace, J_s^2=3+2J_s. Therefore J_s(J_s-2)=3. If H_t(r) counts all (not yet connectivity-filtered) suffix words from position t with XOR boundary r, then H_t=J_s H_{t+1} and EXACTLY

H_{t+1}(r) = (sum_c H_t(r XOR e_s,c) - 2 H_t(r))/3.

All divisions in this application are exact, nonnegative integers by the forward identity and uniqueness of the inverse over rationals. No approximation, division of signed amplitudes, or loss of branch multiplicity occurs. This is a reversible computational transform on the positive-count branch carrier, not an erasure of source history. It is not necessary to store a suffix table for each event.

For stock m_s, the initial product H_0 is built from eight factors
J_s^{m_s}=a_m+b_m J_s,
a_m=(3^m+3(-1)^m)/4, b_m=(3^m-(-1)^m)/4,
including m=0. Proof: recurrence from J^2=3+2J, or eigenvalues 3,-1. One sparse four-term XOR convolution per support suffices after exponentiation.

For a prefix let q be empty, a single color, or MIXED. Let v_c be the XOR boundary of the remaining all-c word, updated by v_c <- v_c XOR e_s,c on removing the first event. With n>0 remaining events, the exact completion weight is H_t(r) minus the number of compatible monochromatic suffixes: for empty q subtract sum_c 1_{r=v_c}; for single q subtract 1_{r=v_q}; for MIXED subtract zero. At n=0, return 1 precisely if r=0 and q=MIXED. The subtractions count distinct constant words when n>0; no empty-word double subtraction. This is the five-state KR connectivity rule specialized to ordered words.

At position t remove its factor, compute the three completion weights at r XOR e_s,c with updated q. They partition the admissible suffixes into lexicographic intervals. Rank adds preceding interval lengths and unrank selects the interval containing the rank, subtracts its lower endpoint, then proceeds. Induction on remaining length proves mutual inverses, for every valid b and schedule, including cases of empty fibers (no rank exists). For N>0, the minimal fixed-width code is ceil(log2 R_m(b)) when R>0. Schedule/inventory and boundary must be known separately. Rank is bijective, so any observer on the entire word can be evaluated after decoding; no native observer is claimed to factor through boundary alone.

Complexity: 4096 group entries (2048 even-total entries suffice), eight sparse convolutions and eight powers for initial counts; O(N*4096) integer arithmetic operations for decoding or ranking after initialization and O(4096) live count entries (plus schedule and output), versus O(N*4096) stored count entries for a materialized event-by-event suffix table. Counts have O(N+1) bits, so streaming additions/small-divisor divisions cost O(4096*N^2) bit operations with elementary big-int arithmetic, plus initialization and input/output. This is a storage improvement, not a sublinear-time claim or a benchmark.

## KU.2 — Exact prefix observer saturation, minimum six-support reserve

For an auxiliary suffix with each support stock either zero or >=2, its set of attainable local XOR boundaries is exactly {0,e_1,e_2,e_3} for each occupied support. For m=2 use two equal/different colors; for m=3 use three distinct colors for zero or one single and a repeated pair for any nonzero; append repeated pairs to obtain all larger m. Thus the reachable global boundary subgroup is the span of all occupied triangle-edge masks. For an undirected graph these edge XOR vectors span exactly the vectors even in each graph component: every edge is even, and any pair of vertices in the same component is the XOR along a path. Isolated Port vertices impose zero. Hence all 2048 even-total boundaries are reachable iff the occupied Port graph is connected on all twelve vertices.

Each occupied triangle corresponds to a cube vertex and its three incident cube edges (Ports); two triangles intersect iff their cube vertices are adjacent. Full Port coverage means the occupied cube vertices form a vertex cover; triangle-union connectivity means their induced cube subgraph is connected. The smallest connected vertex cover of the cube has six vertices. Proof: the excluded vertices must be independent. Any independent set of >=3 vertices lies in one cube bipartition class, since a nonadjacent opposite-parity pair is antipodal and no third vertex is nonadjacent to both. Removing three vertices of one class leaves at most one vertex of that class, which touches only three of the four opposite-class vertices; the fourth is isolated. Conversely removing any nonadjacent pair leaves a connected six-vertex graph and covers every cube edge. By cube symmetry the pair is distance 2 or 3: for distance 3 the remainder is a six-cycle; for distance 2 remove 000,110 and paths 010-011-001-101-100 and 011-111-101 span the remainder. There are 28-12=16 such pairs. Every seven-vertex set and the full set also qualify (deleting one cube vertex leaves a connected cube).

If a suffix word achieving the target leaves the full word monochromatic, replace two equal colors at any occupied suffix support by two copies of another color. Its XOR stays unchanged. Since total suffix length >=12 in the saturation regime, an original-color occurrence remains and connectivity is achieved. Thus this reserve guarantees every even residual, regardless of the prefix colors, with connected final quotient.

For a fixed schedule and b in F, if the tail after h events has zero or >=2 stock at each support and contains a connected vertex cover of the cube, then EVERY one of the 3^h color prefixes extends to a valid connected word in the same b fiber. The observer returning the first h edge colors therefore has precisely 3^h values on that fiber. Its minimal fixed-width residual cost is ceil(h log2 3), attainable by the base-3 prefix integer. Same lower bound holds for the observer giving cumulative XOR after each of the first h events: consecutive differences identify each chosen color uniquely at its known support. For this observer alone (and subsequent readouts of the retained prefix, stock and final boundary), histories with equal prefix can be identified; retaining only a final boundary cannot supply the prefix observer. This is a declared finite-horizon factorization, not a certificate for later hidden-state accesses or native signed BRC operations. A six-support reserve of 12 events is sharp among suffixes with every stock either zero or >=2 for covering ALL even-total residuals; no minimality assertion for suffixes with singleton stocks or restricted target residuals.
