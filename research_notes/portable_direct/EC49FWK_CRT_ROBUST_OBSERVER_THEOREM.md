## Distance principle

A multiset of binary probe readings is specified by integer multiplicities n_j≥0. Two supports U,T yield readout strings at Hamming distance

d_n(U,T)= Σ_(j∈U△T) n_j.

With the starting fiber already known, the answer to every probe can suffer up to r erasures at known sites and still be uniquely decodable iff all distinct fiber members have distance at least m=r+1. Correction of e *unknown adversarial bit flips* is possible iff all distinct fiber members have distance at least m=2e+1. This is the ordinary Hamming-distance criterion, restricted to each given spectral fiber. It does not rely on a distribution of measurement failures.
