## 1. Exact discrete cutoff recursion

For integers `N>=1`, real cutoff `y>=1`, and real `0<=t<=1`, define

\[
\Phi_N(y;t)
=\frac1N\sum_{n\le N}(1-t)^{h_y(n)},
\qquad
h_y(n)=\#\{p\mid n:p>y\}.
\]

The prime-cutoff jump law gives

\[
\Phi_N(q;t)-\Phi_N(q^-;t)
=t\frac{N_q}{N}\Phi_{N_q}(q;t),
\qquad
N_q=\left\lfloor\frac Nq\right\rfloor.
\]

Since `Phi_N(N;t)=1`, summing all jumps above `y` yields

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_N(y;t)
=1-t\sum_{y<q\le N}
\frac{N_q}{N}\Phi_{N_q}(q;t).
}
\]

The sum is over primes. This recursion is exact at every finite `N` and
contains the finite product budget through the quotient `N_q`.

## 2. Rooted Volterra/BRC operator

For a state field `F(N,y)`, define

\[
\boxed{
(\mathcal K F)(N,y)
=
\sum_{y<q\le N}
\frac{\lfloor N/q\rfloor}{N}
F(\lfloor N/q\rfloor,q).
}
\]

Every transition has two monotonicities:

\[
N\longmapsto\lfloor N/q\rfloor<N,
\qquad
y\longmapsto q>y.
\]

Hence `K` is a finite directed acyclic operator on all states with `N<=X`.
It is nilpotent.

More precisely, repeated application gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
(\mathcal K^rF)(N,y)
={}&
\sum_{\substack{y<q_1<\cdots<q_r\\q_1\cdots q_r\le N}}
\frac{\lfloor N/(q_1\cdots q_r)\rfloor}{N}
\\
&\times
F\!\left(
\left\lfloor\frac{N}{q_1\cdots q_r}\right\rfloor,
q_r
\right).
\end{aligned}
}
\]

The nested floor identity

\[
\left\lfloor
\frac{\lfloor N/a\rfloor}{b}
\right\rfloor
=
\left\lfloor\frac{N}{ab}\right\rfloor

and telescoping quotient weights prove the formula.

Thus one path of length `r` is exactly one rooted squarefree `r`-arm factor
simplex with strictly increasing prime labels. The operator does not merely

## 3. Exact continuum recursion

Let

\[
C_N(y;t)
=R_t\!\left(\frac{\log N}{\log y}\right),
\]

where `R_t` is the generalized Dickman carrier, extended by
`R_t(u)=1` for `0<=u<=1`.

The delay equation

\[
uR_t'(u)=-tR_t(u-1)
\]

and the cutoff derivative give

\[
\frac{d}{dy}C_N(y;t)
=
\frac{t}{y\log y}
R_t\!\left(
\frac{\log(N/y)}{\log y}
\right).
\]

Since `C_N(N;t)=1`, integration gives

\[
\boxed{
C_N(y;t)
=1-t\int_y^N
\frac1{s\log s}
R_t\!\left(
\frac{\log(N/s)}{\log s}
\right)ds.
}
\]

This is the continuum Volterra equation matched to the discrete rooted
recursion.

## 4. Exact discrepancy equation

Define

\[
E_N(y;t)=\Phi_N(y;t)-C_N(y;t).
\]

Define the one-step quadrature source

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
Q_N(y;t)
={}&
\sum_{y<q\le N}
\frac{N_q}{N}
R_t\!\left(\frac{\log N_q}{\log q}\right)
\\
&-
\int_y^N\frac1{s\log s}
R_t\!\left(
\frac{\log(N/s)}{\log s}
\right)ds.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

Subtracting the two recursions gives

\[
\boxed{
(I+t\mathcal K)E=-tQ.
}
\]

Because `K` is nilpotent, the inverse is an exact finite polynomial:

\[
\boxed{
E
=-t(I+t\mathcal K)^{-1}Q
=-t\sum_{r\ge0}(-t\mathcal K)^rQ,
}
\]

where the series terminates at the largest admissible increasing-prime
product depth.

Written layerwise,

\[
E=-tQ+t^2\mathcal KQ-t^3\mathcal K^2Q+\cdots.
\]

Therefore the full alternating rooted factor-face hierarchy is generated by
one source `Q` and no additional independent high-dimensional source.

This is a finite Duhamel theorem. It does not show that the resulting signed
sum is small.

## 5. Source decomposition: prime measure plus floor-depth repair

Define the finite-population prime-incidence measure

\[
\alpha_N
=
\sum_{q\le N}
\frac{\lfloor N/q\rfloor}{N}\,\delta_q
\]

and the continuum prime measure

\[
d\lambda(s)=\frac{ds}{s\log s}.
\]

Put

\[
g_{N,t}(s)
=R_t\!\left(
\frac{\log(N/s)}{\log s}
\right).
\]

Then the source splits exactly as

\[
\boxed{
Q_N(y;t)
=
\int_{(y,N]}g_{N,t}(s)\,d(\alpha_N-\lambda)(s)
+
\varepsilon_N^{\rm child}(y;t),
}

where

\[
\boxed{
\varepsilon_N^{\rm child}(y;t)
=
\sum_{y<q\le N}
\frac{N_q}{N}
\left[
R_t\!\left(\frac{\log N_q}{\log q}\right)
-
g_{N,t}(q)
\right].
}
\]

Thus the true source has only two typed coordinates:

1. the one-prime discrete-versus-continuum incidence measure;
2. the exact floor-induced shift of the child population.

## 6. The floor-child source is uniformly small

For real `0<=t<=1`, the generalized Dickman carrier satisfies

\[
0\le R_t(u)\le1
\]

and is globally `t`-Lipschitz in `u`: it is constant on `[0,1]`, while for
`u>1`

\[
|R_t'(u)|
=\frac{t}{u}R_t(u-1)
\le t.
\]

If `q>sqrt(N)`, both

\[
\frac{\log N_q}{\log q}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\frac{\log(N/q)}{\log q}
\]

lie in `[0,1]`, so the corresponding summand in
`epsilon_child` is exactly zero.

For `q<=sqrt(N)`,

\[
0\le
\log(N/q)-\log N_q
<\frac1{N_q}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{N_q}{N}
\left|
R_t\!\left(\frac{\log N_q}{\log q}\right)
-R_t\!\left(\frac{\log(N/q)}{\log q}\right)
\right|
\le
\frac{t}{N\log q}.
\]

Summing trivially over primes `q<=sqrt(N)` gives

\[
\boxed{
|\varepsilon_N^{\rm child}(y;t)|
\ll\frac{t}{\sqrt N}.
}
\]

Hence the power-scale source is the one-prime incidence discrepancy, not the
floor shift in the Dickman argument.

The original floor weights remain inside `alpha_N`; they are not discarded.


absolute-value expansion of the resolvent does not give the square-root

In particular:

- the subcritical contraction controls propagation, not source size;
- `tau=1` is already outside the strict positive contraction regime;
- the smooth-number endpoint `t=1` traverses the full finite resolvent;
- prime-label cancellation in `alpha_N-lambda` remains essential;
- total variation still erases the desired signed cancellation.

No RH proof is claimed.
