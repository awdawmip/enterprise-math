`FOR ALL i != j: E_i PERP_E E_j`.

The classical Euclidean `90 degree` right angle is not the native Enterprise right-angle definition. It remains valid only inside the external/effective Euclidean model.

This is a definition, not a theorem to be proved.

`c=0`.

`L_E^2 = n_AB^2+n_AC^2+n_AD^2+n_BC^2+n_BD^2+n_CD^2`.
