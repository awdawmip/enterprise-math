## 4. Theorem C: fixed-potential best-neighbor dynamics has only two-cycles

Let G=(V,E) be a finite undirected simple loopless graph with nonempty neighbor set N(v) at each vertex v. Let P:V→R be a fixed scalar function. Suppose every v has a unique P-maximizing neighbor and set f(v)=argmax_{w∈N(v)}P(w). Then EVERY directed cycle of f has length 2.

**Proof.** Length 1 is excluded because G has no loops. If v_0,...,v_{k-1} is a directed cycle with k≥3, the predecessor v_{i-1} and the selected successor v_{i+1} of each v_i are distinct allowed neighbors of v_i. Uniqueness therefore implies P(v_{i+1})>P(v_{i-1}) for every cyclic i. Summing over i produces ∑P(v_i)>∑P(v_i), contradiction. Thus only 2-cycles remain. QED.
