## Sources actually opened

1. Harry Buhrman and Ronald de Wolf, *Complexity Measures and Decision Tree Complexity: A Survey* (2002). Author-hosted PDF: <https://homepages.cwi.nl/~rdewolf/publ/qc/dectree.pdf>. Read the introduction and the certificate-complexity definition in section 4.1. The paper develops the familiar model of adaptively querying input bits and relates decision-tree complexity to certificates and other Boolean complexity measures.
2. Ferdinando Cicalese, Eduardo Laber and Aline Medeiros Saettler, *Diagnosis determination: decision trees optimizing simultaneously worst and expected testing cost*, ICML 2014, PMLR 32. Primary proceedings PDF: <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/cicalese14.pdf>. Read the abstract and section 1's definitions. The model assigns costs to tests, follows answer-dependent branches, and evaluates worst and expected total testing cost.

## What these references establish

Adaptive choice of an observed coordinate, partial-information certificates, and optimization of a decision tree with test costs are established frameworks. The present report uses those frameworks; it does not claim to originate them.

The complete allocation task is a search relation with possibly several correct keys. A fixed key's or region's safety is a Boolean predicate, so certificate terminology is applicable there. Boolean-function query-complexity bounds are not imported as allocation bounds without a separate reduction and proof.

The concrete work here is the inherited triadic program's arbitrary-bit vacancy evidence, exact full-key/output-dependent command weight, the high-half family where information and business-word lower bounds are attained simultaneously, the finite future-target recurrence with controlled request replacement, and its actual BRC compilation and scoped executions. Novelty is stated at that model-specific level. This search does not establish exhaustive absence of an equivalent result elsewhere.
