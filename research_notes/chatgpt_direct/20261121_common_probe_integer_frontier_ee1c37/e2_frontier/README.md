# Exactly two embedded classes: cost at least 9,901

This package excludes every integer cost from 9,867 through 9,900 for a twelve-label table with exactly two embedded classes. Here embedded means contained in a legal thirteen-point mother, with no restriction on the class size. Together with the predecessor universal bound E=2 implies cost at least 9,867, it proves E=2 implies cost at least 9,901. It does not raise the unrestricted twelve-label lower bound by itself.

The full size/embedding catalogue is enumerated at each exact price. For E=2, two parent mothers have at least three absent center values on each of the three axes. Each corresponding fiber has at least 21 points outside their union. The ten proper classes covering those points therefore have total positive excess over two per fiber at least one. Thus the sum of proper marginal defects is at least nine. The pinned exact single-price defect upper bounds exclude every price pattern whose capacity is below nine. Any remaining typed pattern is sent to the unchanged native exact-cover program; ignoring embedded/proper flags enlarges its candidate family and hence preserves exclusion soundness.

The frozen interval contains 34 complete cost layers, 472 exact typed price patterns, and 60 native profiles. All native profiles have found=0. New discovery computations used 353,564,187 cover nodes, 615 geometric nodes and 192 tail roots. Reproducing these computations is not counted again as new discovery work. No partial next layer is included.

`python3 -B replay.py --output verification` audits all required source/data bindings, regenerates every necessary price family and verifies complete receipt coverage. Use `--mode full` to additionally recompile and rerun every native profile. Optional `--first` and `--last` limit a replay; an interval starting above 9,867 does not on its own certify the global E=2 bound. Dependencies use sibling C/D artifact directories by default; `--mother`, `--defect`, `--marginal`, `--lower`, and `--previous` override them. A bound prepared input directory may be supplied via `--native-inputs`; otherwise full mode regenerates it with the predecessor lower replay entry point and checks its hashes. The reused bank is complete for every required size/price type; no missing type is silently treated as empty.
