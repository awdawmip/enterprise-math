## 1. General quotient-to-ideal lemma

Let `f,g in Z[u]` with `f` monic, and put

\[
A:=\mathbb Z[u]/(f).
\]

Suppose the class of `g` satisfies

\[
\boxed{g(\bar u)=c\varepsilon}
\tag{SCI-1}
\]

for an integer `c>=1` and a unit `epsilon in A^times`.

Then the principal ideals generated by `g(ubar)` and `c` in `A` are equal:

\[
(g(\bar u))=(c).
\]

Taking preimages under `Z[u] -> A` gives

\[
\boxed{(f,g)=(f,c).}
\tag{SCI-2}

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb Z[u]/(f,g)
\cong
(\mathbb Z/c\mathbb Z)[u]/(\bar f).
}
\tag{SCI-3}

Since `f` is monic of degree `r`, the quotient is a free `Z/cZ` module of rank `r`:

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb Z[u]/(f,g)
\cong
(\mathbb Z/c\mathbb Z)^r
}
\tag{SCI-4}

as an abelian group.

If `c=1`, the ideal is the unit ideal.

\[
\boxed{

This recovers the resultant magnitude `p^(phi(m))` by counting the finite quotient.
