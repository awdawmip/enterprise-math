## Research stance

The program intentionally does not ask how to factor a composite, recover endpoints, or improve search complexity. It studies the forward multiplicative structure generated by seed 6 before any algorithmic interpretation.

Primary objects:

- bridge triangle `T_r={6,2r,3r}`;
- column `C_r=(2r,3r)`;
- bridge rectangle from columns `C_p,C_q`;
- three pairing states of atoms `{2,3,p,q}`;
- gluing/cycles of many local cells;
- degeneration when atoms repeat or supports overlap;
- transfer of accepted Seed-6 invariants to other two-carrier seeds.
