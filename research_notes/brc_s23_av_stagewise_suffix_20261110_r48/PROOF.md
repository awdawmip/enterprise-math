## Theorem R48.1: minimum at an individual stage endpoint

Fix an integer endpoint j and a finite historical partition `I_0,...,I_(m-1)` of the circle into disjoint half-open arcs. Assume the last H sources `[j-H,j)` hit every historical arc. Define the last hit in each arc

`tau_k(j)=max{t in [j-H,j):{t theta} in I_k}`

and its age `r_k(j)=j-tau_k(j)`. Then the **smallest** integer h>=1 for which all m arcs are hit in the suffix `[j-h,j)` is

`h_j = max_(k=0,...,m-1) r_k(j)`.

Proof: for h=h_j, every tau_k lies in the suffix; thus all arcs are hit. For any 1<=h<h_j, choose k with r_k=h_j. Its last hit tau_k=j-h_j is older than the suffix start, and by maximality of tau_k there is no later hit within the original H window. Thus arc k is missing. The proof applies to integer j of any size or sign; it preserves original owner labels. QED.

A backwards streaming algorithm scans t=j-1,j-2,..., remembers the first hit for each previously unseen label and stops immediately when all m labels have been seen. The stopping age is exactly h_j. This is the R37 saturation principle applied to the proven R47 all-offset upper bound H=225, without a new assumption about large source indices.
