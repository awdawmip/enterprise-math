Full graph components: d isolated crossing K2; m-2e-2h ordinary K_(p-1); e bowties of size 2p-3; h hubs K_(2p-2); and v-[(p-1)m-e+2d] isolated vertices.

TOTAL: C_(p,2p-1)=v-(p-2)m-h-d.
