## 1. Primitive-shell pair map

Let `Phi` be the 12 signed primitive A3 roots of squared length `2`. For unordered distinct pairs, the exact dot-product counts are

- dot `-1`: 24 STAR/safe pairs;
- dot `0`: 12 orthogonal pairs;
