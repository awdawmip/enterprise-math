## A. Why the monotonic-healing row is downgraded

Chen and Wang, *Comparison on the coefficients of characteristic quasi-polynomials of integral arrangements*, JCTA 119 (2012), prove coefficient monotonicity under divisibility of moduli.

For a rank-two affine line arrangement in the distinct-slope regime, write

`#complement mod q = q^2 - k q + b(q)`.

Our concurrence defect is

`delta(q)=C(k,2)-b(q)`.

Their divisibility monotonicity

`a|b -> b(b)>=b(a)`

specializes at `a=p^r`, `b=p^(r+1)` to

`delta_(r+1)<=delta_r`.

Therefore the monotone-healing inequality is not a new general theorem.

The explicit determinant-depth statement remains useful:

if every rational triple determinant is nonzero and

`nu_p=max v_p(Delta_ijl)`,

then `delta_a=0` for all `a>nu_p`.

This is retained as a transparent specialization / proof certificate, not a novelty claim.
