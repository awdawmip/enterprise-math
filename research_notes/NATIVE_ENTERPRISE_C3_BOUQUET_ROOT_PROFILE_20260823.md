## 1. Three quadratic lanes

`F_-(m)=6m^2-2m+1`

`F_0(m)=6m^2+1`

## 2. Root sets are disjoint for q>5

Pairwise differences are

`F_0-F_- = 2m`,

`F_+-F_0 = 2m`,

`F_+-F_- = 4m`.

If two lanes had a common root modulo odd `q`, then `m=0 mod q`.  But all three lanes equal `1` at `m=0`.

Therefore for every odd `q>5` the three root sets are pairwise disjoint.

`Delta_outer = (-2)^2 - 4*6*1 = -20`.

`nu_outer(q)=1+Legendre(-20/q)`

`omega(q)=3 + 2*Legendre(-20/q) + Legendre(-24/q)`.

`omega(q) in {0,2,4,6}`.
