## Conditional all-input derivation

Assume a real-linear three-port map S commutes with all port permutations, fixes (1,1,1), preserves the declared amplitude squared norm, and is nonidentity. Permutation symmetry gives S=alpha I+beta J. Fixing the common vector gives alpha+3beta=1. On the zero-sum plane S is multiplication by alpha, so norm preservation forces alpha=+1 or -1. Excluding identity yields S=(2/3)J-I uniquely.

For s=x0+x1+x2, the integer numerator rule D=3S is yi=2s-3xi, with diagonal -1 and off-diagonal 2. Direct expansion proves sum(yi)=3s, sum(yi^2)=9 sum(xi^2), and D^2=9I. All 27 inputs in {-1,0,1}^3 checked these laws and cyclic covariance; finite checks do not replace the symbolic proof.

This is the known three-state Grover coin, not a new quantum gate. Liu et al., arXiv:2006.08992v1, page5 equation(4), supplies the identical matrix. The actual PDF parsed text was read; screenshot and download attempts failed, so no visual figure check is claimed. Lovett et al., arXiv:0910.1024, supplies prior discrete-walk universality, not universality of this particular graph/program.

A signed permutation alone maps a single directly encoded basis state to another single basis state. It cannot supply a splitter in that encoding. Thus outward relabeling does not itself generate this junction; an actual native constitutive bridge is still required.
