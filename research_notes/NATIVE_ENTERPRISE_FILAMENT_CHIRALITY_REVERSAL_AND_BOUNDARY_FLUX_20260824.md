## 4. Exact boundary-flux formula

`A=F_q^2 minus union_{j=1}^{k-1} L_j^+`

be the states surviving the common interior coordinates.

Then

`N_k^+ = |A minus L_0^+|`,

`N_k^- = |A minus L_k^+|`.

`N_k^+ - N_k^-`

`= |A intersect L_k^+| - |A intersect L_0^+|`.

So every odd-k chirality imbalance is a pure boundary flux: the number of interior-surviving points entering through the new right boundary minus those leaving through the old left boundary.
