## Exact question
For distinct windows H2,Hh with integer h>=3, demand signatures {1,2,4,8} for **each** window, i.e. eight labeled terminal signed-X6 paths. Every path has a positive complete key K=(x,y,z), all original six-operation permutations and c/d splits remain admissible, and the cost is the number of distinct nonempty **signed native microstep prefixes**. Minimize over any number of full keys. A construction using at most two distinct keys also proves a bound for the <=5-key variant.

Native words use a=(-1,-1), c=(1,2,-1,-2), d=(2,1,-2,-1), e=(3,1,1,-3), f=(1,-1), g=(2,-2). Their nine-state DFA and signatures are encoded verbatim in VERIFY_EC49FFT.py; positive keys for horizon h have quotas #a=z-1, #e=z, #c+#d=x+y-h-1, #f=h+1-y-2z, #g=h+1-x. All counted paths return the same signed-X6 endpoint (2,0,0,0,0,0).

## Theorem 1 — unbounded explicit upper construction
For all integer h>=3, with r=h-2:
- H2 paths (signature 4,8,2,1 respectively): ce, ec, ed, de. Their common key is K2=(3,1,1) and their nonempty signed-prefix union has 28 edges.
- **Extension family** at Hh: ce g^r, ec g^r, ed g^r, de g^r. Their key is Kh=(3,h-1,1), their original DFA signatures are 4,8,2,1, and their union with H2 has cost **8h+12**. Every one of four distinct terminal paths adds 2r new edges.
- **Late-split rerouting family** at Hh: ce g^r, e g^r c, e g^r d, de g^r. All have Kh and signatures 4,8,2,1. The first and fourth paths add 2r edges each. The shared e g^r trunk reuses exactly one native microstep of the existing H2 path ed (the +2 following e), then adds 2r-1 new edges; the final distinct c/d branches add 8. Hence cost 28+4r+(2r-1)+8=**6h+23**.

Thus **Cmin(H2,Hh) <= min(8h+12, 6h+23)**, attained with exactly two complete keys. The constructions switch at **h=6**: 6h+23 is strictly smaller from h=6 onward.
