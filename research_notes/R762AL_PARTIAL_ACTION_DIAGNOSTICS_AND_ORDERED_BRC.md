**Theorem AL1.** The minimum cardinality of a complete probe set for this exact eight-state system is four. One optimal set is {S,C,T,CT}. It also separates all nine states when ⊥ is included.

## AL2 — Exact ordered positive-BRC language and safe compression

Let (X,A,δ,O) be a finite deterministic partial transition system, with a dedicated absorbing failure state after totalization; X includes it and O has a finite output alphabet. For each initial x define its formal **ordered observer series**
F_x = Σ_{w∈A*} [O(δ_w x)] · w,
where w is a monomial in the **free noncommutative word algebra** over A; [o] is a separate formal basis vector for the output symbol o. Equality is coefficientwise at every finite word and does not assert convergence of an analytic sum.

**Theorem AL2.** F_x=F_y if and only if x and y have equal observations after every permitted labeled future word. For N=|X|, equality of coefficients at word lengths at most N−1 is sufficient to decide equality for all words. Consequently the distinct F_x classes are exactly the coarsest full-future observer-safe state quotient.

**Proof.** Monomials indexed by different event words are linearly independent in the free word module, so coefficient equality is precisely O(δ_w x)=O(δ_w y) for every w. Define ~_k by equality for words of length ≤k; ~_0 is observer equality, and ~_(k+1) is equal current output together with successor equivalence for each a∈A. This is equivalence-relation refinement; each strict step adds at least one class. At most N−1 strict steps occur, and once stable the relation is a transition congruence, hence stable forever. QED.

For an exact positive-BRC weight assignment e↦q_e>0, replace word w=e_1…e_k by the ordered coefficient q_e1…q_ek·w. The same equivalence follows because coefficients remain distinct by word provenance and nonzero weights. The conservation of multiplicity and order matters: projecting to the *commutative* scalar sum may collapse genuinely different future languages.

**Sharp two-letter counterexample.** Construct two deterministic initial states x,y, an observer with outputs 0/1 and two event labels a,b. From x, only the exact word ab leads to output 1; from y, only ba leads to 1; every other word, including every extension of either accepted length-two word, leads to output 0. Intermediate and starting outputs are 0. These automata are realized using one intermediate state and an absorbing zero sink on each side, with a transient accepting state that goes to zero after any further symbol. The labeled word ab distinguishes x and y. Yet for arbitrary positive commuting weights q_a=u and q_b=v, the total weight of output-1 paths is uv at k=2 and zero at all other lengths, **identical** from x and y. Their noncommutative accepted series are uv·ab and uv·ba, distinct. Thus even variable positive commuting event weights retain counts but need not retain order.
