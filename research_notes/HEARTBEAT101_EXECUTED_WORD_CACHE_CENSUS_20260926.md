## Residual-safe cache theorem and executed counterexample

If E^T x=E^T y, increments ECE^T x and ECE^T y agree but Tx-Ty=x-y. Cache an increment and add each caller's complete row; do not cache full output under a projection-only key.

An actual BRC root4 witness u=e0+h,v=e0-h with nonzero tail h in the root active-plane complement has equal full norms, first two components and low-rank projections, yet different complete outputs and equal increments. This rejects norm/readout/projection-only output caching. The actual census has no extra nonempty-word cross-row projection collisions, so no additional projection-cache gain is claimed for this data.
