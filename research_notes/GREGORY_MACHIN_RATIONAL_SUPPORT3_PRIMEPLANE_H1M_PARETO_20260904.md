## 3. Exact three-column circuit law in a plane

For three generated atoms with free vectors

\[
v_1=(x_1,y_1),\quad
v_2=(x_2,y_2),\quad
v_3=(x_3,y_3),
\]

the primitive rank-two kernel is given directly by signed `2x2` minors:

\[
\boxed{
(c_1,c_2,c_3)
\propto
(\det(v_2,v_3),\det(v_3,v_1),\det(v_1,v_2)).
}
\]

A minimal circuit requires all three minors nonzero.  Divide their gcd, then apply the already-proved `C8` target theorem:

\[
c_1\varepsilon_1+c_2\varepsilon_2+c_3\varepsilon_3
\]

must be odd modulo eight; the least-absolute odd scaling then gives the diagonal endpoint coefficients.

This is a number-theoretic specialization of existing typed circuit machinery, not a new global circuit tool.
