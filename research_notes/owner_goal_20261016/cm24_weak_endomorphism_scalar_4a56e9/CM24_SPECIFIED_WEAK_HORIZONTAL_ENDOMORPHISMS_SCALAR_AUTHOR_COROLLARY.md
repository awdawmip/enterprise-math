## 3. A horizontal End matrix is regular at the four finite boundaries

Let T satisfy (1). At a in E define the convergent Laurent matrix

    C_a=H_a^-1 T H_a

on a common boundary annulus. Differentiating with the displayed gauge
identity and (1) gives exactly

    x C_a'=N_a C_a-C_a N_a=ad_(N_a)(C_a).                 (2)

No transpose, coefficient sigma or Frobenius pullback is involved. Since
N_a^2=0, ad_(N_a)^2(U)=-2N_a U N_a and ad_(N_a)^3=0 for every matrix U.
If C_a=sum_(j in Z) C_(a,j)x^j, the genuine Laurent coefficient equation is

    (j Id-ad_(N_a))(C_(a,j))=0.

For every nonzero integer j this operator over K is invertible, with
inverse j^-1 Id+j^-2 ad_(N_a)+j^-3 ad_(N_a)^2. Therefore C_(a,j)=0 for
all j!=0, while ad_(N_a)(C_(a,0))=0. These are exact coefficient
vanishings, not a series obtained by summing inverses with uncontrolled
small denominators. We do not need an estimate uniform in j.

It follows that T=H_a C_(a,0) H_a^-1 extends analytically to the entire
finite disc |x|<1. Thus EVERY entry of T is regular at all four removed
