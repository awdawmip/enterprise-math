## 3. At most one conditional paper action

The only defensible continuation selected by this assessment is a typed reuse bridge for the R1-02 common-reference-section question. It is an integration action, not a claim of new general mathematics.

Before a proof unit is frozen, its input must state:
- the actual frozen upstream carrier and the source/target fiber of every edge;
- whether each edge map is total on its declared fiber and bijective, or only partial/noninvertible;
- the actual port-composition relation, including enabledness and any word restrictions;
- which connectivity is meant and precisely which loops are quantified;
- whether the loop family includes formal inverse edges for algebraic comparison, or consists only of physically/operationally allowed directed words;
- whether a single common fixed point is required, rather than a possibly different fixed point for every loop;
- whether an explicit finite total-action image is available for existing T7, and, if so, an actual proof of that factorization.

A suitable conditional statement to audit is: in a declared connected graph of nonempty fibers with total bijective edge maps, compatible reference sections correspond to common fixed points of the based algebraic path-groupoid holonomy action. This is the standard stabilizer/propagation mechanism in the existing section calculus, expressed on graph transport. Its translation and precise applicability may be useful; the generic mechanism is not new.

This candidate statement deliberately does not equate:
- connected underlying graph with strongly connected directed operation graph;
- formal inverse comparison maps with legal reverse transitions;
- individual fixed points with a common fixed point;
- a finite mod-two quotient with the full integral/torsion carrier;
- a total bijective edge with a partial port or noninvertible homomorphism.

If the intended claim quantifies only genuine directed loops, its actual hypotheses must justify that replacement of the algebraic loop family; weak connectivity alone must not silently supply it. If edge maps are partial or noninvertible, the proposed groupoid reduction is outside that stated interface. In that branch the original simultaneous equations remain the question; no sufficiency theorem or minimal counterexample is supplied by this direction report.

The smallest deliverable, if the upstream input and ownership permit it, is one short paper interface: exact hypotheses; a declared reduction to the existing section mechanism; a scope comparison for actual versus formal loops; and an explicit list of rejected substitutions. No experiment, scan, general solver, new quotient engine, new Task or publication is recommended merely to produce this interface. If the upstream exact result already contains it, reuse that result and stop.
