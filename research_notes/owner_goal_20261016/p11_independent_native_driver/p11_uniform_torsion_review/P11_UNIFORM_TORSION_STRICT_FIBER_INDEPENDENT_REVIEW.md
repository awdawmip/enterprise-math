## 2. The explicit auxiliary map has the right target and degree

The argument is over Q, so its characteristic-zero and rational torsion classification hypotheses are met.

For x!=0 define X=x+a+b/x and Y=y(1-b/x^2). Then X-a=x+b/x, so X^2-2aX+a^2-4b=(x-b/x)^2. Since X=y^2/x^2 on E, multiplication verifies Y^2=X(X^2-2aX+a^2-4b) exactly. The sign of Y fixes one of the two harmless isogeny conventions; its opposite would compose with negation, not change the kernel or torsion argument.

The rational map extends uniquely to the smooth projective curves. At O, X and Y have pole orders two and three and its image is O. At (0,0), x has order two and y has order one as local functions since b!=0, so b/x and -by/x^2 give the same pole orders and its image is also O. These are the two omitted affine points. A rational map from a smooth projective curve to a projective curve has this extension; neither point is discarded.

Generically x satisfies x^2+(a-X)x+b=0, while y is recovered from Y/(1-b/x^2). Thus the degree is at most two.
