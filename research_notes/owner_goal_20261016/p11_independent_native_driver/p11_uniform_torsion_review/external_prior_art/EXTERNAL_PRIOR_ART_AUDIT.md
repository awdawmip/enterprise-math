The strongest related primary is [Halbeisen--Hungerbuehler, Pairing Pythagorean Pairs, arXiv:2101.08163v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08163v1). Its introduction defines Gamma(a,b)=y^2=x(x+a^2)(x+b^2). Proposition 7, printed pages 6--7, gives torsion C2 times C4 for a Pythagorean pair. Theorem 8 and Corollary 12 relate positive rank to the paper's own double-pythapotence problem and obtain infinitely many inequivalent pairs. These are prior family-specific torsion and rank/infinitude precedents, not just general textbook language.

This is a prior-art model comparison, not a new construction or a claim that target E equals E'. It marks the auxiliary Pythagorean torsion result as already published.
