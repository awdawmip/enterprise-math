## 3. The interpolation velocity Gamma — PASS

The barycentric weights of the equally spaced nodes X_i are a common nonzero multiple of w_i. Differentiating the Lagrange basis therefore gives the displayed

J_ii=sum_(k!=i)1/(X_i-X_k).

Evaluation of degree<=n polynomials is an isomorphism onto R^m. Under it J is ordinary polynomial differentiation. Hence J^m=0, im J lies in the values of degree<n polynomials, and those values are exactly E by the finite-difference annihilator. In particular J^n vanishes on E.
