## Why the global derived arrow and all-n compatibility follow

The globalization is substantive and is adequately proved. Since Y is separated and quasi-compact over the affine base, choose a finite affine open cover. Finite intersections are affine. Its ordered alternating Cech complex is finite in Cech degree; together with de Rham degrees0,1, it gives a bounded total complex C_A.

On each intersection, its coordinate algebra is A-flat because Y/A is flat. Sections of the locally free log-differential sheaf are flat over that coordinate algebra and therefore over A. Finite direct sums preserve flatness. Thus C_A is a bounded complex of flat A-modules, even though these modules need not be finite free over A and its cohomology is not proved flat. The de Rham differential is A-linear; it need not be O_Y-linear for this argument.

Each individual sheaf term is quasicoherent and has vanishing higher etale cohomology on the affine intersections, so this Cech total computes its hypercohomology. Affine sections, the cover/intersections and relative log differentials have the indicated base changes.
