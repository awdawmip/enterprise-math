## 2. The integral chart obstruction is exhaustive

Let A_n=(O/p^n)[[t]], with the associated standard log chart N→A_n, 1↦t. At the closed point no positive power of t is a unit. The associated log monoid therefore has a generator image of the form (u,k), u a local unit and k∈N; its ring image is u t^k. This necessary condition applies to the stated log base itself, not to an arbitrary replacement log structure. The local power-series unit criterion is invertibility of the constant coefficient.

For τ(t)=(1+t)^p−1 the constant coefficient is zero and the coefficient of t is p. For n≥2 this p is nonzero and is not a unit, since O is the stated unramified p-adic DVR. If k=0, u t^k is a unit and cannot have that zero constant term. If k≥2 its coefficient of t is zero. If k=1, the constant coefficient of u would have to be p and hence nonunit. These alternatives exhaust N. The same argument holds integrally over O[[t]]. Nonreduced coefficients at finite level do not make the nonzero p into a unit or remove the coefficient comparison.

Modulo p, however, all intermediate binomial coefficients vanish and τ=t^p. There k=p and u=1 are valid. θ=t^p satisfies the chart condition at every precision. Thus congruence of the two ring lifts modulo p does not furnish two endomorphisms of this same integral standard log base at precision p² or higher. This proves exactly the new chart claim. It does not prohibit changing the log structure, parameter or comparison category, all of which would require explicitly new data.
