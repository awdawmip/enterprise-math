## 4. Finite Witt period and fixed constants, with proofs

We use the two distinct coefficient facts

                 sigma^e=Id on K, K^sigma=Qp.               (5)

For the finite period, let q0=p^e and a_bar in F_q0. There is a unique
root t in O of X^q0-X with residue a_bar. Here is a direct lifting
argument. Start with any lift t1; its polynomial value is in pO and
its derivative q0 X^(q0-1)-1 is-1 modulo p. If t_n solves the equation
modulo p^n, choose the residue of d uniquely so that

 F(t_n+p^n d)=F(t_n)+p^n d F'(t_n) modulo p^(n+1)

vanishes. These corrections define a Cauchy sequence; completeness gives
a root. Two roots with the same residue have difference times a unit
as their polynomial difference, so separatedness forces equality.
This proves existence/uniqueness without a numerical iteration.

Sigma^e(t) is another root with the same residue, since its action on

by successively subtracting this unique lift of its residue and dividing

Sigma preserves p^nO and is continuous, so a is sigma^e-fixed. Inverting
p proves the first equality in(5).

For the second equality, the reused fixed-digit proof applies: a
