## 2. Theorem AJ1 — exact future-safe residual versus passive relabeling

Under **passive simultaneous relabeling only**, cycle type is future-safe. On the full set `S_3`, it gives exactly **three** readout classes of sizes 1,3,2. On the even subgroup `C_3=\{e,c,c^{-1}\}`, it gives exactly **two** classes of sizes 1,2.

Under **physical left source updates** by `c,c^{-1}`, the two currently indistinguishable states `c,c^{-1}` are *future distinguishable*: `h(cc)=C` whereas `h(cc^{-1})=E`. Thus the minimal deterministic future-safe state on the reachable `C_3` is the **full three-state residue** `r\in\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z`, requiring two fixed binary bits when all three states are reachable.

If the physically effective odd interchange `t` is also admitted, then the generated group is `S_3` and **every pair of its six states is future distinguishable by the cycle-type observer**. The minimum future-safe deterministic state therefore has **six** values, requiring three fixed binary bits for full support. A distinguishing future word of length at most **two** suffices for every distinct pair.

**Proof.** Conjugation preserves the cycle decomposition, so `h(gsg^{-1})=h(s)` for every `g,s\in S_3`; the current three cycle types are exact stable equivalence classes for language I. For language II, let `s\neq u` be reachable. The allowed left-update word `w=s^{-1}` sends `s` to identity but `u` to `s^{-1}u\neq e`; since the identity is the only state with observer output `E`, the two future outputs differ. All `S_3` elements are generated by `c,c^{-1},t`; each inverse multiplier is one of `e,c,c^{-1},t,ct,c^{-1}t`, hence a word of length at most two. The restriction to `C_3` needs at most one such left generator. Finally a binary fixed-length injective encoding of six states needs `\lceil\log_2 6\rceil=3` bits, and three suffice. QED.

**General form.** For any finite group `G` acting on itself by left multiplication through generators, any current readout with a *singleton identity fiber* is a fully distinguishing future observer whenever every element of `G` is generated by allowed future moves. This is a distinguishability theorem, not a claim that any particular physical generator exists.

## 3. Theorem AJ2 — an exact six-state semidirect residual and sharp reachability

Every element of `S_3` has a unique normal form `c^r t^\epsilon` where `r\in\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z`, `\epsilon\in\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z`. The exact composition law is

```text
(r,epsilon)*(s,delta)=(r+(-1)^epsilon*s mod 3, epsilon XOR delta).
```

Thus source-history memory can be collapsed to this six-state **noncommutative** residual under the declared future alphabet, without retaining the complete operation history. For left actions, the updates are `c:(r,epsilon)\mapsto(r+1,epsilon)`, `c^{-1}:(r,epsilon)\mapsto(r-1,epsilon)`, `t:(r,epsilon)\mapsto(-r,epsilon XOR 1)`. Passive conjugation by `t` instead sends `(r,epsilon)\mapsto(-r,epsilon)`, without generating an odd source parity. Hence a passive frame flip and a physical odd interchange have provably different update semantics.

Starting from the identity, with exactly `k` hidden macros in `\{c,c^{-1}\}`, the support sizes are `1,2,3,3,\dots` for `k=0,1,2,3,\dots`. Adding a genuinely source-moving `t` changes them to the sharp sequence `1,3,5,6,6,\dots`. The six states first all become reachable with exactly **three** macros.

**Proof.** The group identity `tct=c^{-1}` yields the normal form and multiplication law. At length 1 the three generators give three different states. At length 2 the states are `e,c,c^{-1},ct,c^{-1}t`, with `t` absent, giving five. At length 3, `c^3=e`, `t^3=t`, `c^2t=c^{-1}t`, `c^{-2}t=ct`, and suitable products of three copies of `c^{\pm1}` produce `c,c^{-1}`, so the support is all six. Left multiplication by `c` is a bijection, so full support persists for every larger exact length. For the cyclic-only case, the exponent sums of `k` signs have the asserted residues. QED.

