## All-real tagged-parallel tensorization theorem

Conditional on j, a finite-horizon binary-law experiment has complete-history image \`F_j\` and seedless likelihood-ratio (time, absolute LR)-policy image \`L_j\` for the four observer coordinates \`(e_P,e_Q,T_P,T_Q)\`. Assume at every controllable time the absolute LR labels of **different** components are disjoint, including declaration decision nodes. Require no post-tag cross-component transition, shared control coin, or resource constraint. The policies on separate branches can then be chosen independently in either class. The outputs are additive unconditional error probabilities and stopping-time expectations (a fixed common time offset b may be added).

Set \`D_j=diag(p_j,q_j,p_j,q_j)\`. With Minkowski sums of **actual** sets,

\`F_parallel=b+Σ_j D_j F_j\`,  \`L_parallel=b+Σ_j D_j L_j\`.

For signed real \`w∈R^4\` let \`m_X(w)=min_{x∈X}w·x\` and \`δ_j(u)=m_Lj(u)-m_Fj(u)≥0\`. Compactness follows because finite behavioral policy parameter cubes map continuously to finite-dimensional observations. Direct-product minimization proves, **for arbitrary real behavior probabilities**,

**\`δ_parallel(w)=Σ_j δ_j(D_j^T w)≥0\`.**

Hence **\`conv(F_parallel)=conv(L_parallel)\` iff \`conv(F_j)=conv(L_j)\` for every j**. In the forward direction, if local hulls differ, separation gives some u with δ_j(u)>0. Since D_j is invertible, choose global \`w=D_j^{-T}u\`; every other component contributes a nonnegative gap and the total gap is positive, contradiction. Conversely if every local gap vanishes for all signed weights, the global gap vanishes for all weights, hence its compact convex hulls coincide. This uses the elementary equivalence between compact convex-hull equality and equality of all linear support minima, not sampling of directions.

If \`F_j=L_j\` as **actual** images for every j, the two Minkowski-sum representations also imply \`F_parallel=L_parallel\`. Thus if the global hulls coincide but actual sets differ, at least one component **already has equal hulls and unequal actual images**: a tagged-parallel composition of harmless components cannot create the mother-problem counterexample.
