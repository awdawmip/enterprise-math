## 1. Additive closure of the primitive roots

Let

`Phi_A3={(±1,±1,0),(±1,0,±1),(0,±1,±1)}`

be the 12 primitive signed FCC roots.

Every root has even coordinate sum. Therefore the lattice generated by `Phi_A3` is contained in

`D3={k=(x,y,z) in Z3: x+y+z is even}`.

Conversely,

`(1,1,0)`, `(1,-1,0)`, `(1,0,1)`

are A3 roots and generate a rank-3 index-2 integer lattice. Their determinant has absolute value `2`, exactly the index of `D3` in `Z3`. Hence

`boxed: span_Z(Phi_A3)=D3`.

So the additive/convolution closure of the primitive A3 frequency alphabet is the full rank-3 D3/FCC root lattice, not the six-ray set.

## 2. The closure already contains a rescaled full cubic lattice

Exact identities

`2e1=(1,1,0)+(1,-1,0)`,

`2e2=(1,1,0)+(-1,1,0)`,

`2e3=(1,0,1)+(-1,0,1)`

show

`2 Z3 subset D3`.

Thus the D3-supported Fourier subspace contains all fields whose Fourier support lies in `2Z3`, i.e. spatially rescaled copies of arbitrary periodic fields.

Navier–Stokes convolution preserves `2Z3`. Therefore a hypothetical global-regularity proof whose only structural input after the first generations were

`support subset D3`

would in effect have to handle a rescaled copy of general periodic 3D Navier–Stokes dynamics.

This does not prove formal logical equivalence of every metric normalization without specifying the torus scaling, but it is a decisive structural no-go against treating D3 membership itself as a dimensional reduction.

## 4. BRC/observer consequence

The following quotient is unsafe for nonlinear future prediction:

`exact mode history -> additive-lattice membership D3`.

It erases precisely the information that distinguished safe from dangerous first-generation branches:

`(root-line labels, parent pair, radial scales, helicity signs, complex phases, cubic defect output)`.

In particular, the fact that a frequency belongs to D3 does not tell whether it arose from

- a safe equal-shell STAR closure,
- an orthogonal axis-defect pair,
- an acute `211` pair,
- or a later composite interaction.

Positive support membership is therefore not an adequate BRC observer for regularity.
