## Model and claim

Let `x[0],...,x[953]` be the increasing integer coordinates generated by the frozen `VERIFY_R5_COST_SPECTRA.py` source. There are 28 original inter-block gaps of length 3, numbered 0 through 27. For a subset `B` of these indices, shorten exactly those gaps from 3 to 2, retaining **all original 954 event indices**. A size-`K` center subset is feasible if every event has nearest-center distance at most 2; its cost is the sum of those distances. Write `Opt_B(K)` for the **entire family of optimal indexed-center subsets**, not just the optimum value or number of minimizers.

Let `P={15,17}` and `T={0,...,27}\\P`.

**Theorem (two-bit high-budget optimizer family).** For every `B⊆{0,...,27}` and **every integer `293≤K≤954`**,

```
Opt_B(K) = Opt_(B∩P)(K).
```

Thus all `2^28=268,435,456` gap-closure patterns have at most **four** optimizer families at each such budget, completely determined by the two bits 15 and 17. This is a *set-family* theorem, not merely a result about scalar cost or multiplicity.

At `K=292` and `315≤K≤954`, these four families coincide and are independent of every closure bit. At every `K=293,...,314` the four families are distinct: closing either 15 or 17 alone strictly enlarges the original optimal family, while closing both yields a strictly larger family still.

**Cooperative optimizer phenomenon.** For `294≤K≤313`, there are optimal configurations for `{15,17}` which are not feasible under either singleton closure. At `K=293` and `K=314`, no configurations in that exclusively-joint class exist. All four patterns still have **exactly the same optimal cost** at these budgets.

**Reconfiguration corollary.** Any quantity built solely from the indexed optimal-set families at budgets `K` and `K+1` (including minimum deletion distance, minimum symmetric difference, and nestedness existence) factors through the same two bits for every `292≤K≤953`. For `315≤K≤953` it is independent of all 28 closure bits. This does not compute the numerical reconfiguration distances.

## Exact argument

1. **Monotone optimizer inclusion.** The preceding exact gap-shortening lemma proves that closing a gap preserves feasibility and cost of every previously feasible index-center subset: a feasible center already covers an event at distance at most two without using the formerly length-three gap, and shortening that gap cannot improve an already feasible nearest-center distance. The previous whole-spectrum theorem also proves `J_B(K)=J_empty(K)` for all `K≥214`. Hence, whenever `A⊆B` and `K≥214`, `Opt_A(K)⊆Opt_B(K)`.

2. **Eight exact endpoints.** Independently for each `s⊆P`, calculate the complete optimal cost and indexed minimizer count for the two closure patterns `s` and `T∪s`, using the proven chosen-center path recurrence. For **each** of the 662 budgets `293,...,954`, both optimal cost and count agree exactly:
```
(cost_s(K), |Opt_s(K)|) = (cost_(T∪s)(K), |Opt_(T∪s)(K)|).
```
This is 4×662=2,648 exact endpoint budget-pair equalities. The 954-event coordinates are reconstructed from the same immutable seed, not replaced by sampled data. Full program and reproducible machine-generated rows are in `VERIFY_R5_TWO_BIT_OPTIMIZER.py`.

3. **Sandwich proves every intermediate background.** Fix `B` and write `s=B∩P`. Then `s⊆B⊆T∪s`, so the finite families obey
```
Opt_s(K) ⊆ Opt_B(K) ⊆ Opt_(T∪s)(K).
```
Endpoint families have equal **integer cardinality** at every `K≥293`, hence the two inclusions are equalities. No enumeration of all 268 million masks is claimed or required.

4. **Four distinct fibers and joint-only configurations.** The exact singleton optimizer counts exceed the no-closure count for **every** `K=293,...,314`; the both-closed counts exceed each singleton count. A new feasible indexed subset created by closing only gap 15 but not feasible before must have a previously uncovered event adjacent to that gap (the gap endpoints lie at indices 194/195), now covered by an opposite-boundary center across distance two. Closing only gap 17 leaves the gap at 15 length three and cannot repair that event: gap 17 lies at indices 245/246, separated from 15 by a 36-event block. The symmetric statement applies to gap 17. Therefore
```
Opt_{15}(K) ∩ Opt_{17}(K) = Opt_empty(K)
```
for these budgets. The singleton families are incomparable, and the four families form a strict inclusion diamond.

Let `N_0,N_15,N_17,N_2` be their exact cardinalities. The **number of optimizers requiring both closures**, feasible under neither singleton, is precisely
```
N_both_only(K) = N_2(K) - N_15(K) - N_17(K) + N_0(K).
```
Exact path counting gives `N_both_only(K)>0` for every `294≤K≤313`, and zero for `K=293,314`. Since the cost is constant, this is a genuinely hidden interaction in the solution set, not an improvement of the objective value.
