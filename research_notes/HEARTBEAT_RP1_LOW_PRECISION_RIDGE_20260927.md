## 4. Explicit useful-ridge and precision contracts

Let G be the output set for which the fixed order-blind postprocessor returns a verified factor, and p_b=P_b(G). From the definition of total variation,

    p_b >= p_64 - TV(P_b,P_64).

For full normalized terminal states retaining orthogonal history labels, work and residual coordinates, set e_G=||Pi_G(Psi_b-Psi_64)||. Triangle inequality in the successful subspace gives

    p_b >= (sqrt(p_64)-e_G)_+^2.

Thus exact pointwise replication of the high-precision field is a sufficient verification baseline, not necessary for producing useful answers. The pilot computes p and TV on its three small inputs; it does not claim a cheap e_G oracle.

A scalar ridge-margin condition is also sufficient within its declared observer: if max_R f-max_outside_R f=Delta>2eta and ||f-f_tilde||infinity<=eta, every maximizer of f_tilde belongs to R. This is proved by comparing maxima and subtracting eta twice. It does not establish a relation between an arbitrary geometric ridge and an order; R must itself be decoder-useful.

The inherited unit-vector precision certificate can be specialized to the actual64 comparator. Symbolically fix u*=(1,q,0,...)/sqrt(1+q^2), with NO numerical reference run. The BRC constructor floors its first two S=2^b-scaled components and completes the remaining squares to normS. Hence <u_b,u*> >=1-sqrt(2)/S, so ||u_b-u*||<2*2^(-b/2). For the complete certified map R_b=D0(2u_b u_b^T-I), the unit-projector norm identity gives ||R_b-R*||<4*2^(-b/2). Comparing both b and64 to the same symbolic target yields delta_b=4*(2^(-b/2)+2^(-32)). This reuses Stage80, not a new general approximation theorem.

Retain all measurement labels as an isometry and telescope actual ordered operations; no noncommuting roots are swapped. Count level3 twice as root4. For the standard schedule,

    g(t)=2(t-2)+sum_(m=4..t)(t-m+1)=(t-2)(t+1)/2,
    TV(P_b,P_64) <= min(1,g(t)*delta_b).

For t10, g=44: the bound is about0.042969 at b24 and0.002686 at b32, but is vacuous at b8. The positive8-bit evidence is therefore a finite experiment, not a useful uniform8-bit guarantee. This bound compares to the actual64 bank with the SAME target brackets; it does not bound target-bracket error against an ideal QFT. Carrier feasibility and total construction/query costs remain obligations.

Gate precision and output coordinate resolution are distinct. Fractions1/R and1/(R-1) differ by1/(R(R-1)); a coarse output bin can conflate them. Low-b success does not imply fixed small t suffices for arbitrary order. Additional samples/decoders may help only with their own proof and cost accounting.

For iid trial cost/outcome pairs with finite mean cost c_b and success probability p_b>0, the renewal identity gives expected repeat-until-success cost c_b/p_b, plus initial certification. Cache warmup and batch timings require separate treatment. The useful research objective is verified answers per total resource, not smoothness or a high peak alone.
