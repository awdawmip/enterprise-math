## 0. Scope

Continue the first nontrivial `3^e` torsion level, `e=2`, so `m=9` and `zeta=zeta_9` is primitive. The aim is to identify the exact arithmetic meaning of

`Tr_{Q(zeta_9)/Q}(P_(9N)(zeta_9))`.

Assume `N=pq` is a distinct odd semiprime and use the explicit branch

`H2: p ≡ q ≡ 2 (mod3)`.

For RSA-270, `N≡1 (mod9)`.

No factor is obtained.

## 1. Exact trace table for one W-block

For `W_s(u)=sum_(j=0)^(s-2) u^(2-s+2j)`, the Ramanujan trace from `Q(zeta_9)` is

`Tr(zeta_9^t)=6` if `9|t`, `-3` if `3|t` but `9∤t`, and `0` otherwise.

Hence `Tr(W_s(zeta_9))/6`, as a function of `s mod9 = 0,1,...,8`, is exactly

`[-1, 0, 1, 0, 1, -1, 1, -1, 0]`.

## 2. Exact 9-multiplier trace formula for every odd n ≡ 1 (mod3)

Let `n` be odd, `3∤n`, `n≡1 (mod3)`, and let `P_(9n)` be the divisor-profile coefficient.

Define the real residue function `h_9` on `(Z/9Z)^*` by

- `h_9(1)=h_9(2)=1`,
- `h_9(4)=h_9(5)=0`,
- `h_9(7)=h_9(8)=-1`,

and the mod-3 odd character

`chi_3(d)=+1` for `d≡1 (mod3)`, `-1` for `d≡2 (mod3)`.

Then

**Theorem.**

`Tr(P_(9n)(zeta_9))/6 = 2 sum_(d|n) h_9(d) + sum_(d|n) chi_3(d)`.

Proof sketch: every divisor of `9n` is `3^j d` for `j=0,1,2`, `d|n`. The `j=0` and `j=2` trace terms pair to the two reciprocal copies of `h_9(d)`; the middle `j=1` term depends only on `d mod3` and is exactly `chi_3(d)`. The verifier checks the identity for every odd `n<1000` with `n≡1 mod3`.

## 3. h_9 is one Fourier component of a primitive order-6 character

The unit group `(Z/9Z)^*` is cyclic of order 6 generated by 2. Let `psi` be the primitive Dirichlet character modulo 9 defined by

`psi(2)=zeta_6 = exp(pi i/3)`.

Then exactly

`h_9(d) = (2/sqrt(3)) Im( zeta_6 * psi(d) )`.

Consequently the twisted divisor sum

`A_psi(n) := sum_(d|n) psi(d)`

is the natural multiplicative Fourier coefficient behind the level-9 BRC trace.

Its generating series is the elementary Lambert series

`sum_(n>=1) A_psi(n) q^n = sum_(d>=1) psi(d) q^d/(1-q^d)`.

For primitive odd `psi`, `A_psi(n)` is also the standard positive-index coefficient of a weight-1 Eisenstein series with nebentypus `psi`; this identification is contextual only and does not provide a fast coefficient algorithm at a 895-bit index.

## 4. Semiprime H2 + N≡1 mod9 collapses the trace to one character-splitting sum

For a distinct semiprime `N=pq`,

`A_psi(N)=(1+psi(p))(1+psi(q)) = 1+psi(N)+psi(p)+psi(q)`.

Under H2, `sum_(d|N) chi_3(d)=0`.

If additionally `N≡1 mod9`, then `psi(N)=1`, so `psi(q)=psi(p)^(-1)` and `A_psi(N)` is real. The `h_9` Fourier projection therefore gives

**Theorem (level-9 character split).**

`Tr(P_(9N)(zeta_9)) = 12 A_psi(N)`.

Thus N itself gives the **product**

`psi(p) psi(q)=psi(N)=1`,

while the torsion trace gives the corresponding **sum**

`psi(p)+psi(q) = A_psi(N)-2`.

Sum + product determine the unordered character values as the two roots of

`X^2 - (psi(p)+psi(q)) X + psi(N)=0`.

This is the exact arithmetic content of the first torsion lift bit.

For the two H2 RSA-270 residue branches modulo 18:

- `{p,q}≡{5,11}` -> mod9 `{5,2}` -> `A_psi(N)=3` -> `Tr=36`;
- `{p,q}≡{17,17}` -> mod9 `{8,8}` -> `A_psi(N)=0` -> `Tr=0`.

Therefore the prior result

`Tr=36 -> S≡160 (mod216)`, `Tr=0 -> S≡88 (mod216)`

is exactly a character-splitting decision.

## 5. Cubic subcharacter and the conductor-9 real cubic field

Let `rho := psi * chi_3`. Since `chi_3(2)=-1=zeta_6^3`, `rho` is a primitive order-3 character modulo 9. On H2 primes, `chi_3(p)=-1`, hence

`psi(p) = -rho(p)`.

Because `N≡1 mod9`, `rho(p)rho(q)=1`, so the two possibilities are:

1. `rho(p)=rho(q)=1`;
2. `{rho(p),rho(q)}={omega,omega^2}`.

The trace becomes

`Tr(P_(9N)(zeta_9)) = 12 [2-rho(p)-rho(q)]`,

hence it is 0 in case 1 and 36 in case 2.

Now let

`K = Q(zeta_9 + zeta_9^(-1))`,

whose defining polynomial is

`f(x)=x^3-3x+1`

and discriminant is `81`.

For every prime `r!=3`, the splitting type of `f mod r` is controlled by the image of `r` in `(Z/9Z)^*/{±1}`:

- `r≡±1 mod9` -> `f` splits completely into three linear factors;
- all other unit classes -> `f` is irreducible of degree 3.

Under H2 only residues `2,5,8 mod9` occur. Therefore:

- branch `{8,8}`: both prime factors split completely in K;
- branch `{2,5}`: both are inert in K, with inverse nontrivial Artin symbols.

Equivalently, under H2 and `N≡1 mod9`,

`Tr(P_(9N)(zeta_9)) = 0`

iff

`x^3-3x+1 ≡ 0 (mod N)`

has a solution, while trace 36 corresponds to no solution modulo either prime factor.

This is the **cyclic-cubic analogue of a hidden residuosity/splitting problem**: the composite residue `N mod9=1` fixes only the product of the two Artin/Dirichlet symbols and does not reveal whether they are both trivial or a nontrivial inverse pair.

The verifier checks the splitting rule for every prime `<500`, `p!=3`, and checks representative semiprime branches.

`BRC multiplied-profile trace`
