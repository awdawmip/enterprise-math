## Mother direction

Do not treat additive or factor-token edit distance as the primitive geometry of a multiplication/division problem. Model coprime carrier sectors as natively separated under an explicitly declared carrier-preserving operation alphabet, and study the operations that can bridge those sectors. ## Coordination

The four tasks are intentionally parallel. No task may import an unreviewed conclusion from another as theorem truth. They may later be reconciled by Driver review.

The natural merge questions are:

- Does the structural task validate a nontrivial notion of native separation?
- Does prior art absorb the proposed BRC bridge language?
- Does BRC supply existence only, or an observable one-sided collapse?
- Does endpoint recovery survive factor-blind and total-cost audit?

Only if endpoint recovery produces a non-prior-art factor endpoint should a separate competitive factorization benchmark be considered. No such successor is pre-authorized by this index.
