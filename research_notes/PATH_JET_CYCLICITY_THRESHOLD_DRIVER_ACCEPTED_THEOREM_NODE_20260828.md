## Canonical research-theorem statement

Let

`V_n=Sym^n(Z^2)`

and let `L_n` be generated by all oriented two-segment path jets

`J_n(u,v)=det(u,v) Sym^n([u v]) w_n`,

where

`w_n=((X+Y)^(n+1)-X^(n+1))/Y`.

For prime `p`, define

`Lbar_(n,p)=(L_n+pV_n)/pV_n`.

Then

\[
\boxed{
 n<p(p-1)
 \Longrightarrow
 Lbar_{n,p}=\operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathbb F_p^2).
}
\]

At the critical degree

\[
n=p(p-1),
\]

let

\[
C_p=
\{F\in\operatorname{Sym}^{p(p-1)}(\mathbb F_p^2):
F|_{\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)}\text{ is constant}\}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{Lbar_{p(p-1),p}=C_p,}
\]

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{codim}C_p=p,}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
(V_{p(p-1)}/L_{p(p-1)})_{(p)}
\cong(\mathbb Z/p)^p.
}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{FIRST\_BAD}_p=p(p-1).}
\]

`z=x^p-x`

`tau_a=C(k,a+1)+1_(k-a+r>=p)C(k,a)`
