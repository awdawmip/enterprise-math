## Minimal faithful semantic checkpoint

### A. Single-scalar reduction and torsion/value observers

For the semiprime form `N=pq`, let `S=p+q`. The represented line repeatedly reduces the Enterprise reformulations to the same scalar:

`S = sigma(N) - N - 1`, and `A=S/2` is the Fermat midpoint.

For

`W_s(u)=sum_{j=0}^{s-2}u^(2-s+2j)`

and a primitive `m`-th root `w_m`, the source line records the exact value formula

`g_m(r)=W_s(w_m)=sin(2*pi*(r-1)/m)/sin(2*pi/m)`, `r=s mod m`.

The fiber classification reported there is: odd `m` gives an injective residue map; even `m` gives the stated two-point involution fiber. Consequently profile values at torsion points retain only residue information about `S` (up to the even-`m` symmetry), while `u=1`, `u=-1`, moments, and later derivative/energy observers are full-`S` affine or polynomial images and are therefore factoring-equivalent information if they can already be evaluated.

The represented research boundary is: no torsion/value member was found to be both cheaply `N`-only computable and information-complete.
