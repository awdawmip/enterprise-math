## Target, typed observer, and result

Finite strictly-positive statistical P/Q stopping BRC as in the predecessor: two positive-weight hidden sources h=A,B have first likelihood ratio 1; they may stop at t=1 or proceed to common second-stage ratios r_j with source-dependent Q kernels K_h, satisfying sum K_h=1 and sum r_j K_h(j)=1. At t=2 they may stop or use a source-independent, strictly-positive terminal Q kernel M_j(s) with sum M_j=1 and sum s M_j(s)=r_j, then declare at t=3. P is generated by multiplying every Q path mass by its likelihood ratio. FULL sees source and ratio, seedless LR only time and ratio. O4=(e_P,e_Q,T_P,T_Q), with e_P=P(declare Q), e_Q=Q(declare P). Decisions may randomize over all real probabilities. This finite experiment is an auxiliary observer/readout compatible with P000 provenance preservation, not a claim about native X6 dynamics.

**Constructive quiet-refinement theorem.** Fix any finite distinct positive ratio alphabet R containing 1 in its convex hull, and any *different* strictly-positive first kernels K_A,K_B on R, both Q-normalized with mean ratio 1. There exists a shared finite terminal alphabet S and strictly-positive source-independent kernels M_j, arbitrarily close (in total variation) to deterministic s=r_j, such that the FULL and seedless LR *complete O4 convex hulls are strictly separated*. If K_A,K_B additionally have the same lower and upper pure-time stopping roots under the predecessor's time-only criterion, the two *time-only projected hulls remain equal*, even as O4 separates.

**Proof.** Let d=K_A-K_B. As d is a nonzero signed vector on distinct ratios, its call/hinge transform C_d(k)=sum_j d_j(r_j-k)_+ is nonzero for some k>0: its piecewise-linear slope jumps recover every d_j, and an identically zero transform would force d=0. For the classification support L_k(r)=max(-r,-k), define A_h(k)=sum K_h(j)L_k(r_j)-L_k(1)=sum K_h(j)(r_j-k)_+-(1-k)_+. Convexity gives A_h>=0; their difference is C_d(k) !=0. Put w_A,w_B>0, w_A+w_B=1 and c=w_A A_A+w_B A_B>0. The time/classification direction n=(-1,-k,0,-c) charges only Q stopping time. Choose S to contain R and endpoints s_0<min R, s_N>max R. Every r_j lies strictly in the convex hull of S, so construct a strictly-positive Q vector q_j on S of mean r_j. Define M_j=(1-lambda) delta_{r_j}+lambda q_j, 0<lambda<1. Since the convex Jensen gain B_j=sum_s M_j(s)L_k(s)-L_k(r_j)=lambda[sum q_j L_k-L_k(r_j)] tends uniformly to zero, choose lambda so B_j<c for every j. The optimal t=2 action is then stop. At t=1, the source-dependent continue advantage is X_h=A_h-c. The source-blind continuation advantage is sum w_h X_h=0. Backward induction yields
h_FULL(n)-h_LR(n)=sum_h w_h(X_h)_+ = w_A w_B |A_A-A_B| = w_A w_B |C_d(k)| >0.
All terminal Q and P masses are strictly positive. Pure-time supports are determined only by K_h, because terminal declaration payoffs vanish and terminal kernel probabilities sum to one; when the two pure-time roots coincide, the predecessor's root lemma proves equality in every (0,0,g,d) direction. QED.

This establishes a constructive regime and does not assert strict separation for *every* possible late kernel. It is a useful adversarial control for searches seeking a complete O4-hull equality despite hidden source information.
