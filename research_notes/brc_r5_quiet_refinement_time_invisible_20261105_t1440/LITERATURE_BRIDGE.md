# Literature bridge — admissible decision rules and constrained Blackwell comparison

Research observation date: 2026-11-05. Literature source: Brendan van Rooyen, *Beyond Blackwell: Constrained Data Processing Inequalities*, arXiv:2611.00510, initial arXiv record dated 2026-10-31, https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00510. Classical background: David Blackwell, *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments*, 1953, DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177729032.

The 2026 preprint explicitly develops data processing and a constrained randomization theorem for decision-rule constraints stable under post-processing. It also gives a finite equalized-odds example where two experiments not distinguished by one unconstrained Bayes risk are separated by a constrained risk. This is relevant methodology for R5's FULL versus seedless LR policy restrictions, but the paper's theorem is not used as proof of the attached R5 quiet-refinement theorem.
