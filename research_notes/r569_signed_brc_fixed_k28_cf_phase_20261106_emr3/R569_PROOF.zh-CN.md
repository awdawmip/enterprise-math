```
H(t)=binom(r+t,2t) * sum_{q=0}^{min(k,floor((t-1)/2))} binom(t-1,2q)*4^q*C_(k-q)
```
其余 t 取0。与独立的原始带符号式
```
H(t)=binom(r+t,2t)*sum_{q=0}^{r-t} binom(r-t,q)*
   (binom(2t,r-2q)-binom(2t,r-2q-1))
```
