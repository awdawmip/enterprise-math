## 2. Common-order argument and the exact role of a sign flip

The assumptions are a finite Abelian group A generated by six labeled x_i, thirteen pairwise distinct labels 0 and ±x_i, and automorphisms inducing every signed coordinate permutation. The surjection f:Z^6 -> A therefore has finite-index kernel K. Equality on the generators gives f(gv)=phi_g(f(v)); thus K is invariant under the whole declared group. Existence of each specified automorphism does not leave a hidden coherence choice: two maps agreeing on the generating tuple agree on A, so they compose according to the coordinate action.

All x_i-x_j with i!=j have the same exact order r>=2, since ordinary permutations act transitively on ordered distinct pairs. Given v in K, subtract its transposition at i,j. The relation becomes

    (v_i-v_j)(x_i-x_j)=0.

Hence r divides every coordinate difference, K is contained in D_r={v: all v_i congruent mod r}, and

    |A|=r^5[D_r:K].

The index r^5 follows by quotienting the r^6 residue tuples by their r-element diagonal subgroup; it needs neither primality nor a classification of finite Abelian groups. Considering |A|<=128 loses no candidate for a smaller minimum or the equality case. Since 3^5=243>128, that range forces r=2.

Thus all 2x_i have one value t. Signed-label separation gives t!=0, since 2x_i=0 would identify x_i with -x_i. Reversing coordinate i while keeping any distinct j fixed transforms 2x_i=2x_j into -2x_i=2x_j. It follows that -t=t. Therefore t has order exactly two, rather than merely nonzero or some unspecified even order. This is the crucial additional consequence that the old S6-only problem did not supply.

For v in K, all its coordinates have the same parity. If that common parity were odd, invariance under the flip R_i would give

    v-R_i v=2v_i e_i in K,
    0=2v_i x_i=v_i t=t,

a contradiction. Here the last equality uses both oddness and the already proved order two of t. Thus K is contained in (2Z)^6. Each 2e_i is excluded from K, so the inclusion is proper. Finite index gives

    |A|=64[(2Z)^6:K]>=128.

The argument covers every smaller possible cardinality, not just powers of two. Carriers larger than 128 need no restriction on r for the asserted minimum theorem.
