## 1. Question closed in this note

The previous notes reduced the useful higher BRC channel to the faithful twisted divisor coefficient

`A_e(n)=sum_(d|n) chi_e(d)`

at conductor `3^e`, and showed that the repeated ternary Cartier section `C_1^e` of the original BRC product feeds this coefficient through an explicit primitive-character Galois projector.

The next natural hope was that the product-side recursion might close on a **fixed finite state** as ternary digits are read.

This note proves that such a uniform fixed finite-state model cannot compute the exact reduced coefficient sequence, already for each fixed `e>=2`.

The result does **not** rule out a state dimension growing with e or with the target length; in particular it does not rule out polynomial/polylogarithmic-state algorithms.

## 2. Finite-field faithful reduction

Fix `e>=2` and set

`L=phi(3^e)=2*3^(e-1)`.

Choose a rational prime `ell` with

`ell ≡ 1 (mod L)`

and choose `g in F_ell^*` of exact order L.

Because 2 generates `(Z/3^e Z)^*`, define the faithful finite-field Dirichlet character

`bar_chi_e(2^j mod 3^e)=g^j`,

extended by `bar_chi_e(n)=0` when `3|n`.

Define

`bar_A_e(n)=sum_(d|n) bar_chi_e(d) in F_ell`.

It is multiplicative and satisfies exactly

`bar_A_e(3n)=bar_A_e(n)`

for every n, because divisors carrying a factor 3 contribute zero.

## 3. Theorem: bar_A_e is not 3-automatic

**Theorem.** For every `e>=2` and every faithful reduction above, the finite-valued sequence

`(bar_A_e(n))_(n>=1)`

is not 3-automatic.

### Proof

Assume it is 3-automatic.

Map the multiplicative monoid `F_ell` injectively into complex numbers by

- `0 -> 0`;
- choose a generator `h` of `F_ell^*` and send `h^j -> exp(2 pi i j/(ell-1))`.

This recoding preserves products, so the recoded sequence is still multiplicative, complex-valued and 3-automatic. It also satisfies `a(3n)=a(n)`.

By the classification theorem for multiplicative automatic sequences of Konieczny--Lemanczyk--Mullner (Mathematische Zeitschrift, 2021/2022), a 3-automatic multiplicative sequence with `a(3n)=a(n)` must be eventually periodic. Hence `bar_A_e` itself would be eventually periodic; let T be an eventual period.

Put

`M=lcm(T,3^e)`.

Choose a unit residue class `u mod M` such that

`u ≡ 2 (mod 3^e)`.

(For the part of M coprime to 3, take `u≡1`; on the 3-primary part take any lift of 2.) Let

`v=u^(-1) mod M`.

Dirichlet's theorem provides arbitrarily large primes

`p ≡ u (mod M)`,
`q ≡ v (mod M)`,
`r ≡ 1 (mod M)`.

Choose p,q,r beyond the eventual-period threshold and p,q distinct. Then

`pq ≡ r ≡ 1 (mod T)`.

Let `z=bar_chi_e(p)=g`; then `bar_chi_e(q)=z^(-1)` and `bar_chi_e(r)=1`. Therefore

`bar_A_e(r)=2`,

whereas

`bar_A_e(pq)=(1+z)(1+z^(-1))=2+z+z^(-1)`.

These are unequal: `z+z^(-1)=0` would imply `z^2=-1`, hence z has order 4, but z has order

`L=2*3^(e-1)>=6`.

Thus two arbitrarily large indices congruent modulo T have different values, contradicting eventual periodicity. QED.

## 4. Corollary: no fixed finite-dimensional linear Cartier state over F_ell

Allouche--Shallit's k-regular framework identifies a finite-dimensional module generated by the k-kernel with a finite-dimensional linear digit/state representation. Over a finite coefficient ring/field, a k-regular sequence is k-automatic because its value set and state/module reductions are finite.

Therefore:

**Corollary.** The F_ell-linear span of the 3-kernel

`{ n -> bar_A_e(3^j n+r) : j>=0, 0<=r<3^j }`

is infinite-dimensional.

Equivalently, there is no fixed finite-dimensional exact F_ell-linear Cartier-state realization of the reduced twisted coefficient sequence.

Consequently any characteristic-zero finite-dimensional algebraic-integer realization whose matrices/output admit faithful reduction at such an ell would also contradict the theorem after reduction.

Scope boundary: this is a uniform-sequence no-go. It does not forbid an algorithm specialized to one target N, and it does not forbid state dimension increasing with input length or level.

## 5. Finite kernel-rank experiment: maximal growth through tested depth

The attached exact finite-field experiment computes `bar_A_e(n)` by divisor-sum sieve and forms the sampled ternary kernel rows

`K_(j,r)(n)=bar_A_e(3^j n+r)`.

Three faithful reductions were used:

- `e=2`: `ell=7`, `g=3`, order 6;
- `e=3`: `ell=19`, `g=2`, order 18;
- `e=4`: `ell=109`, `g=36`, order 54.

Observed row ranks over the corresponding finite field:

- e=2, depths 0..5: `1,3,9,27,81,243`;
- e=3, depths 0..5: `1,3,9,27,81,243`;
- e=4, depths 0..4: `1,3,9,27,81`.

At every tested depth the `3^j` kernel rows are linearly independent: the sampled rank is the maximum possible.

This is finite evidence, not an exponential-rank theorem. It is however strongly consistent with the exact infinite-dimensional no-go and argues against a hidden tiny linear state.

## 6. Relation to prior art

The proof uses two established automatic-sequence facts rather than claiming a new general automatic-sequence theorem:

1. J.-P. Allouche and J. Shallit, *The ring of k-regular sequences*, Theoretical Computer Science 98 (1992), 163--197: k-regular/linear-state framework; over a finite coefficient ring, finite-valued regular sequences are automatic.
2. J. Konieczny, M. Lemanczyk, C. Mullner, *Multiplicative automatic sequences*, Mathematische Zeitschrift 300 (2022): classification of multiplicative automatic sequences; in particular, a p-automatic multiplicative sequence invariant under multiplication by p is eventually periodic.

S. Yazdani's 2001 nonautomaticity results for classical multiplicative functions such as divisor sums are additional context, but the twisted-character coefficient here is handled by the direct proof above.

## 7. Updated computational frontier

The paired radix-3 picture is now sharper:

- algebraically, repeated `C_1` sections + primitive-character projection recover `A_e` exactly;
- computationally, **constant-state ternary closure is impossible** for the full coefficient sequence;
- empirically, the sampled 3-kernel rank grows at the maximal rate `3^j` through the tested depths.

Therefore do not pursue a fixed finite automaton or fixed-dimensional transfer matrix for the exact coefficient sequence.

The remaining potentially useful regimes are:

1. **target-adaptive sparse state**: exploit that RSA factor lifting only has 2--3 compatible candidates at each level and needs only 2--4 traces, not the full coefficient sequence;
2. **growing but compressed state**: prove or disprove an `poly(e)` / `poly(log N)` representation for only the required trace functionals;
3. **lower bound for adaptive traces**: show whether even the target-adaptive trace state must grow exponentially.

The next preferred route is (1): derive product-side recurrences directly for the previously proved 2--4 local residue-flow traces, bypassing the globally nonautomatic `A_e(n)` sequence.
