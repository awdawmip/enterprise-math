## 2. Faithfulness and the leading row-End equation

For an actual zero operator sum h^(i)A_i, applying it to each constant
basis row first gives A_0=0. If lower coefficients through A_(k-1) have
vanished, apply it to r^k times either basis row. Derivatives above k
vanish, lower coefficients are zero, and the remaining row is k! times
the corresponding row of A_k. Characteristic zero forces A_k=0. Thus
formal coefficients describe actual operators faithfully and uniquely;
the order and leading matrix of every nonzero actual operator are defined.
No point evaluation or fundamental solution was introduced.

Leibniz and derivative stability of R give closure under composition.
Inductively L^N has leading derivative coefficient I in the same R.

For nonzero T of order N, expand L(T(h))-T(L(h)). The h^(N+1) terms
cancel. At order N, contributions involving A_(N-1) cancel when N>0.
Differentiating the highest right coefficient and the right multiplication
by D leaves exactly

    A_N' + A_N D - D A_N.

The orientation is essential: T applied to hD contributes D A_N at the
highest relevant order, while L applied to T contributes A_ND.
Faithfulness permits coefficient comparison. Therefore a commuting T has
A_N'=D A_N-A_ND, exactly the accepted same-ring horizontal-End equation.
It follows that A_N=c_N I with c_N in K. Since the actual leading matrix
is nonzero, c_N is nonzero. Arbitrary leading matrices were not assumed

calculation already gives the complete constant-order answer.
