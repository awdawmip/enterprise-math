## A. Arrangement-side references that materially narrow the claim

1. Hidehiko Kamiya, Akimichi Takemura, Hiroaki Terao,
   **Periodicity of hyperplane arrangements with integral coefficients modulo positive integers**, J. Algebraic Combinatorics 27 (2008), 317--330.

   Relevance: characteristic quasi-polynomial / intersection-lattice periodicity for integral arrangements modulo arbitrary positive integers.

2. Hidehiko Kamiya, Akimichi Takemura, Hiroaki Terao,
   **Periodicity of non-central integral arrangements modulo positive integers**, Ann. Comb. 15 (2011), 449--464.

   Relevance: affine/non-central generalization.

3. Beifang Chen, Suijie Wang,
   **Comparison on the coefficients of characteristic quasi-polynomials of integral arrangements**, J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 119 (2012), 271--281, DOI `10.1016/j.jcta.2011.09.001`.

   Relevance: if positive integers `a|b`, the quasi-polynomial coefficient functions satisfy coefficient monotonicity. In rank two this directly subsumes the monotonicity row `delta_(r+1)<=delta_r` for moduli `p^r|p^(r+1)`.

4. Ye Liu, Tan Nhat Tran, Masahiko Yoshinaga,
   **G-Tutte Polynomials and Abelian Lie Group Arrangements**, IMRN 2021(1), 150--188, DOI `10.1093/imrn/rnz092`.

   Relevance: broad point-counting / arithmetic-Tutte framework that contains characteristic quasi-polynomial constituents.

5. Masamichi Kuroda, Shuhei Tsujie,
   **The Characteristic Quasi-Polynomials of Hyperplane Arrangements over Residually Finite Dedekind Domains**, Enumerative Combinatorics and Applications 4(1) (2024), arXiv:2208.14952.

   Relevance: extends the arithmetic arrangement framework beyond Z. This further weakens any claim that the A-package is a new general theory of arithmetic reduction.
