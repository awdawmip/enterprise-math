## 2. Indices, dimensions, and assumptions

The probability model is external classical probability, not native Cell space.
Let `S_t` be a stationary target process in `{−1,+1}^M`. All expectations below
are exact population expectations. Set

`m=E[S_t]` in `R^M`,
`C_l=E[(S_(t+l)−m)(S_t−m)^T]` in `R^(M×M)`, `l=0,1,2,…`.

Choose q distinct labelled original target coordinates `i_1,…,i_q` and let
`H` be their `q×M` coordinate-selection matrix. Write

`Y_t=H S_t` in `{−1,+1}^q`, `v=H m`,
`Γ_l=E[(Y_(t+l)−v)(Y_t−v)^T]=H C_l H^T` in `R^(q×q)`.

For each route `r in {1,2,3}`, let `E^(r)_t` be a stationary `{−1,+1}^q` error
process. Define the route reports, means, and gains by

`X^(r)_(a,t)=E^(r)_(a,t)Y_(a,t)`, `1≤a≤q`,
`η_(r,a)=E[E^(r)_(a,t)] in (0,1]`,
`D_r=diag(η_(r,1),…,η_(r,q))` in `R^(q×q)`,
`μ_r=E[X^(r)_t]` in `R^q`.

The four sigma-algebras generated by the whole processes `S`, `E^(1)`, `E^(2)`,
and `E^(3)` are mutually independent. Equivalently, one may impose mutual
independence of the three whole error processes and independence of their joint
process from the whole target process. This is stronger than fixed-time route
independence or separate marginal tests. Inside one route, temporal and spatial
dependence are unrestricted.

All routes refer to the same target coordinate and time labels, with no
uncalibrated time shift or disturbance of the target. These are model assumptions;
the formulas do not establish them from observations. Coordinate selection is
essential to the cancellation `Y_(a,t)^2=1`; the three-view gain formula below is
not asserted for arbitrary real linear mixtures `H S_t`.

## 3. Raw products and centered cross-covariances

For `r≠s`, define the raw same-time, same-coordinate scalar

`g_(rs,a)=E[X^(r)_(a,t)X^(s)_(a,t)]`.

Also define the full raw cross-moment matrix and the centered matrix, with row
index a at time `t+l` and column index b at time t:

`R_l^(rs)=E[X^(r)_(t+l)(X^(s)_t)^T]` in `R^(q×q)`,
`G_l^(rs)=E[(X^(r)_(t+l)−μ_r)(X^(s)_t−μ_s)^T]` in `R^(q×q)`.

Thus `g_(rs,a)=[R_0^(rs)]_(a,a)` and
`G_l^(rs)=R_l^(rs)−μ_r μ_s^T`. In general `g_(rs,a)` is not the corresponding
entry of G. At positive lags neither matrix is asserted to be symmetric.

The exact insertion proposed for the original §5 is:

> 定义跨路中心化协方差
> `G_l^(rs)=E[(X^(r)_(t+l)−μ_r)(X^(s)_t−μ_s)^T]`，其中
> `μ_r=E[X^(r)_t]`；上文 `g_(rs,a)` 仍为同位置同刻的原始乘积矩。
> 全部坐标可见时 `G_l^(rs)=D_r C_l D_s`；仅上述 q 个原坐标可见时为
> `G_l^(rs)=D_r Γ_l D_s`。

For example, at a target coordinate of mean `1/2` with noiseless routes, the
same-time raw product is 1 but its centered cross-covariance is `3/4`. This only
refutes substituting the raw moment for G; it is not a counterexample to the
centered identity.

## 4. Factorization and calibration, step by step

First, independence of target and errors gives, for each a,

`[μ_r]_a=E[E^(r)_(a,t)]E[Y_(a,t)]=η_(r,a)v_a`,

so `μ_r=D_r v`.

Second, for any a,b and `r≠s`, whole-process independence applies even if l is
nonzero and the routes each have memory. It gives

`[R_l^(rs)]_(a,b)`
`=E[E^(r)_(a,t+l)E^(s)_(b,t)Y_(a,t+l)Y_(b,t)]`
`=η_(r,a)η_(s,b)E[Y_(a,t+l)Y_(b,t)]`.

Stationarity makes the gains and means independent of t. Therefore

`R_l^(rs)=D_r(Γ_l+v v^T)D_s`.

Third, subtract the mean product computed in the first step:

`G_l^(rs)=D_r(Γ_l+v v^T)D_s−(D_r v)(D_s v)^T`
`=D_r Γ_l D_s`.

Here D_s is real diagonal, so `D_s^T=D_s`. In the fully visible ordering `q=M`
and `H=I_M`, this is precisely `G_l^(rs)=D_r C_l D_s`. The derivation concerns
different routes; no corresponding same-route whitening identity is claimed.

Fourth, same-time multiplication at one coordinate cancels the target sign:

`g_(rs,a)=E[E^(r)_(a,t)E^(s)_(a,t)Y_(a,t)^2]`
`=η_(r,a)η_(s,a)`.

Consequently all three g values are strictly positive and

`η_(1,a)=sqrt(g_(12,a)g_(13,a)/g_(23,a))`,
`η_(2,a)=sqrt(g_(12,a)g_(23,a)/g_(13,a))`,
`η_(3,a)=sqrt(g_(13,a)g_(23,a)/g_(12,a))`.

The positive-bias assumption chooses the positive roots and makes every D_r
invertible. Target means may be zero. The raw g values, not centered diagonal
entries of G, are the inputs to these three-view formulas.

Finally, after gain calibration the exact recovery formulas are

`v=D_r^−1 μ_r`,
`Γ_l=D_r^−1 G_l^(rs) D_s^−1`, `r≠s`.

The centering uses the observed population means μ_r,μ_s and therefore requires
no prior target mean or gain estimate. The different choices of routes must
agree under the assumptions; agreement is a necessary check, not a proof of
whole-process independence.

## 5. Interface to the existing affine-moment and rank results

This paragraph requires the additional inherited C34/C33 target-family relation
`C_l=A^l Σ`, with `Σ=C_0`, and its specified stationary binary update model.
It is not inferred from calibration alone.

When all target coordinates are visible and the inherited strictly positive
refresh model gives `Σ>0`, recovered `C_0,C_1` yield `A=C_1 Σ^−1`. This step
does not require `A` to be invertible. It does not prove membership in the
update model from moments; the original model restrictions remain required.

With only the q selected coordinates visible, the recovered sequence is
`Γ_l=H A^l Σ H^T`. For positive block counts u,w, its block Hankel matrix
`K_(u,w)=[Γ_(i+j)]_(0≤i<u,0≤j<w)` has size `uq×wq` and factorization

`K_(u,w)=[H;HA;…;HA^(u−1)] [ΣH^T,AΣH^T,…,A^(w−1)ΣH^T]`.

The inner dimension is M. Hence any certified exact rank ρ of such a block
satisfies `ρ≤M`, and at least `max(0,ρ−q)` original target coordinates are
unobserved. This is the existing model-conditional lower bound applied to
calibrated target covariances; it neither constructs a legal ρ-coordinate binary
realization nor identifies a full hidden graph. Uncalibrated single-route
instrument modes are not substituted for the recovered Γ sequence.
