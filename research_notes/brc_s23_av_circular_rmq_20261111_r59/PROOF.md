## Exact youngest-only circular range-minimum theorem

Fix irrational `0<theta<1`, `m,H>=1`; history boundary `b_z={z theta}` for original label `z=0,...,m-1`. The labelled circular history intervals are the successive half-open arcs beginning at `b_z`. An age `r in [1,H]` contributes phase `{x-r theta}` at observation phase `x`. The required youngest-age observation is `tau_z(x)=min{r: {x-r theta} belongs to historical interval z}`, with EMPTY for an unoccupied interval.

Construct one exact ascending circular ordering of the `H` labelled points `y_r={-r theta}`, retaining each original age `r`, and one ascending ordering of the `m` labelled historical boundaries. Build a static range-minimum tree over the age payload in the source-point ordering. At query phase `x`, rotate the original historical interval `[b_z,b_next)` by `-x`, obtaining the circular arc `[b_z-x,b_next-x)`. Its source points are precisely those `y_r` whose ages belong to historical interval `z` at phase `x`. The arc occupies one contiguous interval of the sorted source array or two intervals separated by the circular seam; predecessor searches locate the indices and the tree minimum returns exactly `tau_z(x)`, restoring original label `z`. For `m=1` the unique arc is the whole circle and `tau_0=1`.

**Proof.** `{x-r theta}={x+y_r}`. Circular translation is bijective and preserves half-open arc membership. A contiguous circular arc has at most two linear pieces in an ascending cut-array. A range minimum of their original integer ages gives the youngest age and is EMPTY precisely when both pieces contain no source. Original labels are retained in the boundary array. QED.

After a correct source and history order and an exact phase representation have been constructed, preprocessing the segment-tree index takes `O(H+m)` word operations and `O(H+m)` words; each youngest-only checkpoint costs `O(m log(H+1))` word operations and writes `m` outputs. The full observer for `Q` given phases therefore costs `O(H+m+Q m log(H+1))` in addition to constructing the ordered phases. The full source age-phase table remains available for provenance and recovery. This is a word-operation bound; comparisons/modular arithmetic on `b`-bit rational numerators must be charged at their actual integer bit costs.

### Exact order construction and rank-input accounting

R54's Farey successor, with admissible adjacent denominators and a bound `K`, enumerates positive `{s theta}` in decreasing order. Therefore the order for `K=H` directly enumerates `{-r theta}` in increasing order in `H` successor updates; `K=m-1` reversed, with the boundary zero prepended, enumerates historical labels in increasing order. The two Farey bracket constructions and arithmetic costs are separate preprocessing terms. An ordinary exact sort of `H+m` points is an alternative (with its comparison-sort cost). When requests are cut ranks rather than phases, R54 must additionally generate through the maximum requested rank `kmax`, costing `O(kmax)` successor operations after the bracket for `K=m+H-1`.

R58 grouped-difference recovery visits `H` sources per requested checkpoint, giving `O(kmax+Q(H+m))` after initial state. R59 scans only `m` translated arcs and is advantageous as an indexing route when `m log H` is small relative to `H` and enough queries amortize preprocessing. The two approaches can be selected per regime; for this exact observer, an adaptive post-common-setup bound is `O(kmax+H+m+Q min(H+m,m log(H+1)))`. The bound includes the requested `Qm` output fields but excludes explicit full owner-array requests, which still cost at least `QH` output writes.

### Finite exact verification

`CHECK_R59.py` implements the R54 phase order, a static segment-tree RMQ, the phase-midpoint rank convention from R58, an independent direct rational-phase oracle, and grouped-difference recovery. All calculations use integer residues modulo `2q`. Rational witnesses have reduced denominator `q/gcd(p,q)>m+H-1` to prevent finite phase coincidences. Results recorded in `CERTIFICATE_R59.json` and full local research bundle.

* 1,440 reproducible dimension/rational cases (`m=1..12`, `H=1..15`, eight p/1009 per pair), 8,640 unordered/duplicate checkpoint queries: circular RMQ, direct oracle and grouped source-event replay agree exactly.

R58 youngest recovery scans 3,825 age entries per endpoint for these queries; arc/tree operations are not identical units to that scan.
* Large discriminating regime `m=5,H=100003,K=100007,Q=12`: all 60 youngest outputs agree with exact direct classification; 72 circular RMQ subqueries and 854 tree-node reads, versus 1,200,036 age visits for Q full scans. Repeated matched Python measurements: R58 grouped median 159.155 ms, R59 RMQ median 44.081 ms (8 runs, 3.61x on this environment). In frozen S23's small-H/m-heavy regime, R58 median 1.583 ms vs R59 median 1.979 ms (30 runs): R58 is preferable.
