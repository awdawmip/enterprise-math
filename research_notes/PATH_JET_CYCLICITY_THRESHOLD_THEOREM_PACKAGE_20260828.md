## 1. Objects

For `n >= 0`, let

\[
V_n=\operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathbb Z^2).
\]

Let

\[
w_n=\frac{(X+Y)^{n+1}-X^{n+1}}{Y}\in V_n.
\]

For primitive or nonprimitive integer vectors `u,v in Z^2`, define the oriented two-segment path jet

\[
J_n(u,v)=\det(u,v)\,\operatorname{Sym}^n([u\ v])\,w_n.
\]

Let `L_n subset V_n` be the integer lattice generated by all `J_n(u,v)`.

For a prime `p`, write

\[
\overline L_{n,p}:=(L_n+pV_n)/pV_n
\subseteq \operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathbb F_p^2).
\]

Equivalently, modulo `p`, singular matrices contribute zero through the determinant factor, while every `g in GL_2(F_p)` contributes a nonzero scalar multiple of `Sym^n(g)w_n`. Therefore

\[
\overline L_{n,p}
=\langle GL_2(\mathbb F_p)\cdot w_n\rangle.
\]

The problem is therefore an exact modular cyclic-vector problem for `w_n`.

## 2. Main theorem — exact first bad degree

For every prime `p`,

\[
\boxed{
 n<p(p-1)
 \Longrightarrow
 \overline L_{n,p}=\operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathbb F_p^2)
}
\]

whereas at the critical degree

\[
n=p(p-1)
\]

the image is a proper codimension-`p` subspace.

Hence

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{FIRST\_BAD}_p=p(p-1).}
\]

More precisely, at the critical degree define

\[
C_p=
\{F\in\operatorname{Sym}^{p(p-1)}(\mathbb F_p^2):
F\text{ is constant on }\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)\}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\overline L_{p(p-1),p}=C_p,}
\]

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{codim} C_p=p,}
\]

and integrally

\[
\boxed{
(V_{p(p-1)}/L_{p(p-1)})_{(p)}
\cong (\mathbb Z/p)^p.
}
\]

Thus the first modular loss and the exact critical `p`-primary defect agree.

## 3. A2 — subcritical cyclicity via the affine Borel and `z=x^p-x`

Set

\[
m=n+1.
\]

After Weyl exchange of `X,Y`, dehomogenize the seed as

\[
f_n(x)=\frac{(1+x)^m-1}{x}.
\]

Let the affine Borel subgroup

\[
H=\{x\mapsto cx+d:c\in\mathbb F_p^\times,\ d\in\mathbb F_p\}
\]

act on `P_n`, the polynomials of degree at most `n`.

Define

\[
\boxed{z=x^p-x.}
\]

Then for every `c != 0` and `d in F_p`,

\[
z(cx+d)=c\,z(x).
\]

Write

\[
m=kp+r,\qquad 0\le r<p.
\]

Because `z` is monic of degree `p`, every polynomial has a unique expansion in the basis `z^a x^b`, `0<=b<=p-1`. In the entire subcritical range one gets the exact `H`-stable decomposition

\[
P_n=
\bigoplus_{a=0}^{k-1}z^aP_{p-1}
\oplus z^kP_{r-1},
\]

with the last summand omitted when `r=0`.

For `0<=b<=p-1`, the unique maximal `H`-submodule of `P_b` is `P_{b-1}`. Indeed, any polynomial of exact degree `b` yields all lower degrees by repeated translation differences, because `b! != 0 mod p`. Therefore each block `z^aP_b` has one-dimensional `H`-top, with torus character

\[
c\mapsto c^{a+b}.
\]

This reduces cyclicity to exact top-coordinate control.

## 4. Exact full-block top coordinate and the carry law

Write

\[
f_n=\sum_a z^a h_a(x).
\]

For a full block `a<k`, let `tau_a` be the coefficient of `x^{p-1}` in `h_a`.

Since

\[
x f_n+1=(1+x)^m
\]

and

\[
x^p=x+z,
\]

the top term `tau_a z^a x^{p-1}` contributes exactly `tau_a z^{a+1}` at `x`-degree zero after multiplication by `x`. On the other hand,

\[
(1+x)^m=(1+x+z)^k(1+x)^r.
\]

In the subcritical region only zero or one extra `z` can be created in this reduction. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\tau_a=
\binom{k}{a+1}
+
\mathbf 1_{k-a+r\ge p}\binom{k}{a}.
}
\]

There are two cases.

### No carry

If `k-a+r<p`, then

\[
\tau_a=\binom{k}{a+1}\ne0.
\]

### One carry

If `k-a+r>=p`, then Pascal gives

\[
\tau_a=
\binom{k+1}{a+1}\ne0,
\]

because subcritical carry forces `k<=p-2`.

At the final subcritical edge `m=p(p-1)`, i.e. `k=p-1,r=0`, one still has

\[
\tau_a=\binom{p-1}{a+1}\ne0
\]

for every `0<=a<=p-2`.

The partial block, when `r>0`, has top coordinate exactly `1`, since `f_n` is monic of degree `n` and `z^k x^{r-1}` is the unique monomial of that ordinary degree in the decomposition.

Hence every block top receives nonzero seed mass.

## 5. The unique top-character collision

The full blocks have pairwise distinct top characters

\[
\chi^0,\chi^1,\ldots,\chi^{k-1}.
\]

If `r>0`, the partial block has character

\[
\chi^{k+r-1}\quad\text{mod }p-1.
\]

If

\[
k+r\le p-1,
\]

there is no collision. The single seed already generates the complete semisimple top, hence all of `P_n`.

If

\[
k+r\ge p,
\]

there is exactly one collision, at

\[
\boxed{a_0=k+r-p.}
\]

It occurs between the full block `z^{a_0}P_{p-1}` and the partial block `z^kP_{r-1}`.

This exactly explains finite examples in which the Borel orbit alone loses rank although the full `GL_2` orbit remains cyclic.

## 6. Weyl repair of the unique collision

The Weyl mate dehomogenizes to

\[
\widetilde f_n(x)
=x^n f_n(1/x)
=(x+1)^m-x^m.
\]

Using `z(x+1)=z(x)` and `x^m=(x+z)^k x^r`,

\[
\widetilde f_n
=
\sum_{a=0}^k
\binom{k}{a}z^a
\left((x+1)^{k-a+r}-x^{k-a+r}\right).
\]

After reducing powers with `x^p=x+z`, every full-block top coordinate of `\widetilde f_n` is zero. Its partial top coordinate is the leading coefficient of `(x+1)^m-x^m`, namely

\[
r=m\bmod p.
\]

On the unique duplicated top, the two seeds therefore map as

\[
f_n\mapsto(\tau_{a_0},1),
\qquad
\widetilde f_n\mapsto(0,r).
\]

Thus

\[
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
\tau_{a_0}&1\\
0&r
\end{pmatrix}
=r\tau_{a_0}\ne0.
\]

The duplicated simple top is fully generated. All other top summands were already reached. Consequently

\[
Hf_n+H\widetilde f_n=P_n.
\]

Both seeds lie in the `GL_2(F_p)` orbit of the original homogeneous seed, so

\[
\boxed{
\langle GL_2(\mathbb F_p)\cdot w_n\rangle
=\operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathbb F_p^2)
}
\]

for every `n<p(p-1)`.

For `p=2`, the subcritical cases `n=0,1` are immediate and the critical case `n=2` is handled separately in A1.

## 7. A1 — exact critical image

Now set

\[
n=p(p-1).
\]

Define

\[
D_1=X^pY-XY^p,
\]

and

\[
D_2=
\frac{X^{p^2}Y-XY^{p^2}}{D_1}.
\]

Then `deg D_1=p+1` and `deg D_2=p(p-1)=n`. The form `D_1` vanishes simply at every point of `P^1(F_p)`, while `D_2` evaluates to `1` at every such point.

With

\[
r_0=n-p-1=p^2-2p-1,
\]

one gets

\[
\boxed{
C_p=\mathbb F_pD_2\oplus D_1\operatorname{Sym}^{r_0}(\mathbb F_p^2).
}
\]

At the critical degree `w_n` itself evaluates to `1` on all `P^1(F_p)`, hence

\[
w_n=D_2+D_1q
\]

for a degree-`r_0` form `q`.

In affine coordinate `t`,

\[
D_2(1,t)-1=(t^p-t)^{p-1}.
\]

The associated Hermite expansion shows that all finite Hermite jet data of `q` is concentrated at one point, `t=-1`, with local jet vector

\[
(1,1,\ldots,1)
\]

through order `p-3` after the stated normalization. The affine stabilizer

\[
t\mapsto -1+\lambda(t+1)
\]

moves this to

\[
(\lambda,\lambda^2,\ldots,\lambda^{p-2}).
\]

Vandermonde over `\lambda in F_p^*` spans the entire local `(p-2)`-dimensional jet block, and translations move the block to every finite point.

The Hermite map

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^{r_0}
\longrightarrow
\bigoplus_{a\in\mathbb F_p}\mathbb F_p^{p-2}
\]

is an isomorphism: its kernel would be divisible by `(t^p-t)^{p-2}` of degree

\[
p(p-2)=r_0+1,
\]

impossible for a nonzero degree-`r_0` polynomial.

Hence orbit differences generate all of

\[
D_1\operatorname{Sym}^{r_0},
\]

and subtracting the residual from `w_n` gives `D_2`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\overline L_{n,p}=C_p.}
\]

Since functions on `P^1(F_p)` have dimension `p+1` and the constant-value condition imposes `p` independent conditions,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{codim}C_p=p.}
\]

For `p=2,n=2`, one checks directly that `w_2=X^2+XY+Y^2` spans the one-dimensional space of quadratic forms constant on the three `F_2`-projective points.

## 8. Exact critical `p`-primary cokernel

At the same critical degree define integer path generators

\[
w_s=J_n(e_1,se_1+e_2).
\]

Coefficientwise `w_p-w_0` is divisible by `p`. Let

\[
\delta=\frac{w_p-w_0}{p}\pmod p.
\]

Its projective evaluation is a delta function at the finite point `t=-1`, zero at every other projective `F_p` point including infinity. Integer lifts of `GL_2(F_p)` move this delta to every projective point.

Thus the saturation classes span

\[
\operatorname{Fun}(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p),\mathbb F_p)/\text{constants},
\]

which has dimension `p` and is exactly `V_n/C_p` modulo `p`.

Let

\[
L_n^{[1]}=\{x\in V_n:px\in L_n\}.
\]

The reductions of `L_n^{[1]}` fill `V_n/pV_n`, so

\[
pV_n\subset L_n+p^2V_n.
\]

For the finite `p`-primary quotient `A=(V_n/L_n)_{(p)}`, this gives

\[
pA=p^2A.
\]

Hence `pA=0`, and since its mod-`p` dimension is `p`,

\[
\boxed{A\cong(\mathbb Z/p)^p.}
\]

## 9. Why the first failure occurs exactly at `p(p-1)`

At the critical degree

\[
n=p(p-1),
\qquad
m=n+1=(p-1)p+1,
\]

so

\[
k=p-1,\qquad r=1.
\]

The first full-block top coordinate becomes

\[
\tau_0
=
\binom{p-1}{1}+
\binom{p-1}{0}
=-1+1
=0.
\]

The Weyl mate still has zero full-block top coordinate. Thus the duplicated top can no longer be repaired by the two-seed mechanism.

This is the exact structural transition:

\[
\boxed{
\text{first unrepairable carry-induced top collision occurs at }
 n=p(p-1).
}
\]

A1 identifies the resulting image and exact defect; A2 proves no earlier degree can fail.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\texttt{PATH-JET CYCLICITY THRESHOLD CLOSED.}
}
\]
