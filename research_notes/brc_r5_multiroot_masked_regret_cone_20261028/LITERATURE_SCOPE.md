# R5 — Literature boundary for behavioral strategies and information compression

Date: 2026-10-28. Source-checked bibliographic context, not an exhaustive novelty search, independent review or theorem admission. Relevant author proof: `PROOF.md` in this folder.

The finite-tree mathematical result here must not be conflated with the classical perfect-recall mixed/behavior-strategy equivalence. Harold W. Kuhn's 1953 chapter *Extensive Games and the Problem of Information*, in *Contributions to the Theory of Games II* (Annals of Mathematics Studies 28, pp. 193–216), is the historical perfect-recall reference. Bibliographic source: https://bibbase.org/network/publication/kuhn-extensivegamesandtheproblemofinformation-1953 ; book source: https://books.google.hu/books?hl=en&id=ulrGpTmQ8wQC . This research's full-history strategies retain the ordered history; seedless LR policies deliberately *identify* distinct hidden histories with equal current likelihood ratio, so perfect recall for those hidden distinctions cannot be assumed. For a linear objective in the specified finite model, the local-product action map is multi-affine and has an optimal deterministic vertex even though the set of all observation vectors under seedless local randomization need not be convex. Kuhn's theorem is not used to assert that compressed behavioral image equals the convex hull of its deterministic corners.

David Blackwell (1953), *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments*, *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 24(2), 265–272, DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177729032, provides a classical comparison-of-experiments viewpoint: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1362544419209410176 . A later general-state-space paper is M. Ali Khan, Haomiao Yu and Zhixiang Zhang (published February 2025), *A simple proof of Blackwell's theorem on the comparison of experiments for a general state space*, *Economics Letters* 247, article 112146, DOI 10.1016/j.econlet.2024.112146: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econlet.2024.112146 . Its abstract concerns Blackwell sufficiency versus the Bohnenblust–Shapley–Sherman more-informativeness criterion for general state spaces. That is a **related setting**, not a source proof of this file's masked-regret identity, of restricted four-observable convex-hull equality, or of failure of a seedless sequential policy to realize a given four-vector.

Precisely supported claim: R5's derivation is an author-level exact lemma for a declared finite shared-gate information structure. These citations delimit adjacent known concepts; they do not certify worldwide novelty, external adoption, or P000 physical applicability. The unresolved mother question remains **equality of all signed support values** together with **inequality of real seedless observation images** for an actual strictly positive model with unrestricted stops and declarations.
