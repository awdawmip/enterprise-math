## 1. Setup

Let

`Lambda=(-Delta)^(1/2)`

and let `P_+`, `P_-` be the standard divergence-free helical projectors. Let `P` be the Leray projector.

Take a smooth divergence-free **pure positive-helicity** state

`v=P_+ v`,

so

`curl v = Lambda v`.

At a state with no negative-helicity component, the nonlinear forcing into the negative sector is

`F_-(v) := -P_- P[(v dot grad)v]`.

Using

`(v dot grad)v = grad(|v|^2/2) - v cross curl v`

and Leray annihilation of gradients gives the exact identity

`boxed: F_-(v)=P_- P[v cross Lambda v]`.

(The negative-helicity case is identical after exchanging signs.)

## 2. New exact shell-defect identity

For every real scalar `kappa`,

`v cross (kappa v)=0`.

`boxed: F_-(v)=P_- P[v cross (Lambda-kappa)v]`.

`Hdot^(1/2) -> L^3`,

`L^(3/2) -> Hdot^(-1/2)`.

`boxed: ||F_-(v)||_(Hdot^-1/2) <= C ||v||_(Hdot^1/2) ||(Lambda-kappa)v||_(Hdot^1/2)}`.
