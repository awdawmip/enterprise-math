## Confirmed defects and limits

### R1. Unsafe whole-fiber exclusion by prefix wheel

Round14 pipeline uses M=2^12*3^8*5^4=16796160000 and applies the 30030 coprimality wheel to a prefix r, then treats rejection as exclusion of all p=r+M*x. This is invalid for wheel primes not dividing M.

Explicit witness (both factors independently primality-checked):

- p=33592320007=7+2*M;
- q=33592320191=191+2*M;
- N=1128443969633679361337=p*q;
- gcd(7,30030)=7 but gcd(p,30030)=1.

The wheel rejects the real factor's prefix, even though x=2 lies inside its own tested window. This refutes the generic pruning rule, not a claim to know RSA-270's actual factors. This is a local rule witness, not an end-to-end execution of the full RSA script.

BRC diagnosis: branch population is the integer lifts of r; the wheel observer is not constant on this fiber. Correctly, for a prime ell coprime to M, forbid only x == -r*M^(-1) (mod ell), subject to the factor-equals-ell boundary. If ell divides M, the residue observer is constant. Preserve quotient congruence information instead of deleting a whole fiber. This is COMPOSE_APPLIED at the observer/fiber law, not an executed EM library call.

### R2. Finite search failure is not global infeasibility

Failure in that finite window or budget does not prove absence of larger admissible x. Distinguish PROVED_EMPTY(domain) from NOT_FOUND_WITHIN_BUDGET/UNKNOWN. The hardcoded multiplier 12960000 does not repair an invalid exclusion premise.

### R3. Two named filters provide no discrimination in this pipeline

## Collision law and computational boundary

For multiplicative order r and Q=q*r+s, 0<=s<r:
K_Q=(r-s)*q^2+s*(q+1)^2=(Q^2+s*(r-s))/r.
For Q>=n^2, nearest-integer recovery r=(2*Q^2+K_Q)//(2*K_Q) is valid. The implementation keeps a dictionary over the generated subgroup, with support eventually r; it performs O(m*r) dictionary updates in the saturated regime, before accounting for integer bit costs. This is a finite exact baseline, not evidence of efficient classical Shor simulation. The next substantive opportunity is to compute the required observer without enumerating all subgroup states, while accounting for retained information and cost.
