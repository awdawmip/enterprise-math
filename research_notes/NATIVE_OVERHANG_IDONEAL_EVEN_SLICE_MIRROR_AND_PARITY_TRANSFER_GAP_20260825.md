## 1. Overhang family

The extra-gate overhang problem uses

`A_(r,u)=(2u+1)^2+16r`,

so it is the fixed-`y=4` odd-x slice of the binary quadratic form

`x^2+r*y^2`.

The exact finite compatibility evidence gives powers of two

`r=1,2,4,8,16`

as compatible and shows explicit odd square-class obstructions beginning at `r=32`.

The threshold exactly matches the power-of-two part of the classical Euler idoneal/convenient-number list.

This note records the exact mathematical overlap and the still-missing implication.

## 2. Classical non-idoneal criterion

A positive integer `r` is idoneal iff it cannot be written

`r=a*b+a*c+b*c`

with

`0<a<b<c`.

Thus any non-idoneal r has such a triple.

## 3. Exact even-slice square collapse

Assume

`r=ab+ac+bc`.

Then

`a^2+r=(a+b)(a+c)`,

and cyclically.

Therefore at the even x-values

`x_a=4a`, `x_b=4b`, `x_c=4c`,

we have

`x_a^2+16r=16(a+b)(a+c)`,

`x_b^2+16r=16(a+b)(b+c)`,

`x_c^2+16r=16(a+c)(b+c)`.

Multiplying:

`product_(x in {4a,4b,4c}) (x^2+16r)`

`=16^3 (a+b)^2(a+c)^2(b+c)^2`

`=[64(a+b)(a+c)(b+c)]^2`.

Freeze:

`NON-IDONEALITY -> EXACT THREE-TERM SQUARE-CLASS COLLAPSE ON THE EVEN y=4 SLICE`.

This is an identity, not a numerical observation.

## 4. Every power 2^t with t>=5 is explicitly non-idoneal

The non-idoneal decomposition can be given uniformly by exponent class.

### t odd

Since `2^t=2 mod3`, put

`a=1`, `b=2`, `c=(2^t-2)/3`.

Then

`ab+(a+b)c=2+3c=2^t`.

For `t>=5`, `1<2<c`.

### t=2 mod4

Since `2^t=4 mod5`, put

`a=1`, `b=4`, `c=(2^t-4)/5`.

Then

`ab+(a+b)c=4+5c=2^t`.

For `t>=6`, `1<4<c`.

### t=0 mod4

Since `2^t=1 mod5`, put

`a=2`, `b=3`, `c=(2^t-6)/5`.

Then

`ab+(a+b)c=6+5c=2^t`.

For `t>=8`, `2<3<c`.

Therefore every power of two

`2^t`, `t>=5`,

is non-idoneal by an explicit elementary witness.

This agrees with the classical list, whose power-of-two idoneal members stop at16.

## 5. Why this does not yet prove the odd-overhang conjecture

The overhang square-class criterion samples only

`x=1,3,5,...,2r-1`.

The idoneal collapse above samples

`x=4a,4b,4c`,

which are even.

A square-class relation on the even slice does not automatically imply one on the odd slice.

Direct checks show that the actual odd relations at r=32,64,128 are not obtained by a uniform `x -> x+/-1` translation of the idoneal triple.

Therefore the statement

`POWER-OF-TWO OVERHANG COMPATIBLE IFF POWER-OF-TWO IDONEAL`

is currently only a conjectural mirror, not a theorem.

## 6. Precise parity-transfer problem

The missing lemma can be isolated as follows.

Let

`r=2^t`, `t>=5`,

and choose an explicit idoneal-obstruction triple `a,b,c` from Section4.

The even-slice identity gives

`[(4a)^2+16r] + [(4b)^2+16r] + [(4c)^2+16r] = 0`

in the F2 square-class group notation.

Required parity-transfer target:

prove that the odd-slice square classes

`[(2u+1)^2+16r]`, `u=0,...,r-1`,

contain an odd-cardinality zero-sum relation.

Equivalently, prove that no homomorphism from their generated F2 square-class space can take value1 on every odd-slice generator.

This is exactly the remaining overhang compatibility conjecture for powers of two.

## 7. Rational norm action does not immediately solve parity transfer

The quadratic form has square-norm multipliers

`(r*k^2-1)^2 + r*(2k)^2 = (r*k^2+1)^2`.

Composition with such a multiplier gives rational Mobius transformations preserving the square class of a represented value.

However, these transformations do not uniformly send all three even idoneal points to admissible odd integral points with fixed y=4. Finite controls show that componentwise transfer fails in small cases.

So the parity-transfer lemma, if true, must be collective rather than a trivial pointwise norm action.
