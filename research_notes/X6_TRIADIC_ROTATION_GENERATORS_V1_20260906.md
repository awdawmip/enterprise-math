## 2. Intrinsic oriented triad generator

`S=(i,j,k)`.

Let `rho_S` be the positive-axis 3-cycle

`e_i -> e_j -> e_k -> e_i`

and fix the complementary three axes.

Let `J_S` flip the signs of exactly the selected three axes and fix the complement.

Define

`Q_S := J_S rho_S`.

Equivalently on the selected axes,

`Q_S(e_i)=-e_j`,

`Q_S(e_j)=-e_k`,

`Q_S(e_k)=-e_i`.

`J_S` and `rho_S` commute. Hence

`Q_S^2=rho_S^2`,

`Q_S^3=J_S`,

`Q_S^6=1`.

Thus `Q_S` is an intrinsic signed C6 frame generator; every second phase recovers a positive-axis C3 cycle, and the third power is the selected-triad half-turn/sign reversal.

For the opposite chirality use `Q_S^{-1}`.
