    Phi_g(w) = (g s_1,...,g s_m).

Then

    d(Phi_g(w)) = g d(w),
    Phi_g(w concat v) = Phi_g(w) concat Phi_g(v),
    Phi_g Phi_h = Phi_(gh).

Every `Phi_g` is a length-preserving bijection, preserves step ordering and individual branch provenance when branch tags are carried with words, and preserves immediate reversal because `g s = -g t` iff `s=-t`. It therefore gives equivariant bijections of fixed-endpoint primitive path fibers and of their no-immediate-backtracking subfibers. This also proves

    N_min(gd)=N_min(d),  B_min(gd)=B_min(d)

for the current exact shortest-path formula `N_min(d)=sum |d_i|`, `B_min(d)=N_min(d)!/prod |d_i|!`.

    N_(m+n)(d)=sum_(u in Z^6) N_m(u) N_n(d-u).

For **no-immediate-reversal** counts, one must carry boundary letters. If `C_m(d;s,t)` counts such words with first letter `s` and last `t`, then for `m,n>=1`,

Both identities follow by the unique split after `m` letters and, for the latter, the necessary and sufficient reversal check at that split.
