### Complete rational hyperplane-intersection certificate

Let the four exact integer linear forms on one slot be `n-m, n+m, n-2m, n+2m`. Apply them to each of the four slots `input1, input2, output1, output2`, obtaining **16** rational linear forms `L_0,...,L_15` on `Z^4` (outputs composed with the exact matrix A). For any integer vector z, let `Z(z)={i:L_i(z)=0}`.

Since `qJ(v)=1-(number of zero forms among its four forms)/4`,
```
D(z)=[|Z(z) cap {0,...,7}|-|Z(z) cap {8,...,15}|]/4.
```
For any z, the rational annihilator subspace of `Z(z)` is the intersection of the hyperplanes `L_i=0`. Its defining row span has rank at most four, and is therefore generated by **at most four rows** among these 16. Conversely, given any such row span, one can choose a rational vector in its common nullspace avoiding every other hyperplane not vanishing identically on that nullspace (finite union of proper hyperplanes cannot cover a nonzero rational vector space); scale to integers. The zero vector realizes the rank-four nullspace case. Therefore the possible `Z(z)` are exactly the *closures* of independent subsets of at most four of the 16 row forms.

An exact rational rank enumeration over `sum_{r=0}^{4}binom(16,r)=2517` candidate subsets, discarding dependent generators and coalescing equal closures, gives **102** distinct realizable zero-sets. The complete defect distribution over these 102 closures is:
```

closures   4     6    16   50    16      6      4
```
No other zero patterns are possible, proving the global upper bound `|D|<=3/4`. Together with the explicit witness, the theorem follows. The calculation uses exact integer/rational matrix rank only; there is no finite-input-box-to-universal extrapolation.

**Minimal reproducible exact enumeration (SymPy):**

from itertools import combinations
A=s.Matrix([[2,-2,-1,2],[1,-1,-1,2],[-1,2,2,-2],[-1,2,1,-1]])
L=s.Matrix([[1,-1],[1,1],[1,-2],[1,2]])
P=s.Matrix.hstack(s.eye(2),s.zeros(2))
Q=s.Matrix.hstack(s.zeros(2),s.eye(2))
rows=[tuple(r) for B in (L*P,L*Q,L*P*A,L*Q*A) for r in B.tolist()]
counts={}

assert {j:sum(v==j for v in counts.values())
