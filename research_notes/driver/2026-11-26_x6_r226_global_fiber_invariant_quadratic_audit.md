## New extension: all globally fiber-invariant homogeneous degree <=2 strict predicates
Normalize t+B+D=1, an open triangle, so homogeneous linear/quadratic restrict to affine polynomials of degree <=2. Fix the 47 distinct support lines; their full ternary fibers are relatively open convex arrangement faces. Require each query's strict Boolean value to be constant on EVERY full ternary fiber of the WHOLE positive domain, not merely its reached state. A leaf has the same output contract as R226.
Claim: Up to constants and branch exchange, every such query is a library single-factor/product/square query or the complement of a singleton zero-dimensional fiber.
Proof: If a nonzero polynomial takes both signs in the open triangle, some adjacent full-dimensional arrangement chambers have opposite sign, because their adjacency graph is connected and nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on an open chamber. The shared open edge has polynomial identically zero by continuity. Its supporting affine form divides the polynomial. The quotient is affine of degree <=1; if its zero line crosses the open triangle and is distinct from the first line, it must likewise be a support line (otherwise at a generic interior point it changes the strict predicate inside a chamber). A nonconstant affine factor with no interior zero has constant strict sign; coincident factors give a square. Degree one is the same argument without the quotient. If the polynomial never takes positive values, the strict predicate is constant false. If it is nonnegative and has an interior zero, at that zero the gradient vanishes and the quadratic Hessian is positive semidefinite, so the whole zero locus in the affine chart is an affine subspace (point, line, plane). A proper zero line must be a support line, else strict positivity varies on a full-dimensional chamber. An isolated zero must itself constitute a zero-dimensional fiber, else its fiber has other points with positive value. If no interior zero, predicate constant true. This proves the classification including all equality faces. Conversely a vertex singleton exclusion is realizable by sum of squares of two independent support forms vanishing at that vertex, and every library predicate is invariant. Constant nodes may be removed. No assumption of query invariance restricted merely to reached cells is made.
