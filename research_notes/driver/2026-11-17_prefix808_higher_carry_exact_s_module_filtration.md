Let K be any field, h >= 2, e|d|h, n=d/e, H=h/d, S=K[U_0,U_1,U_2,U_3], A=U_0U_3 and B=U_1U_2, I=(A,B) in S. Put

D_h=S[z_0,z_1]/(z_0^h-A,z_1^h-B).

Let C_d be the total-z-degree 0 (mod d) subring of D_h, let M_k be its total-degree ek (mod d) character summand, 0<=k<n, and let J_d=(z_0^a z_1^{d-a}:0<=a<=d) in C_d. The previously proved t-factor image law is J_d^c M_k for c=floor((sum j_i)/n), k=(sum j_i) mod n. This note studies **all** positive integers c, whether or not a particular t-tuple has been chosen.

## Theorem: complete higher-carry cokernel as an S-module

For c>=1 let E_{k,c}=M_k/J_d^c M_k. For each q>=0 put

r_q=ek+qd,
N_q=# {(a,b):0<=a,b<h, a+b=r_q}
    =max(0,min(h-1,r_q)-max(0,r_q-h+1)+1),
m_q=max(0,ceil((c-q)/H)).

Then there is an explicit normal-form **S-module isomorphism**

**(1) E_{k,c} = direct_sum_{q>=0} (S/I^{m_q})^{N_q}, with summands having m_q=0 or N_q=0 omitted.**

The isomorphism is by the normal monomials z_0^a z_1^b (0<=a,b<h) with a+b=r_q, with each monomial representing a separate cyclic S-coordinate. **This is an S-module decomposition, not a claim that the displayed summands are C_d-submodules**; the C_d-action mixes normal-form coordinates.

Consequences, for c>=1:

**(2) Ann_S(E_{k,c})=I^{ceil(c/H)} and Supp_S(E_{k,c})=V(I).**

**(3) Fitt_{0,S}(E_{k,c})=I^{sum_{q>=0} N_q m_q}.** This is the Fitting ideal **over S**, not the earlier Fitting statement over C_d.

At the generic point of each of the four codimension-two components of V(I), the length of E_{k,c} is

**(4) L_{k,c}=sum_{q>=0} N_q * m_q(m_q+1)/2.**

For 1<=c<=H, the formula simplifies to

**(5) E_{k,c} = (S/I)^{c(ek+1)+d c(c-1)/2} as an S-module.**

At c=H+1, an I^2-thickened summand first appears, with multiplicity N_0=ek+1. For c=1, equation (1) recovers the independently documented one-carry formula E_{k,1}=(S/I)^{ek+1}. All statements remain valid in characteristics dividing h or n; no root of unity, Kummer separability, or division by an integer is assumed.

## Proof

1. **Canonical normal-form basis.** Because z_0^h-A and z_1^h-B are monic in their respective variables, D_h is free as an S-module with basis z_0^a z_1^b, 0<=a,b<h. As d|h, reduction by z_i^h=A or B preserves total-degree mod d. Thus M_k is the direct S-sum of the basis coordinates whose exponent sum is ek (mod d), equivalently a+b=ek+qd, q>=0. Each such q has exactly N_q coordinates.

2. **Describe the J_d^c-image coordinatewise.** By the complete-interval multiplication lemma in the pinned higher-carry theorem, J_d^c M_k is generated over C_d by the full monomial interval of degree T=ek+cd. Consider a normal-form coordinate z_0^a z_1^b with a+b=ek+qd. An S-monomial A^u B^v times that coordinate is in J_d^c M_k **if and only if** its unreduced z-total-degree a+b+(u+v)h is at least T. For the forward direction, each generator has total-degree T and remaining invariant monomial degree is nonnegative. For the converse, when total degree is >=T, one can select a generator z_0^x z_1^{T-x} with 0<=x<=T such that x<=a+uh and T-x<=b+vh; the leftover monomial has nonnegative total degree divisible by d, hence belongs to C_d. Monomial reduction cannot mix distinct S-basis coordinates. Thus the coefficient ideal in the (a,b) coordinate is precisely generated by A^u B^v for (u+v)h >= (c-q)d, i.e. I^{max(0,ceil((c-q)/H))}=I^{m_q}. This proves (1).

3. **Annihilator and Fitting ideal.** All m_q are at most m_0=ceil(c/H), and the q=0 block is nonzero (N_0=ek+1). Powers of I form a descending chain. Intersecting the cyclic S-annihilators I^{m_q} gives precisely I^{m_0}. Fitt_0 is multiplicative under finite direct sums; Fitt_0(S/I^m)=I^m. This proves (2)-(3). The I-adic associated graded of S is (S/I)[X,Y] because A,B form a regular sequence, so over the generic point of each codimension-two component each S/I^m summand has length binomial(m+1,2). This proves (4).

4. **First H carries.** If c<=H, m_q=1 for 0<=q<c and m_q=0 otherwise. All q<c satisfy r_q=ek+qd<h (since ek<d and q<=H-1), hence N_q=r_q+1. Summing yields c(ek+1)+d c(c-1)/2 and gives (5). At c=H+1, m_0=2, proving the onset claim. QED.

## Exact independent finite checks and example

A direct divisor-chain / exponent-pair enumeration was run for 2<=h<=16, all e|d|h, all 0<=k<n, c=1..6, all 0<=a,b<h with a+b congruent ek mod d, and all 0<=u,v<=3. Each of **769728** tests independently enumerated every possible generator x in [0,T] and verified that a normal coordinate times A^u B^v belongs to the image exactly when u+v>=m_q. A second arithmetic consistency check across 2<=h<=24, c=1..8 and all 0<=u,v<=4 covered **241616 normal-coordinate instances** and matched the formula. These finite tests supplement, not replace, the universal monomial-basis proof.

Minimal reproducible checker (a reduced range is selectable by changing HMAX):

```python
def check(HMAX=16, CMAX=6, UV=3):
    tests=0
    for h in range(2,HMAX+1):
        for d in range(1,h+1):
            if h%d: continue
            for e in range(1,d+1):
                if d%e: continue
                n,H=d//e,h//d
                for k in range(n):
                    for c in range(1,CMAX+1):
                        T=e*k+c*d
                        for a in range(h):
                            for b in range(h):
                                if (a+b-e*k)%d: continue
                                q=(a+b-e*k)//d
                                m=max(0,(c-q+H-1)//H)
                                assert m==max(0,(T-a-b+h-1)//h)
                                for u in range(UV+1):
                                    for v in range(UV+1):
                                        X=a+u*h; Y=b+v*h
                                        observed=any(x<=X and T-x<=Y for x in range(T+1))
                                        assert observed==(u+v>=m),(h,d,e,k,c,a,b,u,v)
                                        tests+=1
    return tests
assert check()==769728
```

For h=d=4,e=1,k=0,c=3 (so H=1), one has N_0=1, N_1=3, m_0=3, m_1=2, giving

E_{0,3} = S/(A,B)^3 direct_sum (S/(A,B)^2)^3.

Its S-annihilator is (A,B)^3, S-Fitting ideal is (A,B)^9, and generic length on each codimension-two component is 6+3*3=15. This distinguishes higher-carry thickening from the first-carry reduced residue.
