## Theorem (all even heights, all characteristics)

Let `h=2k>=2`, `k>=1`, and let `K` be **any field**. In `K[Y_0,Y_1,Y_2,Y_3]`, put

`U_i=Y_i^h`, `Z_0=Y_0Y_3`, `Z_1=Y_1Y_2`, `A=U_1U_2`, `B=U_0U_3`.

The paired-hypersurface ring from the earlier result is

`D_h=K[U_0,U_1,U_2,U_3,Z_0,Z_1]/(Z_0^h-B, Z_1^h-A)`.

The previously identified index-one canonical cover `C_h^{can}=D_h^{mu_2}` is **explicitly**

```text
C_h^{can} ~= K[U_0,U_1,U_2,U_3,X,Y,Z]/
  (Y^2-XZ, X^k-U_0U_3, Z^k-U_1U_2),
X -> Z_0^2,   Y -> Z_0 Z_1,   Z -> Z_1^2.
```

The three displayed relations form a regular sequence; hence `C_h^{can}` is a **four-dimensional affine complete intersection and Gorenstein**. It is normal by the previously pinned saturated-lattice description. This is a uniform integral construction: no division by 2 is used, and it holds in characteristic 2 and when `char(K)` divides `h`.

As a module over `R=K[U_0,U_1,U_2,U_3]`, the cover is finite free of rank

```text
2*k^2 = h^2/2,
basis = { X^i Z^j, Y X^i Z^j : 0<=i,j<k }.
```

The original Reeve observer algebra `R_h=D_h^{mu_h}` is a subring of this cover, with generic degree `[Frac(C_h^{can}):Frac(R_h)]=h/2` as established in the earlier lattice theorem. The `h^2/2` rank above is over the **polynomial source algebra** `R`, not over the Reeve observer ring.

For `h=2`, `k=1`, eliminating `X=B,Z=A` reduces the presentation to `K[U_i,Y]/(Y^2-AB)`, consistent with index one and the earlier `h=2` observer hypersurface.

## Proof

The `mu_2` action on `D_h` is the `Z/2` character grading in which `Z_0,Z_1` have odd degree and all `U_i` have even degree. This is true for the diagonalizable group **scheme** `mu_2`, including in characteristic 2; the invariant subring is precisely degree zero.

First, the even-total-degree subalgebra of `K[Z_0,Z_1]` is generated by `X=Z_0^2,Y=Z_0Z_1,Z=Z_1^2`. Every monomial of even total degree has either both exponents even (`X^i Z^j`) or both odd (`YX^i Z^j`). The only relation is `Y^2=XZ`, as follows either by these disjoint exponent normal forms or by the toric lattice kernel. Therefore

`K[U_i,Z_0,Z_1]_0 ~= K[U_i,X,Y,Z]/(Y^2-XZ)`.

Next, because `h=2k`, both `Z_0^{2k}-B` and `Z_1^{2k}-A` have **even grading**. If `P=P_0 direct_sum P_1` is the original graded polynomial ring and `I` the ideal they generate, then `I_0=(Z_0^{2k}-B)P_0+(Z_1^{2k}-A)P_0`, since any even-degree component of an expression in `I` uses even-degree multipliers. Thus `(P/I)_0=P_0/I_0`, giving exactly the three displayed equations with no extra relation. This proves the presentation scheme-theoretically in every characteristic.

Independently, `D_h` is free over `R` with basis `Z_0^a Z_1^b`, `0<=a,b<2k`, by its two monic equations. Its even-grading part is the direct summand with `a+b` even, and has exactly `2k^2` basis elements. They correspond bijectively to `X^i Z^j` and `Y X^i Z^j`, `0<=i,j<k`. This establishes finite freeness and the stated explicit basis without an appeal to geometric-point invariants.

The quotient is therefore finite free of positive rank over four independent `U_i`, hence has Krull dimension four. Its ambient polynomial ring has dimension seven and its defining ideal is generated by three elements of height three. In a regular polynomial ring, these equations form a regular sequence. Consequently the cover is a codimension-three complete intersection, hence Gorenstein. The preexisting canonical-cover lattice proof supplies normality; it also establishes the fraction-field degree `h/2` over `R_h`. QED.

## Finite exact corroboration

For every even `h=2,4,...,28` (14 values), enumerate the proposed `2k^2` basis elements and verify their exponent pairs `(2i+e,2j+e)`, `e in {0,1}`, biject to all `(a,b)` with `0<=a,b<h` and `a+b` even. For every ordered pair of such basis elements (total **510,748** pairs), reduce the product first by `Y^2=XZ,X^k=B,Z^k=A` and independently by `Z_0^h=B,Z_1^h=A`. The resulting `B,A` exponents and residue-exponent pairs agree in every case. The verifier uses integer exponent arithmetic only; in particular its agreement is characteristic-independent. The general proof above is the complete certificate.

```python
total = 0
for h in range(2, 29, 2):
    k = h // 2
    basis = [(i, j, e) for e in (0, 1)
             for i in range(k) for j in range(k)]
    images = {(2*i+e, 2*j+e) for i, j, e in basis}
    expected = {(a, b) for a in range(h)
                for b in range(h) if (a+b) % 2 == 0}
    assert images == expected and len(basis) == h*h//2
    for i, j, e in basis:
        for l, m, f in basis:
            I, J, E = i+l+(e+f)//2, j+m+(e+f)//2, (e+f)%2
            qb, ii = divmod(I, k)
            qa, jj = divmod(J, k)
            db, aa = divmod(2*(i+l)+e+f, h)
            da, bb = divmod(2*(j+m)+e+f, h)
            assert (qb, qa, 2*ii+E, 2*jj+E) == (db, da, aa, bb)
            total += 1
assert total == 510748
```
