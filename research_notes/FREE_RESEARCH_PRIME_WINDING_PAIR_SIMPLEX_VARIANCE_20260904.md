## 2. Universal quotient 2-simplex

\[
q_a(n):=\left\lfloor\frac na\right\rfloor.
\]

\[
\boxed{q_b(q_a(n))=q_{ab}(n).}
\tag{2.1}
\]

For a real field `f` on finite scales, define the signless quotient-edge defect

\[
\delta_a f(n):=f(n)+f(q_a(n)).
\]

For every pair `a,b`, the commuting triangle

\[
n\xrightarrow{a}q_a(n)\xrightarrow{b}q_{ab}(n),
\qquad
n\xrightarrow{ab}q_{ab}(n)

has one direct edge and one two-edge route. Their parity mismatch gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
2f(n)
=\delta_a f(n)+\delta_{ab}f(n)-\delta_bf(q_a(n)).
}
\tag{2.2}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\boxed{
4|f(n)|^2
\le3\left(
|\delta_a f(n)|^2
+|\delta_{ab}f(n)|^2
+|\delta_b f(q_a(n))|^2
\right).
}
\tag{2.3}
\]
