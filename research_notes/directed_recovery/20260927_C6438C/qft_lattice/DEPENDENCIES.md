## External counting theorem

The specific primary source is Alexander Barvinok and Kevin Woods,
*Short rational generating functions for lattice point problems*,
[arXiv:math/0211146v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0211146v1).
Theorem 3.1 supplies a polynomial-time short generating function in fixed
dimension. Theorem 2.6 and the following all-ones evaluation remark permit the
finite exact count, despite poles in individual rational summands. The proof
applies these statements to explicitly bounded rational polytopes.

READING_EVIDENCE.json identifies the actual retrieved version and limited
sections read. Theorem 3.1 cites an earlier Barvinok–Pommersheim result; neither
the root record nor this packaging claims that earlier proof was separately
retrieved. No PDF binary or PDF SHA is provided. This is an external theorem
dependency, not an implemented or newly admitted BRC primitive.
