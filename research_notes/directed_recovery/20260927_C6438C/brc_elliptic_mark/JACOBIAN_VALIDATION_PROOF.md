For the admitted smooth odd-modulus Montgomery curve `B*y^2=x^3+A*x^2+x`, B is a unit. The change `x'=B*x`, `y'=B^2*y` gives

`y'^2=x'^3+a2*x'^2+a4*x'`, with `a2=A*B`, `a4=B^2`.

The initial point `(x,y)=(2,1)` has Jacobian representation `(X,Y,Z)=(2B,B^2,1)`, where `x'=X/Z^2`, `y'=Y/Z^3`. Its homogeneous equation is

`Y^2=X^3+a2*X^2*Z^2+a4*X*Z^4`.

Use the exact doubling polynomials

`Z3=2YZ`, `M=3X^2+2a2XZ^2+a4Z^4`, `S=4XY^2`,

`X3=M^2-2S-a2Z3^2`, `Y3=M(S-X3)-8Y^4`.

On the affine overlap these follow from slope `(3x'^2+2a2*x'+a4)/(2y')`, then `x'(2P)=slope^2-a2-2x'` and `y'(2P)=slope*(x'-x'(2P))-y'`. Homogenization gives the displayed polynomials. No inverse is evaluated by this program.
