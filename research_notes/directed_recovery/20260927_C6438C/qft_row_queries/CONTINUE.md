# Continue the mathematics from any conversation

Goal: QFT/Shor dequantization with an honestly charged complexity bound.
Read README.md, then select one bounded research unit below. These are
mathematical tasks, not role claims or environment/tool prerequisites.
Existing research admission rules still apply to formal Task/Result actions.

## A. Approximate row queries with certified mass-weighted error

Inputs: the actual recurrence v_(h,b)(z)=[v_h(z)+(-1)^b T_h v_h(P^-1z)]/2,
the exact-row-to-order reduction, and the mass-weighted approximation bound.
Find a compact u_h(w) with a provable bound on the aggregate raw error over
all measured prefixes, and charge its construction, point queries and
certificate discovery. A complete bounded answer may be a proved family,
a counterexample to a proposed compression, or a conditional theorem with
its missing input explicitly identified. Do not assume per-history norm
sampling access, known order, or small entanglement without establishing it.

## B. Exact modular fibre sums with ordered matrix coefficients

At a fixed prefix, the row is a sum over binary paths reaching a specified
modular endpoint. Coefficients are products of the actual time-ordered
matrices and cannot be commuted. The current checkpoint/backward contraction
costs about 2^(i/2) terms. Identify verifiable relations that reduce this
cost without first solving order finding. The all-zero prefix reduction
shows why a uniform exact improvement to polynomial time would be major.
Typical-prefix restrictions are allowed if their sampling coverage/error
is proved rather than inferred from a few runs.

## C. More than one character-certified suffix bit

Jacobi(a,N)=-1 certifies the last bit for the standard square schedule.
To certify ell bits, seek an efficiently evaluable homomorphism to a cyclic
group of size 2^ell that maps a to a generator, with a proof for the exact
initial state and schedule. Include the cost of constructing and verifying
the character. An assumed character based on unknown factors/order is not
a solution. If it is unavailable, the unchanged sampler remains usable.

## Completion of a research unit

Record its exact proposition, inputs, proof or counterexample, assumptions,
source ancestry, what was actually executed, and the total resource vector.
Compare to the executed baseline, including unfavorable cost movements.
Preserve signed amplitudes and full residual coordinates or prove an exact
codec at the actual word boundary. Publish the smallest faithful research
artifact through an authorized available route and link it to the current
activity. A local result, an immutable source, and an admitted Result are
different statuses and must remain distinguishable.

If a particular tool is unavailable, continue the independent mathematics
and preserve the result plus the exact missing write/check. Do not invent
a receipt, silently drop an error event, or mark the mathematics impossible
because an optional implementation mechanism is missing. Actual platform
denials retain their stated boundary and are not replayed through another
identity or channel. No unperformed test is represented as completed.
