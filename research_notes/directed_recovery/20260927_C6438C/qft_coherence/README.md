## New results

1. A fixed work-label partition bounds all-history absolute native coherence
   by the affinity of two classically sampleable preparation marginals. A
   layer can therefore use fair scores under an independently verified joint
   error certificate without constructing a projected state. The complete
   derivation includes raw history weights, a counterexample to multiplying
   by selected-history mass, exact confidence rules and automatic fallback.
   See `partition_theory/PARTITION_COHERENCE_CERTIFICATE.md`.
2. A real full-D61 sampler executes this certificate at an intermediate layer,
   then resumes complete exact row queries. It handles both zero-reference
   parent labels and zero row pairs. Three fixed attempts give an exact skip,
   a nonzero-error skip at N129/a4, and an exact fallback. However, matched
   viable paths have identical final distinct-query sets (26/26 and 20/20):
   later recursive queries reconstruct the omitted ancestors. Training and
   validation remain additional costs. See `coherence_sampler/`.
3. A Gaussian quartic character supplies a computable four-color map on bare
   work labels from a public primitive sum-of-squares witness. Its arithmetic,
   composite-denominator laws and complete native suffix checks are in
   `arithmetic_partition/`. It adds label access, not a general witness
   discovery algorithm or a solution to the remaining coherent query problem.
4. There are precise limits to marginal-only certificates. An opaque-support
   separation test needs birthday-scale samples even with a known pairing;
   learning a small separating partition can require linear information.
   Under a separately proved flatness promise a collision certificate reaches
   the birthday scale. More directly, the standard Shor preparation marginal
   has an exact affinity formula: once its address interval is long compared
   with the odd part of the order, even the finest label partition gives a
   bound near 1/2 per replaced layer. These are limits of stated access models
   and certificates, not lower bounds on all classical algorithms or on actual
   output TV. See `partition_limits/PARTITION_AND_COLLISION_LIMITS.md`.
