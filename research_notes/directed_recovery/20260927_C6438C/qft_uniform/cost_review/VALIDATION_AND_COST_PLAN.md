## The new claim to verify

For each immutable complete word `G`, the full-carrier certificate must bind the actual columns, source, direction, dimension, rational `s`, and every checked entry of `B=(I-G)^T(I-G)` and `B^2=sB`, with `0<=s<=4`. It must not infer a rank-two identity from ideal target angles or drop residual rows. Failed witnesses stay failures; reference selection is a valid fallback.

For oldest-word omission, `T=L G` and `S=L`, with the same orthogonal ordered suffix `L`. This gives `||(T-S)x||<=sqrt(s)||x||` without commuting factors. The sufficient scalar test is `8s-s^2<=16e^2` for remaining charge `e`; no per-prefix covariance or private latent state is required by this test. The underlying sampler still needs exact mass/correlation work. A uniform sufficient certificate can accept fewer candidates than a state-specific certificate; equal outputs or acceptance sets are not assumed in advance.

Source review should verify that the warm policy does not call the old full-covariance `defect` routine, `_left/_right_transpose` for candidate testing, or an equivalent hidden transformation. Actual observer labels and explicit counters should corroborate zero state-specific candidate-defect calls. Ordinary Gram recurrence transforms remain separately charged. Full-covariance computations used only by the independent checker must be separated from policy execution.

## Cost boundaries

1. **Cold word cost:** complete native-column construction/admission, construction of `B`, verification of `B^2=sB`, scalar witness extraction, positive-path operations, native calls, source/column hashing, evidence bytes and elapsed interval where recorded. Distinguish original construction from an additional actual verification replay. A previously saved certificate is not automatically a free trusted runtime object.
2. **Warm policy cost:** each fresh leaf object's candidate checks, certificate lookup/binding operations, immutable snapshot/hash checks, scalar observers, accepted/committed omissions, reference fallbacks, and any repeated certificate verification. No unmeasured host hashing cost should be reported as zero.
3. **Sampler cost:** actual word-boundary vector-adapter calls, Gram requests/cache hits, completed base/recurrence queries, independent retained matrices, peak slots and integer bit lengths. Calls to a cached column adapter are distinct from native-kernel invocations. Audit deduplication is not an executed shared cache.
4. **Checker cost:** explicit-state vector actions if instrumented, positive-path observers, TV comparison, and all full-law replay overhead. Missing measurements remain unknown, not inferred from other counters.
5. **Shared modular cost:** factory objects and their separately constructed inverse objects, once per program. Counter snapshots are cumulative across low/reference/checker/control uses. Do not sum reports per leaf or collapse same-multiplier factory/inverse instances. Construction verification receipts may repeat in the schedule while actual verification ran once; count according to the source cache.
6. **Controls:** successful restore/replay, malformed-cursor replay failures, budget interruption and same-bit retry, source/program/certificate mutation rejections. Include failed work actually performed. Distinguish early schema rejection from rejection after an expensive deterministic replay. No cache/RNG restoration is implied by public-prefix replay.

Cold and warm categories can share native admission work in this bounded process. Report the actual measured placement and reuse; do not add a counterfactual fresh setup charge once per leaf or erase actual repeated validation. A break-even estimate requires commensurate measured costs and a stated reuse count, not division of vector-action counts by unrelated native-core calls.

## Minimal controls and publication boundary

Retain the existing cursor mutation checks and rollback/retry fixture. Add certificate-binding negatives only to the extent needed to demonstrate that wrong word/direction/dimension/source or altered `s` cannot reuse a valid witness. A failure to verify must never create a certified omission. Restore must bind the same certified word collection and policy as the original public-prefix recipe.

The eventual extractor will read the final designated raw gzip and summary, verify hashes against frozen source files, and import no scientific module. It will preserve per-route and control counts, reconstruct shared-table instance totals from recorded snapshots, and identify unmeasured costs. This plan does not require another scientific run solely to populate a missing optional counter.

The useful result is whether prefix-independent certification removes the measured repeated-defect overhead at the actual bank and stated reuse level. Even a positive result leaves the potentially large exact Gram query problem, paid modular arithmetic and whole-algorithm complexity open.
