## Public clock and common kernel size

Modulo L_p, p equals sigma_p, whence

    E = pq-sigma_p sigma_q
      = sigma_p(q-sigma_q) mod L_p.

Thus gcd(E,L_p)=gcd(L_q,L_p), since multiplication by the integer sign does not change a gcd. Interchanging p and q gives the same result for L_q. Therefore both local power maps have exactly d=gcd(L_p,L_q) elements in their kernels. This is an exact identity and does not furnish a free computation of d. Both local orders are even, so d is even.

## Negative branch and the primitive observer

## Exact probabilities and CRT mixture

For a fixed nonempty type orientation, regular traces are inverse pairs of eigenvalues in the local cyclic group after removal of +1 and -1. Each remaining inverse pair has two members; there are (L_r-2)/2 trace parameters. E is even, so both exceptional eigenvalues solve the positive equation and neither solves the negative equation. Consequently the positive fiber contains (d-2)/2 traces and the negative fiber contains d/2 traces exactly when L_r/d is even. Dividing by the trace count gives

    a_r=(d-2)/(L_r-2),
    b_r=d/(L_r-2) if L_r/d is even, else 0,
    c_r=1-a_r-b_r.
