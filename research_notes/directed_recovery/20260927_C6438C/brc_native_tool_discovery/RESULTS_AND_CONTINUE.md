## 4. Symbolic frontier and its boundaries

`PARTIAL_COLLISION_CANDIDATE.md` proves the relative and reciprocal reductions and identifies failure boundaries. A proper partial collision is a weaker information requirement than complete order recovery, not a superset of successful full-return ports: a full return gives gcd N. For a squarefree semiprime, unequal local orders can permit a proper partial collision before the first full return; equal local orders prevent this fixed power-pair family from producing one. Those unknown orders are analysis variables, never free algorithm inputs.

`WITNESS_QUOTIENT_CANDIDATE.md` proves the exact finite-schedule histogram theorem and separates it from a weaker search that returns any valid factor. An exact backward success oracle would permit witness extraction, but constructing and evaluating that oracle remain charged. Compact ideal closure preserves a common-to-all condition, not the desired exists-a-factor condition. A product observer can support batch gcd, but a short symbolic product recurrence is not a proof of cheap evaluation; gcd N saturation and witness descent still need repair. None of these interfaces supplies a free small predicate, order table or conditioned sampler.

`TRACE_OR_FACTOR_CANDIDATE.md` offers a more permissive merge contract. Retain one certified unit lift per trace key. For an incoming equal-trace lift, a typed gcd of their difference certifies SAME, INVERSE, or a proper FACTOR. SAME can merge; INVERSE can merge only under the declared compatible future; a proper gcd can terminate even if the lifts also happened to be inversion partners. Before a conflict factor, continuation agrees with the inversion quotient. A new conflict factor changes the search outcome/time law, so it must be labeled as a new event rather than claimed to preserve the old first-hit distribution.

This lemma holds for arbitrary composite moduli, including prime powers and even N. A trace integer alone loses the lift needed for extraction. The original gcd observer does not generally descend through trace at prime powers. The quadratic satisfied by two successor traces can have extra composite-ring roots, so taking every root would add false branches. A correlated neighboring-trace pair has a short standard Lucas/Dickson recurrence, but usually retains enough information to recover the original lift. These are useful interface facts, not newly named number theory or a demonstrated speedup.
