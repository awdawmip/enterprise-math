## Mathematical and executed wiring checks

The conic representation retains `(u,v)` with `uv=1 mod N`. Its paired actions
`(cu,c^-1 v)` and `(c^-1 u,cv)` are exchanged by coordinate swap. Equal microscopic
weights therefore permit canonical exchange, while
`gcd(u-1,N)=gcd(v-1,N)` keeps the factor/first-hit observation. This does not
license separately named asymmetric future actions or erase arbitrary endpoint
provenance. The source uses two identity occurrences `2[1/4]`, and transports
absorbed factor ports by `[1]`.

All 12 complete saved layer histograms match the old folded laws, including
weights and multiplicities, not merely total mass. All final factor/failure
histograms match. All 27 retained live-point invariant receipts were followed
through the actual recorded modular multiplication operations. The source has
no standalone per-state inverse certificates; multiplier/inverse-multiplier
table setup and queried columns are still paid. The `(2,2)` modulo 15 negative
has recorded product 4 and is rejected. N15/a14 preserves total failure mass one.

For each of the 12 trace cuts the reader binds every point, its unchanged
histogram and conic receipt to the earlier saved population. It consumes the
actual addition and reduction operation references, retains the source's first
representative per key, and checks the actual gcd and proper-factor division
receipt for each collision. Both independent unit inverses in each of the five
fixtures are also paid and checked.

The trace theorem is valid for general composite N, including prime powers:
equal traces imply `(u-v)(uv-1)=0 mod N`; a gcd of N means SAME, a gcd of 1
forces INVERSE, and a proper gcd is a factor. INVERSE is not an exclusive
mathematical category: the N15 reciprocal pair can legitimately produce a
proper gcd. The result is classified by the recorded gcd, as required.

The five fixture outcomes are SAME at N7, INVERSE at N7, FACTOR 3 for the N15
reciprocal pair, FACTOR 5 at N25, and DISTINCT_TRACE at N35. All are obtained
