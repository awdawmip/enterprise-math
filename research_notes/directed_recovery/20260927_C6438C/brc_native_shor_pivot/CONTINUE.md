Concrete next research unit: specify and either construct or reject a native BRC representation/observation interface that produces a nonzero exponent-difference collision witness without an order oracle or enumeration hidden in a primitive. The labelled four-branch return-port law in NATIVE_ROUTE_RESET.md is the first candidate, not a mandatory architecture for every future approach. A different native instrument is welcome if its output is demonstrably sufficient for factorization and all operations are accounted.

A non-unit gcd is a valid factor-producing branch. Available reuse: BRC typed composition, observation-safe quotient semantics, finite-fiber collision semantics and actual typed modular arithmetic. The square-body common_collapse module is not a generic modular witness backend.
