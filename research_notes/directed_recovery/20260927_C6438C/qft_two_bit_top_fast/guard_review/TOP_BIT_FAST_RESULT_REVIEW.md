## Chronological links and cache semantics

The reader follows each request into its actual setup index. A first key
consumes the saved typed V/M construction, L/P products and exact half; a hit
must refer to the already completed setup without new setup arithmetic. A
piece coefficient is consumed at its first actual segment, then later hits
must refer to that prior complete record. Every saved setup and coefficient
must be reached by this chronology; extra unattached cache entries are rejected.

The segment start includes any first coefficient construction. Its separate
`moment_evaluation_signed_operations_start` begins the table calculation.
Both references are checked without charging the setup twice. Singleton
records are linked through their d/V quotient/remainder, z/2 parity, the
appropriate low/high overlap, weight, slope correction and signed product.
They contain no fabricated table or coefficient calls. Empty segments retain
their typed zero. Longer segments retain the old two-table moment calculation,
five differences, weighted sums, ten products and exact division by three.

All saved signed outputs are linked to their referenced typed outputs and
every actual retained adder cell is linked to the source-bound eight-column
native catalog. All moment nodes, child/range/cache records and saved table
outputs are checked by the inherited I/O cursor. The cursor consumes recorded
outputs rather than recalculating scientific arithmetic.
