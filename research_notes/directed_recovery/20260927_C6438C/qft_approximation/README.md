## Concrete advances

1. **Projective and direct-coherence error contracts.** The walker scores
   are invariant under one common nonzero row-family scale. A projective
   error bound can therefore certify exact sampling even when raw L2 error
   is large. Replacing a score with a fair bit has exact reference-weighted
   joint cost `L_h/(2M_h)`. Approximate support separation bounds that cost
   by `sqrt(epsilon(1-epsilon))`. The native checker passed 120 projective
   and 86 leakage checks in both D6 and full D61. Proof and execution scope
   are in `observer_contracts/`.
2. **An actual suffix-path row estimator with a measured limitation.**
   Fixed iid paths define a consistent full signed row query function. All
   16 target histories at N21/t4 were checked in D6 and D61, including 512
   ordered two-path means in total. The exact aggregate variance is
   `(2^ell-1)/m`; this is a raw field-error identity, not a sampling-TV lower
   bound. Full actual table instances are retained in the final evidence.
3. **Multiple exact terminal bits from a public arithmetic witness.**
   The primitive power-sum witness plus negative Jacobi certifies a
   two-primary order divisor without a factor/order input. The integrated
   native walker skips consecutive row queries and preserves the complete
   joint output law, including the latent work label. Checks cover 52 joint
   child laws, eight paired trajectories and eight later full-row
   restorations. Large Fermat examples are structure-verifier cases only.
   An independent public-root verifier extends the condition to
   `b^(2^(ell-1))=-1 mod N`, with no root-discovery algorithm assumed.
   It checks suffix lengths up to 16 on supplied examples; those longer
   examples are verifier tests, not hard-composite simulation benchmarks.
4. **A constructive work-envelope approximation.** The unconditional
   work-label marginal can be sampled with the actual classical preparation
   walk. Frozen candidate sets and independent binomial validation give a
   charged statistical certificate for a projected full-D row oracle.
   Orthogonal projection errors obey `E_i <= E_(i-1)+delta_i` rather than
   accumulating linearly in their square roots. Implementation, test
   selection, fallback and finite results are in `structured_approx/`.
   The executed outer runner covers accepted approximation, automatic
   exact fallback after a failed test, and a separate N33/a4 contrast with
   nonzero joint TV about 0.1571. Its declared one-attempt unconditional
   budget is only 9/16 relative to the native instrument: an interface
   prototype, not a high-accuracy or asymptotic benchmark.

## Honest complexity comparison

The same arithmetic witness also narrows elementary classical trial
division. A typed comparator finds factors of 65, 4097 and 4294967297 in
1, 2 and 10 candidate divisions, respectively. These are genuine executed
outputs but do not reproduce the QFT law. A useful future speedup must beat
a baseline that is given the same public witness.

Work sparsity alone does not close the general problem. Before preparation
collisions, an approximation retaining only K work rows has aggregate
squared error at least `1-K/2^i`. This also holds for common-scalar
projective error. It is a limitation of that representation and sufficient
certificate, not an impossibility result for all classical samplers.

Native sampling approximation and the previously admitted bank's error
relative to ideal QFT are separate budgets. The small t=4 fixtures are
finite interface/theorem checks, not default-width Shor factoring
benchmarks. Training, validation, witness verification, exact fallback,
bit precision, modular columns and evidence storage are not free.
