## 2. Exact physical-space Schur step

Reorder the 2N-cycle into even retained vertices and odd hidden vertices. With

D=(8N^2+u)I_N

for the diagonal even/odd blocks and nearest-neighbor coupling matrix B, one has

B B^T=16N^4(2I+R_N+R_N^*).

Schur elimination of the odd block gives

S=D-BD^(-1)B^T

and direct coefficient comparison yields

S = [16N^2/(8N^2+u)] [A_N+u' I],

u'=u+u^2/(16N^2).

Taking determinants,

det(A_(2N)+uI)
=(8N^2+u)^N det S
=(16N^2)^N det(A_N+u'I).

Since `(2N)^(4N)=16^N N^(4N)`, normalization cancels exactly and

`Delta_(2N)(u)=Delta_N(u+u^2/(16N^2))`.

This is a finite rational block-elimination theorem. It is the q=2 instance of the broader positive-u port-collapse frontier, but it requires no Chebyshev/Fourier representation.

## 3. Coefficient carrier recursion

`e_j(2N)=sum_(r=0)^floor((j+1)/2) binom(j+1-r,r) (1/(16N^2))^r e_(j-r)(N)`.

All operations are finite rational polynomial operations.

`e_j(infinity)=2/(2j+2)!`.

Thus the coefficientwise limiting determinant is the purely rational/factorial formal series

`Delta_infinity(u)=sum_(n>=1) 2u^n/(2n)!`.
