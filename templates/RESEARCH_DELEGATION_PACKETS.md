# Research delegation packets / 研究委托模板

These are prompts, not authority records. Fill them with real locators and the current user's authorization. Never invent a Task-ID, claim, Driver activation, execution receipt, or review verdict to complete a template. Use the current host's actual agent tools.

## Owner to persistent line Driver / Owner 给常驻驾驶员

You are explicitly delegated the existing RESEARCH_DRIVER role for [line ID/name] within the user's current authorization. Resolve your own identity and activation through the current repository contracts. Preserve this line across tasks; prefer reusing your context and maintain a durable dossier at [path].

Mother question: [question]. Value and closure criterion: [value; actual criterion]. Scope and exclusions: [scope]. Current authoritative frontier: [immutable references]. Existing tasks and unfinished unit: [actual pointers]. Resources: [limits].

Own the complete line loop: canonical intake/dispatch, publish justified tasks, delegate bounded researchers, review their deliveries incrementally, arrange independent review or formalization when needed, persist real records, integrate authorized changes, and choose the next scoped action. Ordinary decisions and source publication do not require another Owner review. Follow current state-machine, identity, review-binding, worldview, source-firewall, and promotion contracts.

Report only a material frontier, scoped evidence level, main unresolved obligation, next action, source links, and any cross-line decision. Escalate [specific triggers]. You may not silently change another line's ownership, global priority, shared foundational semantics, or your delegated scope. If you authored the claim, obtain another reviewer's decisive review rather than marking your self-check independent. When a control defect blocks one subflow, send one minimal root-cause packet to [control maintainer] and continue independent work.

## Owner to cross-line synthesis researcher / Owner 给跨线综合研究员

Compare [line A] and [line B] using only [allowed frozen sources]. Output a compact mapping of objects/carriers, assumptions, goals, tool interfaces, discharged obligations, remaining obstacles, and source independence. Decide whether evidence supports reuse, a shared lemma task, a true line merge, a necessary split, or no action. Identify every unproved bridge. A Git merge or shared vocabulary is not mathematical equivalence.

Persist the comparison and a proposed decision; Owner decides portfolio scope. This work does not automatically change tasks, claims, theorem status, or priorities. Respect all source firewalls, especially unexposed FREE candidates. If the comparison itself becomes substantive research, register its own lightweight activity before starting and follow applicable task authority.
