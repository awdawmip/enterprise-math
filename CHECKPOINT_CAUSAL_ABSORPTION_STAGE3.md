5. **P018 complete-block bridge**: fixed quotient/detail/carry calculus is causally generated when scale d is an actual complete block capacity. Quotient-only floor state is future-safe under addition iff all allowed increments are multiples of d.
6. **Bulk + structural continuation**: `(bulk,tau)` is a useful schema, but the true minimum can be bulk-dependent `disjoint_union_b T_b`; value/relation separation does not imply Cartesian product.
7. **Causal capacity bounds**: finite structural type plus r bounded-increment additive integer bulk channels has at most polynomial state-capacity growth of degree r; exponential copy/history tasks cannot be represented by any fixed such schema.
8. **Causal state-growth dimension**: exact polynomial continuation capacity has a finite-difference growth order, kept distinct from physical/relation dimension unless a bridge theorem proves agreement.
9. **Hidden motion / dimension agreement**: fixed-total m-slot LEGO fibers have hidden zero-sum motion rank m-1 and fiber multiplicity finite-difference degree m-1. In the free A_p regime hidden rank, fiber-growth degree, and graph-ball growth degree all equal p.
10. **Block redistribution decomposition**: retaining k separate block totals hides rank m-k; forgetting them to one grand total adds exactly k-1 cross-block redistribution freedoms. Set-level LEGO fiber decomposition and generalized convolution are the counting shadows.
11. **P008 causal completion bridge**: causally generated strictly increasing complete growth V feeds the P008 right-adjoint root/collapse. Plateaued V must be future-refined or causally quotient by plateau classes before root recovery.
12. **Basin dimension lowering**: P008 basin width is Delta V. Polynomial growth degree p lowers to p-1; free H_m strengthens to exact lower-slot fiber H_(m-1); A_p ball basin equals graph shell.
13. **State boundary vs relation boundary**: A_p state shell only lowers growth degree, while directed primitive cut relations satisfy `E_(p,alpha)(r)=|B_(p-1)(r)|` and total `E_p(r)=p(p+1)|B_(p-1)(r)|`.
14. **Variable basin precision**: exact state is `(complete level,detail,current basin width)`. Premature detail erasure can manufacture nonassociativity; square-growth complete-level join gives `(3*4)*4=6` versus `3*(4*4)=5` while exact amount addition remains associative.
15. **Collapse composition safety hierarchy**: representative-level coherence is weaker than fiber congruence. Linear fixed-block representatives are associative, but floor quotient alone is not addition-congruent on basin members.
16. **Unbounded-basin obstruction**: if basin widths are unbounded, no fixed positive additive translation is globally future-safe on the level-only quotient. Any positive complete-growth polynomial of degree >=2 therefore needs detail/continuation state for positive additive dynamics.
17. **Periodic basin positive case**: periodic width sequence of period p and period capacity T yields exact safe translation `V(k+p)=V(k)+T`, preserving detail. Constant-width P018 is p=1.
18. **Higher grade coherence**: pair-grade defect D3 is the three-body rebracketing correction; any D3 derived from one pair grade satisfies the four-body pentagon identity. Arbitrary ternary corrections can fail pentagon and require higher compatibility data.
19. **Coupled/graded LEGO core**: free fibers, graded fibers, kappa support/multiplicity, gamma grade shift, continuation type, interaction basis, and causal collapse spectra remain the common generators; traditional convolution/matrix/tensor/semiring formulas are special observation shadows.
