## Raw debt claims

### D1 — Composable extensive relational quantity

Prior finite-relational candidates did not derive a quantity that is both locally updateable and compositionally coherent across enlargement, partition, transfer, and aggregation.

Question: does the native relational substrate admit a finite-information extensive carrier or quotient whose composition law is derived internally rather than imported from classical measure/geometry?

### D2 — Recurrent process with compositional fraction/rate semantics

Finite recurrence was easy; exact closure alone was insufficient to connect recurrence to controlled rate change, drift, or comparable fractional progress.

Question: can a finite recurrent native process derive a clock/rate/fraction structure from its own update algebra without importing angle, radians, continuous time, or classical periodic definitions?

### D3 — Conservation plus genuine relaxation plus scale

Some candidates conserved quantities but did not relax; others stabilized or propagated but lacked a conserved extensive carrier or principled scale composition.

Question: can one small finite mechanism derive local conservation, nontrivial relaxation/mixing, and explicit scale/refinement behavior in the same state system?

### D4 — Bounded recurrence/operator structure with ordered response

Finite recurrence did not by itself provide a native explanation of ordered, state-sensitive bounded response families.

Question: can a finite relational mechanism generate a bounded operator/feedback structure with internally ordered or separated recurrent responses and controlled state-dependent shifts, without importing continuum wave/Fourier/eigenfunction definitions?

### D5 — Traceable readout / metrology interface

All first-generation mechanisms stopped below quantitative engineering prediction.

Question: what is the weakest typed bridge from finite native observables to externally traceable quantities and uncertainty envelopes? This may be a calibration/readout debt rather than a native-ontology debt and must be typed accordingly.

### D6 — Shared low-information state across multiple macroscopic roles

No first-generation candidate achieved a validated shared explanation across multiple independent effective domains.

Question: can one low-information native state or update law causally control multiple later readouts through one uniform bridge, rather than through per-target adapters? This may be an evaluation criterion rather than a primitive and must be typed accordingly.

## Generation discipline after factorization

Only after the debt basis is frozen may new-generation candidate mechanisms be proposed.

Candidate generation must remain native-side:

- no classical pi numerical target;
- no center/distance/equidistance/radius/circle primitive;
- no angle/radian primitive;
- no continuum measure/PDE/Fourier/Gaussian definitions;
- no direct access to R046 target protocols, numerical tolerances, or R047C calibration scores during generation;
- no candidate-per-debt patchwork by default;
- no requirement to preserve any old R047 candidate.

Old R047 mechanisms are historical shoulders only. They may be consulted later if the researcher explicitly gets stuck, but must not be preselected at startup as the route.

## What would count as progress

Strong progress is not “one candidate has six named features.” Strong progress is a smaller internally motivated mechanism from which multiple previously separate debts become derived consequences, while preserving finite information, explicit typing, composition closure, and falsifiable theorem/counterexample boundaries.
