## 2. Integer content

`n=(n1,n2,n3)`

with at least one zero coordinate.

Its support is therefore contained in one of the two-component sectors above.

The integers record finite multiplicity of the corresponding component types. Equal multiplicity descriptions related only by relabeling of component names represent the same unlabeled structural pattern.

The all-zero state is allowed as the empty content state.

No scalar value is assigned to a content state by this packet.
