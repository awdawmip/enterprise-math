# Enterprise Math research backflow and foundation stewardship

Current policy: 2026-11-13. Mathematical questions stay open; researchers choose their methods, depth and steps. This replaces the former ban on the steward becoming a primary investigator, mandatory FQ handoff followed by stopping investigation, compulsory dual-track administration and fixed backflow loop.

Stewards, Drivers and researchers may investigate existing material, revisit old questions, synthesize routes, test counterexamples, correct errors and reuse results according to mathematical value. Keyword tool matching is a discovery hint, not a sufficiency judgment or exclusive catalog. Follow exact definitions, sources, scope and known task relations to other useful methods.

Backflow is optional support, not a ritual on every turn. FQs, Feedback Packets, classes, phase labels and handoffs are used when helpful. There is no required template, count, order, score or completion receipt. Investigate directly or collaborate; absence of FQ, Packet, audit stage or matching historical state does not prohibit mathematics. Empty link arrays do not mean no results and do not require manufactured tasks.

GitHub is the scientific source of record. When later work solves, corrects, generalizes or usefully relates to an older question, update its relevant entry, definition explanation, tool route or task note as useful, citing actual sources and scope without duplicating the scientific body. Distinguish candidate connections, proved conclusions and negative boundaries. Control-plane relation hints neither prove mathematics nor claim work or change owners. Keep immutable reports/reviews; use a new attributed explanation for corrections.

Formalization is deferred; Lean is not a default progress or ordinary acceptance prerequisite. Use the proposition's reproducible human proof or recalculation when verification is needed. Do not promote conjectures, numerics or successful receipts to universal theorems. Preserve definitions, quantifiers, hypotheses, scope and negative boundaries as the content requires.

Continuous external observation can follow the existing Driver/steward work naturally: no new scheduled task and no required full-portfolio retrospective or repeated scan each turn. `foundation_backflow.json` holds optional relations; exact-task ordinary continuation can return matching hints without new startup reading. The known H0N/H0O and GEO6 entries record only the original source's successor relationships, not new mathematical conclusions.

Actual identity, contribution history, scientific isolation and live owners remain intact. Permission to investigate mathematics does not change native roles, transfer another claimant's work or promote candidates to Foundation truth; formal shared writes retain their actual own scopes and owner CAS. Phase-A blind reading is not exposed to later research by these hints. Existing #82/#164/#240 and Task publication remain available when useful, without a compulsory administrative prelude.
