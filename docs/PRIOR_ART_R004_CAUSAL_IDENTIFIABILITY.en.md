# Prior Art — R004 / FQ-007 Finite Counterfactual Completion

Status: `L4 CANONICAL CANDIDATE / PRIOR-ART CALIBRATION`

This note calibrates the finite counterfactual-completion no-go against established mathematics. It does not claim that Enterprise Math invented contingent plans, auxiliary-randomness functional representation, convex mixing, or deterministic latent completion.

## 1. Complete contingent plans and extensive-form strategy representation

Kuhn's extensive-game framework is a canonical prior-art reference for complete contingent strategies and the relationship between pure/mixed/behavior strategy representations under the relevant game-theoretic hypotheses. [SRC-KUHN-EXTENSIVE-GAMES-1953]

R004 uses a different project-native object — a finite action-indexed counterfactual master tree for relation/support and rational-kernel processes — but the idea that a complete contingent response plan may be selected before the realized path is known is not a generic novelty claim.

The Enterprise Math claim is narrower: for the stated finite relation/support construction, the union of deterministic-master outcomes equals the raw A4 relation-word support exactly; for the stated total-rational construction, one ex-ante master measure reproduces every declared finite-horizon word law and deterministic adaptive visible-history law.

## 2. Functional representation by auxiliary randomness

Functional-representation results in probability and information theory establish broad representations of random outputs using deterministic functions plus auxiliary randomness. Li and El Gamal's strong functional representation lemma is one modern calibration point. [SRC-LI-ELGAMAL-SFRL-2017]

R004's total-rational master compiler is treated as an elementary finite recursive/product-coupling specialization for its declared finite-state/action setting, not as a new general functional-representation theorem.

## 3. What is project-local

The project-local contribution is the precise routing through existing Enterprise Math boundaries:

- FQ-004: exact state, present observation, and declared-future-safe equality remain distinct;
- FQ-006: partial-operation legality/disabledness remains explicit;
- A4: multivalued relation/support remains relation-valued rather than being silently replaced by a function;

The verified no-go answer is that they do not, whenever the corresponding hidden counterfactual completion remains admissible.

## 4. What is not imported as an axiom

Bell-locality, measurement independence, Bell/CHSH inequalities, common-cause factorization, bounded latent capacity, and other substantive causal/physical restrictions are not adopted merely because they can obstruct some hidden completions.

They may serve as prior-art examples or application-specific admissibility laws, but choosing one requires independent justification outside the finite completion theorem itself.

## 5. Novelty boundary

No generic novelty is claimed for:

- complete contingent plans;
- mixed/behavior strategy representation;
- random-seed or auxiliary-randomness determinization;
- finite product couplings;
- convex mixtures of deterministic response tables;
- generic hidden-variable completion as a mathematical representation technique.

The publishable Enterprise Math content is the finite project-native T01–T03 construction, its executable exact-arithmetic cross-checks, and the carefully limited Foundation conclusion that unrestricted admissible latent completion defeats generative identifiability in the declared finite-horizon language.
