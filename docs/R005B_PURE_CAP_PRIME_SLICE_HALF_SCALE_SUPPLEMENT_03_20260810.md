## 1. Executive result

The pure horizon cap from Supplement 02 can be compressed further.

Its non-forced candidate witnesses are not an arbitrary subset of primes below
the factor horizon.  They are exactly the primes in one contiguous integer
slice

\[
\boxed{L_{p,k}\le q\le S_p(k)},
\]

where `S_p(k)=floor(sqrt(k^p))` and L has an exact closed integer formula.

At the same time, the post-horizon protecting gap

\[
G_p(k)=\frac{U}{S_p(k)}-F_p(k)
\]

admits an exact decomposition into the **drift of the factor horizon across the
basin** plus one bounded square-remainder correction.

For even collapse exponent `p=2m`, the drift is exactly the interior width of
the half-exponent m-power basin:

\[
\boxed{

=(k+1)^m-k^m-1
=L_m(k).
}
\]

Thus the even-dimensional full-forcing gap scale is generated by a lower
collapse scale already present inside the same arithmetic hierarchy.

For p=4 this says the dominant protecting gap is exactly the square-basin
interior width `2k`, explaining the Legendre boundary by exponent halving rather
than by exponent numerology alone.

## 5. B18 — exact post-horizon overshoot window

Ignore the other pure-cap lower bounds for a moment and retain only
non-forcing

\[
qR>U
\]

and `q<=S`.  The raw integer q-window is

\[

S
\right].
\]

Its exact number of integer points is

\[
\boxed{
W_{p,k}
=\max\left(0,S-\left\lfloor\frac UR\right\rfloor\right).
}
\]

Let

\[
D=RS-U.
\]

When `D>0`,

\[
\frac UR=S-\frac DR,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
W_{p,k}=\left\lceil\frac DR\right\rceil.
}
\]

Thus the amount by which the post-horizon prime R overshoots the threshold is
converted almost directly into the width of the candidate q-window.

If

\[
g=R-F
\]

is the actual prime gap after F and

\[
G=\frac US-F

is the threshold from Supplement 02, then

\[
D
=RS-U
=S(g-G).
\]

Hence, whenever `g>G`,

\[
\boxed{
W_{p,k}
=
\left\lceil\frac{S(g-G)}{R}\right\rceil.
}
\]

Since `S/R` is near one in the critical regime, the q-window width is, to first
order, the **excess** of the post-horizon prime gap above its survival
threshold.

The actual pure-cap prime slice `[L,S]` can only be smaller, because the other
three lower bounds in B17 may trim its left edge.

## 6. B19 — exact horizon-drift decomposition

Define

\[
\boxed{d=F-S}
\]

and the upper horizon-square remainder

\[
\boxed{\rho=U-F^2}.
\]

By definition of F,

\[
0\le\rho\le2F.
\]

### Theorem B19.1

The protecting gap threshold satisfies the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
G_p(k)
=d+rac{d^2+\rho}{S}.
}
\]

### Proof

Since `F=S+d` and `U=F^2+rho`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
G
&=\frac US-F\\
&=\frac{F^2+\rho-FS}{S}\\
&=\frac{F(F-S)+\rho}{S}\\
&=\frac{(S+d)d+\rho}{S}\\
&=d+\frac{d^2+\rho}{S}.
\end{aligned}
\]

∎

So the short-prime threshold has a direct geometric meaning in the collapse
arithmetic:

\[
\boxed{
\text{protecting prime gap}
=
\text{factor-horizon drift}
+
\text{square/remainder correction}.
}
\]

For fixed p>=3 the correction is lower order than d, recovering the earlier
asymptotic

\[
G_p(k)\sim\frac p2 k^{p/2-1}.
