# P025 Supplement 135 — Direct premise arity can be traded for derivation depth

Status: `PROVED_WIP + EXECUTABLE_AUTHORED / NOVELTY_UNVERIFIED`  
Owner: `program/p025-closure-basis-stage130`

## 1. A scalable synergy family

Fix independent raw antecedents

\[
a_1,\ldots,a_k,
\qquad k\ge2,
\]

a final root `z`, and helper labels

\[
e_2,\ldots,e_{k-1}.
\]

Use the binary implication chain

\[
a_1a_2\Rightarrow e_2,
\]

\[
e_{j-1}a_j\Rightarrow e_j
\qquad(3\le j\le k-1),
\]

and

\[
e_{k-1}a_k\Rightarrow z.
\]

For `k=2`, the single rule is simply `a_1 a_2 -> z`.

Let `cl_k` be the closure generated by these rules.

## 2. A k-ary rooted direct circuit survives

The raw antecedent set

\[
A_k=\{a_1,\ldots,a_k\}
\]

forces `z`.  Moreover, if any one raw antecedent is removed, the helper chain breaks before reaching the final root. Hence

\[
\boxed{A_k\Rightarrow z}
\]

is itself a rooted minimal implication of premise arity `k`.

Therefore the Stage-130 direct circuit horizon satisfies

\[
\boxed{h_{\rm circ}(cl_k)\ge k.}
\]

By Stage 133, any depth-one sound complete single-head representation must contain this direct `k`-ary rule.

## 3. Binary iterative compilation

The displayed helper basis has exactly

\[
\boxed{k-1}
\]

rules, every premise has arity exactly two, and from raw seed `A_k` the helper states are generated one layer per parallel round. The final root appears after exactly

\[
\boxed{k-1}
\]

rounds.

Thus the same closure admits an iterative presentation with

\[
\boxed{
(\text{max premise arity},\text{rule count},\text{raw-seed depth})
=(2,k-1,k-1).
}
\]

while its direct one-round law contains an irreducible arity-`k` circuit.

## 4. Unbounded arity/depth exchange

As `k` grows, the maximum premise arity required by direct one-round semantics grows without bound, whereas the iterative basis keeps premise arity fixed at two.

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\text{direct relation-law arity is not an invariant of semantic closure once iterative helper computation is allowed.}
}
\]

One can lower stored rule arity by accepting deeper future computation.

## 5. Important boundary for the next stage

The reduction from arity `k` to arity two uses **additional helper labels**. It therefore changes the internal law/state alphabet even though it preserves the intended raw conjunction consequence.

Stage 136 must separate:

- derivation depth;
- rule premise arity;
- auxiliary/helper-state dimension.

Without that separation, this supplement could be misread as saying depth alone always reduces arity.

## 6. Prior-art boundary

Compiling multi-input conjunctions through binary helper gates is classical circuit/Horn logic. No generic novelty claim is made. The P025 contribution is the exact precision-resource separation and the explicit warning that direct circuit arity and iterative rule arity live under different runtime/state contracts.
