# P019 Supplement 24 — Smith Quantum of the Weighted Relation Map and an Intrinsic Precision Scale

Status: `RESEARCH WIP / INTEGER-LATTICE STRUCTURE PROVED`

## 1. Goal

The weighted relation field

\[
Z_{ij}=m_jc_i-m_ic_j
\]

inherits common divisibility from block capacities. This supplement upgrades that observation into an exact integer-lattice statement about the whole relation map and its behavior under partition coarsening.

## 2. Relation map

Fix positive integer capacities

\[
m=(m_1,\ldots,m_k)^T.
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{
\phi_m:\mathbb Z^k\to\bigwedge^2\mathbb Z^k,
\qquad
\phi_m(c)=c\wedge m.
}
\]

In the standard pair basis, `phi_m(c)` has coordinates `Z_ij`.

## 3. Capacity gcd and primitive capacity vector

Let

\[
\boxed{g=\gcd(m_1,\ldots,m_k)}
\]

and write

\[
\boxed{m=g\hat m,\qquad\gcd(\hat m_i)=1.}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\phi_m=g\phi_{\hat m}.}
\]

Every weighted relation entry is therefore divisible by `g`. A particular state may have an even larger entry gcd; `g` is the structural quantum forced by the capacity pattern, not the accidental gcd of one sample field.

## 4. P019-X84 — Every nonzero Smith invariant of the relation map is `g`

Because `hat m` is primitive, a unimodular basis change sends it to `e_1`. Under the corresponding domain and exterior-square codomain basis changes, `phi_hatm` becomes

\[
c\mapsto c\wedge e_1.
\]

Its kernel is `Z e_1`, its image is freely generated by `e_i wedge e_1` for `i>=2`, and all nonzero Smith invariants are one. Multiplying by `g` yields

\[
\boxed{
SNF_{nonzero}(\phi_m)=(g,\ldots,g)
}
\]

with `k-1` nonzero invariant factors.

Thus

\[
\boxed{rank\,\phi_m=k-1}
\]

and every independent relation direction carries the same structural integer quantum `g`.

Smith normal form and unimodular reduction of primitive vectors are established integer linear algebra; P019 makes no general priority claim.

## 5. P019-X85 — Equal relation fields differ by a primitive-capacity shift

If `phi_m(c')=phi_m(c)`, then `(c'-c) wedge m=0`. Since `hat m` is primitive,

\[
\boxed{\ker\phi_m=\mathbb Z\hat m.}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{c'=c+t\hat m,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z.}
\]

This is the exact translation ambiguity of the tree-independent relation field.

## 6. P019-X86 — Field-preserving grand-total period

Let total capacity be

\[
M=\sum_i m_i.
\]

The primitive field-preserving shift `c -> c+hat m` changes grand total by

\[
\boxed{\tau=M/g.}
\]

Thus all grand totals compatible with one fixed relation field lie in one arithmetic progression `C=C_0+t tau`, and

\[
\boxed{g\tau=M.}
\]

`g` and `tau` are dual integer scales induced by capacities.

## 7. P019-X87 — Relation quantum can only stay the same or coarsen under partition sums

Every coarse capacity is a sum of fine capacities, so the fine gcd divides every coarse capacity:

\[
\boxed{g_{fine}\mid g_{coarse}.}
\]

Along a coarsening chain, relation quantum is monotone in divisibility. Since `M` is fixed, translation periods satisfy the reverse divisibility

\[
\boxed{\tau_{coarse}\mid\tau_{fine}.}
\]

## 8. Primitive relation state plus scale tag

Since

\[
m=g\hat m,\qquad Z=g\hat Z,
\]

a current relation state can be represented as

\[
\boxed{(g;\hat m,\hat Z,C),\qquad\gcd(\hat m)=1.}
\]

Here `g` is a relation-precision scale, `(hat m,hat Z)` is primitive relation structure, and `C` selects the translation coset.

## 9. P019-X88/X89 — Partition coarsening produces multiplicative integer scale carry

Coarsen primitive capacities/relations by partition `A`:

\[
\tilde m=A\hat m,
\qquad
\tilde Z=A\hat Z A^T.
\]

Let

\[
\boxed{h=\gcd(\tilde m).}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{g'=gh,}
\qquad
\boxed{\hat m'=\tilde m/h,}
\qquad
\boxed{\hat Z'=\tilde Z/h.}
\]

All divisions are exact. Along a coarsening chain with carries `h_1,...,h_r`,

\[
\boxed{g_r=g_0\prod_t h_t.}
\]

Thus coarsening can be read as primitive relation quotient plus an integer scale carry, with no continuous renormalization.

## 10. Interface with P018 precision

This gives a concrete finite-precision chain

\[
\boxed{
\text{partition}
\to
\text{capacity gcd }g
\to
\text{relation quantum}.
}
\]

Refinement may reduce a common capacity factor and expose finer relation quantum; coarsening cannot create finer quantum. This does not replace P018's general precision lattice, but supplies a geometric integer example generated by the relation structure itself.
