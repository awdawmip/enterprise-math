## 2. Numerical semigroups

Rosales and García-Sánchez's monograph *Numerical Semigroups* is registered as the main prior-art reference for finitely generated numerical semigroups, gaps, Frobenius-type structure, Apéry sets, and conductors [SRC-ROSALES-GARCIA-SANCHEZ-2009-NUMERICAL-SEMIGROUPS].

P024 therefore does **not** claim as inventions:

- finitely generated additive submonoids of the nonnegative integers;
- normalization by a gcd;
- numerical-semigroup gaps;
- Apéry sets;
- Frobenius numbers;
- conductors;
- eventual co-finiteness after gcd-one normalization.

The P024 use is narrower: for a one-sided integer translation language, reachable cumulative displacements are a numerical semigroup, and its gaps correspond exactly to missing translated observation boundaries in the predictive precision partition.

## 3. Bézout, group completion, and cyclic groups

Bézout identities, gcd-generated subgroups of the integers, finite-group element orders, subgroup closure under inverses, and the classification of subgroups of a finite cyclic group are standard algebra.

P024 does **not** claim these results.

The project-specific statement under test is their precision consequence: a genuinely two-sided integer action monoid equals its additive group completion, while a forward-generated monoid inside a finite cyclic phase group automatically becomes a subgroup.  Hence the same action magnitudes can retain semigroup holes on an unbounded ordered chain but lose those holes after finite periodicization.

## 4. Predictive equivalence and minimization

Generic future-behavior equivalence and finite-state partition refinement are already registered under E002/P023 through Moore-style sequential-machine distinguishability and Hopcroft-style automaton minimization.

P024 does not re-claim those algorithms or abstractions.  P023 remains the owner of the generic statement that a declared future operation/observation language determines a coarsest future-safe quotient.

P024 only derives an explicit arithmetic form for one-dimensional integer translation systems with ordered threshold observations.
