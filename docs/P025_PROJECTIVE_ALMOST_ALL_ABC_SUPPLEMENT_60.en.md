## 1. A projective success implies a radical power bound once the exponents separate

Fix

\[
M>1.
\]

Choose

\[
0<\eta<1-\frac1M.
\]

On every triple satisfying `PCC_eta`, the c-oriented projective term gives

\[
\frac{c}{R(S(a)+S(b))}
<c^\eta.
\]

Hence

\[
R
>
\frac{c^{1-\eta}}{S(a)+S(b)}.
\]

Using the elementary bound from Supplement 46,

\[
S(a)+S(b)
<
\frac{\log c}{\log2},
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
R
>
(\log2)\frac{c^{1-\eta}}{\log c}.
}
\]

Because

\[
1-\eta>1/M,
\]

the ratio

\[
(\log2)\frac{c^{1-\eta-1/M}}{\log c}
\]

tends to infinity. Therefore, beyond a constant depending on `M,eta`,

\[
\boxed{R>c^{1/M},}
\]

or equivalently

\[
\boxed{c<R^M.}
\]

Thus every sufficiently large Oesterle-M failure must be a PCC_eta failure.
