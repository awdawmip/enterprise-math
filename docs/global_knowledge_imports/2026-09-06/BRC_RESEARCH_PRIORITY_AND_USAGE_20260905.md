## 1. Priority

BRC is a first-line structural research method and a preferred bridge between Enterprise coordinates, algebra, discrete computation and observable selection. Do not leave it as an optional afterthought or mention its name without applying its laws.

`PROBLEM_SELECTED -> BRC_APPLICABILITY_AND_OBSERVER_AUDIT -> NARROWEST_ADEQUATE_REUSE_OR_EXPLICIT_BOUNDARY -> SUBSTANTIVE_WORK`.

This is priority of consideration and actual typed reuse, not compulsory reduction of every problem to positive branch mass. When BRC is inapplicable, state the exact missing carrier or operation and continue with adequate methods; do not construct artificial branching. In legal, health and other non-mathematical work, domain evidence and governing rules remain primary; BRC analogies are never substitute evidence.

## 2. Required use points

Apply the BRC gate after an exact question is selected; before introducing a general-purpose mechanism; before discarding branches/provenance by a quotient, normalization, average or total-only summary; at a substantive bottleneck or cross-branch synthesis; and before claiming a proof closes an infinite-scale problem.

At a bottleneck, retrace the existing route once with the current relevant BRC interfaces. Preserve verified prior work rather than rerunning it as new discovery. Record the information retained, the information erased and the exact next unresolved mathematical unit. Repeated name-only retracing is not progress.

## 3. Information and observer discipline

Declare the population, branch identity, alternative/serial composition, observed outputs, allowed future operations and scale/precision horizon before compression.

Keep distinct: Boolean support; counts; positive total/dominant mass; exact-weight histogram; prime-valuation data; moment/jet data; labeled provenance and ports; signed or complex amplitudes. Use the smallest sufficient carrier, not the smallest convenient carrier.

A total-only result can hide a scientifically important fiber contrast. Before using a coarser observer, prove factorization/fiber constancy for the intended output and future operations, or retain an explicit repair coordinate. Port collapse is permitted only under its actual interface lease; hidden-state access invalidates a port-only equivalence.

Integer and positive-rational arithmetic, finite valuation vectors and symbolic identities should be retained before logarithmic/analytic readout when they are adequate. LN is a derived observer, not a new primitive state. Exact symbolic representation does not by itself imply low bit complexity.

## 4. Reuse must be real

Read exact current sources from `awdawmip/enterprise-math` only as needed. Follow `tool_invocation_policy.json` and the current source control precedence.

Every relevant match receives a concrete resolution: `REUSE_APPLIED`, `REUSE_EXECUTED`, `COMPOSE_APPLIED`, `EXTEND_EXISTING_TOOL`, `REUSE_IDENTIFIED_EXECUTION_UNAVAILABLE`, `CAPABILITY_GAP_CONFIRMED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Lookup is not execution. For a theorem interface, write and apply the precise input/output law. If a needed adequate implementation is available, execute it unchanged rather than silently reimplementing it. An unavailable execution environment is not a mathematical capability gap. New jets, observers or applications normally extend an existing family; they do not automatically create a new top-level BRC family.

## 5. Non-negotiable boundaries

`POSITIVE_WEIGHTED_BRC != SIGNED_OR_PHASE_CANCELLATION`.

`COUNT_AT_MOMENT_ZERO != BOOLEAN_SUPPORT_PRESERVES_MULTIPLICITY`.

`FINITE_RECURRENT_CERTIFICATE != UNIFORM_INFINITE_SCALE_BOUND`.

`POSITIVE_SEMIDEFINITE_GRAM != OFFDIAGONAL_NONPOSITIVITY`.

`FULL_PERIOD_ORTHOGONALITY != LOCAL_WINDOW_CANCELLATION`.

`NONNEGATIVE_VARIANCE != PROVED_DISSIPATION_OR_MARTINGALE`.

`FINITE_IDENTITY_OR_NUMERICAL_CERTIFICATE != ASYMPTOTIC_THEOREM`.

`RH_EQUIVALENT_REFORMULATION != RH_PROOF`.

A claimed martingale/refinement law must identify a common probability space, nested sigma-algebras or a proved transport law, and an actual conditional-expectation relation. Changing populations, measures, ports or normalization produces a defect that must be retained and estimated.

A finite stability/gauge certificate used over growing systems requires uniform constants and admissible inter-scale embeddings. Taking absolute values, squaring early, discarding provenance or passing to totals must not silently erase the cancellation being studied.
