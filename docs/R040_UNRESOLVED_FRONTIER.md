5. Decide whether `s=4`, nonsquare `r>=5` rounded-dilation limits admit a standard beta-expansion or other classical exact identification.
6. If a full dimension theorem is pursued, separate the roles of no integer-state recoalescence, asymptotic cylinder separation, and exact-hit pruning; do not infer geometric separation from cardinality alone.
