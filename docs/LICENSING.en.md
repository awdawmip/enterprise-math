## Why there is no translated legal license

Enterprise Math maintains canonical research prose in parallel English and Chinese versions. The legal license is treated differently to avoid creating two texts that could be interpreted as competing legal grants.

Chinese documentation may explain the license, but it does not replace or modify `LICENSE`.
