## 1. Conditional family

Assume

\[
\boxed{
1+2r=3^m,
\qquad
r=\frac{3^m-1}{2}\text{ prime}.
}
\]

This is a conditional family theorem: it applies to every exponent for which the displayed repunit is prime. It does **not** assume or claim that infinitely many such exponents exist.

Because `2r` is squarefree, P025-T17 gives immediately

\[
\boxed{\eta_{\min}=m.}
\]

The new point is that the exact floor-access radius `nu` also has a closed formula.

## 2. P025-T34 — the exponent must be prime

If

\[
r=(3^m-1)/2
\]

is prime, then `m` is prime.

### Proof

If `m=ab` with `a,b>1`, then

\[
3^m-1=(3^a)^b-1
\]

has the nontrivial factor `3^a-1`. After dividing by `3-1=2`, the base-3 repunit

\[
\frac{3^m-1}{2}
=1+3+\cdots+3^{m-1}
\]

also factors nontrivially through the standard repunit factorization. Hence primality forces `m` prime. ∎

Thus `m` is odd. If `m>3`, then

\[
m\equiv1\text{ or }5\pmod6.
\]

## 3. Floor-access equation

Supplement 10, with `q=2` and the other prime equal to `r`, reduces the floor witness to

\[
\boxed{
r u+2v=m3^{m-1},
\qquad
x_3=1.
}
\]

Since `r` and the target are odd, every solution has

\[
\boxed{u\equiv1\pmod2.}
\]

All solutions therefore move through the odd integers in the `u` coordinate, with `v` determined by

\[
v=\frac{m3^{m-1}-ru}{2}.
\]

The real balance ratio is

\[
\frac{m3^{m-1}}r
=
\frac{2m3^{m-1}}{3^m-1},
\]

which lies only slightly above `2m/3`.

## 4. P025-T35 — exact closed witness and radius

The minimum-`L_infinity` floor witness is obtained by the closest admissible odd `u` to the balance ratio.

### Case `m=3`

\[
r=13,
\qquad
(u,v)=(3,-6),
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\nu=6.}
\]

### Case `m>3`, `m congruent 1 mod 6`

Set

\[
\boxed{u=\frac{2m+1}{3}.}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
v=\frac{2m+1-3^m}{12}.}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\nu
=
\frac{3^m-(2m+1)}{12}.
}
\]

### Case `m>3`, `m congruent 5 mod 6`

Set

\[
\boxed{u=\frac{2m-1}{3}.}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
v=\frac{3^m+2m-1}{12},}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
\nu
=
\frac{3^m+2m-1}{12}.
}
\]

The complete floor witness in coordinate order `(2,r,3)` is `(u,v,1)`.

### Proof of optimality

The parity condition forces `u` to range over odd integers. The chosen `u` is the odd integer nearest the real ratio `H/r`, where `H=m3^(m-1)`.

Changing to any other admissible `u` changes it by at least `2`, hence changes `v` by an integer multiple of `r`. The chosen residue satisfies `|v|<=r/2`; every other admissible solution has

\[
|v'|\ge r-|v|\ge|v|.
\]

For `m=3`, direct inspection gives `|v|=6>=|u|=3`. For prime `m>3`, the displayed closed formulas give `|v|>|u|`. Therefore the chosen solution minimizes `max(|u|,|v|)`, and its maximum coordinate is exactly the stated `nu`. ∎

## 6. Strong separation of arithmetic and geometric certificate cost

Within this conditional family,

\[
\eta_{\min}=m
\]

grows only linearly with the exponent, while for every prime `m>3` in the family

\[
\nu
=
\frac{3^m+O(m)}{12}.
\]

Thus the arithmetic obstruction floor and the geometric cost of accessing that floor can live on radically different scales.

This does not prove an unbounded-family statement because the infinitude of base-3 repunit primes is not assumed. It gives an exact formula for every actual member and shows the scale separation already strongly in the known working examples above.

## 7. Why this matters to P025

This family is an unusually clean calibration of the four witness quantities:

\[
\text{support/valuation obstruction}
\to
\eta_{\min}
\to
\text{floor-access geometry}
\to
\nu.
\]

The two costs are generated by the same relation but are not comparable by one scalar precision rule.

In particular, a small arithmetic redundancy target does not imply a nearby certificate in the prime-coordinate lattice.

## 8. Prior-art discipline

Repunit factorization, the fact that a prime repunit requires prime exponent, parity constraints, and two-variable Diophantine minimization are classical mathematics.

P025 claims no novelty for those ingredients. The project-specific role is to use this family as an exact certificate-precision calibration and to preserve the separation between `eta_min` and `nu`.
