## 1. Why the Pair layer is not yet the full spectrum

Supplement 14 writes deterministic time as a monotone kernel chain:

\[
K_0\subseteq K_1\subseteq K_2\subseteq\cdots,
\qquad
K_n=\kerpair(F^{[n]}).
\]

The new binary pairs

\[
K_{n+1}\setminus K_n
\]

say exactly which history pairs become indistinguishable for the first time at step `n+1`.

But the P011 collision polynomial

\[
K_F(t)=\sum_y\big((1+t)^{m_F(y)}-1\big)
\]

records history subsets of every cardinality `k`, not just pairs.

The question is therefore whether one deterministic coarsening of a kernel partition directly determines the newly created collision spectrum in every degree. It does, by finite integer formulas.

## 2. Old-block decomposition of one new fiber

Suppose one new fiber `A` at step `n+1` is the disjoint union of old fibers:

\[
A=A_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup A_r,
\qquad
|A_i|=m_i,
\]

with total size

\[
M=\sum_{i=1}^r m_i.
\]

Before merging, these blocks contribute

\[
\sum_{i=1}^r\big((1+t)^{m_i}-1\big)
\]

to the collision polynomial. After merging, they contribute

\[
(1+t)^M-1.
\]

## 3. P018-T137 — Exact time increment of one fiber merge

Status: `PROVED / EXECUTABLE`

Define the new collision polynomial generated by that merge:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_A(t)
=
(1+t)^M-1
-
\sum_{i=1}^r\big((1+t)^{m_i}-1\big).
}
\]

Its degree-`k` coefficient is

\[
\boxed{
[t^k]\Delta_A(t)
=
\binom{M}{k}
-
\sum_{i=1}^r\binom{m_i}{k}.
}
\]

### Combinatorial meaning

`C(M,k)` counts all `k`-history subsets in the new fiber `A`. Those already colliding before this step are exactly the subsets lying entirely inside one old fiber `A_i`, counted by

\[
\sum_i\binom{m_i}{k}.
\]

The difference therefore counts exactly the `k`-history subsets that become contained in one collision class for the first time at step `n+1` and that cross at least two old fibers.

Thus a P011 spectral increment can be interpreted as a count of new higher-order indistinguishability events at one finite time step.

## 4. P018-T138 — The quadratic coefficient is exactly the number of history pairs newly entering the kernel

Status: `PROVED / EXECUTABLE`

For `k=2`,

\[
[t^2]\Delta_A(t)
=
\binom{M}{2}
-
\sum_i\binom{m_i}{2}.
\]

The elementary identity gives

\[
\boxed{
[t^2]\Delta_A(t)
=
\sum_{1\le i<j\le r}m_i m_j.
}
\]

The right side counts unordered pairs chosen from different old fibers. Such pairs were off the kernel at step `n` and enter one common new fiber at step `n+1`. Hence

\[
\boxed{
[t^2]\Delta_A(t)
=
\#\{\text{history pairs newly entering the diagonal/kernel at this step}\}.
}
\]

This makes the Supplement 12 Pair/kernel substrate and the quadratic P011 collision observable literally the same time-resolved event count, rather than merely analogous concepts.

## 5. P018-T139 — General degree `k` is the higher-order kernel-event lift

Status: `PROVED STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION`

For every `k>=2`,

\[
[t^k]\Delta_A(t)

counts `k`-history subsets that first become members of one new partition block at step `n+1`.

Define the time-resolved higher-order event count

E_{n+1,k}

Pair/kernel is the weakest relational substrate at `k=2`; all P011 `J_k` are higher combinatorial lifts of the same partition-coarsening process.

## 6. P018-T140 — The full step polynomial increment is the sum over new fibers

Status: `PROVED / EXECUTABLE`

Let the step-`n+1` new fibers be `B`, each a union of old fibers. Define

\[
\Delta_{n+1}(t)
:=
K_{F^{[n+1]}}(t)-K_{F^{[n]}}(t).
\]

Then exactly

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{n+1}(t)
=
\sum_B\Delta_B(t),
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{n+1}(t)\in\mathbb N[t].
}
\]

This refines P011 coefficientwise monotonicity: every nonnegative integer coefficient gained at a step has the direct interpretation of a newly formed higher-order history collision at that time.

## 7. P018-T141 — Time increments telescope exactly

Status: `PROVED / EXECUTABLE`

For every finite time `N`,

\[
\boxed{
K_{F^{[N]}}(t)-K_{id}(t)
=
\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\Delta_{n+1}(t).
}
\]

More generally, for `a<b`,

\[
\boxed{
K_{F^{[b]}}(t)-K_{F^{[a]}}(t)
=
\sum_{n=a}^{b-1}\Delta_{n+1}(t).
}
\]

This is finite telescoping and requires no infinite series or limiting process.

Combining Supplement 14 finite saturation with

\[
N_H=\max_{x\in H}s(x)
\]

on a finite observation set `H` gives

\[
\boxed{
K_{S|_H}(t)-K_{id_H}(t)
=
\sum_{n=0}^{N_H-1}\Delta_{n+1,H}(t).
}
\]

The final irreversibility spectrum is therefore a finite exact sum of time-local collision events.

## 9. Important boundary: the polynomial still does not recover labelled history

Even the complete sequence of step increments `\Delta_n(t)` need not identify which specific histories merged. The polynomial records block-size statistics and higher-order counts, not the full labelled partition.

The hierarchy remains:

1. **kernel relation / partition** — labelled indistinguishability structure;
2. **collision polynomial** — higher-order integer statistics of the fiber-size multiset;
3. **step increment polynomial** — the statistics newly created at each time.

P011 already proves that the collision polynomial is complete for fiber-size multiplicities, not for labelled partitions. This stage preserves that boundary.
