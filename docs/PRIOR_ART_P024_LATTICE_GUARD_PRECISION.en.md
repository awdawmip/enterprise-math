# Prior Art — P024 Lattice Guard Precision

## 1. Scope

P024 Supplement 01 combines established hyperplane-arrangement and affine-semigroup mathematics with the Enterprise Math future-compatible precision interpretation.

Historical novelty of the combined interpretation remains `NOVELTY_UNVERIFIED`.

## 2. Hyperplane arrangements

Thomas Zaslavsky's *Facing up to Arrangements: Face-Count Formulas for Partitions of Space by Hyperplanes* is a standard prior source for the combinatorics of spaces partitioned by finite hyperplane arrangements. [SRC-ZASLAVSKY-1975-HYPERPLANE-ARRANGEMENTS]

P024 therefore does **not** claim as inventions:

- finite affine hyperplane arrangements;
- cells/faces induced by hyperplane cuts;
- classical region/face counting theory;
- the general fact that linear threshold sign data is constant on arrangement cells.

P024 uses this language only after the future action system has determined which translated integer guard boundaries are actually relevant.

## 3. Affine semigroups, saturation, and holes

Hemmecke, Takemura, and Yoshida explicitly study commutative semigroups generated by finite vector sets in `Z^d`, their saturation, and the set of holes between a semigroup and its saturation; their paper also exhibits an infinite family of holes in a transportation semigroup. [SRC-HEMMECKE-TAKEMURA-YOSHIDA-2009-AFFINE-HOLES]

P024 therefore does **not** claim as inventions:

- affine semigroups generated by integer vectors;
- saturation/normalization-style completion inside a rational cone and generated group;
- holes of affine semigroups;
- the possibility that a higher-dimensional affine semigroup has infinitely many holes;
- conductor/saturation-point questions for affine semigroups.

The explicit small witness

`<(2,0),(0,1),(1,1)>`

is used only to show why the one-dimensional numerical-semigroup finite-hole picture cannot be promoted unchanged to higher-dimensional action monoids.

## 4. Integer lattices and group completion

Integer lattice images, gcd/Bezout arguments, subgroup generation, positive integer relations, Hermite/Smith normal forms, and integer linear feasibility are established mathematics.

The criterion

`N A = Z A iff the generators admit a strictly positive integer zero relation`

is presented in P024 with an elementary proof because it is the exact boundary needed by the action-language interpretation; no historical priority is claimed for the algebraic fact itself.

The A3 relation-quotient research route already studies hidden guard-image lattices and exact reachability of linear-threshold branch patterns inside coarse fibers, including standard hyperplane-arrangement complexity bounds.

P023 remains the owner of the general statement that a declared future operation/observation language determines a coarsest future-safe equivalence.
