## 1. Mandatory project-level result scale

Every mature result package must receive one leading level and may list a separately typed candidate upgrade.

### L0 — new representation

A new coordinate display, encoding, indexing, diagram, state storage scheme, or computational presentation whose mathematical information is already recoverable from a known object.

### L1 — classical-equivalent normal form

An exact reformulation, reduction, compact state, or normal form whose theorem content is classically equivalent at the stated scope. Implementation convenience may be real, but classical attribution is controlling.

### L2 — native invariant or interpretive compression

A project-native invariant, self-localizing readout, relation structure, finite obstruction, or explanatory compression that is not merely a cosmetic display, while making no unproved claim of stronger classical distribution, complexity, or theorem power.

### L3 — transferable tool

A method, operator, certificate calculus, or interface demonstrated to transfer across distinct Enterprise problem families beyond one theorem-local use. Existing Toolbox ownership and composition must be checked before promotion.

### L4 — complexity or theorem-strength improvement

A proved asymptotic, complexity, relation-yield-per-cost, theorem-strength, or information-strength improvement over a fair and explicitly matched baseline.

Freeze:

`NEW REPRESENTATION != NEW MATHEMATICAL INFORMATION`.

`CLASSICAL EQUIVALENCE != L4 IMPROVEMENT`.

`FINITE SUCCESS != COMPLEXITY GAIN`.

`PACKAGE PASS != AUTOMATIC TOOL PROMOTION`.
