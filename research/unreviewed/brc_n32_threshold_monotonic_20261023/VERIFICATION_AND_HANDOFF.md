## Second N32 theorem: binary multi-pulse absorbing no-gain zone
Companion BINARY_ABSORPTION_THEOREM.md proves the exact count law for every
nonadaptive {0,2} word with s>=1 zeros and r>=1 pulses, with final δ depending
only on (s,r). It then proves:
- F(s,r+1)-F(s,r)<=0 for r>=1; for r>=2 the upper bound is
  -4/3^(s+r+1)<0.
- F(s+1,r)-F(s,r)<=-1/3^(s+r+1)<0 for r>=2.
Thus after the second 2, if at least one 0 preceded it, each later
increment Δδ is nonpositive. Consequently all later residual increments
