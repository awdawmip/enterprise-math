## 0. Governing rule

Publication language must distinguish:

`THEOREM_TRUTH != INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_MODE != LEAN_COVERAGE != HISTORICAL_NOVELTY != PRESENTATION_VALUE`.

No stronger claim may be inferred from a weaker category.

## 1. Package-level contribution claims

### Allowed

- `Prime Fusion presents a coherent structural interface joining classical Gaussian and Eisenstein norm channels, low-order cyclotomic factors, CRT quotient structure, idempotent/phase readouts, reciprocity constraints, and finite corridor consequences.`
- `The package contains fifteen retained theorem rows whose frozen statements have been independently audited with explicitly typed evidence.`
- `The publication contribution is primarily structural organization, typed readouts, evidence discipline, and a bounded Lean-checked finite-algebra kernel.`
- `Most theorem rows are standard consequences, classical compositions, or project-specific repackagings.`
- `T9 and T14 are exact combinations for which this review did not locate a reliable exact antecedent; novelty is not established.`

### Forbidden or unsupported

- `Prime Fusion proves fifteen new theorems.`
- `Prime Fusion is a new number system replacing Gaussian/Eisenstein arithmetic.`
- `Prime Fusion establishes a new general theory of primes.`
- `Prime Fusion is historically novel.`
- `T9/T14 are first-known results.`
- `No prior art exists for T9/T14.`

Reason: absence of an exact hit is not proof of priority or novelty.

## 4. T10 mandatory scope guard

Every publication statement of T10 must explicitly define

`M_{p,q}={x mod pq : x^2+1=0 mod p and x^2+x+1=0 mod q}`.

The four-phase statement is permitted only for this channel-oriented mixed locus:

`M_{p,q}={r,r^5,r^7,r^11}`.

The publication must retain the negative control:

for `H=91`, the oriented locus has four elements

`{18,44,60,86}`,

while the full root set of

`(x^2+1)(x^2+x+1)`

modulo `91` has eight elements

`{9,16,18,44,60,74,81,86}`.

### Allowed

`On the channel-oriented mixed locus, the four CRT phases form the stated order-12 unit orbit.`

`The H=91 example shows that the complete fused-root set can be strictly larger than the oriented locus.`

### Forbidden

`The fused polynomial has exactly four roots modulo pq.`

`{r,r^5,r^7,r^11} is the complete root set of F modulo pq.`

`T10 classifies all roots of the fused polynomial.`
