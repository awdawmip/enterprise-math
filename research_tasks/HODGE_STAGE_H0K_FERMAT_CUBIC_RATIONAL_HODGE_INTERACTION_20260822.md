`X_F = {x0^3+x1^3+x2^3+x3^3+x4^3+x5^3=0} subset P^5_C`.

`G = (mu_3)^6 / Delta(mu_3)`

act diagonally on `X_F`.

The character group is represented by tuples

`alpha=(a0,...,a5) in (Z/3Z)^6`

with

`a0+...+a5=0 mod 3`.

For primitive middle cohomology only the nonzero-coordinate characters are relevant:

`a_i in {1,2}` for all `i`.

Define

`|alpha| = (sum_i <a_i>)/3`

with `<a_i> in {1,2}`.

The researcher must independently derive/replay the Fermat eigenspace/Hodge-type criterion from the declared symmetry/residue source. Do not merely paste a theorem statement.

- each relevant primitive character eigenspace over `C` is one-dimensional;
- the Hodge type is determined by `|alpha|`;
- for dimension `4`, `(2,2)` occurs exactly at the appropriate middle value;
- rational Hodge blocks are obtained from Galois-stable character orbits under `(Z/3Z)^*={1,2}`.

These are source/control facts from classical Fermat Hodge theory. They may be cited, but the finite carrier used below must be explicitly constructed and checked.
