# FQ010 — Line-Scale Semantic Admission and Comparative Refoundation

Task-ID: `RS-FQ010-LINE-SCALE-SEMANTIC-ADMISSION-COMPARATIVE-REFOUNDATION`

Foundation Question: `FQ-20260823-010-LINE-SCALE-SEMANTIC-ADMISSION`

Lineage: `CONTINUATION` from R065 Phase A.

Intended owner branch:

`research/fq010-line-scale-semantic-admission`

## 0. Scope and provenance

This task begins **after** R065's clean Phase-A freeze. It is not blind discovery.

R065 evidence must remain typed exactly:

- clean independent evidence supports the same-component/type equivalence relation at relation strength;
- the cardinality of that relation is mathematically definable in the frozen R065 construction language;
- R065 did not uniquely select that cardinality among all intrinsic scalars;
- R065 did not assign any scalar the semantic role of line magnitude, length, norm, or squared scale.

Do not rewrite post-freeze comparison as though it had occurred inside blind Phase A.

This task may compare the declared inputs directly.

## 1. Hard target

Classify:

`LINE_SCALE_SEMANTIC_ADMISSION_COMPARATIVE_REFOUNDATION_CLASSIFIED`.

The task must decide whether the relation/readout chain

`typed component content -> same-component relation -> finite cardinality -> squared-line-scale role`

should be admitted as a Foundation refoundation of the current sector scalar, and whether it should **replace**, **refactor**, **coexist with**, or **fail to displace** the FQ008 scalar-first interface.

A numerical equality proof alone is insufficient.

## 2. Freeze the two compared interfaces

### Interface K — relation/readout formulation

From the R065 accepted relation, write

`R_type={(x,y): tau(x)=tau(y)}`.

At relation strength, determine whether this is exactly the kernel equivalence relation of the already-supplied component typing and prove the strongest choice-independence / relabeling statement justified by the current base.

Define only at readout strength:

`Q_K := |R_type|`.

For component multiplicities `(n_c)`, derive rather than assume its finite formula.

Do not call `Q_K` squared line scale until the semantic-admission section explicitly evaluates that role assignment.

### Interface F — FQ008 scalar-first formulation

Use the exact integration-candidate definition at source `b6ec6eb58f1c724657db7c5bd7deb82827774439`.

Its sector scalar interface consists of:

- one-dimensional axis square calibration;
- local transverse scalar independence;
- derived sector sum-of-squares.

Treat its current source status separately from its theorem content.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_TWO_INTERFACE_FREEZE.md`.

## 3. Relation canonicity and maximal-preservation theorem

Prove or falsify, at the exact semantic strength available, the statement:

> among equivalence relations on the finite unit-token carrier through whose quotient the exact component observation still factors, `R_type` is the unique greatest relation.

Separate:

- existence of the component observation;
- definability of `R_type`;
- maximality among component-preserving equivalence relations;
- invariance under token renaming;
- equivariance under component relabeling;
- whether any orientation, metric, current line formula, or scalar target is used.

If the theorem needs a stronger hypothesis than the current base, state the weakest missing hypothesis.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_RELATION_ADMISSIBILITY_AND_MAXIMALITY.md`.

## 4. Cardinality readout and valuation theorem

Classify the step

`R_type -> |R_type|`

separately from relation definability.

At minimum determine:

1. whether finite cardinality is a canonical N2 readout of the relation object;
2. the exact formula for `|R_type|` in terms of component multiplicities;
3. whether cardinality is uniquely selected among finite relation valuations under any minimal package such as isomorphism invariance, disjoint-union additivity, and unit normalization;
4. which of those valuation conditions are mathematical conventions/definitions, which are extra semantic conditions, and which are actually needed for the proposed line-scale role;
5. whether another scalar readout of the same relation competes at the same claimed semantic strength.

Do not infer semantic scale meaning merely from uniqueness of cardinality as a valuation.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_CARDINALITY_READOUT_CLASSIFICATION.md`.

## 5. Exact theorem equivalence with FQ008

Prove both directions on the declared sector domain.

### K -> F

From `Q_K` alone, determine whether one obtains:

- axis square calibration;
- vanishing mixed second difference / transverse independence;
- sector sum-of-squares.

### F -> K-scalar

From the FQ008 scalar axioms, determine whether the scalar field is uniquely equal to `Q_K` on every sector-supported content.

Then state precisely what does **not** follow in the reverse direction. In particular, determine whether scalar equality reconstructs the relation object `R_type` or only its numerical cardinality.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_FQ008_EQUIVALENCE_AND_INFORMATION_STRENGTH.md`.

## 6. Information-strength comparison

Classify the information order among:

1. primitive typed content;
2. `R_type` as a relation/partition object;
3. `Q_K=|R_type|` as a scalar;
4. the FQ008 scalar field `Q`.

Provide explicit collisions showing information loss where applicable.

For example, if two inequivalent component partitions can share the same scalar value, preserve that witness and state what relation-level information the scalar cannot reconstruct.

The task must distinguish:

`same scalar theorem output`

from

`same relational information content`.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_INFORMATION_STRENGTH_CLASSIFICATION.md`.

## 7. Primitive and semantic budget comparison

This is theorem-critical.

Build a dependency DAG for each interface and count/classify **typed assumptions**, not prose clauses.

For Interface F classify at least:

- component/sector substrate already present;
- scalar object `Q`;
- axis square calibration;
- transverse independence;
- semantic identification with squared native line scale.

For Interface K classify at least:

- component typing already present;
- derived `R_type`;
- cardinality readout;
- semantic identification of that readout with squared native line scale.

Determine whether K gives:

- genuine reduction of primitive assumptions;
- ontological compression but theorem-equivalent scalar content;
- mere notational packaging;
- or an increase in hidden assumptions.

A statement such as “one formula instead of two” is not a proof of compression.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_PRIMITIVE_SEMANTIC_BUDGET_COMPARISON.md`.

## 8. Observation-resolution countermodels

Pressure-test the semantic choice of stopping at component identity.

Construct and classify at least:

- a finer observation retaining occurrence identity;
- the component-typed observation;
- a coarser observation forgetting component identity.

For each, classify the corresponding canonical relation and natural finite cardinal readouts.

Use these to decide exactly which part of the proposed squared-scale role is forced by N0 structure and which part is an N2 observation-resolution choice.

Do not treat the existence of alternative observation resolutions as an automatic rejection; determine whether the line semantics independently singles out one resolution without circular use of the current scalar.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_OBSERVATION_RESOLUTION_COUNTERMODELS.md`.

## 9. Explanatory-compression calibration

- distinction between transition/word length and native line scale;
- axis scaling;
- two-component sector additivity/transverse independence;
- preservation of component provenance needed by current trace semantics.

Classify each comparison as exact recovery, domain-restricted recovery, unresolved, or nonrecovery.

Do not claim explanatory superiority merely because the target formula is recovered.

Required artifact:

`research_results/FQ010/FQ010_EXPLANATORY_COMPRESSION_CALIBRATION.md`.
