# N-coupled CRT product-naturality break without scalar support exposure

## Hard target

`CRT_PRODUCT_NATURALITY_BREAK_WITHOUT_SCALAR_SUPPORT_EXPOSURE_CONSTRUCTED_OR_DECLARED_NONRING_GRAMMAR_OBSTRUCTED`.

Start from public `N` and a declared factor-blind initial state. Freeze one exact non-ring grammar whose semantics are not reducible to a fixed polynomial/rational term in `Z/NZ`.

Admissible candidate state types include public history/context, canonical integer representatives, quotient/remainder or carry state, public completion rules, and variable-time evolution. The grammar must be finite and exact enough to audit symbolically.

A positive result must exhibit a state/operator observable that is singular or nonunit on exactly one hidden CRT channel and nonsingular on the other, while every declared scalar projection before that selective-collapse layer has gcd `1` or `N` with `N`.

A no-go result must be exact for the frozen grammar and identify the smallest missing semantic capability; it must not generalize beyond the declared class.

## Mandatory guards

- No hidden `p`/`q`, candidate-prime schedule, factor-aware selector or manually injected idempotent.
- No branch whose predicate already yields a proper factor by gcd.
- No Pollard p-1 / Williams p+1 / generic order-annihilation rename.
- No generic rho/collision or congruence-of-squares rename.
- No finite hit-rate or benchmark score as theorem evidence.
- The accepted `G_poly-loc` N-erasure/determinantal-support theorem remains a regression firewall.

## Required evidence

Freeze the exact grammar, symbolic mechanism theorem or exact obstruction, a deterministic checker for all finite claims, execution provenance, and a NEW Result-ID with complete Git-blob/SHA256 bindings.
