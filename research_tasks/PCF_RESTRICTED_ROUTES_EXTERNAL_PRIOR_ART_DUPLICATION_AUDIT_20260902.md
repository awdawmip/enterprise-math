# PCF restricted routes external prior-art / duplication audit

Status: `READY / DRIVER REVIEW FOLLOW-UP / SOURCE-BACKED AUDIT`

## 0. Mother question

At the exact accepted strengths of PCF5 and PCF6, which load-bearing ingredients are standard classical or algebraic antecedents, which are strict specializations, and which project-specific typed residues remain after a source-backed external prior-art and duplication audit?

## 1. Frozen inputs and scope

Freeze `RR-D4F90C15C5BB4261230D` at the restricted fixed-kappa covered-family support-compression strength only. Freeze `RR-6F3A91D2C5E74B08A621` at the corrected free-rank-2 oriented mixed-realization obstruction only.

For PCF5, compare explicitly against Strassen/Pollard-Strassen style factoring, factorial/product-tree/batch-gcd constructions, and standard support/product compression. For PCF6, compare explicitly against CRT idempotents, product-ring decomposition, trace/determinant selectors, and standard finite-algebra realization language.

Do not upgrade either accepted Result into a universal factoring algorithm, a factoring speedup, a factoring lower bound, or a general H-dependent impossibility theorem. Project-internal naming is not evidence of novelty, and `NO_MATERIAL_MATCH` is not a novelty certificate.

## 2. Hard target and required outputs

Hard target:

`PCF5_PCF6_EXTERNAL_PRIOR_ART_AND_DUPLICATION_BOUNDARY_EXACTLY_CLASSIFIED`

Required outputs:

1. a cited comparison matrix for every load-bearing PCF5/PCF6 ingredient;
2. classification of each row as `EXACT_ANTECEDENT`, `STRICT_ANTECEDENT`, `STANDARD_METHOD_REPACKAGING`, `PROJECT_SPECIFIC_TYPED_RESIDUE`, or `NO_MATERIAL_MATCH`;
3. exact statement of which accepted project-local boundary remains useful after standard antecedents are removed;
4. explicit treatment of Strassen/Pollard-Strassen, factorial/product trees, batch gcd, CRT idempotents, product-ring decomposition, and trace/determinant selector language;
5. a fresh execution record and current-schema Result with complete dual-digest evidence bindings.

Any `NO_MATERIAL_MATCH` row must state that the audit has not proved historical novelty.

## 3. Research value to preserve

The accepted PCF5 and PCF6 mathematics is useful as a sharply scoped internal boundary even if substantial mechanisms are classical. This audit prevents standard batch-gcd/product-tree or CRT/idempotent machinery from being renamed as a new factorization mechanism while preserving any genuinely project-specific typing or obstruction statement that survives exact comparison.

The task is comparative and evidentiary. It must not mutate PCF5/PCF6 mathematics or generate a stronger algorithmic claim.

## 4. Success, kill, and return criteria

Success is a source-backed duplication boundary for both accepted Results with enough citation detail for an independent Driver to verify every classification.

Kill any novelty, superiority, speedup, or lower-bound claim that depends only on renamed standard machinery. If a strongest comparison cannot be completed from reliable sources, return those rows as unresolved rather than inferring novelty.

Stop once both Results have complete comparison rows and the surviving project-local residue, if any, is stated at exact scope. Do not create a new factorization algorithm or mathematical continuation inside this audit.
