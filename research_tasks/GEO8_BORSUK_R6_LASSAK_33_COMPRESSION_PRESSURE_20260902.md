# GEO8 Borsuk R6 Lassak-33 constructive compression pressure

Status: `READY / P1 / HIGH LEVERAGE / EXTERNAL EUCLIDEAN CONTINUATION`

## Mother question

The reviewed GEO7 bridge fixes the dated external status

`7 <= b(6) <= 33`

and closes the finite native-interface question. This task asks a narrower and harder Euclidean question:

**Can the load-bearing constructive upper bound `b(6)<=33` be rigorously reduced to `32` or below, or can one prove that a precisely frozen Lassak/truncation construction class cannot achieve such a reduction?**

This task is entirely external Euclidean geometry. It does not modify P000 and does not infer a native metric from six named axes.

## 1. Exact reconstruction before optimization

Reconstruct the six-dimensional specialization of Lassak's published proof of

`b(n) <= 2^(n-1)+1`

from the primary article and reliable secondary explanations only as needed for notation. Freeze:

- the normalization on the input set;
- the enclosing/covering body or parallelotope/simplex construction actually used;
- the exact family of partition pieces;
- the strict-diameter certificate for each piece;
- where the count `2^(6-1)+1=33` enters;
- which steps are dimension-independent and which are genuinely six-dimensional.

Do not optimize a paraphrased or guessed version of Lassak's construction.

## 2. R4 truncation/UCS transfer audit

The 2026 Tolmachev–Voronov R4 work is adjacent evidence, not an R6 theorem. Extract only its exact structural moves and test them against the frozen R6 Lassak reconstruction.

For every proposed transferred ingredient, classify it as:

- `EXACTLY_TRANSFERABLE_TO_R6`;
- `TRANSFERABLE_WITH_NEW_R6_LEMMA`;
- `DIMENSION_4_SPECIFIC`;
- `TYPE_OR_HYPOTHESIS_MISMATCH`.

If a new R6 geometric lemma is required, state it explicitly and either prove it or isolate it as the exact obstruction. Do not promote computational plausibility to a covering theorem.

## 3. Universal continuous certificate requirement

A valid improved upper bound must certify a partition or covering for **every** bounded positive-diameter subset of Euclidean `R^6` after the declared normalization.

For an asserted `b(6)<=k`, every one of the `k` pieces must have a rigorous strict-diameter bound. Legal proof modes include exact convex geometry, symbolic inequalities, certified semialgebraic optimization, interval/rational enclosure with a proof of global coverage, or another mathematically complete continuous certificate.

The following are insufficient as proof of a universal bound:

- finite point clouds;
- random or adversarial samples only;
- floating-point local optimization without global certification;
- a finite catalog of input sets;
- graph-coloring certificates without a Euclidean realization/cover theorem;
- numerical evidence that each sampled piece appears to have smaller diameter.

Finite computation may discover candidates but must be separated from the final continuous proof.

## 4. Frozen-template obstruction route

If a strict improvement cannot be proved, freeze one explicit construction class derived from Lassak and/or a precisely stated R4-style truncation analogue. Then prove an exact obstruction of the form:

`EVERY_ADMISSIBLE_MEMBER_OF_TEMPLATE_REQUIRES_AT_LEAST_33_PIECES`

or another theorem that rules out `<=32` within that exact class.

A template obstruction must quantify over the whole frozen parameter family. Failure of an optimizer to find 32 pieces is not an obstruction theorem.

The return must state the smallest additional geometric freedom that escapes the obstruction, if identifiable.

## 5. External-status firewall

Preserve:

- `b(6)>=7` from the regular simplex;
- retained published upper bound `b(6)<=33` until a stronger proof is actually completed;
- `b(6)=7` remains open unless this task truly proves it;
- the R4 `<=8` result and R63 `>=65` claim do not transfer to R6 by dimension analogy;
- preprints retain their publication-state labels.

No P000 theorem, Working Truth, Foundation authority, canonical native-geometry promotion, or novelty certificate follows from this task.

## Hard target

`BORSUK_R6_LASSAK_33_BOUND_STRICTLY_IMPROVED_OR_TEMPLATE_OBSTRUCTION_EXACTLY_CLASSIFIED`

## Required outputs

1. A primary-source exact reconstruction of the R6 specialization of Lassak's `33`-part construction.
2. A theorem dependency graph identifying every inequality/covering lemma that makes the 33-part proof work.
3. An exact audit of the 2026 R4 truncation/UCS mechanism against those R6 dependencies.
4. Either:
   - a fully rigorous continuous certificate for `b(6)<=32` or a stronger upper bound; or
   - an exact quantified obstruction for a clearly frozen Lassak/truncation construction class.
5. Deterministic checkers for every finite or symbolic subcertificate, with exact arithmetic where possible.
6. A source manifest separating published theorems, preprints, standard convex-geometry facts and project-local lemmas.
7. An adversarial audit targeting uncovered regions, equality/near-equality diameter pairs, degeneracies and boundary cases.
8. A fresh execution record and writer-conformant Result with complete Git-blob SHA-1 and SHA-256 bindings.

## Valid terminal outcomes

- `B6_CONSTRUCTIVE_UPPER_BOUND_STRICTLY_IMPROVED`;
- `FROZEN_LASSAK_COMPRESSION_TEMPLATE_CANNOT_BEAT_33`;
- `R4_TRUNCATION_METHOD_R6_TRANSFER_EXACTLY_BLOCKED`.

A result that merely reports that no 32-part construction was found is nonterminal and does not meet the hard target.
