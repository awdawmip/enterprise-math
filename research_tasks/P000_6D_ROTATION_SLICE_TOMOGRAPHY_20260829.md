# P000 六维进取空间的旋转—切面—层析魔方

Status: `PUBLISHED_REGISTERED / P000-BOUND / NEW_DIRECTION / SIX-DIMENSIONAL`

## Mother question

Accept P000 without proof or falsification:

- reality has exactly six native spatial dimensions plus one time dimension;
- Enterprise space is a discrete Cell space;
- the existing three-axis construction is a research slice, not the full world;
- rotation is the characteristic geometric transformation;
- time is the trace/order clue of relational change.

Under those premises, what is the smallest exact discrete geometry in which a full six-dimensional Cell state can be rotated, a three-axis slice can be selected and observed, omitted spatial information can become visible after rotation, and repeated time-ordered observations can recover or distinguish hidden structure?

The task is not to decide whether P000 is true. It starts after that decision.

## Frozen inputs and scope

### P000 premises

The following are unconditional task premises:

`REALITY_DIMENSION=7`.

`ENTERPRISE_SPACE_DIMENSION=6`.

`ENTERPRISE_TIME_DIMENSION=1`.

`ENTERPRISE_SPACE_KIND=DISCRETE_CELL_SPACE`.

`NATIVE_SPATIAL_DIMENSION=NATIVE_SPATIAL_AXIS_COUNT=6`.

`CURRENT_THREE_AXIS_MODEL=RESEARCH_SLICE_OF_6D_SPACE`.

`ENTERPRISE_GEOMETRY_PRIMARY_TRANSFORMATION=ROTATION`.

`TIME_ROLE=TRACE_AND_ORDER_OF_RELATIONAL_CHANGE`.

Do not reduce six dimensions by classical carrier rank, affine relations, Euclidean embedding dimension, projection dimension, or visualizability. Do not reopen three-dimensional or four-dimensional reality as an alternative.

### Existing three-axis mathematics

The exact `E_1,E_2,E_3` circle-cell construction, its `120 degree` Enterprise orthogonality, discrete addresses, line/path objects and any later exact theorem proved inside that construction remain usable only at their proved three-axis slice strength.

The earlier A3/Rubik shell work is therefore retyped as a lower-dimensional prototype of slice-local alignment/support behavior. It is not the ontology of the six-dimensional world and cannot supply the six-dimensional rotation group by itself.

### What is deliberately not frozen

P000 fixes the dimension and role types, but does not yet fix:

- the complete six-axis angle/incidence table;
- a global six-dimensional metric;
- a specific six-dimensional packing carrier;
- a global rotation group;
- which triples of axes form admissible `120 degree` slices;
- a unique shell/radius definition in six dimensions;
- a time metric or time rotation.

Those structures must be constructed at the weakest exact strength needed by this task. Classical `SO(6)`, `Z^6`, `A_n`, FCC/HCP, Euclidean orthogonality or continuous spacetime may be used only as typed external/carrier comparisons after a native object is defined; none may be silently installed as the native definition.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target:

`P000_6D_ENTERPRISE_ROTATION_SLICE_TOMOGRAPHY_EXACTLY_CONSTRUCTED`.

A successful return must contain all of the following.

### 1. Full six-dimensional Cell state type

Define a native state type `X_6` carrying six spatial coordinates/relations without assuming that an omitted coordinate is zero. The representation must distinguish:

`FULL_CELL_STATE` from `VISIBLE_SLICE_STATE`.

If numeric coordinates are introduced, state exactly which equivalences are native and which are only representation relations.

### 2. Three-axis slice family

Define a typed family of observation maps

`Pi_I : X_6 -> X_I`,

where `I` is an admissible three-axis selection from the six spatial axes. At minimum, recover the existing `E_1,E_2,E_3` geometry as one exact slice instance at its current proved strength.

Do not assume that every three-element subset is admissible. Classify or axiomatize the smallest admissible slice family required by the construction and separate proven structure from open structure.

### 3. Native discrete rotation

Define a nontrivial rotation action or rotation relation `R` on full six-dimensional Cell states. It must be native/discrete and must specify:

- legal domain;
- what Cell relations it preserves;
- composition/inversion strength actually available;
- how it changes the selected slice or the information visible in a fixed slice.

A mere relabeling of the three visible axes does not close this task.

### 4. Hidden-to-visible witness

Construct an explicit pair of full states `x != y` with the same initial three-axis observation,

`Pi_I(x)=Pi_I(y)`,

but a legal rotation and later observation separates them,

`Pi_J(R x) != Pi_J(R y)`

for a declared slice `J`.

This is the minimum decisive witness that the additional spatial dimensions are operationally present in the geometry rather than decorative metadata.

### 5. Rubik-style partial rotation

Define the six-dimensional analogue of a Rubik move as a rotation restricted to a declared Cell region/layer/support. Do not import the old A3 radial shell definition as global truth. Either derive a native nested-region/shell notion from six-dimensional Cell relations or work with an explicitly typed support predicate.

The operation must distinguish:

`ROTATION_PHASE`,

`SUPPORT_DOMAIN`,

`SLICE_SELECTION`,

and `OBSERVATION`.

The old three-axis/A3 prefix-support model must appear, if used, only as a regression/special case after hypotheses are matched.

### 6. Time-ordered relational trace

Use the seventh dimension as the ordering clue for change. Define a minimal trace such as

`(X_t, R_t, I_t, O_t)_{t=0}^m`,

with

`O_t = Pi_{I_t}(X_t)`

and

`X_{t+1} = R_t(X_t)`

or the exact relation-valued replacement if rotation is not deterministic.

No continuous time metric, Lorentz structure or time rotation is required.

### 7. Tomographic identifiability boundary

State exactly when a finite sequence of rotated slice observations distinguishes two full six-dimensional states. Give either:

- an injectivity/reconstruction theorem for a declared finite observation family; or
- the exact indistinguishability relation and a smallest collision witness showing what remains hidden.

This is the core `magic cube -> tomography` upgrade.

### 8. Lower-dimensional regression

Show how the existing three-axis circle-cell geometry and the prior A3 shell calculations embed, project, or specialize into the new typed system without promoting their carrier dimension to the full world dimension.

A valid old theorem may survive as:

`SLICE_THEOREM`.

It may become a full six-dimensional theorem only after a proved rotation/equivariance/gluing lift.

## Research value to preserve

The purpose of the magic-cube route is not puzzle simulation. It is to make hidden spatial dimensions operational through rotation.

The decisive conceptual transition is:

`3D_SLICE_ALIGNMENT`

becomes

`6D_FULL_STATE -> ROTATE -> SELECT_3_AXIS_SLICE -> OBSERVE -> TIME_ORDER -> COMPARE`.

If successful, the construction gives Enterprise Geometry a concrete experimental language for asking which parts of a six-dimensional discrete Cell state are visible from one slice, which become visible only after rotation, what information remains invariant, and how repeated observations recover bulk structure.

This also supplies a corrected basis for later shell tomography, holonomy, path, motion and physical-bridge work without restoring the superseded three-dimensional world ontology.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Success requires an exact typed six-dimensional rotation/slice calculus plus at least one nontrivial hidden-to-visible witness and an exact finite-observation identifiability statement.

The following do not close the task:

- proving or disputing P000;
- replacing six dimensions by classical three-dimensional carrier rank;
- calling an `A3`, ordinary Rubik cube or 3D shell model the full Enterprise world;
- adding three unused coordinates while every rotation remains confined to the original visible slice;
- assuming a continuous `SO(6)` model without deriving a native discrete interpretation;
- defining time as a seventh spatial direction;
- claiming six-axis pairwise `120 degree` relations without construction;
- running a larger finite census without producing a hidden-to-visible rotation witness or an identifiability boundary.

If no deterministic native rotation group can yet be constructed, return the strongest exact rotation relation/groupoid, its legal domains, a hidden-to-visible witness if possible, and the smallest obstruction preventing stronger composition.

If finite observations cannot reconstruct the full state, return the exact observational equivalence relation rather than forcing uniqueness.
