# P000 local-to-global background equivariance derivation V18

Status: `READY / GENERATION-18 / P0 / P000-BOUND`

Task: `RS-P000-L1-NATIVE-CARRIER-CONTACT-BRIDGE`

## Mother problem

Gen17 accepts two atomic downstream transparency gates:

- `PF10_STRUCTURAL_AUT_EQ`;
- `CONNECTION_STRUCTURAL_AUT_EQ` when an independent connection is declared.

They repair the Gen16 symmetry leak, but are still global conditions quantified over all structural automorphisms. The next question is whether these gates are consequences of more local intrinsic laws rather than information that must be postulated globally.

## Hard target

`P000_LOCAL_TO_GLOBAL_PF10_AND_CONNECTION_S4_EQUIVARIANCE_CRITERIA_EXACTLY_DERIVED_OR_INFORMATION_THEORETICALLY_OBSTRUCTED`

## A. Frozen inputs

Consume Gen17 exactly. Do not alter G15 or Gen17 atomic costs. Preserve P000, carrier/native separation, no-kernel-quotient and time-fixed guards.

## B. PF-10 local-to-global ladder

Classify, separately, the strength of:

1. stabilizer/orbit regularity at one Cell;
2. the same local orbit type at every Cell;
3. equivariance under frozen generator `a` only;
4. equivariance under frozen generator `b` only;
5. equivariance under both `a,b` with coherent Cell transport;
6. full `PF10_STRUCTURAL_AUT_EQ`.

Prove exact implications/equivalences and construct countermodels for every failed implication. In particular test whether coherent `a,b` generator equivariance is exactly equivalent to full structural transparency when the structural group is generated by the accepted `a,b` action.

## C. Independent connection ladder

Classify:

1. edgewise local transport type regularity;
2. inverse-edge consistency;
3. path composition consistency;
4. generator naturality for `a` only / `b` only / both;
5. holonomy conjugacy on a generating cycle basis;
6. full `CONNECTION_STRUCTURAL_AUT_EQ`.

Do not equate flatness with symmetry. Nontrivial holonomy is allowed when equivariant.

## D. Coupling question

Determine whether PF-10 and connection transparency can be derived from one common intrinsic local transport principle without hiding two independent information constraints inside a macro. If a genuine common theorem exists, prove why it is not just syntactic conjunction packing. Otherwise prove independence by models satisfying either side alone.

## E. Information-cost theorem

If neither global gate is derivable from weaker accepted local laws, quantify the irreducible information needed to select the transparent subclass. Use stabilizer/orbit or finite model counts where exact. Distinguish logical axiom count from information bits; do not silently identify them.

## F. Positive regression

Reconstruct the Gen17 conditional K4 faithful/canonical result from the weakest successfully derived local criteria, without directly assuming the global gate if an equivalent local theorem was proved.

Mandatory negatives: PF10 `e1` profile, marked-edge connection, P4 structural no-lift, Gen13 nonsplit and split/noncanonical regressions.

## Valid terminal classes

- `LOCAL_GENERATOR_EQUIVARIANCE_EXACTLY_EQUIVALENT_TO_GLOBAL_BACKGROUND_TRANSPARENCY`;
- `PF10_LOCAL_DERIVATION_SUCCEEDS_CONNECTION_REQUIRES_IRREDUCIBLE_GLOBAL_INFORMATION`;
- `MULTIPLE_IRREDUCIBLE_BACKGROUND_EQUIVARIANCE_INFORMATION_GATES_PROVED`;
- `LOCAL_TO_GLOBAL_EQUIVARIANCE_OBSTRUCTION_EXACTLY_CLASSIFIED`.

No P000 root promotion is authorized by this task.
