# Issue 1159 primitive spectral arithmetic and decimation semigroup

Status: `READY / FREE-RESEARCH INTEGRATION`

## Mother question

Does the finite Dirichlet rotation spectrum carry a self-contained divisor and Galois arithmetic—generated by multiplicative spectral decimation—whose primitive factors satisfy exact mass, gcd, discriminant, resultant, irreducibility, conductor, and trace laws before ordinary cyclotomic theory is named?

## Frozen inputs and scope

Read the #1159 state-machine handoff first. Consume the already verified finite continuant, complement symmetry, two-fold decimation, real finite spectrum, and parity-product facts. Use the internal-phase research branch at the pinned commit as the source of later theorem-candidates.

The intended native objects are finite integer/rational polynomials and their finite spectral roots. Ordinary roots of unity and classical cyclotomic theorems may be used only to compare a completed native theorem with its classical image; they are not proof inputs for a claim labeled native.

Preserve two corrections. The earlier native-resultant proof outline had a circular multiple/nonmultiple induction and must be replaced by the later aggregate `A_m(d)` plus resultant-Mobius route or another genuinely noncircular argument. For odd `d>1`, the corrected projective midpoint mass is `|Omega_d(4)|=1`.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target: `PRIMITIVE_SPECTRAL_ARITHMETIC_AUDIT`.

Deliver a coherent theorem package containing, when true:

1. Integer polynomial definitions of the multiplicative decimation maps `R_n`, proof of `R_mn=R_m o R_n=R_n o R_m`, and the normalized determinant cocycle `H_mn(u)=H_m(u)H_n(R_m(u))`.
2. Prime-power generation filtration and exact root-product mass of each prime-adic spectral generation; state multiplicities and complement behavior without ambiguity.
3. Divisor embeddings of finite spectra and primitive factors `Psi_d`; prove the primitive mass law by Mobius inversion and determine exact degrees.
4. Monic divisor lattice consequences such as `Q_m | Q_n` iff `m|n`, polynomial gcd/CRT laws, and full/primitive discriminant formulas.
5. A noncircular proof or disproof of the native prime-power resultant law, including the unit-resultant case for non-prime-power ratios.
6. Exact irreducibility criteria and the native Galois action on projective primitive factors. Distinguish field equality, generator complement, and denominator labels.
7. Recheck the prime-power Eisenstein/Frobenius congruence claims and the projective conductor/complement-sheet identities, including the corrected odd midpoint value.
8. Independently rederive the proposed Ramanujan-sum realization `Tr(C_n(2-alpha_d))=c_d(n)` and identify its exact hypotheses, endpoint conventions, and denominator transition.
9. Give a separate compatibility section explaining the relation to ordinary cyclotomic trace/resultant laws only after the native package is closed.

## Research value to preserve

If correct, this turns the Wallis/sine finite carrier into a substantial arithmetic object rather than a one-formula approximation device: divisibility of scale, prime-power ramification, primitive spectral mass, and Galois traces become different faces of one finite decimation algebra.

Several later BRC and zeta directions use these primitive factors. A single audited package prevents those later routes from depending on an unnoticed circular argument or on the corrected midpoint formula in its old form.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Success is a noncircular exact finite theorem package with reproducible symbolic checks for representative composite and prime-power cases, plus a precise boundary between native spectral arithmetic and classical compatibility.

Kill any proof that invokes an ordinary cyclotomic resultant, root-of-unity Galois group, or trace formula as the reason the native statement is true. Such facts may validate the answer afterward but cannot establish a native claim.

If a proposed law fails, return the smallest counterexample, the corrected law, and which downstream notes must be downgraded or repaired.
