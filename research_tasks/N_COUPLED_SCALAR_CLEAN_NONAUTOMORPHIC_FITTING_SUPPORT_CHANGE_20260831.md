# N-coupled scalar-clean nonautomorphic Fitting-support change

## Hard target

`SCALAR_CLEAN_NONAUTOMORPHIC_FITTING_SUPPORT_CHANGE_CONSTRUCTED_OR_DECLARED_GRAMMAR_OBSTRUCTED`.

Start from public composite `N` and a declared factor-blind seed whose hidden channels have equal relevant rank/support. Freeze one exact data action that is **not** merely left/right multiplication by integer-unimodular matrices and that can in principle change a determinantal/Fitting ideal.

Admissible candidate classes include:

- factor-blind non-invertible projection or compression;
- dimension-changing completion or quotient;
- quotient/carry-derived module generator or relation that changes the presented module;
- typed kernel/restriction semantics not reducible to a scalar nonunit test;
- another finite exact non-scalar action whose effect on Fitting support can be audited symbolically.

A positive result must produce a new one-sided hidden-channel support/rank event while every declared scalar projection available before the selective-collapse layer satisfies `gcd(N,s) in {1,N}`.

A negative result must be exact for the frozen grammar and must identify the smallest missing semantic capability rather than generalize to all non-ring computation.

## Mandatory mechanism firewall

For every proposed construction, include a table comparing exact public input, evolving state, stopping event and extraction step against:

- Pollard p-1 / Williams p+1 / ECM group-order or smoothness mechanisms;
- Pollard rho collision/cycle mechanisms;
- Fermat/Lehman/Hart and CFRAC/Dixon/QS/NFS congruence-of-squares or relation mechanisms;
- named-prime p-adic/Hensel lifting;
- direct zero-divisor, nonunit, idempotent or nontrivial-root gcd endpoints.

Also distinguish `UPDATE_MAP_CRT_NATURALITY` from `PROCESS_STOPPING_CRT_ASYMMETRY`. Either may be relevant, but neither is by itself a factorization theorem.

## Regression firewalls

The accepted `G_poly-loc` N-erasure/determinantal-support theorem and the accepted `G_hist-U` unimodular-support-invariance theorem are mandatory negative controls. Finite hit rates or benchmark performance are not proof.

## Required evidence

Freeze the exact grammar, all-m/all-trace theorem or exact obstruction at the declared scope, deterministic exact checks for finite claims, execution provenance, and a NEW Result-ID with complete Git-blob SHA-1 plus SHA-256 bindings.
