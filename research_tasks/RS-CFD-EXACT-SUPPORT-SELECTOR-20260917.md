# CFD exact support recognition and spectral selection

## Mother question

Can an exact arithmetic description of a retained Fourier support identify nontrivial flow families for which a sparse host callback stays cheaper over a full trajectory, after all recognition, support maintenance and fallback costs are included?

## Frozen inputs and scope

This is a CONTINUATION of RS-CFD-SPECTRAL-HYBRID-20260910, blocked on GV-CFD-HOST-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-20260917 and RS-CFD-PRIOR-ART-AUDIT-20260910. No prototype execution is granted by merely recording the hypothesis. Start from the actual host packet, not from an invented replacement solver.

Use a frozen finite periodic three-dimensional incompressible Fourier-Galerkin ODE and the same host integrator. An admissible test carrier is L intersected with the declared retained cube, where L is an integer additive subgroup. Addition outside the retained cube is handled by the declared Galerkin truncation, not wrapped modulo the FFT size. Preserve exact integer frequency labels, full vector components, signed/complex amplitudes, conjugates, mean and Nyquist conventions, and raw pressure information.

The checkpoint's example annihilator (1,-7,6), with generators (7,1,0) and (-6,0,1), is a test witness, not a new theorem or a generic three-dimensional flow claim. Reuse mature integer-kernel or normal-form methods where adequate. Document forcing and boundary assumptions; off-carrier forcing or nonzero off-support coefficients invalidate the certificate or trigger fallback. Small magnitude is not exact zero.

## Hard target and required outputs

1. State and prove the exact finite-dynamics invariance condition used by the detector, including all retained-output and integrator hypotheses. Distinguish a subgroup carrier from an arbitrary sparse set which may immediately densify.
2. Implement a checkable support descriptor and a conservative recognition/selection rule that retains every nonzero coefficient in the declared arithmetic model. Do not erase floating leakage to manufacture exact support.
3. Preserve both velocity and host-pressure observations. Report the full model, coordinate conventions, compiler/precision and host revision.
4. Compare baseline FFT, existing hybrid and detector-assisted hybrid on held-out nontrivial invariant families, general random fields, growing supports, mean/Nyquist edge cases and tiny off-carrier coefficients.
5. Account for setup, recognition, calibration, allocation, support scans, fallback, trajectory computation and readout; separately report unavoidable exclusions and one-time costs. Avoid uncontrolled cross-machine comparisons.
6. Return an exact restricted applicability statement or a reproducible negative obstruction, a source manifest, all raw timings/failures and a verifier independent of candidate selection decisions.

## Research value to preserve

The value is a discriminating test of persistent structural sparsity, not another single-call speed ratio. A detector that costs too much or invalidates pressure/support semantics should be closed with its evidence retained. BRC-style grouping must be justified for the actual signed/complex observer and downstream operations; positive mass alone is not a cancellation certificate.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

SUCCESS requires the invariance argument, correct host observations and a demonstrated complete-cost advantage on a declared nontrivial family with reliable fallback outside it. A correct detector without cost benefit is a scoped negative engineering result, not a general acceleration success.

KILL claims relying on tolerance-as-zero, discarded generated modes, equal-shell direction collapse, altered time dynamics, circular reuse of the tuning cases, or incomplete cost accounting. No conclusion here establishes a continuous-PDE certificate, turbulence accuracy, physical validation or native P000 fluid law. Return through the Driver review ledger without creating an automatic next stage.
