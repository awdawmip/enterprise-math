# R057G — Pi-Supervised Geometric Collapse Synthesis

Status: `READY / P0 / SIBLING ARM TO R057-A / SUPERVISED DISCOVERY / NOT CANONICAL`

## Mother question

Use known classical pi and teacher circles to discover **what geometric object each local lattice-boundary packet should collapse into**.

The task is explicitly allowed to fit the target. It is not required to rediscover pi blindly.

R057G runs in parallel with R057-A. Its first serious grammar must be discovered independently from R057-A's selected algebraic grammar.

Use the frozen packet as the complete mathematical and experimental specification.

## Stage 0 — freeze the geometric evolution rules

Before expensive teacher-corpus generation, packet enumeration or synthesis, create, freeze and return:

- `R057G_GEOMETRIC_DISCOVERY_PROTOCOL_SHA256`
- `R057G_INITIAL_TEACHER_GEOMETRY_REGISTRY_SHA256`
- `R057G_GEOMETRIC_GRAMMAR_META_PROTOCOL_SHA256`

The frozen meta-protocol must explicitly preserve:

- teacher pi/circle/center/radius/chord/tangent/arc/area are allowed;
- post-selection and overfitting are allowed;
- K may expand inside R057G;
- geometric surrogate families may expand inside R057G;
- teacher radii/phases may expand inside R057G;
- every meaningful change enters genealogy;
- fitted discovery and theorem status remain distinct;
- target-literal degenerate programs are labeled rather than confused with structural geometry;
- first-round R057G does not import R057-A's best grammar.

Stop after Stage 0 and return the three hashes for Driver review.

## Stage A — teacher corpus and packet census

Generate the frozen initial teacher corpus and a cyclic/D6-aware packet catalog.

Teacher corpus must include multiple radii and multiple sub-cell phases/orientations.

Packet types may depend on size, turn word, chord, radial/tangent/area invariants and later context.

## Stage B — initial geometric grammar search

Search the packet's G0 grammar, including at least:

- RAW polyline;
- whole chord;
- all contiguous chord partitions/compositions;
- selected multi-chord breakpoints;
- triangle surrogate;
- tangent surrogate;
- local arc surrogate;
- area/sector-style surrogate;
- effective collapse-count maps `c(k,turn_word,context)`.

Return an early complexity/error/phase-robustness frontier before aggressive expansion.

## Stage C — target-guided grammar evolution

If the first grammar is underexpressive, expand K, context, surrogate families, fitted coefficients or teacher data inside the same generation.

For every meaningful version change record:

`parent -> motivating error/pattern -> change -> score before/after -> complexity before/after -> teacher data visible at decision time`.

Post-selection is allowed. Hidden post-selection is not.

## Stage D — geometric compression

Compress successful large lookup grammars into compact geometric laws where possible:

- `k -> effective collapse count` laws;
- partition/composition recurrences;
- finite-state turn-word rules;
- formulas in chord/radius/tangent/area invariants;
- small reusable surrogate families;
- multiscale recursion.

The objective is explanatory compression, not retroactive claims of blind discovery.

## Stage E — robustness and independent checkpoint

Before reading R057-A's selected grammar, freeze the first serious R057G grammar checkpoint and its genealogy.

Only after that checkpoint may a later comparison task examine algebra/geometry correspondence.

## Stage F — theorem candidates

After supervised discovery and compression, attempt proofs of selected structural statements. Keep theorem status separate from fit status.

## Primary success modes

High-value returns include:

- `COMPACT_GEOMETRIC_COLLAPSE_GRAMMAR_FOUND`;
- `GEOMETRIC_COLLAPSE_COUNT_LAW_FOUND`;
- `HIGH_ACCURACY_GEOMETRIC_LOOKUP_ONLY`;
- `GEOMETRIC_COMPRESSION_FAILED`;
- `TARGET_LITERAL_ONLY_FIT`;
- `GEOMETRIC_PI_SUPERVISED_DISCOVERY_OPEN`.

## Interpretation boundary

R057G is a supervised discovery experiment. A fitted rule may be excellent without being foundational. A later theorem may explain a fitted rule without changing the historical fact that pi was used to discover it.
