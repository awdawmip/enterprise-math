# P000 六维离散旋转与层析结构的外部先例和重复性审计

Status: `READY / DRIVER REVIEW FOLLOW-UP / P000-BOUND`

## Mother question

Which parts of the accepted minimal six-dimensional rotation/tomography construction are standard external mathematics, which parts are merely typed recombinations of known structures, and which claims remain internally distinctive after exact comparison?

## Frozen inputs and scope

P000 is an internal project axiom and is not under external validation in this task.

Audit only the mathematical mechanism accepted in `RR-728A860D0C425719BCDB`: product state space, factor-swap involution, product adjacency automorphism, invariant-support partial move, complementary projections, time-ordered observation, and the two-shot identifiability theorem.

External sources must be reported faithfully as external mathematics. They may classify duplication or suggest carrier tools, but they cannot reduce the six native spatial dimensions or replace P000.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target: `P000_6D_ROTATION_TOMOGRAPHY_EXTERNAL_PRIOR_ART_BOUNDARY_CLASSIFIED`.

Required outputs: authoritative references for each matching external construction; an exact comparison table distinguishing equivalence, special case, analogy and non-match; duplication verdicts for the product/swap automorphism, supported local involution and finite observation theorem; a statement of what remains distinctive only because of Enterprise typing and P000 interpretation; and recommendations on external tools/terminology that may be reused without importing external ontology.

## Research value to preserve

The project needs aggressive mathematical innovation without confusing novelty of interpretation with novelty of mechanism. A clean prior-art boundary prevents rediscovering standard algebra under new names while protecting P000 from being silently overwritten by external geometry.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Success requires source-backed exact comparison, not a generic literature list.

Kill any audit that judges P000 itself, treats conventional physical dimensionality as a falsifier, relies on keyword similarity without structural mapping, or claims novelty merely because terminology differs.

If external literature fully subsumes the mechanism, return that conclusion explicitly and identify the strongest reusable external formalism.
