`B_src = (source carrier, theorem-critical language, permitted source-side operations, permitted source-side lemmas, claimed scope)`.

## 5. What counts as an attribution witness

At least one of the following exact forms may establish attribution:

### A. Representation-normal-form differential

A predeclared structural measure or exact structural property differs between source and realized representation because of the transform.

Example class: coupled operator -> diagonal/block normal form.

### B. Dependency-language differential

A declared source proof obligation requires a larger state/dependency language, while the realized object admits a strictly smaller complete language under an exact theorem.

### C. Obstruction-basis differential

The transform generates a finite/minimal obstruction language not already available in the frozen source baseline.

### D. Compositional differential

The transform turns a noncompositional/global source obligation into exact composable local obligations plus an assembly theorem.

### E. Layer-lowering differential

The source condition is represented at a higher declared layer and the transform gives an independently defined lower operational condition whose correctness theorem is load-bearing.

A bare statement that the Enterprise notation is clearer is not sufficient.
