## 1. Mission

Replace direct cycle construction by the theorem-critical question of **geometric support lowering**.

For a smooth projective complex variety `X`, define geometric coniveau

`N^p H^n(X,Q)`

as the subgroup of classes supported on some closed algebraic subset of codimension at least `p`, equivalently the union/sum of kernels

`ker(H^n(X,Q) -> H^n(X-Z,Q))`

for closed `Z` with `codim_X Z >= p`.

At the special degree `n=2p`, classical localization + purity/resolution + Gysin identify

`N^p H^{2p}(X,Q)`

with the rational span of codimension-`p` algebraic cycle classes.

Therefore the ordinary Hodge conjecture can be typed as

`H^{2p}(X,Q) cap H^{p,p} subset N^p H^{2p}(X,Q)`.

This classical equivalence is **control mathematics only**. It earns no Enterprise credit.

H0L asks whether an independently generated Enterprise-style **downward-collapse/support certificate** can prove or operationally expose this support lowering with strict proof leverage beyond fair coniveau/local-cohomology source mathematics.

### Hard prerequisite A

`DEGREE_2P_ALGEBRAICITY_CONIVEAU_EQUIVALENCE_TYPED_WITHOUT_OVERCLAIM`.

The researcher must state and prove the exact rational degree-`2p` equivalence at the declared smooth-projective scope, including singular-support/resolution typing. Do not claim geometric and Hodge coniveau coincide in general; that would be the generalized Hodge conjecture.

### Primary hard target B
