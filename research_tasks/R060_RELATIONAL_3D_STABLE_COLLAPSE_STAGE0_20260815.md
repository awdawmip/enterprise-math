## 5. Native relational contact cut

`CUT_REL(C) = { {x,y} : x in C, y notin C, x ADJ y }`.

`B(C) = |CUT_REL(C)|`.

Typing:

## 6. Primary collapse dynamics D3-L1

Freeze one primary local dynamics:

`D3_L1_LOCAL_CONTACT_CUT_DESCENT`.

A proposed move replaces one occupied packet `u` by one empty adjacent packet `v`:

`C' = (C \ {u}) union {v}`,

subject to all of:

1. `u ADJ v`;
2. `|C'|=|C|`;
3. `C'` remains connected;
4. `B(C') < B(C)` strictly.

No centroid recomputation, distance minimization, path-length minimization, or Euclidean direction is allowed.

If several legal strict descents exist, the dynamics is nondeterministic: every legal choice is a valid branch.

This nondeterminism is intentional because update-order sensitivity is one of the stability tests.

## 7. Exact termination theorem

Stage 0 must prove:

Every `D3_L1_LOCAL_CONTACT_CUT_DESCENT` trajectory from a finite state terminates after finitely many accepted moves.

Preferred proof:

- `B(C)` is a nonnegative integer;
- each accepted move decreases `B` by at least 1;
- therefore no infinite strict-descent trajectory exists.

Do not inflate this elementary termination result into a stability result.

Termination alone is not stable collapse.

## 8. Terminal states
