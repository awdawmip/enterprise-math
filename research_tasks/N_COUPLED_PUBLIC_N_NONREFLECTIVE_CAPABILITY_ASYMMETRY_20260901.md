# N-coupled public-N nonreflective capability asymmetry

## Mother question

After reflection-complete finite typed modules have been shown to scalarize before readout, can a mathematically explicit public-`N` capability discipline retain meaningful typed composition and one-sided hidden support while making complete reflection genuinely unavailable until the final collapse boundary?

## Frozen inputs and scope

Use the accepted `G_reflect-FM` boundary exactly:

- for every finite `Z/NZ`-module `M` with `N=pq`, one-sided hidden support is detected by `gcd(N,|M|)` whenever cardinality is effectively recoverable;
- complete finite ambient membership, quotient equality, complete iterator plus extensional equality, or arbitrary total linear evaluation is enough to recover cardinality and/or a finite presentation;
- ordinary opacity, laziness or withholding a printed matrix is therefore insufficient;
- the accepted external mechanism audit remains the classical-comparison firewall.

This task may study a capability-limited or effectful interface only if its missing reflection operations are part of the formal semantics, not an implementation choice.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target:

`PUBLIC_N_ONLY_NONREFLECTIVE_EFFECTFUL_TYPED_SUPPORT_CONSTRUCTED_OR_EXACTLY_OBSTRUCTED`.

Freeze one exact capability calculus `G_cap` with public input `N` only. The frozen contract must specify:

- the type of support-bearing handles or states;
- the permitted constructors and compositional operations;
- whether handles may be copied, replayed, compared, enumerated or probed, and under what typing/effect rules;
- the complete set of scalar observations permitted before readout;
- the exact final collapse/readout program.

`G_cap` must retain nontrivial pre-readout composition. Simply returning an unreadable token is not a research mechanism.

A positive construction must prove both:

1. some reachable state has genuine one-sided hidden support or an exactly equivalent typed asymmetry; and
2. every permitted pre-readout scalar remains gcd-clean, with a theorem showing that complete reflection, finite-presentation recovery and equivalent support scalarization are not definable from the frozen capabilities.

The final readout cannot be introduced as a primitive whose specification already contains a hidden factor, nonunit, CRT side or factor-aware selector. It must be implemented from the frozen public operation vocabulary plus the explicitly declared collapse boundary.

A negative result may prove that the declared capability calculus still reconstructs reflection/support or that its apparent asymmetry is only a classical mechanism in different notation.

Required frozen outputs: the formal capability/observation contract, symbolic construction or no-go, deterministic exact checks for finite claims, execution provenance, and a NEW Result-ID with complete Git-blob SHA-1 plus SHA-256 bindings.

## Research value to preserve

This task separates genuine information-flow/capability asymmetry from superficial data hiding. It preserves the exact mathematical lesson that support change, representation hiding and reflection capability are distinct layers.

If a positive survivor exists, it would be the first route in this N-coupled line not already killed by ring-term erasure, unimodular invariance, explicit-presentation determinantal scalarization, or complete-reflection cardinality scalarization. If no survivor exists in the frozen capability class, the negative theorem should identify the next minimum semantic capability rather than claim a universal factoring lower bound.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Success requires an explicit capability calculus and a theorem-level nonreflection argument together with a genuine delayed-readout one-sided support construction. Merely asserting that clients cannot inspect internals is insufficient.

A valid negative return is an exact theorem that the frozen capability calculus reconstructs enumeration/equality/presentation/support, or an exact reduction to the accepted classical-mechanism map.

Kill and reject:

- hidden factors, factor-aware idempotents or candidate-prime schedules;
- a final primitive that is already a factor oracle by specification;
- implementation secrecy without a semantic nonreflection theorem;
- unrestricted complete iterator/equality, ambient reflection or arbitrary standard-basis probing that recreates the accepted no-go;
- Pollard/Williams/ECM order-smoothness, rho collision, congruence-of-squares/relation collection, named-prime p-adic lifting, or direct nonunit extraction disguised as typed support;
- finite hit rates as proof of the semantic claim.

No positive mechanism should be interpreted as novel until compared against the existing reviewed prior-art boundary.
