# Native Tri-sector N2 Scalar Closure Foundation Independent Admission Audit

Status: `PUBLISHED_REGISTERED / CONTINUATION / INDEPENDENT_FOUNDATION_ADMISSION_AUDIT`

## Mother question

Does the accepted source-exposed result `RR-DDF27FE0D58FF6F9B8E4` independently survive Foundation-level verification at exactly native `s=3` N2 invariant-readout strength?

The target is deliberately narrower than the existing coupled research theorem. The audit asks whether current P0/P1 plus already accepted allocation-torsor facts really suffice to reconstruct the frame-free scalar/set/relation chain

`3 -> (5,7) -> 9 -> 35 -> 105 -> 53`

without importing a preferred physical lane, a breaker primitive, the arbitrary odd-`s` comparator family, or the target output itself as an upstream premise.

## Frozen inputs and scope

Treat current P0/P1 Foundation, the accepted shell-allocation torsor result, and the exact immutable parent return/result as evidence inputs. Do not assume the parent's native-status labels merely because the formulas are correct.

Independently check at least:

1. the most-balanced `S_3` orbit and frame-independent label multiset;
2. `Z(r)=ceil(mean L_r)=1+(3r^2+epsilon(r))/2` for `r>=2`, including the `r=1` boundary;
3. the semantic move from the scalar parity law to the branch-covering predicate and the unique nonsingular odd characteristic `q_b=5`;
4. the transfer from the audited ramification theorem to breaker-coprime capacity `k_*=9`;
5. the unordered transverse saturation characteristic set `{5,7}`;
6. the sharp grade/parity extremal distance multiset `{k-4,k-2}` and its specialization `{5,7}` at `k=9`;
7. the final scalar arithmetic `35,105,53`;
8. every dependency edge against NSA-01, NSA-02, NSA-13, NSA-14 and NSA-18.

Keep breaker-coprime capacity `9` logically separate from the independently established native prime-incidence island cap `9`. Keep the arbitrary odd-`s` uniqueness theorem and named physical rail ontology outside the proposed Foundation consequence unless independently derived at matching semantic strength.

No Foundation primitive file is mutated inside this audit.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target:

`NATIVE_TRISECTOR_N2_SCALAR_CLOSURE_FOUNDATION_ADMISSION_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED_OR_REJECTED`

Required outputs:

1. an independent derivation or exact counterexample for the balanced-orbit barycenter readout;
2. an adversarial semantic dependency ledger covering every native-status edge;
3. independent checks of `q_b=5`, `k_*=9`, transverse `{5,7}`, longitudinal `{5,7}`, and `35/105/53`;
4. an explicit audit of the `r=1` exclusion and whether removing finitely many initial shells is harmless for each periodic claim;
5. an explicit noncircularity audit showing that native `s=3` is input, not recovered from the desired closure;
6. a separation certificate for breaker-coprime capacity `9` versus the prime-incidence island cap `9`;
7. a final classification of T-A, T-B and T-C at the strongest independently supported semantic strength;
8. one final recommendation among `FOUNDATION_N2_CONSEQUENCE_ADMIT`, `FOUNDATION_N2_CONSEQUENCE_NARROW`, or `FOUNDATION_N2_CONSEQUENCE_REJECT`;
9. a durable return under the task's own research-return namespace.

## Research value to preserve

The parent task changed the route materially: it supplied a frame-free barycenter readout and closed the previously open native breaker/capacity/coupling chain at N2 strength. Repeating the old bridge work would waste that advance.

The remaining question is governance-critical rather than another formula search: whether a source-exposed derivation is strong enough, noncircular enough and semantically typed tightly enough to enter maintained Foundation consequence space. An independent audit can either admit a genuinely compressed native explanation or freeze the exact edge that still prevents admission.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Freeze exactly one primary disposition:

- `FOUNDATION_N2_CONSEQUENCE_ADMIT` — all native `s=3` N2 scalar/set/relation edges independently reproduce with matching semantic strength and no target leak;
- `FOUNDATION_N2_CONSEQUENCE_NARROW` — only an exact proper subset survives, with the first failed edge and weakest valid restatement frozen;
- `FOUNDATION_N2_CONSEQUENCE_REJECT` — a counterexample, hidden semantic premise, frame dependence, circularity, or strength mismatch blocks the proposed Foundation consequence.

Do not promote pointwise allocation labels, named physical lanes/rails, a breaker primitive, or arbitrary odd-`s` comparator geometry. Do not identify the two distinct meanings of the number `9`. Finite regression may falsify or support checks but does not replace the exact proof.

Stop when the independent admission classification is frozen and return it for Driver/Steward control-plane handling.
