## 0. Driver correction and mission

The previously issued H0A equivariant-presentation-fiber taskbook is **suspended before execution**.

The reason is not that equivariance/groupoid/presentation-family methods are wrong. The deeper issue is that H0 and the first H0A taskbook used **information non-factorization against an overloaded classical comparison object `C(X)`** as the principal qualification gate for an Enterprise realization.

That gate mixes three distinct questions:

1. coordinate / presentation reparameterization;
2. information equivalence or recoverability;
3. theorem / proof leverage.

These must be separated before any new Enterprise carrier search continues.

The single hard target of H0A0 is:

`HODGE_RELEVANT_REALIZATION_CRITERION_IS_WELL_TYPED_AND_NONVACUOUS`

H0A0 is a **criterion-repair stage**, not a carrier-construction stage and not a Hodge-proof stage.

Do not construct H1 cohomology. Do not execute the suspended equivariant-family search. Do not require a new-information witness merely because the legacy H0 R2 definition did.

The intended repaired principle is:

> A realization can be completely recoverable from, or even equivalent to, a classical realization and still carry major proof value if it moves theorem-relevant structure into a different primitive/derived operational layer and supplies an exact, non-tautological proof-leverage certificate.

The aim is not:

`classical does not know A, Enterprise knows A`.

The relevant possibility is:

`classical theorem-critical structure is global / analytic / high-level / hard to control`

`-> Enterprise realization`

`-> local incidence / integer multiplicity / collapse obstruction / finite algebraic constraint / normal form / other lower-layer operational structure`

with a rigorous comparison theorem in both directions required by the claim.

## 7. Define an exact proof-leverage certificate

The phrase “easier to prove” is too weak by itself.

Define a machine-auditable `PROOF_LEVERAGE_CERTIFICATE` schema.

For a source theorem-critical structure/predicate/operator `P_C` and an Enterprise realization `F_X`, an admissible certificate must include at minimum:

1. **typed source object**: exact `C_*` level and HX/N layer;
2. **typed Enterprise object**: exact Enterprise primitive/derived layer;
3. **independent Enterprise definition** of `Q_E` using Enterprise-side primitives/operations;
4. **comparison theorem** relating `P_C` and `Q_E` in the exact scope claimed;
5. **non-tautology audit** showing `Q_E` was not defined merely as `P_C o F_X^{-1}` or by copying the target answer;
6. **strict operational-leverage certificate** from at least one accepted class below;
7. **presentation/naturality/functoriality scope** required by the candidate;
8. **target-leakage audit**;
9. **prior-art/novelty status**;
10. exact downstream theorem obligation unlocked by the new form.

Accepted strict leverage classes may include:

### `LAYER_LOWERING`

A theorem-critical condition moves from a higher/global/analytic derived layer to a genuinely independently defined lower Enterprise operational layer.

### `LOCALIZATION`

A global condition becomes a finite/local incidence rule together with a proved local-to-global theorem.

### `NORMAL_FORM`

A coupled/operator relation becomes a declared normal form with exact equivalence and nontrivial structural consequences.

### `DEPENDENCY_REDUCTION`

A condition with declared global/unbounded dependency becomes bounded/local/compositional under an exact theorem.

### `FINITE_OBSTRUCTION_BASIS`

An a priori broad condition becomes equivalent to finitely many exact obstruction classes/checks.

### `INTEGRALITY_POSITIVITY_MONOTONICITY`

A difficult analytic relation becomes an exact integer/positivity/monotonicity constraint that yields a new theorem or exclusion mechanism.

### `COMPOSITIONAL_FACTORING`

A global proof obligation decomposes into exact composable local obligations with a proved assembly theorem.

A stage may propose additional classes, but each must be exact and auditable.

No universal scalar “proof complexity” is required. Candidate-specific strict measures are allowed, but the measure must be declared before using the candidate as a success and must not be chosen post hoc solely to force improvement.

## 8. Repaired R0–R3 operational classification

Freeze the following primary ranks or a provably better equivalent with Driver-readable mapping.

### R0 — `INERT_REPARAMETRIZATION`

The realization only relabels/recoordinates/transports theorem-critical structures item-by-item.

No independently defined Enterprise operational structure and no strict proof-leverage certificate survives.

Information equivalence is allowed but not decisive.

### R1 — `DERIVED_REORGANIZATION`

The realization creates a genuine derived representation or useful computational organization, but no theorem-critical strict proof-leverage certificate is yet established.

It may be fully information-equivalent or information-enriched relative to a weak readout.

### R2 — `NONTRIVIAL_OPERATIONAL_REALIZATION`

The realization may be fully recoverable from the declared classical/algebraic realization.

It qualifies only if all of the following pass:

- exact comparison typing;
- independently generated Enterprise operational structure;
- exact comparison/correctness theorem;
- at least one strict `PROOF_LEVERAGE_CERTIFICATE`;
- presentation/naturality/functoriality at the scope required by the claim;
- no target leakage;
- no mere definition renaming;
- prior-art status stated honestly.

R2 means:

> Enterprise has changed the theorem-critical operational form in a mathematically load-bearing way.

It does **not** mean Enterprise has added information.

### R3 — `HODGE_RELEVANT_OPERATIONAL_REALIZATION`

R2 plus an explicit Hodge bridge interface:

`Hodge-class-side structure`

`-> Enterprise operational constraint`

`-> algebraic-cycle lifting interface / exact lifting obligations`.

R3 does not require the final lifting theorem to be proved in H0A0, but it must specify a non-tautological interface precise enough to generate later proof obligations.

**H1 may not open merely because R2 exists. H1 readiness requires an accepted R3-class candidate or an explicit later Driver override.**

H0A0 itself is only repairing the criterion and therefore cannot by itself make H1 admissible.

## 9. Redefine “mere reparameterization” correctly

The following are no longer sufficient to classify a realization as mere reparameterization:

- invertibility;
- equivalence of categories;
- full information recoverability;
- existence of an inverse comparison map;
- failure of information non-factorization against `C_full^an`.

A realization is `INERT_REPARAMETRIZATION` only when its theorem-critical operational structure is merely transported with no independent Enterprise definition and no strict proof-leverage certificate.

Conversely, a noninvertible or information-enriched construction is not automatically nontrivial.

Arbitrary hidden labels, colors, unused fibers, duplicated state, or a deliberately weak classical readout remain R0/R1 even if they satisfy information non-factorization.

Required anti-gaming rule:

`WEAK_READOUT_NONFACTORIZATION_DOES_NOT_EARN_R2`.
