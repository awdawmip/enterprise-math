# Native Tri-sector Invariant-Readout Foundation Derivation Integration Audit

Status: `PUBLISHED_REGISTERED / FOUNDATION_QUESTION / CONTINUATION / DEPENDENCY_AUDIT`

## Mother question

After the exact shell-allocation audit proved that the six native serializations form a `D_3 ~= S_3` torsor and that `G`-invariant orbit label sets/multisets descend without selecting a physical frame, how much of the already admitted

`NATIVE_TRISECTOR_COUPLED_CLOSURE_THEOREM`

is now a theorem of the **current** P0/P1 Foundation rather than a model-specific selection theorem?

The question is not whether the admitted arithmetic is correct. Its research-theorem status is already settled. The question is dependency provenance:

> Can every load-bearing input to T-A/T-B/T-C be reconstructed from current native objects and `G`-invariant readouts without a preferred frame, universal-breaker assumption, target-selected capacity rule or circular use of `s=B=3`?

## Frozen inputs and scope

Treat the following as frozen evidence, not as conclusions to re-prove:

1. Current P0/P1 native shell/address structure and three-axis relabeling action.
2. The accepted shell-allocation torsor: shell `n` receives the forced consecutive interval, the six frame serializations form a free transitive `S_3` torsor, and no frame-independent injective point label exists.
3. For every native `G`-invariant orbit, its serialization label set/multiset and all symmetric functions descend frame-independently.
4. The even-shell balance orbit descends to the exact unordered triple
   `{6m^2-2m+1, 6m^2+1, 6m^2+2m+1}`.
5. `NATIVE_TRISECTOR_COUPLED_CLOSURE_THEOREM` remains an `AUDITED_RESEARCH_THEOREM / DRIVER_ADMITTED` node. Its prior Foundation review was `NOT_FOUNDATION_ADMITTED` because the then-missing allocation bridge and other model-side semantics had not been natively justified.

Do not introduce a distinguished physical axis, start ray, orientation or center lane. A chosen frame is N1 presentation data only.

Audit at least these theorem-facing layers separately:

- T-A centered-lane extremal uniqueness `(3,5)` and `(3,7)`;
- the split-hyperbola/sign-orbit support calculation and the semantic step from support to a universal breaker;
- the definition and status of breaker-coprime capacity `k_*`;
- T-B simultaneous boundary closure `k_*-4=2s-1`, `k_*-2=2s+1` and the `q_b<=5` input;
- T-C specialization `s=B=3`, `M_9=35`, `3M_9=105`, `106=2*53`;
- every use of allocation or lane ordering, checking whether only an unordered orbit/multiset is actually needed.

Classical finite-field, Burnside and Joukowski identities may be used as support mathematics, but their correctness alone does not make the Enterprise semantic interpretation native.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target:

`NATIVE_TRISECTOR_COUPLED_CLOSURE_FOUNDATION_DERIVATION_CHAIN_VERIFIED_OR_EXACT_REMAINING_GAP_FROZEN`

Required outputs:

1. A machine-auditable dependency ledger from current P0/P1 objects to each theorem-facing object.
2. Every edge classified as one of `DERIVED_NATIVE_INVARIANT`, `PRESENTATION_ONLY_BUT_DESCENDS`, `EXTRA_SEMANTIC_PREMISE`, or `CIRCULAR_OR_TARGET_LEAK`.
3. Explicit proof that every allocation/lane-dependent use either descends through the torsor or remains non-native.
4. Explicit audit of universal-breaker semantics and the `q_b<=5` bound: identify whether these are derived native facts, conditional theorem assumptions, or research-model choices.
5. Explicit audit of `k_*`: distinguish breaker-coprime capacity from any unrelated prime-run/island cap.
6. A final classification of T-A, T-B and T-C separately.
7. If a Foundation-derived subset exists, state it at the strongest noncircular invariant-readout strength without changing primitive Foundation definitions inside this task.
8. If any gap remains, isolate the smallest exact remaining semantic bridge and explain why the current theorem cannot be reclassified beyond it.
9. A durable return at `research_returns/NATIVE_TRISECTOR_INVARIANT_READOUT_FOUNDATION_DERIVATION_INTEGRATION_AUDIT_RETURN_20260827.md`.

No Foundation file mutation, theorem-node reclassification, or downstream promotion occurs automatically from the research return; those are Driver decisions after review.

## Research value to preserve

The previous Foundation review rejected a premature promotion because the theorem consumed model-side allocation and semantic objects. The allocation issue has now changed qualitatively: absolute point labels are impossible, but the exact frame torsor exists and invariant orbit readouts descend. This removes one major obstruction while making the correct symmetry quotient explicit.

A clean dependency audit can therefore distinguish two very different possibilities that must not be conflated: either the admitted theorem was only presentation-dependent because the correct torsor quotient had not yet been articulated, or genuinely extra breaker/capacity semantics still remain. Resolving that distinction is the shortest path to a justified Foundation reclassification or a sharply frozen no-go.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Freeze exactly one primary verdict:

- `FOUNDATION_DERIVED_AT_INVARIANT_READOUT_STRENGTH` — every load-bearing theorem input is derived from current P0/P1 or is presentation data whose required output descends equivariantly;
- `FOUNDATION_DERIVED_WITH_EXACT_SCOPE_NARROWING` — a proper theorem subset is natively derived but one or more admitted conclusions remain conditional;
- `RESEARCH_THEOREM_ONLY_REMAINING_NON_NATIVE_SEMANTIC_GAP` — allocation is no longer the blocker, but an exact breaker/capacity/coupling premise remains extra structure;
- `CIRCULAR_OR_TARGET_LEAK` — a proposed derivation uses the desired theorem or selected numeric outcome to justify an upstream premise.

Do not call the theorem Foundation-derived merely because its arithmetic output is frame-invariant. Every semantic arrow before the arithmetic must be justified.

Do not repair a gap by postulating exactly the rule needed to recover `5,7,9`. If a new primitive law would be needed, return that fact as an extra-semantic-premise boundary rather than adding it.

Stop after the strongest exact dependency classification is frozen and hand the result to Driver review.
