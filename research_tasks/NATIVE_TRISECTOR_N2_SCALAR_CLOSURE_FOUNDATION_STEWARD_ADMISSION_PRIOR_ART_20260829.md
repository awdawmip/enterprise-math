# Native Tri-sector N2 Foundation Steward Admission and Prior-art Boundary

Status: `READY / DRIVER REVIEW FOLLOW-UP / FOUNDATION-BOUND`

## Mother question

Given the Driver-accepted independent audit of the native `s=3` N2 scalar/set/relation chain, what part of the mathematics is standard external prior art, and should the exactly typed consequence be admitted to maintained Foundation knowledge under explicit Foundation Steward authority?

## Frozen inputs and scope

Consume `DR-9F0EF13296934CCAD8BD` and `RR-20AF98E870D82802D679` exactly. The accepted mathematical ceiling is the native `s=3` N2 scalar/set/relation consequence only. P0/P1 primitives remain unchanged. Physical rail/lane ontology, arbitrary odd-`s` comparator theorems, reverse derivation of native three-ness, and identification of the two distinct meanings of `9` remain out of scope.

The prior-art phase must use authoritative external sources and distinguish exact duplicate, partial antecedent, adjacent method, and no material match. A no-match result is not a novelty theorem. External prior art classifies duplication/lineage; it does not override the internal proof.

Actual Foundation maintenance requires an explicitly active `FOUNDATION_STEWARD` role. Task publication, Driver acceptance, and a Driver-ID do not grant that authority.

## Hard target and required outputs

Hard target: `NATIVE_TRISECTOR_N2_FOUNDATION_STEWARD_ADMISSION_AND_PRIOR_ART_BOUNDARY_RESOLVED`.

Required outputs are: an external prior-art ledger for each decisive mathematical ingredient; exact overlap classifications; a Steward-facing dependency/semantic ceiling packet; and, under explicit Steward authority, one maintained-surface disposition among exact admission, narrower admission, or rejection, with no P0/P1 primitive mutation unless separately authorized by a different Foundation process.

## Research value to preserve

The project benefits from admitting compact native consequences only after mathematical correctness, semantic typing, role authority, and duplication lineage are separated. This prevents both false novelty and unauthorized Foundation promotion while preserving a verified N2 consequence for future work.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

Success requires both a source-backed duplication boundary and an explicitly authorized Steward disposition. Kill any route that equates prior-art absence with novelty, uses external theory to erase native typing, changes Foundation from a Driver-only session, merges the two meanings of `9`, or widens the accepted theorem beyond native `s=3` N2 scalar/set/relation strength.
