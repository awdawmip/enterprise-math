## 4. Shared-axis transition obstruction

The overlaps are the positive axes:

`S12 ∩ S23 = E2-axis`,

`S23 ∩ S31 = E3-axis`,

`S31 ∩ S12 = E1-axis`.

For cyclic orientation, a shared axis is local phase `1` in one chart and local phase `0` in the next chart.

Classify all maps `phi:C4->C4` that could serve as a strict phase transition while preserving the Stage 3 tensor label law.

In particular test:

- group/monoid homomorphisms;
- unit-preserving isomorphisms;
- affine translations `tau_k(x)=x+k mod 4`;
- any other finite transition allowed by the actual process semantics.

A strict unit-preserving homomorphism cannot silently send a nonidentity phase to the identity. Prove the exact consequence for overlap gluing.

Required theorem candidate:

`STRICT_C4_MONOID_OVERLAP_GLUE = NO_GO`.

Do not conclude that all gluing is impossible merely because strict monoid gluing fails.
