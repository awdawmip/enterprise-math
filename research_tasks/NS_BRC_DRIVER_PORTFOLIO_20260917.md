# Driver Portfolio: BRC Jitter and Navier–Stokes Regularity Program

Status: `READY / DIRECT USER RESEARCH INTEGRATION / PUBLISHED_REGISTERED`

## 0. Mother question

How should the Driver organize the existing BRC/Navier–Stokes results into a falsifiable research program whose next tasks maximize the chance of a uniform regularity theorem while preserving independent review and honest theorem strength?

## 1. Frozen inputs and scope

Consume the cited research checkpoints as evidence, not as automatically accepted theorem truth. The program concerns the classical three-dimensional periodic incompressible Navier–Stokes slice and the existing A3/FCC/BRC representations; P000 remains unchanged.

Separate exact identities, conditional or family-specific regularity theorems, computer-assisted certificates, and conjectural bridges toward arbitrary data. Improvements of constants or certified amplitudes are evidence about a method, not completion of the mother problem.

The portfolio may split, reprioritize, replicate, park, or close lines when evidence warrants it. It must not relabel a certificate for a restricted family as a uniform full-equation result.

## 2. Hard target and required outputs

Hard target: `NS_BRC_DRIVER_PORTFOLIO_ROUTED_WITH_REVIEW_GATES`.

Deliver all of the following:

1. A durable line dossier listing the current accepted-for-research frontier, open assumptions, known no-go results, reusable tools, and exact source pins.
2. A ranked portfolio of the active research lines in this publication batch, with one-sentence decision criteria for continue, split, park, or close.
3. An evidence matrix separating analytic proof obligations, finite exact checks, interval/computer-assisted steps, independent replication obligations, and literature-priority checks.
4. A falsification plan for the two most central bridges: uniform control of the causal propagator and integrability/control of the BRC jitter action.
5. A route for harvesting reusable mathematics into a general residual-certification/BRC toolkit without granting it theorem status beyond demonstrated interfaces.
6. A handoff rule: every researcher return must state what new information changes the portfolio and what unresolved unit remains.

## 3. Research value to preserve

The project now contains enough mutually dependent results that unmanaged continuation risks local optimization without progress on the global obstruction. A Driver line preserves the theorem hierarchy, forces competing routes and falsifiers to coexist, and turns the research stream into a portfolio that can absorb independent replication and cross-PDE evidence without losing the NS mother question.

## 4. Success, kill, and return criteria

Success is a compact, source-pinned Driver dossier and an explicit portfolio decision map that can route the published tasks without replaying settled derivations.

Kill any route that treats recent numerical success as roadmap authority, treats a restricted-family certificate as arbitrary-data regularity, suppresses counterexamples/no-go results, or lets a tooling improvement substitute for a mathematical bridge.

Return a concrete portfolio decision after each material task result. Close or park a line when its own kill condition is met rather than maintaining it for momentum.
