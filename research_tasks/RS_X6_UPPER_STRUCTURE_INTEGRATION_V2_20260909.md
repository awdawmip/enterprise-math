# X6 upper-structure operation-safe integration V2

Status: `PUBLISHED_REGISTERED / CLAIMABLE / DIRECT_USER_DIRECTION`

## Mother question

Can the distributed V1–V43 upper-layer results be expressed as one typed decorated X6 state/update interface whose projections are explicit and operation-safe for downstream mathematics?

## Frozen inputs and scope

Native spatial identity remains the current signed X6 Cell torsor. Rotation/path/holonomy, triadic-field/channel/network, event-time/causal state, and carrier/observer repair variables are decorations or relational state over spatial Cells, not extra spatial dimensions.

Consume the V1–V43 handoff and the three sibling successor returns when available. Do not silently choose a law for an unresolved sibling just to complete the schema. This task is integration, not Foundation admission.

## Hard target and required outputs

1. Define a machine-readable decorated-state product/fibration over X6 spatial Cells with separate typed components for rotation/path law, triadic/network law, causal-time state and observer repair.
2. Define update composition and prove type closure, source/target consistency, rotation covariance and preservation of required BRC provenance.
3. Enumerate forgetful maps to spatial endpoint, frame, path support, cycle current, carrier charts, C6/C12 phase and scalar/event clocks; for each, state the exact future-operation lease under which it is safe.
4. Reconcile the two carrier modes from V43 without equating carrier coordinates with native state.
5. Provide a compact downstream interface and at least two nontrivial reuse demonstrations, preferably one number-theory/geometric application and one physical/PDE-typing application, without importing their target definitions into the native state.

## Research value to preserve

A common upper-state interface prevents every downstream project from rebuilding incompatible versions of rotation, time, channels and observer repairs. It also makes information loss auditable and allows later Foundation review to evaluate one coherent candidate instead of dozens of scattered research notes.

## Success, kill, and return criteria

SUCCESS requires a type-closed integration contract with explicit projection safety and reproducible examples. If sibling laws remain unresolved, return a parametrized interface with those law slots explicit rather than fabricating defaults. KILL any proposed simplification when two full states with the same summary have different declared futures. Do not infer Foundation status, physical truth or universal minimality merely from successful integration.
