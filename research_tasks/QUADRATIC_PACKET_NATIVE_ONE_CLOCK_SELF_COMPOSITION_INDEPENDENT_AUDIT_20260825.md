## 0. Audit object

The only candidate under audit is the tightened NC3 principle identified in the frozen semantic audit packet:

`research_inputs/QUADRATIC_PACKET_NATIVE_ONE_CLOCK_SELF_COMPOSITION_SEMANTIC_AUDIT_PACKET_20260825.md`

Treat NC1 `one-clock` and NC2 `genuine finite downward collapse` as declared sector-typing conditions for purposes of this audit. Do not automatically treat NC3 as true, Foundation-native, or implied by those names.

The central question is whether NC3 has independent operational content beyond a restatement of the desired absence of intermediate residual precision.

## 1. Blind-forward phase

Before the independent raw semantic verdict is frozen, the route-specific source whitelist consists only of the frozen audit packet above.

Do not read the originating relation-first theorem, the originating candidate freeze, its Phase-B audit, the prior algebraic rank-two proof/audit, or the higher-residual route during this phase.

The blind-forward phase ends only when the auditor has frozen one complete semantic derivation, one explicit countermodel, one exact weaker replacement, or one rigorous circularity/no-go certificate.

Do not use the desirability of a quadratic/rank-two outcome as evidence for NC3.

## 2. Required independent work

Complete every audit obligation in the frozen packet. In particular:

1. decompose `primitive`, `one-clock`, `downward`, and `observable-complete` into explicit operational predicates and state which of them are premises rather than conclusions;
2. determine whether predictive sufficiency, future-equivalence, composition factorization, well-foundedness, kernel/fiber separation, or a comparably weak operational principle can imply NC3;
3. actively construct finite residual-depth systems of length at least three and decide whether any satisfies a defensible reading of all declared sector predicates;
4. separate a legitimate additional observable type from hidden state and from a merely redundant presentation label;
5. search for a strictly weaker principle than `R_2=empty or R_2~=R_1` that still supplies the required self-composition control without encoding the target conclusion;
6. identify precisely whether any use of `complete` or `primitive` is definitionally equivalent to the candidate itself;
7. keep the semantic verdict independent of any later linear-algebra or dual-number payoff.

Finite exhaustive search is allowed as a countermodel aid, but a bounded census does not prove a universal positive semantic theorem by itself.

If source comparison changes the raw verdict, preserve the original raw verdict and state the correction explicitly.

## 5. Outcome matrix

### PASS-A — independent operational justification

Use PASS-A only if NC3, or a stronger exact statement, is derived from strictly weaker operational premises without defining `primitive` or `complete` to exclude the target intermediate residual by fiat.

### PASS-B — valid but explicitly axiomatic

Use PASS-B if NC3 is coherent, non-circular, and falsifiable but cannot be derived from current weaker semantics. Classify it as an additional sector axiom candidate, not a Foundation theorem.

### PASS-C — weaker replacement

Use PASS-C if a strictly weaker non-circular principle is proved sufficient for the self-composition role and NC3 is unnecessarily strong.

### KILL — refutation or semantic circularity

Use KILL if a defensible finite countermodel violates NC3, or if the candidate has no explanatory content beyond a definition that directly excludes its own falsifier.

### NO-GO — unresolved

Use NO-GO if none of the preceding classifications can be established. Isolate the smallest unresolved semantic lemma and do not upgrade the candidate by plausibility.
