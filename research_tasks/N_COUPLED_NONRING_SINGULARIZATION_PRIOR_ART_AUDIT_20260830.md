# N-coupled non-ring singularization external prior-art audit

## Hard target

`NONRING_CRT_BREAK_PRIOR_ART_CLASSIFIED`.

Audit the exact surviving mechanism class rather than the phrase “non-ring”. The target is a public `N`-only process that uses state not representable by a fixed ring term, creates selective hidden-CRT behavior, and does not reveal a proper factor through a scalar support gcd before the selective-collapse layer.

## Minimum search classes

- Euclidean and continued-fraction state evolution;
- canonical residue representatives, quotient/remainder and carry dynamics;
- p-adic / Hensel-style lifts and valuation-triggered state;
- Pollard/Williams order methods and smoothness-driven state;
- rho/collision and cycle-detection processes;
- congruence-of-squares, Fermat/Lehman/Hart-type state;
- lattice/relation and modular linear-algebra factor extraction;
- any literature on CRT idempotent generation, zero divisors or nonunit discovery from public composite moduli.

For every material candidate, record the exact public inputs, hidden information assumptions, evolving state, stopping predicate, selective-collapse event and final factor extraction. Classify as `EXACT_DUPLICATE`, `STRICT_OR_PARTIAL_ANTECEDENT`, `ADJACENT_METHOD`, or `NO_MATERIAL_MATCH`.

A negative search is not novelty evidence.

## Required evidence

Freeze a source ledger with search date/surfaces/queries, a claim-to-source map, a return stating the duplication boundary, execution provenance, and a NEW Result-ID with complete Git-blob/SHA256 bindings.
