## Purpose

Preserve proof/provenance/semantic rigor during independent discovery **without preloading the example-rich Foundation/native-semantics audit policies or their existing research vocabulary**.

This shell contains only research-integrity constraints. It supplies no research question, axiom category, discovery lens, theorem family, current-result example, or preferred proof strategy.

Freeze:

`RIGOR_DOES_NOT_REQUIRE_AGENDA_PRELOAD`.

`PHASE_A_INTEGRITY_SHELL != PHASE_B_FULL_AUDIT_POLICY`.

## 1. Evidence honesty

Do not fabricate or upgrade:

- proof status;
- computation/tool output;
- hashes or provenance;
- novelty;
- validation coverage;
- canonical status.

Keep observed/computed/conjectured/proved/formally checked/canonical claims distinguishable at the strength actually justified.

A finite computation is evidence only for the scope actually computed unless an independent argument proves more.

## 2. Minimal semantic-layer grammar

When a Phase-A candidate needs semantic typing, use only this layer vocabulary:

- `N0_NATIVE_RELATIONAL` — what the candidate asserts as primitive/native;
- `I0_IMPLEMENTATION_CARRIER` — representation used to encode, calculate or check;
- `N1_DERIVED_OPERATIONAL_SEMANTICS` — structure constructed from the declared primitive layer;
- `N2_READOUT_COLLAPSE` — quotient/aggregate/readout applied after the operative structure;
- `N3_CONTINUUM_CLASSICAL` — external/effective/classical comparison layer.

Do not treat a convenient representation as native merely because it computes the object.

Do not promote a derived/readout/effective property into `N0` without an explicit derivation from the declared primitive substrate plus the relevant choice/relabeling-independence argument.

This grammar classifies a candidate; it does not suggest what candidate to invent.

## 3. Definition is not inherited

Success of an effective description is evidence to explain, not permission to copy its definitions into the primitive premises.

When Phase A proposes a primitive statement, record what is actually assumed rather than silently importing a target-side definition because it is familiar or successful.

## 4. Target-leakage guard

A target-side object, value, answer, benchmark, classification criterion or desired conclusion must not be inserted into the primitive premises merely to select a construction and then counted as having been derived.

If target/effective information is intentionally used, disclose it and type the result at the appropriate non-native layer until an independent native derivation exists.

## 5. Representation-leakage guard

Implementation convenience is not ontology.

If a theorem-critical conclusion depends on a chosen representation, orientation, labeling, ordering, coordinate, seed, tie-break, optimization rule or other implementation choice, do not call that conclusion native unless the dependency is eliminated or independently justified at the required semantic strength.

The shell does not name project-specific examples of these failure modes.

## 6. Discovery evidence and validation evidence

Freeze:

`DISCOVERY_EVIDENCE != INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION_EVIDENCE`.

Evidence used to choose, tune or reformulate the candidate is discovery evidence. It may still support the research, but must not be silently relabeled as an independent confirmation of the adaptively selected candidate.

Later evidence may reclassify the candidate; it must not rewrite the discovery record.

## 7. Negative-result preservation

Exact counterexamples, contradictions and failed candidate consequences are first-class research outputs.

Do not silently change premises after observing failure in order to preserve the original success narrative. A revised candidate is a new/revised candidate with explicit provenance.

## 8. Snapshot / premise ledger

Before candidate freeze, record enough to reproduce the discovery premises:

- primitive-substrate snapshot/ref;
- relevant worldview snapshot/ref or `none`;
- candidate statement;
- semantic layer claimed;
- implementation carrier(s), if any;
- evidence consumed during generation;
- obvious falsifiers/counterexamples already known;
- blindness status.

This is provenance, not a suggested research template beyond what is necessary to audit the candidate's origin.

## 9. Full policy is Phase B

The complete source policies remain authoritative for strong audit:

- `FOUNDATIONAL_LOGIC.md` / `foundational_logic.json`;
- `native_semantics_admissibility.json`.

During clean FREE Phase A, do **not** preload their bodies merely for reassurance. Their richer examples and enumerations are Phase-B audit material after the first candidate has been frozen.

If the user's current instruction explicitly makes one of those full policies part of the research premise, that explicit instruction controls and the resulting provenance is not represented as unexposed blind discovery.

## 10. No research-content payload

This shell intentionally does not contain:

- current theorem/result names;
- current route/task names;
- suggested open questions;
- preferred invariant/obstruction categories;
- discovery-lens examples;
- current project failures/successes;
- concrete classical target examples.

Its only job is to keep Phase-A reasoning honest while leaving question generation to the researcher.
