# R062 Stage 0 — Third-Direction Label Necessity Theorem

Researcher-ID: `EM-R062-7C4A91`  
Status: `UNLABELED_BRIDGE_REFUTED / COMPONENT_LABELED_BRIDGE_ACCEPTED`

## Theorem

On the frozen native carrier, endpoint reachability alone is insufficient to classify membership in a native component-trace line. Component labels/trace typing are necessary and, for the frozen local reverse-third witness, sufficient.

## Exact `(1,1)` witness

Fix translated sector `S_ij(P)`.

Two native trace linearizations are `X_i X_j` and `X_j X_i`. They are distinct prefix trajectories and terminate at the same typed terminal cell. The carrier also has a reverse-third-family one-step edge, denoted `-X_k`, terminating at that same carrier center.

The checker verifies this in all three sectors and all seven translated starts.

## U0 — unlabeled adjacency BRC

If the transition skeleton is reduced to unlabeled nearest-neighbor/end-point adjacency, all three witnesses contribute the same positive terminal support.

Boolean support therefore cannot tell whether the terminal was reached through the declared native component language or through the reverse-third carrier family.

Classification:

`UNLABELED_BRC_CANNOT_CLASSIFY_NATIVE_LINE_MEMBERSHIP`.

This is independent of Boolean multiplicity collapse: even an `N`-valued coefficient on the **wrong unlabeled skeleton** would count a shortcut that is not a member of the native trace.

## L0 — component-labeled BRC

Retain the generator family `step : G -> Rel X` with distinct component labels. The trace context in sector `S_ij(P)` admits the component language generated by `{X_i,X_j}`. The reverse-third carrier edge is labeled by the third family and is therefore outside that language.

Hence component typing separates `same endpoint` from `same native component trace`.

Classification:

`COMPONENT_LABELED_BRC_DISTINGUISHES_SAME_ENDPOINT_FROM_SAME_LINE`.

No graph jump count, carrier Euclidean length, or minimum-path rule is used in this classification.

## Consequence

Boolean BRC has two logically independent loss channels:

1. Boolean idempotence destroys multiplicity/provenance on an otherwise correct typed skeleton;
2. erasing component labels changes the semantic skeleton and can admit a non-native same-endpoint path.

Therefore coefficient enrichment alone cannot repair an unlabeled BRC bridge. The correct bridge is **component-typed first, carrier-enriched second**.
