1. **Zero** — `P=Q`, `D_f=D_r=(0,0,0)`, `BSEG={T_0}`, `SPEC={0,0}`. This is the only locus where inverse trace and canonical reverse trace coincide.
2. **One positive E1 tick** — `D_f=(1,0,0)`, `D_r=(0,1,1)`, `SPEC={1,sqrt(2)}`. Forward is a glued axis trace; reverse is `S_23(1,1)`.
3. **One carrier step opposite E1** — `D_f=(0,1,1)`, `D_r=(1,0,0)`. Same unordered spectrum `{1,sqrt(2)}`, reversed directed assignment, with no native negative axis.
4. **Translated (1,1)** — `D_f=(1,1,0)`, `D_r=(0,0,1)`, `SPEC={1,sqrt(2)}`. Forward has two shuffle representatives; reverse is a glued E3 axis identity.
5. **Translated 3-4-5** — `D_f=(3,4,0)`, `D_r=(1,0,4)`, `SPEC={sqrt(17),5}`. Forward canonical fiber `35`; reverse canonical fiber `5`; inverse forward fiber `35`.

7. **Axis-glued radial two-tick** — `D_f=(2,0,0)`, `D_r=(0,2,2)`, `SPEC={2,2sqrt(2)}`. Forward global axis identity retains two adjacent-chart trajectory supports.
8. **Noninteger radical pair** — `D_f=(2,3,0)`, `D_r=(1,0,3)`, `SPEC={sqrt(10),sqrt(13)}`.
9. **Inverse versus canonical reverse** — on the unit axis, the inverse uses one reverse carrier edge while the canonical reverse uses the positive `(1,1)` trace. On `3-4-5`, inverse-fiber cardinality is `35` versus canonical-reverse cardinality `5`.
