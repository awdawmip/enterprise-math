## Observable quotient theorem

For one connected consistent unanchored XOR component,

`b_v=a xor p_v`.

Choose root `r` and define `d_v=b_v xor b_r`. The `d_v` are invariant under simultaneous complement and separate the two-element complement orbits. Hence every exact complement-invariant function of the component branch assignment factors through the quotient coordinates `d`.

Thus the maximal invariant subalgebra is the fixed algebra `A^Gamma`; on branch coordinates it consists exactly of exact functions of rooted relative parities together with branch-insensitive declared data.

The full registry does not factor because

`C_v=L_v+(U_v-L_v)b_v`

maps under complement to

`L_v+U_v-C_v`.

For a genuine two-endpoint gap this differs from `C_v`.

## Observable typing

Pair XOR, rooted parity, cycle parity, straight relative edges, unanchored satisfiability, affine-conservation truth, completion membership and completion idempotence are complement invariant.

Individual completed values and signed residues are complement distinguishing. They are legitimate post-branch mathematical readouts, but are circular if their candidate values are used to initialize the same branch.

A fixed independently supplied upstream singleton or endpoint certificate is a legitimate anchor only when the complete reduced Boolean feasible set is singleton before branch realization.

## Cross-scale anchors

Stage R separates anchor source from anchor propagation.

A genuine fine singleton propagates to coarse variables; a genuine coarse singleton propagates to fine variables. In a disconnected system only the anchored component becomes absolute. Contradictory multi-anchors give an empty feasible set. A consistent parity cycle plus one anchor has one exact assignment.

Relative synchronization therefore propagates anchors but does not establish where the anchor came from.

## Straightness

Straightness remains only the relative edge law

`b_(k+1) xor b_k=0`.

Without an anchor there are two complementary constant realizations. With independent `b_0=h`, the chain is uniquely `h`. Straightness propagates but does not source the global origin.

## Generalization boundaries

- More than two legal completion states is not a free transitive Z2 branch fiber.
- Nonlinear Boolean relations need not preserve complement or affine-coset structure.
- Disconnected components retain independent origins.
- Cyclic unary-only propagation can lose parity/holonomy information.
- Absolute endpoint-valued observables break quotient sufficiency.
- Observable quotient invariance does not establish physical gauge equivalence.
