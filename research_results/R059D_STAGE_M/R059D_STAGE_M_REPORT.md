## Structural theorems

### Componentwise cone dominance

Every frozen functional has strictly positive launch/incidence coefficients.

Hence if candidate `d` satisfies

`A_d >= A_e` and `I_d >= I_e`

for every competitor, with at least one strict inequality against each competitor, then every frozen grammar uniquely selects `d`.

### Endpoint-functional equivalence

If two microgrammars induce the same endpoint functional for every seed pair, then they produce the same six endpoint-count vector and the same BRC6 winner/unresolved status for every state.

This proves G1/G2/G5 equivalence independently of their internal split location/organization.

This is a strict conflicting-winner counterexample generated by distinct raw histories. It blocks grammar-independent native canonicality on the declared registry.
