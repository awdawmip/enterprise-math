# Enterprise Math Foundational Logic

Status: `ACTIVE / PROJECT-LEVEL FOUNDATIONAL RESEARCH LOGIC`

This file is not a theorem catalog and not a research roadmap. It records the highest-level reasoning constraints that govern how Enterprise Math uses successful classical/continuous mathematics while rebuilding the native foundation.

Project-level definition:

- `PROJECT_DEFINITION.md`
- `PROJECT_DEFINITION.zh-CN.md`
- `project_definition.json`

## Core principle

> **Do not inherit an effective theory's definitions into the native substrate merely because the theory is successful. Preserve the success as evidence and require the native theory to explain why that success emerges.**

Equivalent short form:

> **Definition is not inherited. Success is evidence. Explain the success from a smaller native logic.**

## Project-level clarification — REFOUND, NOT REJECT

Enterprise Math does **not** interpret `Definition is not inherited` as `Concept is forbidden`.

The project-level mode is:

`REFOUND, NOT REJECT`.

This means:

- mature concepts may be retained;
- mature notation and conditional mathematics may be retained;
- successful classical formulas remain evidence, calibration targets, and interoperability targets;
- a classical definition is withheld only from automatic promotion into the native substrate;
- the concept may later receive a rebuilt discrete / precision-aware definition;
- the rebuilt definition must then be compared against the classical object and classified by exact recovery, finite-precision recovery, asymptotic recovery, domain-restricted recovery, systematic deviation, or nonrecovery.

Therefore VECTOR, LENGTH, DISTANCE, ANGLE, NORM, DOT/PAIRING, SIN, COS, TAN, AREA, VOLUME, PI, Euclidean geometry, and continuum models are **not project-wide forbidden vocabulary**.

What is forbidden is circular foundational reasoning: importing the target definition into the premise and then calling its reappearance a derivation.

A task may withhold a tool from its own premise without denying the concept globally. Preferred task-level language is, for example:

- `LENGTH_NOT_USED_AS_THIS_TASK_PREMISE`;
- `ANGLE_NOT_USED_AS_THIS_TASK_PREMISE`;
- `TRIG_NOT_USED_AS_THIS_TASK_PREMISE`;
- `CLASSICAL_LENGTH_DEFINITION_WITHHELD_FROM_NATIVE_PREMISE`.

Detailed geometric-tool policy is recorded in `GEOMETRIC_TOOL_REFOUNDATION_POLICY.md`.

## Active native-base specialization — packet / path foundation

For packet-native Enterprise Math work, `PACKET_PATH_FOUNDATION.md` and `packet_path_foundation.json` are the active base-ontology specialization of this logic.

The specialization fixes the current minimal foundation:

- every crystal packet has native quantity exactly `1` in every dimension and at every declared precision layer;
- dimension belongs to packet relations, not to a dimension-dependent weight carried by one packet;
- finite occupied content is packet counting;
- every actual adjacency transition contributes exactly `1` to transition/path count;
- packet count is a static object count and transition count is a dynamic event count;
- a path is an arbitrary adjacency walk and may revisit, loop, reverse immediately, or reuse an adjacency;
- refinement never fractionalizes the native packet unit: a finer packet is still `1` in its own precision layer;
- classical line, straightness, shortest-path distance, geometric length, edge, boundary, perimeter, chord, angle, curvature, area, volume, and continuum-geometric definitions are not automatically inherited as packet-native N0 primitives;
- those geometric concepts remain legitimate targets for explicit reconstruction from the packet/path substrate or from additional declared semantics;
- effective area/volume/d-dimensional content may later be read from packet counts under carrier/precision semantics, or be rebuilt by richer geometric definitions, rather than being assumed from powers of a prior length at N0;
- path count is not automatically geometric length; a rebuilt LENGTH may later be related to path count by a separately proved correspondence theorem;
- optional finite channel structure is relational and carrier-specific; in an ideal six-channel state `I_x`, `O_x`, and `M_x[a,b]` may be counted, including same-channel ingress/egress, without requiring a classical angle/turn interpretation as an N0 premise;
- temperature is a later physical calibration question, not a mathematical premise for channel relations.

When this specialization and an older edge/line/metric-first construction conflict at N0, this specialization controls the native typing. The older mathematics may remain valid after retyping to N1/N2/N3 or after an explicit refoundation certificate.

## FL-01 — Definition is not evidence of nativeness

A definition belonging to a successful classical, continuous, Euclidean, probabilistic, spectral, or engineering theory is not thereby a native definition of nature.

This rule withholds the **definition**, not the **concept**.

If an effective theory uses objects such as point, center, distance, radius, equidistance, circle, smoothness, real continuum, classical pi, Gaussian density, Fourier mode, or any other derived structure, those objects remain on the effective/output side until independently declared native or derived from the native substrate with an admissible certificate. Their concepts and correct conditional mathematics may still be used at the appropriate layer.

## FL-02 — Engineering success is positive evidence

The empirical and engineering success of classical mathematics is not discarded. It is one of the strongest available constraints on any replacement foundation.

What is preserved is not the inherited ontology, but the demonstrated regularity:

- which controlled inputs lead to which observable outputs;
- over which scale regimes the relation is reliable;
- which independent measurement/engineering channels agree;
- what error envelope is achieved;
- which compositions and interventions preserve the relation.

A candidate foundation that cannot explain a large body of robust engineering success is weakened or killed.

## FL-03 — Engineering-success inversion

The foundational direction is an inverse problem:

`engineering success -> surviving structure -> native mechanism/collapse -> rebuilt tool -> effective classical law`.

Do not start from the classical definition and force the native world to approximate it. Start from the native substrate and ask what minimal native rules, collapse mechanisms, admissible geometric/analytic reconstructions, and readouts can explain why the successful effective law appears.

## FL-04 — No output-copying into the input

A research route is circular if it imports a target-side structure into the native premises and then celebrates recovery of that same structure.

Examples of forbidden shortcuts unless explicitly declared/derived at the correct stratum:

- defining a native circle by equal distance from a center when native distance/equidistance is absent;
- using classical pi as a loss function to select the collapse rule whose output is then claimed to explain pi;
- treating an implementation embedding as native geometry because the effective model uses Euclidean coordinates;
- using a successful continuum law as a hidden premise in a claimed derivation of that continuum law.

## FL-05 — Explain success, do not merely fit one readout

A strong foundational candidate should compress many independent engineering regularities with few native assumptions.

Agreement with one scalar, one formula, one benchmark, or one readout is weak evidence. Strong evidence comes from one native mechanism explaining multiple independent observables, scales, compositions, interventions, and error envelopes without adding the target definitions back into the premises.

## FL-06 — Foundation lane and calibration lane are distinct

Two research lanes may coexist:

- `FOUNDATION`: derive native structures/collapse rules and rebuilt tool definitions without importing effective definitions as native premises;
- `CALIBRATION`: test whether already-derived native/rebuilt structures reproduce or explain established engineering successes.

Calibration may rank explanatory power, but it must not retroactively make the effective definitions native. Agreement between independently derived native structure and engineering success is strong cross-validation. Disagreement is also evidence and must be preserved.

## FL-07 — Effective mathematics is conserved under retyping

A foundational challenge to the definitions of continuous mathematics does not invalidate its correct conditional mathematics or engineering usefulness.

Correct effective results remain usable at their declared semantic layer. Mature tools should be preserved whenever they remain correct and useful.

The research question is why they work so well, where they stop working, what information their abstraction removes, which native structures survive that abstraction, and whether a rebuilt definition can recover the mature tool with clearer precision semantics.

## FL-08 — Operational precision is a downstream result

Only after a native mechanism, its effective bridge/readout, the allowed future language, the physical/computational resolution, and error amplification are fixed may Enterprise Math ask how much precision an effective constant such as classical pi actually needs for a declared engineering task.

Such a precision ceiling is an effective/operational result. It does not by itself define the native constant or prove that the classical definition is foundationally correct.

## FL-09 — Unit packet is dimension-independent

For packet-native work, one crystal packet contributes native quantity `1` regardless of dimension, external shape, or declared precision layer. Dimension changes admissible relations, not the packet's unit quantity.

## FL-10 — Native content is integer packet count

The native content of a finite occupied packet configuration is its packet count. Effective area, volume, or higher-dimensional content may be downstream readouts of this count plus carrier/precision semantics, or later rebuilt geometric quantities. They are not assumed at N0 merely as primitive powers of an inherited classical length.

## FL-11 — Native process is integer transition count

Every actual transition across declared adjacency contributes exactly `1` to transition/path count.

Transition/path count is an event count. It is **not automatically identical to geometric LENGTH**.

A future rebuilt LENGTH is allowed and may coincide with, differ from, or be related to transition count on a proved domain.

## FL-12 — Object count and event count are distinct

Packet/occupancy count and transition/path count are different foundational integer quantities. A revisit may increase event count without increasing object count.

Neither count is automatically promoted to a geometric quantity merely because a classical model uses a similar number.

## FL-13 — Refinement changes language, not the unit

Every finer packet remains native quantity `1`. Refinement may replace one coarse packet by many finer packets, but native fractional weights such as `1/r` are not introduced merely to preserve a continuum measure.

A rebuilt geometric measure may later assign a precision-dependent readout to a configuration, but that is a separate semantic construction.

## FL-14 — Geometry is refounded, not rejected

Classical definitions of line, straightness, distance, length, shortestness, edge, boundary, perimeter, chord, angle, curvature, radius, area, volume, norm, inner product, and continuum geometry are not automatically N0 primitives in packet-native work.

This does **not** mean those concepts are prohibited.

They may be:

- explicitly declared as additional task semantics;
- reconstructed from N0/N1 structure;
- promoted by an admissible definability/invariance certificate;
- used as N2/N3 readouts;
- calibrated against Euclidean geometry and engineering success.

The foundational rule is only that no successful effective definition may be imported into N0 to manufacture its own recovery.

## FL-15 — Mature-tool refoundation requires recovery classification

When Enterprise Math rebuilds a mature tool such as LENGTH, ANGLE, NORM, DOT/PAIRING, SIN, COS, TAN, AREA, VOLUME, or PI semantics, the result should record its relation to the classical object using an explicit recovery class when applicable:

- `EXACT_RECOVERY`;
- `FINITE_PRECISION_RECOVERY`;
- `ASYMPTOTIC_RECOVERY`;
- `DOMAIN_RESTRICTED_RECOVERY`;
- `SYSTEMATIC_DEVIATION`;
- `NONRECOVERY`.

A deviation is not automatically an improvement; it becomes scientifically important only when it is derived, reproducible, and supported by stronger mathematical or empirical evidence.

## FL-16 — Stage-specific withholding is not project-wide prohibition

A task may freeze that LENGTH, ANGLE, TRIG, metric, probability, or another mature tool is not a premise for that task.

Such a statement is local semantic typing only. It must not be rewritten as a project-level claim that the concept does not exist or is permanently forbidden.

## Research discipline

For foundation-facing tasks that consume successful classical/engineering mathematics, record explicitly:

1. the native substrate;
2. the effective/classical definitions being withheld from native premises;
3. the mature concepts/tools retained for later reconstruction or calibration;
4. the engineering-success constraints being consumed as evidence;
5. the candidate native mechanism/collapse/readout bridge;
6. the rebuilt tool definition, if any;
7. the classical recovery/deviation class, if any;
8. which successes are explained and which remain unexplained;
9. whether the explanatory success was obtained independently or by target leakage;
10. the weakest justified ontology statement.

The governing asymmetry is permanent:

`classical definition --X--> native premise`

unless separately declared/derived, while

`classical concept --> retained mathematical tool vocabulary`

and

`engineering success --> evidence constraint`

are encouraged.
