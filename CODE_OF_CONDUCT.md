# Code of Conduct

## Principle

Enterprise Math is a research project built to expose claims to criticism.

Disagreement, attempted falsification, negative results, and counterexamples are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, proofs, definitions, and evidence rather than people.
- State uncertainty and claim status clearly.
- Give credit for prior work and contributions.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately humiliate contributors.
- Do not use project discussions for spam or unrelated promotion.
- Accept that a strong counterexample is a successful contribution.

## Research disputes

When contributors disagree, prefer:

1. precise definitions;
2. explicit examples;
3. proof or counterexample;
4. reproducible computation;
5. primary-source prior art.

Maintainers may separate unresolved interpretations from established mathematical results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect productive research.

Decisions should be based on behavior, not whether a contributor supports or opposes the project's hypotheses.
