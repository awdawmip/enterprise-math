## 3. Arbitrarily long explicit collisions for every fixed d
Let A and B denote two formal letters. Define U_0=A, V_0=B and recursively U_{n+1}=U_n V_n, V_{n+1}=V_n U_n. Both words have length 2^n; U_n starts with A and V_n starts with B, hence they differ as concrete words. Yet J_<=n(U_n)=J_<=n(V_n) for every n>=1.
Proof: Write P_n=S(U_n), Q_n=S(V_n), D_n=P_n-Q_n and let I be the positive-degree ideal. Initially D_0 in I. If D_n in I^(n+1), then D_{n+1}=P_n Q_n-Q_n P_n = D_n(Q_n-1)-(Q_n-1)D_n in I^(n+2), since Q_n-1 in I. Thus all coefficients through degree n vanish in D_n. The leading-letter distinction proves literal-history inequality. No computation or limit argument is used for this universal free-word statement.
Exact independent integer checker: n=1,2,3,4,5,6; word lengths 2,4,8,16,32,64; all pairs equal through degree n and unequal when retaining degree n+1. This finite check corroborates the recurrence, not the universal proof.
