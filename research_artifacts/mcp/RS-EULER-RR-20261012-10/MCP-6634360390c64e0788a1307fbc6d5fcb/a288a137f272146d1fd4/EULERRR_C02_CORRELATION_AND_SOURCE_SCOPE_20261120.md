**Exact correlation transport:** Take any finite ensemble of complete worlds with positive rational weights w_s, sum w_s=1, and labels j_s,a_s,r_s∈F3. Introduce an abstract phase symbol ω satisfying ω³=1, ω≠1, ω²+ω+1=0. Define C_XY=Σ_s w_s ω^{X_s−Y_s} (phases multiply *within the same world* before weighted averaging). For E(j,a,r)=(j,a,r+j−a), the readout r'_s−j_s=r_s−a_s holds pointwise. Therefore C_RS(after)=C_RA(before), without any independence hypothesis. By Reω=Reω²=−1/2, Re C_RS=1−(3/2)(q_1+q_2), where q_k is total positive weight with r−a=k. Thus C_RS=1 iff q_1=q_2=0, i.e. every *supported* world was prepared r=a. This does not require all 27 states to have positive weight.

**Strictly positive full support comparison:** Let U be uniform 27-world law; let P assign 1/9 to nine r=a worlds and zero elsewhere, and M=(P+U)/2. Both U and M have identical uniform one-label marginals, while C_RS(M)=2/3+(1/6)(ω+ω²)=1/2 and C_RS(U)=(1+ω+ω²)/3=0. This is an independent strictly positive full-support instance different from a pure diagonal preparation; it corroborates the author's limitation on marginal-only inference.
