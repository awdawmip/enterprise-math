## Theorem 1: generated group
The subgroup H of GL(6,Z) generated by one such signed cycle for each 3-axis subset is exactly the signed monomial matrices with even *underlying unsigned axis permutation*:
H ≅ (C2)^6 ⋊ A6, order 2^6*(6!/2) = 23040.

Proof. Every generator projects to a 3-cycle, hence H is contained in the displayed group. Its cube is the triple sign-flip F_ijk. For distinct i,j, select two other axes k,l; F_ikl F_jkl=F_ij, producing all pair sign flips (the even-weight sign subgroup). Any F_ijk is odd-weight, so all 2^6 diagonal sign flips are generated. Multiplying each r_ijk by an appropriate diagonal sign flip produces an unsigned 3-cycle. Those generate all A6; the normal sign subgroup and this surjection establish H=(C2)^6⋊A6. ## Theorem 2: operation-safe complete path-word lift
Let Σ* be the free monoid of finite signed primitive-step words, δ(w) its signed endpoint sum. Every g∈H induces a letterwise bijection ĝ on Σ*. For every u,v,w:
ĝ(uv)=ĝ(u)ĝ(v), δ(ĝ(w))=gδ(w), and ĝ has inverse widehat(g^-1).
For all N and d, this is an exact bijection between the full *ordered word fibers*
{w∈Σ^N:δ(w)=d} ↔ {w∈Σ^N:δ(w)=gd}.
Thus full native ordered-path multiplicities and N-BRC counts are equivariant, and future word concatenation is safe as the full word is preserved. Weighted BRC counts transform accordingly only when step weights are invariant under the letter map, or when the weights themselves are transported. Projecting to endpoint, min-zero or branch-collapsed phase requires separate observer/future-operation safety. This theorem does not itself supply native time, Cell trajectory, triadic event legality or a global frame-change law.

Proof. A signed monomial g bijects the signed alphabet. Its letterwise extension preserves concatenation and has inverse g^-1. Endpoint commutation follows from linearity; word fiber and multiplicity follow immediately.
