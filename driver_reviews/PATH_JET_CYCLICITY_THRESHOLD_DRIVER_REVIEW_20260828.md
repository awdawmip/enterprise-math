## 2. Accepted theorem

Let

`V_n = Sym^n(Z^2)`

and let `L_n` be the integer lattice generated by the oriented two-segment path jets

`J_n(u,v)=det(u,v) Sym^n([u v]) w_n`

with

`w_n=((X+Y)^(n+1)-X^(n+1))/Y`.

For a prime `p`, let

`Lbar_(n,p)=(L_n+pV_n)/pV_n`.

Then for every prime `p`:

1. **Subcritical full cyclicity**

   `n < p(p-1) => Lbar_(n,p)=Sym^n(F_p^2)`.

2. **Critical first failure**

   At `n=p(p-1)`,

   `Lbar_(n,p)=C_p`,

   where `C_p` is the subspace of degree-`p(p-1)` binary forms whose restriction to `P^1(F_p)` is constant.

3. **Exact critical defect**

   `codim(C_p)=p`.

4. **Exact integral p-primary cokernel**

   `(V_n/L_n)_(p) ~= (Z/p)^p` at `n=p(p-1)`.

Hence

`FIRST_BAD_p = p(p-1)`.

## 3. A2 proof audit

The accepted A2 mechanism is not finite-rank extrapolation.

The proof uses the affine Borel action on dehomogenized polynomials and the coordinate

`z=x^p-x`,

which transforms by `z(cx+d)=c z(x)`. Writing `m=n+1=kp+r`, the subcritical module decomposes into the exact `H`-stable blocks

`direct_sum_(a<k) z^a P_(p-1)`

plus the partial block `z^k P_(r-1)` when `r>0`.

Each block has one-dimensional top. The full-block top coordinate of the seed is exactly

`tau_a = C(k,a+1) + 1_(k-a+r>=p) C(k,a)`.

No-carry and one-carry cases are both nonzero throughout the strict subcritical range. The only duplicated top character occurs at

`a_0=k+r-p`

when `k+r>=p`.

The Weyl mate has zero coordinate on every full-block top and partial top coordinate `r`. Therefore on the duplicated top the two seeds give

`(tau_(a_0),1)` and `(0,r)`,

with determinant

`r tau_(a_0) != 0`.

Thus the unique Borel top collision is repairable by the Weyl seed below the critical degree, and the full `GL_2(F_p)` orbit spans the entire symmetric power.

This correctly explains the earlier finite phenomenon in which Borel-only rank may drop while `GL_2` rank remains full.

## 4. Critical transition audit

At

`n=p(p-1)`, `m=(p-1)p+1`, so `k=p-1,r=1`.

Then

`tau_0 = C(p-1,1)+C(p-1,0) = -1+1 = 0 mod p`.

The Weyl mate still has zero full-block top coordinate, so the first duplicated top becomes unrepairable exactly at the critical degree.

This structural failure agrees with A1, which independently identifies the critical image as `C_p` and proves exact codimension `p`.

Thus the threshold is not inferred from the A1 defect or from regression: A2 proves that no earlier degree fails, and A1 identifies the first failure exactly.

## 5. A1 proof audit

`D_1=X^pY-XY^p`

`D_2=(X^(p^2)Y-XY^(p^2))/D_1`.

`C_p=F_p D_2 direct_sum D_1 Sym^(p^2-2p-1)`.
