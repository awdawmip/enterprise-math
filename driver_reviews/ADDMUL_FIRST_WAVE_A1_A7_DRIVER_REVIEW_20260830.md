## Cross-route synthesis

The accepted first wave supports the following portfolio-level classification.

1. A1 and A2 are exact defect/cross-effect reconstruction routes: multiplication is recovered from additive data plus a nonlinear observable, with different exceptional/fiber structures.
2. A3 is an exact law-reparametrization route: the binary law itself is changed and transported to multiplication, with injectivity controlled by `c`.
3. A4 is an exact hidden-coordinate/image route: both operations linearize only after a nonlinear transform plus a validity predicate and divisor-locality discipline.
4. A5 is an intentionally lossy invariant route: multiplication is exact, addition requires cancellation/unit information and dynamic precision.
5. A6 is an exact typed linear-coordinate bridge but not an operation-algebra intertwiner.
6. A7 supplies the common audit language needed to compare all of the above without conflating coordinate equivalence, homomorphic image, finite typed embedding, lossy invariant, and correlation probe.

No one route currently dominates the others under a single operation-safe cost order. The new information gap is therefore to build the exact comparison/composability atlas and determine whether there is a minimal common bridge kernel or a proof of irreducible incomparability.
