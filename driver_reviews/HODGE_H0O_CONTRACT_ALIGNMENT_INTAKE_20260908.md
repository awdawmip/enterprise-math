## Why the whole declared family is instantiable

The source family is all pure codimension-three coherent sheaves on the fixed polarized abelian sixfold; no WIT, semihomogeneity or exceptional-projection condition is part of its definition [S2, lines 105–109]. The normalized Poincare bundle and the actual polarization isogeny already define Q and Psi_Q on that same target.

A direct paper-level check separates family existence from existence of a desired member. On this projective sixfold choose a sufficiently high power of an ample polarization so that it is very ample. Three sufficiently general divisors form a smooth complete intersection Z of dimension three; O_Z is pure of codimension three. Thus F=O_Z and the return's Q give an instance without any split or discriminant-minus-one input. Its cycle is a scalar multiple of theta^3 and the inherited projector kills that divisor-cube line. This is an ordinary zero-projection member, not a positive seed and not a counterexample to the conservation theorem.

No new experiment or numerical calculation was used for this observation. It is the standard geometric consequence of the target being polarized and projective. Even if the phrase 'family cannot be instantiated' were intended to mean only a successful positive member, the return proves a necessary-and-sufficient reduction to the unresolved seed-existence problem, not nonexistence or failure of an additional target-side geometric prerequisite.

## What is mathematically worth preserving

Conditional on the frozen H0N carrier, polarization and projector premises, the displayed argument has a substantive chain beyond repeating 'a seed is needed':

1. With td(A)=1 and the Poincare class in H^1(A) tensor H^1(Ahat), integration over A makes the cohomological transform send H^n to H^(12-n). Hence target ch_3 receives only source ch_3; higher/lower Chern components cannot create it.
2. Complementary source directions in the middle-degree Poincare pairing and Rosati conjugation for the polarization yield preservation of each endo B_p block. In particular both W_K=B_0 direct-sum B_6 and its complementary sum are preserved. The scalar selector Pi_W therefore commutes with T_lambda.
3. The Poincare transform and rational isogeny pullback are invertible, so T_lambda is invertible on W_K. The leading Chern-character term of a pure codimension-three sheaf is its support cycle with generic multiplicities.

These give the stated identity and equivalence without a hidden WIT or semihomogeneous-output assumption. In this limited paper audit no defect was found in that conditional cohomological argument. The H0N sources [S4–S5] explicitly distinguish their algebraic projector from a seed and prohibit a blanket theorem about all Fourier–Mukai outputs. Their finite polynomial certificates and external geometric premises were not independently recomputed or re-established here.

The support-cycle condition does not by itself settle existence. A rational algebraic cycle can have signed rational coefficients and need not literally equal a single sheaf's effective support cycle; however, if its exceptional projection is nonzero, linearity gives an irreducible component with nonzero projection, whose structure sheaf supplies an effective pure-support candidate. Thus the existential reduction is meaningful while remaining an unresolved reduction, not a theorem of non-instantiability.
