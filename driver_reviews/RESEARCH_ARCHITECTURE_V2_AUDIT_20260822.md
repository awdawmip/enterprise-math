## Audit question

Does the research control plane preserve independent discovery **and** decisive task execution without letting recent routes, scheduler state, tools, representation choices, successful-stage momentum, Working Truth, Foundation backflow, continuity snapshots, candidate repackaging, or repository read order silently choose the next research question?

## Findings repaired

1. Free research was previously a waiting/topic-supplied role rather than autonomous axiom discovery.
2. Generic scheduler/Common-Surface fields could redirect no-user-task research into exploitation.
3. `RESEARCHER` lacked a machine distinction between free discovery and selected-task execution.
4. Raw axiom candidates lacked a maturity boundary before Working Truth/Foundation intake.
5. Candidate origin could be lost when repackaged as a Driver-selected task.
6. Stage success could mechanically generate another numbered stage.
7. A continuation could be mislabeled `NEW_DIRECTION` to evade a weak gate.
8. Continuation review did not require considering closure/other route/free exploration.
9. Foundation backflow/Steward lacked an explicit raw-candidate maturity gate.
10. Driver Continuity could become theorem/route memory and imply one default next route.
11. Common Surface was too agenda-rich to remain a default context dump.
12. Discovery evidence and independent validation evidence were not explicitly separated.
13. A new Researcher-ID could be mistaken for independent discovery despite shared ambient context.

## Final architecture

Four functions are separated:

- `FREE_AXIOM_DISCOVERY` — foundation-only independent question/axiom search;
- `TASK_RESEARCH` — selected mother-question execution;
- `RESEARCH_DRIVER` — portfolio/routing/Working Truth/origin-lineage/successor/promotion control;
- `FOUNDATION_STEWARD` — shared maintenance and verification.

Candidate lifecycle:

`DISCOVERY -> FROZEN CANDIDATE -> PHASE-B AUDIT -> DRIVER INTAKE -> TASK/FQ/REPLICATION/PARK/REJECT -> NORMAL PROOF/PROMOTION`.

Freeze:

`RAW_AXIOM_CANDIDATE != WORKING_TRUTH != CANONICAL_FOUNDATION`.

`DISCOVERY_EVIDENCE != INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION_EVIDENCE`.

## Origin/provenance invariant

A task created from free discovery must retain:

- `origin_kind=FREE_AXIOM_CANDIDATE`;
- candidate ID;
- audited candidate state.

It may not be relabeled `DRIVER_ROADMAP` merely because the Driver selected it.

`TASK_ORIGIN_AND_LINEAGE_CANNOT_BE_ERASED_BY_RENAMING`.

## Successor-stage invariant

`PASS_IS_NOT_A_SUCCESSOR_TRIGGER`.

A new continuation requires parent task, exact new information gap, why parent does not close it, discriminating outcomes, kill condition, explicit consideration of alternative route/closure/free exploration, and why another stage/task is the better information-producing move.

Explicit Stage 2+ taskbooks must be `CONTINUATION`; renaming a semantic continuation does not reset lineage.

The taskbook contract/tool enforce the lexical and metadata gates. Driver review enforces semantic lineage where lexical checks cannot infer it.

## Anti-anchoring / independence invariant

Free Phase A withholds scheduler/tasks/Relay/PR/recent commits, success/failure catalogs, Working Truth, Driver Continuity, recent project journal/candidate packets, ambient recent project memory, tool availability/convenience, and coordinate/file/name ordering as discovery priors.

A clean blind claim requires a clean pre-candidate context and pinned foundation/worldview snapshot. Already-exposed contexts are typed `ANCHOR_EXPOSED`.

Independent replication uses separate fresh contexts and candidate isolation until each run freezes its own packet. A fresh identity alone does not prove independence; shared ambient context must be disclosed.
