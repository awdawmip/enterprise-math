## 3. Exact remaining gap

The issued F3R Q1 requires:

> classify exactly which `A in GL_2(Z)` can occur in at least one survivor, and classify **all second columns** up to physical equivalence.

The return does not yet prove this.

What is proved is:

1. an exact iff theorem for the **first column**;
2. an exact iff theorem for free blocks preserving the particular support-splitting scalar `q_{p,r}`;
3. for every admissible first column, existence of an infinite arithmetic progression of successful second columns.

What is **not** proved is either of the following exhaustive statements:

- every second column completing an admissible first column is a survivor; or
- every survivor belongs to one of the displayed support-splitting congruence strata `S_{p,r}` (or to another explicitly classified family).

Thus there may still exist free blocks outside every exhibited `S_{p,r}` that admit some other nonnegative conserved scalar.

The positive-cone object `Q(M)` does not by itself close this gap. It classifies all `q` **conditional on a fixed `M`**, but the report does not reduce

`Q(M) != empty`

into an independent necessary-and-sufficient structural criterion on arbitrary `A` (and arbitrary full lifts `(A,B,D)`).

Therefore the current statement

`F3R_FREE_BLOCK_SURVIVOR_FAMILY_CLASSIFIED`

is too strong.
