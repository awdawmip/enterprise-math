## 4. Why the torsor result is not enough

The key logical distinction is:

`INVARIANT ORBIT / MULTISET READOUT != CANONICAL DISTINGUISHED ELEMENT`.

The allocation audit proves that appropriate symmetric readouts survive presentation changes. A universal breaker, however, is theorem-facing semantic structure. Unless the breaker itself is defined as an intrinsic orbit-valued/canonical object, or an invariant predicate proves that every admissible representative yields the same downstream observable, selecting one representative reintroduces exactly the non-native semantic step that the Foundation audit is supposed to eliminate.

Likewise:

`ARITHMETIC BOUND GIVEN BREAKER != NATIVE DERIVATION OF BREAKER CAPACITY`.

A proof of `q_b <= 5` after assuming that `b` is a universal breaker remains useful mathematics, but it cannot certify that `q_b` is already a current-Foundation observable.
