### Dickson/Joukowski value-set theory

Wun-Seng Chou, Javier Gomez-Calderon, Gary L. Mullen,
**Value sets of Dickson polynomials over finite fields**, Journal of Number Theory 30 (1988), 334--344, DOI `10.1016/0022-314X(88)90006-6`.

This literature determines Dickson polynomial value-set cardinalities. Later finite-field work continues to use explicit Dickson image/preimage formulas.

### Small value-set polynomial classification

J. Gomez-Calderon and D. J. Madden,
**Polynomials with small value set over finite fields**, Journal of Number Theory 28 (1988), 167--188, DOI `10.1016/0022-314X(88)90064-9`.

This places Dickson polynomials among classical small-value-set families.

Consequence:

- extremal/small Joukowski image cardinality is not itself a new phenomenon.
