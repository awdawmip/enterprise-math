## Bounded new unit

Because the native gate remains closed, the run performed one new bounded checkpoint using the task's attached independent 3D probe formulas rather than substituting them for spectralDNS. The retained model is classical periodic incompressible Fourier flow with exact complex support and Leray projection.

Frozen surrogate configuration:

- `n=16`, retained cube `|k_i|<=4`, satisfying `N>3K` for the retained-cube aliasing condition used by the attached probe;
- complex128/float64, SciPy FFT `norm=forward`, one FFT worker;
- viscosity `nu=0.01`, zero Source, `dt=5e-4`, five classical fixed-step RK4 steps;
- strict support predicate `h != 0`; no approximate pruning;
- sparse route uses pair-grouped direct convolution; fallback uses the same dense rotational-form FFT nonlinearity and the same projection/retained cube;
- JIT warmup is reported separately from trajectory timing.

A small same-host calibration over 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 and 256 full modes selected a surrogate-only route threshold of 256 modes. All six sparse-vs-dense single-RHS comparisons had relative max errors between `2.87e-16` and `6.69e-16`. This threshold is not portable and is not a native-host policy.

| 91007 | 64 | 1 | 19 | `1.073e-17` | `0` | `0.007825` | `0.009684` |

The fail-closed checker passed all stored assertions: final state mismatch below `2e-11`, energy agreement, route-count conservation, dense/hybrid support-count agreement, divergence below `1e-12`, and the expected 20 nonlinear calls per case.

The main new finding is **support-growth behavior**, not performance. With the exact no-pruning trajectory, the retained support reaches all 729 full modes in the retained cube by the end of the first RK4 step for all three held-out cases. Consequently 90%, 90%, and 95% of nonlinear calls use FFT fallback. Only the first one or two calls remain sparse-eligible under the surrogate threshold. This is consistent with the task's prior warning that exact support fills rapidly and strengthens the requirement that any native benchmark report route/support on every nonlinear call.

The three very short total timings have mixed signs: one hybrid trace is faster and two are slower. At this small grid and duration they are noisy and are retained only as raw observations. They do **not** establish a speedup or slowdown theorem.
