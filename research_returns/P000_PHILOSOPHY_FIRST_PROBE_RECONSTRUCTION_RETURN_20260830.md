## 2. Three equivalence notions kept separate

### Cell isomorphism

A `Cell` isomorphism is a bijection of opaque native Cells preserving native adjacency and all tagged local structure.

### Probe-output isomorphism

A probe-output isomorphism preserves the probe type and rooted relational structure but forgets the opaque identity of the root Cell. Thus two roots can return isomorphic observations without being the same native Cell.

### Presentation relabeling

A presentation relabeling changes local display/channel labels and transports all local readouts accordingly. It is neither native Cell equality nor a native motion unless separately proved.

This separation prevents the exact error Q2 is meant to test: observational equality is not silently promoted to native identity.

## 5. Theorem B — minimal radius-one countermodel

Every finite simple 2-regular graph is a disjoint union of cycles of lengths at least three.

For a root in a cycle:

- in `C3`, the radius-one rooted ball is a rooted triangle;
- in every `C_k` with `k>=4`, the radius-one rooted ball is the same rooted three-vertex path with the root in the middle.

Therefore the radius-one observation profile on `U_2REG` remembers only:

1. total number of Cells; and
2. how many Cells lie in triangle components.

It forgets how the remaining non-triangle Cells are glued into cycles.

At `n<8`, the possible cycle partitions do not yet yield two different non-triangle decompositions with the same Cell count:

- `n=6`: `C6` versus `C3 sqcup C3` are separated by triangle balls;
- `n=7`: `C7` versus `C3 sqcup C4` are separated by triangle balls;
- smaller `n` have no competing 2-regular decompositions.

At `n=8`:

\[
C_8,\qquad C_3\sqcup C_5,\qquad C_4\sqcup C_4
\]

are the possibilities. `C3 sqcup C5` is separated by triangle balls, while

\[
\operatorname{Obs}_1(C_8)
=
\operatorname{Obs}_1(C_4\sqcup C_4).
\]

Yet `C8` has one connected component and `C4 sqcup C4` has two.

Hence:

`MINIMAL_RADIUS1_COLLISION_N = 8`

and, at cycle-partition level through `n<=8`, the collision is unique.

## 7. Theorem D — fixed-radius global no-go

The previous repair does not scale to an unbounded model class if the radius is frozen once and for all.

Fix `r>=1` and choose `m>=2r+2`.

Then neither `C_m` nor `C_(2m)` closes within distance `r` of a root. Every rooted radius-`r` ball in both cycles is therefore the rooted path on `2r+1` vertices.

Both

\[

\[

\]

But `X_r` is connected and `Y_r` is disconnected.

Therefore for every fixed finite `r` there are nonisomorphic states with identical `P_r` profiles.

### Consequence

To obtain reconstruction over an unbounded class, at least one of the following must enter the observation language:

1. a neighborhood radius allowed to grow with native component diameter / model size;
2. a genuinely nonlocal path or connectivity probe;
3. a gluing/transport probe retaining enough overlap-composition data to recover global assembly.

Merely choosing a larger but still globally fixed finite radius cannot solve the reconstruction problem.
