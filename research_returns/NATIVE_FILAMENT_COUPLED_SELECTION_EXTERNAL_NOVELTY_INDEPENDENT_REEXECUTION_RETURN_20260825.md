The paper records the classical order-2 cyclotomic-number formulas. For `q∤B`, substituting the quadratic characters of `B` and `-B` gives the packet's local count

`tau_B(q) = [q-3 + (B/q) + (-B/q)]/4`.

The `q|B` case degenerates to two forbidden transverse residue classes, hence `q-2`; the `q=2` split is an elementary parity check. Once these local factors are known:
