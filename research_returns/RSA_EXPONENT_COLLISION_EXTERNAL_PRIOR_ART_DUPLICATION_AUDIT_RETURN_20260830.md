## 1. Global annihilating-exponent layer

Miller's 1976 paper establishes computational-equivalence results around factoring and arithmetic functions including Euler's `phi`. The original RSA paper explicitly observes that knowing the decryption exponent exposes `ed-1`, a multiple of the relevant group period, and points to Miller for factoring.

HAC makes the reduction operational. Chapter 8, Note 8.5 identifies
\[
\lambda(n)=\operatorname{lcm}(p-1,q-1)
\]
as the RSA universal exponent. In the security discussion leading to Fact 8.6, it writes the exposed multiple in the form
\[
ed-1=2^s t,\qquad t\ \text{odd},
\]
chooses random units, performs repeated squaring, and extracts a factor from a nontrivial square root of `1`. It states expected trials `2`.

Therefore the parent statement

\[
x^e=x^{e'}\ \forall x\in(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times
\Longrightarrow
\lambda(n)\mid(e-e')
\Longrightarrow
\text{classical factoring reduction}
\]

is not a novelty-bearing cryptanalytic route. The only parent-specific contribution here is the interpretation of a *global exponent-map collision* as an exposure mechanism for the already-classical annihilator.

Classification: `EXACT_DUPLICATE`.

## 2. Single local-collision iff depth criterion

The parent theorem freezes the exact criterion
\[
\text{split}
\iff
v_2(\operatorname{ord}_p(x))
\ne
v_2(\operatorname{ord}_q(x)).
\]

This criterion is a very clean reformulation of the classical repeated-squaring mechanism. Under CRT, after stripping the odd part of a valid period, the two local components have 2-power orders. A nontrivial square root appears precisely when one coordinate has already become `1` while the other is still `-1`.

Classical Miller/Rabin/HAC proofs and order-finding expositions explicitly reason through the same local-chain mismatch. Ciet et al. then count exactly the bases whose chains fail under a known global `phi(N)` multiple.

However, the parent theorem is formulated under a weaker exposure hypothesis: the transcript need only supply one `x` and one arbitrary valid `x`-specific period `Delta`; it need not supply a multiple of `phi(n)` or `lambda(n)`. In the bounded search I did **not** locate a primary source stating the exact iff in that arbitrary-local-period exposure model.

Accordingly this layer is not classified as an exact duplicate.

Classification: `PARTIAL_ANTECEDENT`.

## 3. Exact random-unit probability — exact antecedent found

This is the strongest audit correction.

Ciet et al. consider
\[
N=\prod_{i=1}^s p_i,\qquad
p_i-1=2^{k_i}q_i
\]
with each `q_i` odd, and a known multiple
\[
\alpha\phi(N)=2^k r,\qquad r\ \text{odd}.
\]

They define the bad bases as those for which the repeated-squaring chain never yields a nontrivial square root, and count them by CRT.

Specialize to `N=pq`. Write
\[
p-1=2^A u_p,\qquad
q-1=2^B u_q,\qquad
m=\min(A,B).
\]
Because the odd part `r` of a multiple of `phi(N)` contains `u_pu_q`,
\[
\gcd(r,u_p)=u_p,\qquad
\gcd(r,u_q)=u_q.
\]

Their bad-set count specializes to
\[
|B(N)|
=
u_pu_q
\left(
1+\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}4^j
\right).
\]
Now
\[
1+\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}4^j
=
1+\frac{4^m-1}3
=
\frac{4^m+2}3.
\]
Since
\[
\phi(N)=2^{A+B}u_pu_q,
\]
the exact bad-base fraction is
\[
\boxed{
\frac{|B(N)|}{\phi(N)}
=
\frac{4^m+2}{3\cdot2^{A+B}}
}.
\]

This is exactly the parent `F1`, not merely a bound and not merely an analogous Monier/Rabin formula.

Classification: `EXACT_DUPLICATE`.

Interpretive consequence: the parent result's exact probability formula should be cited to Ciet et al. 2002 (or an earlier exact source if later found), not presented as newly derived provenance-wise.

## 4. Relation to Rabin–Monier exact liar theory

Rabin 1980 and Monier 1980 form the classical exact-counting tradition around strong probable-prime tests. Monier gives exact strong-liar formulas expressed through the prime factors and their 2-adic structure.

That literature is highly relevant because it confirms that exact 2-adic CRT counting is old. But it studies the strong-probable-prime exponent derived from `n-1`, while the parent result studies a supplied annihilating period/collision transcript.

Thus Rabin–Monier is important contextual prior art, but it should not be used to erase the exposure-model distinction.

Classification relative to the parent local-collision theorem: `ADJACENT_METHOD` / mechanism-level antecedent.

## 5. Multi-certificate lcm aggregation

For supplied certificates `(x_i, Delta_i)`, the parent sets
\[
L=\operatorname{lcm}(\Delta_1,\ldots,\Delta_k).
\]
Because
\[
\operatorname{ord}_n(x_i)\mid\Delta_i\mid L,
\]
`L` annihilates every generator and therefore the abelian subgroup
\[
H=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_k\rangle.
\]

The group-theoretic core is elementary and should not carry a novelty claim: a common multiple of the generator orders annihilates the generated subgroup, and the exponent of a finite abelian subgroup is an lcm of element orders.

What the bounded search did not locate is the exact *cryptographic packaging*:
- each `Delta_i` comes from an independent local exponent collision,
- take their lcm,
- search combinations in the generated subgroup,
- combinations may split even when every supplied generator fails individually.

Classification: `PARTIAL_ANTECEDENT`.

## 6. Diagonal-graph barrier and Goursat

The parent barrier says that every element of the generated 2-primary subgroup fails exactly when
\[
H_2
\]
is the graph of an isomorphism between its two local cyclic projections.

The abstract graph statement is classical. Goursat's lemma characterizes subgroups of a direct product in terms of normal projection kernels and a graph of an isomorphism between quotient projections. If the two projection kernels are trivial, no quotienting remains, so the subgroup itself is a graph of an isomorphism.

Therefore the **group-structural form** is not new.

The RSA-specific step is stronger and more specialized:
\[
\forall h\in H_2,\ 
v_2(\operatorname{ord}(\pi_p h))
=
v_2(\operatorname{ord}(\pi_q h))
\iff
\text{both projection kernels are trivial}
\iff
H_2\text{ is such a graph}.
\]

No searched source was found that states this exact equivalence as the universal failure barrier for collision-driven 2-adic RSA factor extraction.

Classification: `PARTIAL_ANTECEDENT`.
